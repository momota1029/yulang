## Requested scoped decision

For a reusable production Function descriptor at one fixed original
`(nu,K,D)` fiber, what family of independently admitted punctured caller
contexts defines its inlet challenge domain `D_A`?

The callable and whole argument carrier must be plugged directly into the
context holes. Admission must retain the typed path, contract, origin,
continuation, scope, authority and joint dependency evidence and must not
assume that the Function comparison succeeds. Reached-call-sites-only
validity is insufficient for a reusable contract unless the intended theorem
is changed to be program-relative.

## Options and consequences

### 1. All independently well-typed compatible contexts

For each fixed interface and fiber, quantify over every independently typed
compatible punctured context, including contexts not reached in the current
program. Callable and carrier are direct holes; other environment values must
be independently valid and retain correlations, scope and authority rather
than being arbitrary unrelated assignments. Source-constructor witnesses for
the filled carrier are not required when its complete typed observation is
independently admitted under Option 2.

Consequence: the descriptor has a context-independent reusable meaning. The
larger domain strengthens the domain-inclusion obligation and may reject
programs accepted under a narrower challenge family. The theory must define
admissible higher-order environments, correlated values, returned providers
and latent future calls without recursive membership or comparison-success
circularity. No distinguishing supported-input acceptance witness is
currently established.

### 2. A specified source-derived, reusable context family

Quantify over every independently admitted punctured context generated by an
explicit source-typing/context rule family, across programs and unreached
call sites; do not restrict the domain to sites reached in the current
program. The family must be closed under ordinary composition and later
reuse. Its environment rule may admit Option 2 production-only carrier
observations, but cannot require a source-constructor witness for every
filled carrier. State the family and its independent typing/admission
certificates precisely.

Consequence: this may provide a finite derivation interface for source
contexts while preserving reusable Function contracts. It can yield a smaller
challenge domain than A and therefore needs its own proof that all supported
source callers, complete observations, and future uses are covered. It does
not select Theorem C's narrower decorated source/reference envelope as the
production domain automatically.

### 3. Another precise reusable family

Specify the exact context constructors, environment validity and correlations,
admission evidence, closure under caller composition/reuse, and relation to
Option 2 production-only carriers. State whether it is a proper subfamily of
A or B and prove the supported source-call inclusion required by the goal.
