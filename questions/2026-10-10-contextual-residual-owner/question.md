## Options and consequences

1. **Retain constructor lineage as the residual recipe identity.** Keep each residual recipe and its contextual obligations, even when source rows become equal or residual keys match. Canonical source equality must route every applicable current and future lower to every retained recipe. When an actual parent/copy pair qualifies for same-SCC intrusion, equate the row variables while retaining and replaying all recipes, filters, origins, and contextual edges. This avoids assuming that equal source keys prove residual congruence; it requires implementing exact recipe fanout and proving it preserves ordinary inference and principality.

2. **Coalesce recipes with equal residual keys.** Treat `(source identity, retained heads, residual left weight)` as a canonical residual identity. Before implementation, establish that source equality and every future lower/filter/replay observation give an exact congruence, including reverse feedback from gamma uppers, outgoing right contexts, parent/copy SCC equality, freshening, and rollback. This can reduce duplicate recipes but carries the additional congruence obligation; equality of current keys alone is insufficient.

3. **Choose another representation.** State the identity and ownership invariant that it uses, and explain how it preserves both distinct outgoing contexts and the selected parent/copy intrusion behavior. This may avoid either candidate's costs, but needs a reviewed design delta before implementation.
