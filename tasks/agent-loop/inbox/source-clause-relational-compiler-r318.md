## Constructed result and exact parent application

`Clause` is a separately represented source relation-clause syntax, indexed by
complete original catalogues. Its constructors are primitive, Union,
conjunction, scope, whole rename and registered reference. `Table Joint n`
has original source identities, complete interfaces, the original whole action
family, one typed clause per registered key, and catalogue/identity scope
agreement. `Identity` retains operation owner, receiving declaration, clause,
occurrence and original scope. Table extraction, original local semantic law
validity and semantic legality of its substitution family are independent
source-owner duties; these structures do not certify their own authenticity.

`Derivation` is the finite original relation-rule grammar for that fragment.
It mentions no Graph, final contribution, desired attachment, solver success,
Checking, safety, or source typing. Union requires its ORIGINAL selected
child/result relation witness. Conjunction uses ONE shared whole tuple. A
reference or rename transforms that ENTIRE tuple by the original action.
Scopes and output-dependent provider/future fields remain in their original
catalogues. Child and result packets are separate and may contain different
worlds/providers. No selected output or equality between those coordinates is
assumed. Every `n` is quantified; registered cycles are allowed, with finite
positive derivations. No finite unfolding/acyclicity premise is used.

`graph`, `node`, `inventory`, `alternatives` and occurrence-indexed `relations`
are computed. `lookup`, `registration_retained`, `catalogue_retained`,
`scope_retained`, `inventory_retained`, `union_inventory` and `graph_closed`
prove their static retention and registered-edge closure. Original receiving
declaration and primitive operation owner remain separate. All branches stay
in the static graph and inventory even when a license fiber is empty.

`compileDerivation` constructs R296.Contribution by induction on original
Derivation, including source references and both Union alternatives. Its
public theorem `compiled_contribution_exists` quantifies over arbitrary
Joint/n/Table, source key, shared whole tuple, complete packet and original
finite derivation; it takes NO Graph or contribution input.
