by omitting its unknown-rule row. These fail the sorted, scoped hypotheses
or assume the missing introduction. They are audited inference failures,
not admitted Yulang counterexamples or measured test results.

the pinned clause interpretation/finite-derivation convention changes. It

and all-history/world admission, provider descriptor typing, annotation and
mixed/generalized licensing, recursive discharge, principality, soundness,
source adequacy and production conformance. No gate is promoted, and no new
