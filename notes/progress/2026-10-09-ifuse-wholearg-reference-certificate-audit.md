## Derivation from actual IF-Use

interface/evidence telescope, original registration `reg_t`, and an actual
typed reference occurrence `v : Ref(t,m)` at its original scoped substitution.

Write `in_v(k)` for §3.3's unchanged contextual injection of a carried
interface/evidence record `k` at that index, and `pr_v` for the projection
which recovers it. This notation names the selected slot operations, not a
new semantic relation or membership constructor. On their retained image:

```text
extract_v(in_v(k)) = pr_v(in_v(k)) = k
wrap_v(k) = in_v(k)
wrap_v(extract_v(s)) = s                 [s in the in_v image]
```

The first equation follows because the constructor stores the supplied record
unchanged. The second inverse equation follows by inversion of that same
slot constructor. It is not an inverse equation for every satisfying witness
of the containing Call. Other contextual slots and dependent obligations are
not manufactured by `in_v`. Evidence outside the declared exposed boundary
is not reconstructed from its projections.

Construction §6 supplies, for an independently legal whole action `theta`:

```text

pr_(theta v)(theta(in_v(k))) = theta_t(k).
```

Here `theta_t` is the primitive's supplied action, with its actual domain,
license and dependent substitution laws. The second equation follows by
projecting the first. Identity/composition inherit that same law. These are
established consequences of the selected contextual constructors **relative
to their authentic registration/reference and independent action premises**.
They neither prove the primitive's semantic laws nor construct those premises.

For N, §5's argument Obligation introduces its original kernel witness

## Dependent `e_out` is a distinct application

N §5 retains the telescope in dependency order:

```text

e_a : WholeArgCompatible(...)

```

At a fixed prefix/check pair, call its entire remaining fiber
`T_N(prefix,e_f,e_a)`; use `T_O` for the original result/operation/future
remainder. These are abbreviations for the existing dependent fields, not
new admissibility predicates. An `e_a` projection out of a complete N witness
forgets `e_out` and later fields. Its local leaf wrapper cannot reverse that
forgetting. Multiple extensions or no extensions remain possible; the selected
rules require no proof irrelevance, canonical extension or unconditional
inhabitance.

For a full transport the same reference method must be instantiated again:
an authentic result-kernel declaration/application, equality of the complete
result indices after the transported prefix and specific `e_f,e_a`, and
faithful retention of its exposed result/operation/future evidence. Then
`in_O composed pr_N` at that **dependent** result index transports `e_out`;
repeat in telescope order for the remaining operation and suffix fields.
The whole left inverse/action square follows by dependent record induction.
That is a conditional theorem using those paired applications, rather than
a conclusion of checking-slot inversion.

Original source generation §5 packages result CIncl and its operation `e`
inside a supplied decorated envelope. Initial-context §4.4 makes its dependence
on `e_f,e_a` explicit. Neither equates this envelope's complete image/kernel
indices with N's indices. IF's Call frame preserves its authentic operation's
actual returned provider/current world and own future interfaces; it supplies
no cross-operation equality by retaining them.

Thus checking-fiber equality alone does not establish that every N `e_a`
extends at the original consumer, or that the same extension witnesses are
retained. A successful actual Call and semantic applicability still need
