Computation interface changes the supplied cut. It cannot be advertised as
a counterexample at this fixed e0.

## What a source falsifier must independently supply

For a shortcut claiming to establish original association, a positive source
specimen needs independent source/descriptor/admission premises and a typed
original incidence against which the shortcut can disagree. If instead the
attack claims the required fiber is empty, it needs an independently justified
original clause interpretation and exhaustive exclusion of every original
witness. A supplied opaque primitive, a missing derivation or arbitrarily
assigned false predicate provides no such exclusion.

At the association cut, even conditionally granting complete CALL_TYPE,
the required object remains:

```text
exists (t,w) in I_orig(X).
  t = (beta,s,p0,c)
  and original source ownership / stage / view / scope / provider typing
  and complete-family coverage at the same xi and original binders
```

The display abbreviates the existing fiber obligation; it does not define
I_orig. Source-contract §2.1 supplies independent typed owner/view kernels
as input. Section 2.2 separately requires independently interpreted DescMem;
§3.5 assumes those inputs and the local typing lemmas. Consequently replaying
the constructor inventory cannot validate a candidate original incidence
whose independent interpretation is the disputed premise.

These rows are a bounded feasibility audit, not seven source counterexamples.
In particular, choosing two slots for the one occurrence, zero Slots for the

assign the missing kernel. Complete unknown licensing arms are not silently
excluded. Source-contract §3.7 W/Z/G are a conditional sufficient abstraction

of this source is empty: complete families retain pending observations,
prefixes and future use. No empty full family or empty admitted original
kernel is asserted as a Yulang model.
