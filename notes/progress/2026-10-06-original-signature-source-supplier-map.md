[SV](2026-10-06-directional-source-view-instantiation-construction.md)
§§3,6 supplies Pending receipt, actual result/rebind, capture/read and paired
history extension **on independently typed original rows and compatible
profiles**. The carrier result lift and rebind are source-grounded; prospective
positions do not become actual receiver/result facts. SV therefore adds a
transport supplier, not an original applicability supplier. Its reverse
proof recovers the input profile and cannot establish that profile's original
exhaustive interpretation.

Let `L_C(X,a)` denote original independently interpreted licensing of a
beta-owned slot/contribution incidence on the whole row `X`. This name is a
required interface, not a newly defined predicate. The current missing law
must provide a source constructor/identity, upper contribution, original
signature-position correspondence and scope for every incidence, with both
coverage directions:

```text
GeneratedOriginalIncidence_C(X,a) => L_C(X,a)   [no fabricated incidence]
L_C(X,a) => GeneratedOriginalIncidence_C(X,a)   [exhaustive inversion]
```

It must retain one `X` containing original `xi,U_u,providers,world`, original
binder dependencies and all complete semantic obligations. Inherited packets
must keep their source arms. Neither a separate witness per slot nor a new
completion per history is permitted.

The bounded derivation is: current structural projections expose inputs but
produce no L_C conclusion; H_U/H_S constructs the one known member; H_T and SV
retain an independently supplied incidence; policy assembly quantifies over
supplied licensing; semantic comparison checks interfaces without creating
licensing. Composing those interfaces yields the known member and its images,
but leaves the exhaustive L_C premise at the original signature introduction.
This is an interface-level nonderivation for the audited composition, not an
impossibility result for every conceivable extension of the source rules.

Candidate assumption **not adopted**: `Slots(beta)={a0}`. It would supply the
missing converse by declaration. Equal solved endpoints, a known returned

results retain their prior reviewed bounded scopes; this correspondence
