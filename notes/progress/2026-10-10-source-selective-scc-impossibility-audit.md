## Exact exclusions and the remaining induction step

The previous local exclusions remain valid only with their original stability
hypotheses: a fresh negative checking port has a different negative structural
copy from its positive incoming-incidence copy; a singleton formal self-tail
incidence has literally identical owner and tail parent pairs; and an isolated
fresh owner/tail pair copied together at t cannot capture the owner's incoming
Allowance on the unchanged owner by traversing a shared copied tail. An
intervening selective merge or other captured bound changes those premises.

Canonical row/row admission always stores its physical edge on the row of
greater level (or on the lower endpoint when levels are equal). Therefore those
new direct row edges do not increase level at admission. Extrusion's new
source→copy edge also does not increase level. Neither fact proves that every
row reachable from a copy at t has level at most t: a physical Support or
Allowance edge can reach a younger tail through a reused older row whose live
bounds extrusion never walked.

In particular, suppose computation Q at d has `Q + Support(w[X_d])`, and its
real initializer link admits `Q <: B_t`, where t<d. The link puts `Q - B_t`
on Q and opposite replay can admit the **same** Support to B, creating
`B + Support(w[X_d])`. No Effect-view copy occurs on this route. Thus the
candidate transitions permit `B_t -> Support(w[X_d]) -> X_d`. Group and
initializer slot20 are actual source owners of these computation links.
This is a conditional constructor derivation, not a separately executed
source that realizes every hypothesis or an exact C→S return.

Likewise a true ordinary task `R_t <: S_d` can put `S + R` on S; with
`S - Allowance(v[T_d])` it can replay an Allowance onto R. Whether a
**different** actual tail X on R has a route back to that exact S is the
remaining source premise, not a permanent polarity prohibition at R.
Transported/freshened bounds and all previously qualifying merges must also
be included before claiming a stable-level universal invariant.

The sharply localized residual is therefore successful source generation of
an ascending view-tail edge on a reused old row reachable from exact C,
followed by a real continuation to exact S independent of `(T',T)`.
Its owning operations are the computation-link/opposite replay combination
and source-scoped computation annotation or retained/freshened view. The
two-tail recursive candidate attacks that producer by intending X→S through
cb checking and intending R→X through the body annotation. Parsing blocks
that particular spelling before those premises can be inspected. This is a
reduced unclosed producer, not a quantified impossibility result. No new
semantic restriction or annotation repair is proposed.
