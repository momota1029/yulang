readiness defect in the frozen package: the decision draft and proof define
different role/binder compatibility envelopes.

- The design table supports an `int` Value receiver, limited `int`/unit Value
  parameters, and wildcard-annotated `int` Computation parameters. It places
  type/arity mismatches outside this pilot.

  Computation receivers, and gives arity/type mismatches explicit rejection

  from the one offered for adoption.

  pilot even though `Box` is not in the compatibility envelope.

This is an unresolved design boundary, not an implementation defect. Closure
requires one exact envelope and mismatch rule, aligned through design, proof,
examples and adoption questions, followed by fresh review. R81 expressly
