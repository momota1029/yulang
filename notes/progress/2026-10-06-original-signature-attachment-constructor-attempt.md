## Construct the contribution kernel at the entry cut

Retain the prior source templates `J_f=Result(Name f)` and `J_x=Result(Name x)`.
Their exact returning-Name realization and callee unit-law reduction are inputs
from the original-view-table note; this note does not repeat that proof.

In the actual provider's Value-entry skeleton, receipt precedes the designated
argument Force. Cut **after the separate typed result rebind**; the continuation
`kappa` contains the body and subsequent complete-invocation remainder, and
contains neither that Force nor its rebind. Retain these distinct operands:

```text
z = (returned value v, returned typed packet pi_F, original result roots)
S_F = current source configuration and receiver environment after Force
eta = established original receipt witness
r_F = actual provider receiver identity; V = actual executing view references
Theta = (eta,r_F,V,original lineage/scopes,shared X and xi)

RebindResultPath_X(J,z,S_F;Theta) = Return(a,S_R)
a = (v,pi_R,rho_R,typed path map M,rebind witness zeta_R,
     original dependent roots and packet/receipt/receiver lineage)
S_R = configuration and receiver environment after the actual typed rebind

kappa_X(J,a,S_R;Theta)
```

The displayed Return of rebind is proof notation for its successful ordinary
relation and state-threaded sequencing, not a new runtime operation or a claim
that rebind can be fused into Force. If that independent relation has no
witness, the body continuation cannot be entered. The exact relation retains
the returned source packet and its matching result-path image at the receiving
parameter, the environment binding, the same original receipt and receiver
references, current source state, and all original xi/K/D dependencies.
Any independently present beta lineage remains its own reference; r_F is not
identified with beta's original receiver. This does not create an original
profile or a receipt from payload equality.

J remains the original whole received carrier even when the body binding
exposes only its result: receipt/control/dependency data may still refer to J.
`kappa` is obtained from the provider remainder under H1/H2, not recovered from
a completed source run. Define the two existing sequential continuations:

```text
Suffix_X(J,z,S_F;Theta) =
  RebindResultPath_X(J,z,S_F;Theta)
    >>= (lambda(a,S_R). kappa_X(J,a,S_R;Theta))

Ext_X(J,kappa,S_0;Theta) =
  Force_argument_X(J,S_0;Theta)
    >>= (lambda(z,S_F). Suffix_X(J,z,S_F;Theta))
```

These are expansions of Force **then RebindResultPath then kappa**, with no
premise that Force has already rebound its returned packet. Their conditional
equations keep both sequential rule applications explicit:

```text
Force_argument_X(J,S_0;Theta) = Return(z,S_F)
  => Ext_X(J,kappa,S_0;Theta) = Suffix_X(J,z,S_F;Theta)

RebindResultPath_X(J,z,S_F;Theta) = Return(a,S_R)
  => Suffix_X(J,z,S_F;Theta) = kappa_X(J,a,S_R;Theta)

Force_argument_X(J,S_0;Theta) = Request(q,S_q,k_q)
  => Ext_X(J,kappa,S_0;Theta) = Request(q,S_q,
       lambda(response,S').
         k_q(response,S') >>=
           (lambda(z,S_F). Suffix_X(J,z,S_F;Theta)))
```

Thus an entry request saves the unfinished Force followed by **typed rebind
and kappa**. Resumption uses its actual current S', keeps eta/r_F/Theta and
all original packet/environment/operation/xi dependencies, and establishes
neither a replacement receipt nor a rebind before Force actually returns.
The successful rebind separately determines S_R and a; it is not inferred by
renaming the Force result z.

The complete invocation retains its original receipt establishment and
executing CallView around this extension in their existing positions. No
boundary, consumer or native return is moved across the cut. `Ext`, `Suffix`
and the annotated rebind notation expand existing constructor relations;
they are proof notation, not new source rules or executable wrappers.

This is a relational family on X, not a map choosing independently compatible
ports. Its instance at `J=Delay(J_x)` is the exact source operand. Other J are
parameters of the independent complete-call kernel; no such J is thereby
admitted at U, reachable in the exact inner call, or source-generated.
The exact source's inner Name carrier returns; a request made by a caller's
carrier during step's own entry is a different execution position.

The contribution operand can therefore be emitted as one source-labelled

original provider/owner/view predicates. It can be emitted unsolved. It is

## Exhaustive inversion of the extension, and its exact limit

Induct on finite derivations of this **fixed constructor extension**. Preserve
the concrete continuation frame and the original typed witnesses.

1. The complete invocation's entry premise supplies the actual receiver r_F,
   original receipt eta, view/control references and the initial environment
   in Theta. Extension inversion carries that premise; it cannot derive an
   established receipt from the carrier or a Force Return.
2. A Return from the designated Force extracts z and S_F. The recovered suffix
   is `Suffix_X(J,z,S_F;Theta)`, not kappa. No provisional/Pending result or
   payload equality authorizes a rebound body binding.
3. **Typed rebind has its own rule inversion.** A successful suffix that enters
   kappa must first supply an actual
   `RebindResultPath_X(J,z,S_F;Theta)=Return(a,S_R)` witness. Inversion extracts
   its input pi_F/result path, matching map M, output pi_R/environment rho_R,
   rebind witness zeta_R, actual S_F-to-S_R transition and original
   receipt/receiver/lineage/xi incidences. Only then does the next premise
   enter `kappa_X(J,a,S_R;Theta)`. If the rebind premise is absent, no post-rebind
   derivation is recovered. A total invocation Return also requires the
   remainder's real consumer/return witnesses.
4. An exposed entry Request extracts the same q, operation witness, original
   raw handle and pending Force-plus-RebindResultPath-plus-kappa suffix. A
   typed response/resumption continues at current S', retains J and Theta,
   and reuses the saved suffix. Invert the shorter development inductively;
   receipt is not replayed and rebind still waits for actual Force Return.
5. A proper finite prefix may end during Force or after its Return but before
   a successful typed rebind. The former remains in Force; the latter retains
   z/S_F and the pending rebind/suffix. Neither creates a body binding or a
   completed invocation output. Arbitrarily long nonreturning Force executions
   are represented by finite prefixes; no single infinite witness is inferred.
6. A transition/emission handled inside Force uses the supplied local
   execution-context kernel with the same outer rebind-plus-kappa frame and
   Theta. Its pre-dispatch observation is retained even if no outward Request
   survives. The displayed Return/Request equations alone do not prove this
   local-kernel case. No new request/handler rule for rebind is introduced.
7. A development after successful rebind inverts the independently interpreted
   kappa remainder with its actual a and S_R. This includes body, native return,
   consumer, latent construction or an original raw resumption. Latent return
   executes no future latent port; future use needs its own typed premise.

The phase alternatives are Force, Force Return awaiting typed rebind, successful
rebind and the subsequent remainder; suspension/internal emission remains in
its original phase. H2/H3 still supply local primitive/receipt/rebind rules.
This is not exhaustive inversion of their entire source-typing/handler calculus.
Reconstruction applies both ordinary sequential composition rules with the
**recovered rebind witness** between them. Both directions retain original xi,
provider/carrier, packet and environment witnesses, actual current-state
transitions, receipt/receiver references, original operation/response witnesses
and the actual typed rebind map. No bijection or rebind totality follows merely
from the Force return equation; the result is conditional on those rule inputs.

Under H3 a matching entered upper CallView at p0 observes entry-Force emissions
as well as body/consumer emissions before dispatch. This is conditional typed
observation, not protection of every provider-origin event. Any operative
`Path`/`Inc_C` still needs the original profile, matching receipt and exact live
receiver/handler. The actual f invocation receiver is not identified with
beta's original receiver or with the invocation receiving the captured f view.
No profile is pasted onto f's private bindings. Provider-lower marks keep their
own origin; the upper constructor adds no protection to their lower position.

## Falsifier, failure conditions and unverified scope

A falsifier of the new extension claim would be a finite complete Value-entry
derivation with H1–H3's same receiver/control kernel whose designated Force,
actual separate rebind or remaining suffix cannot be reconstructed by the
Force-then-RebindResultPath-then-kappa frame with its original witness. A second
falsifier is an extension derivation entering kappa without an actual matching
rebind witness. Reconstruction must not change returned/rebound packets, typed
path map, receiver environment, receipt/receiver lineage, event/operation
witness, current-state transition, native delimiter, xi or pre-dispatch
observation. This must be a whole derivation on X, not a changed provider
or a newly chosen xi.

Analytical mutations expose its premises: delete J from the continuation
index and carrier-dependent receipt evidence can be lost; use just the payload
value instead of the separate z-to-a rebind witness and typed rebound lineage
can be lost; skip RebindResultPath after Force Return and invent a body binding;
replace the saved request suffix by kappa alone and omit the suspended typed
rebind; drop current S' or S_R and resumption/remainder uses stale state;
move the consumer across native return and the control witness changes; omit internal emissions and outward support loses
observations; apply the extension to retained entry and an entry Force is
invented. These were not executed mutations or admitted source counterexamples.

A failure of attachment soundness/exhaustiveness would instead require a typed
original attachment/licensing witness and its source rule on the same X.
No such counterexample or complete-row witness was constructed. Independent
admission of J at U, complete profiles, original row nonemptiness, generic
actual entries, annotations, recursive/generalized source formation, all-view
principality and production A/2 containment remain unverified. Q is never an
admission or attachment premise here. No solver algorithm or rejection policy
is inferred.
