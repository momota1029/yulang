# 2026-09-17

## Syntax completion-policy reset

The user explicitly approved ending the exhaustive pre-implementation CST-slot evidence loop after it consumed the available model/review budget with diminishing architectural information.

Recorded decision:

- retain the one-lossless-Rowan-CST architecture, structural `Missing`/raw `Error`/`Invalid` recovery facts, accepted-input/recovery contracts and eventual parser-ledger retirement;
- freeze the current accepted grammar/direct-CST topology as `syntax-v0` for the next implementation phase;
- replace the old requirement to audit every recovery-bearing slot before implementation with representative structural proof;
- permit a deterministic generic CST-derived structural diagnostic for uncataloged occurrences, with no parser-ledger/relex/replay fallback;
- treat only a concrete structural collision, accepted-input bug, active vertical-slice requirement, explicit new feature or release certification as a reason to reopen syntax/schema design;
- stop using unmapped catalog rows as an automatic queue;
- move next to a shadow CST diagnostic interpreter, then effective-table unification and a smallest-existing-fixture CST -> HIR/type vertical slice.

Authority: `notes/design/2026-09-17-syntax-freeze-and-vertical-implementation-amendment.md`.

Repository record synchronization replaces the accumulated `tasks/current.md` history with a concise active-task map. The old task log remains available in git history and older durable evidence under `notes/progress/`.

No compiler/runtime code changes are part of this record-only transition, so no build, test suite or benchmark is required for this commit.

## Gate 1 shadow CST diagnostic interpreter — walk shape fixed

Working tree change: `crates/yu-syntax/src/tests/recovery_output.rs` (test-only).

Gate 1 itself is not closed. The change fixes the *shape* of the future
whole-tree walk that the CST-derived interpreter will perform, and proves that
the CST alone can supply every fact that shape needs. No production module was
touched; the temporary parser ledger was not consulted or altered.

The walk shape now proved from CST alone:

- one preorder pass over every Rowan `syntax` child, `Missing` nodes as
  zero-width `Missing` occurrences;
- structured `Invalid` nodes as one `Error` occurrence whose extent is the
  node's own range, followed by their nested content;
- raw `Error` **tokens** grouped into maximal adjacent runs at one immediate
  parent, reported as one occurrence over the run's byte extent;
- a source/preorder ordinal that keeps same-slot same-offset encounters
  distinct.

Two structural facts the walk relies on were confirmed rather than assumed:

1. Every structured `Invalid` is the outermost recovery node at its offset.
   Direct children of both `Root` and a `ParenthesizedExpression` hold at most
   one node whose range starts at a given offset, so occurrences at one offset
   are never ambiguous between siblings.
2. A raw run of several adjacent `Error` leaves is already one committed
   record. Grouping the leaves is therefore a faithful CST-derived
   reconstruction and not a lossy merge; the leaves of one group always belong
   to one immediate parent.

One concrete collision was found and is recorded as an operator-level, not
structural, hazard: two encounters of the *same* recovery kind at the *same*
offset would be indistinguishable to the walk. The reachable structural space
avoids it (fact 1 above), and no observed accepted-input recovery produces it.
It must be reported as a collision, not silently merged, if a future owner ever
produces it.

The retained focused evidence is the required subset from `tasks/current.md`:
precise catalog match, same-slot/same-offset nesting, maximal raw group,
structured `Invalid` over a deliberately uncataloged slot, multi-byte and CRLF
range witnesses, and a deterministic generic-fallback sample.

Verification: `cargo test -p yu-syntax --lib shadow_walk` passes (3 tests);
`cargo test -p yu-syntax` is 1435 passed / 6 failed, and all 6 failures
(`owners::delimited_owner_consumes_wrong_closes_before_settling_its_own_close`,
`owners::parenthesized_primary_owns_its_sequence_and_outer_ml_tail`,
`type_expr::parenthesized_close_{after_separator,initial,malformed_retry,post_head}`)
reproduce unchanged on a stashed working tree, so they are pre-existing on
`yulang3` and are not caused by this change. `cargo fmt --check -p yu-syntax`
is clean. No production behaviour, snapshot or diagnostic expectation changed.

Remaining Gate 1 work: the production walk does not exist yet. This change
only carries the test-level proof of its shape.
