### Exploratory adjudication (superseded)

Every candidate wall-time range overlaps its paired baseline range. Timing noise remains visible,
especially in the direct cases, but there is no separated-run regression signal. Peak RSS remains
at the same scale: the largest 1k candidate increase is 256 KiB; the 10k direct peak differs by at
most 4 KiB, while the 10k AST candidate peaks remain within or below the baseline ranges.

Static inspection found no runtime companion mode, trait object, function-pointer/closure field,
ordinary-path Derives probe, second result vector, replay, rescan, clone, cache, or extra direct-CST
allocation. The synthetic AST path owns the one required result vector and item boxes; its direct
path streams once without constructing or replaying that vector. Gate 10 must repeat the
companion-heavy measurements through the real production companion entrypoint.

The three-run ranges above were retained as exploratory evidence, but the independent performance
audit rejected them as insufficient for the §14 rollback decision: 10k medians were slower in all
four ordinary paths, the host noise was large, and three pairs could not separate ordering effects
from a small regression. The following predeclared pinned protocol supersedes that provisional
adjudication.

- run two warmup rounds, then 24 measured rounds across all eight ordinary cases;
