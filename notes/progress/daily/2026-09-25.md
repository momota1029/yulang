## F5c flat indexed stack-independent design direction

The user chose to explore deep F5c support with explicit work stacks instead
of a fixed structural-depth limit. A Sol architect review found that true
stack independence must cover ownership and destruction as well as visitation:
F5c drafts need to remain flat from production through normalization,
finalization input, and checked-error cleanup. Pair the solver-owned flat
draft with a `yu-types`-owned indexed finalization transaction; an iterative
callback over the existing recursive boxes is insufficient.

Prepared the design-only M3 draft in
`notes/design/2026-09-25-f5c-flat-indexed-stack-independent-draft.md`. It
preserves practical-input semantics, Q/R and normalized order, §44's
first-member representative, and atomic route/publication behavior. It does
not yet settle a deterministic charged-work limit, and it does not weaken the
existing F5 §26/§34 resource contracts. The M3 review and two focused delta
rounds are complete with no remaining blocking/major finding in assigned
scope. The proposal is Reviewed, not implementation-authoritative. The user
approved the first-stage practical-source, charge-site, and scale/resource
investigation only; numeric limits and separate approval remain prerequisites
to production code. No production-path prototype, API, or semantic change is
authorized.

## F5c first-stage source/scale investigation

The read-only source audit found 73 stable-core cases (16 public-signature
fixtures); their source/expected-signature surface is small, but there is no
compiler CLI/corpus harness connecting it to expanded F5c graph size or work.
Repository evidence supports only a repository-bounded envelope, not the
distribution of external Oracle inputs. Existing stress tests cover selected
deep traversals and capacity ledgers but not exact independent counts for
shared-DAG path amplification, deep candidate deduplication, R fixed-point
replay, or a proposed indexed finalizer.

The source/charge-site and indexed-validation findings are recorded in §13 of
the design draft. They require one checked solver work meter charging candidate
pairs and structural comparison visits, actual summary-edge expansions,
root-local analysis/replay/substitution/R visits, and copied replay elements.
The proposed indexed validation `O(V+E+B+Q)` bound is conditional on direct
dense Q/R indexing and iterative three-color DFS; the current callback's nested
linear searches do not establish it. Existing F5a callback acceptance of
ordered R-bound subsets remains unchanged.

Verification: `cargo test -p yu-solver --lib f5c_ -- --test-threads=1` passed
162 tests (0.64 s); `cargo test -p yu-solver --lib f5c_deep --
--test-threads=1` passed 2 tests. These are correctness checks, not benchmark
samples. No peak-memory or timing experiment was run; no code/API, numeric
boundary, or F5 clause changed. Next gate: propose a focused test-only scale
probe plan, without a production-path prototype, then independently review
repository-bounded results. F5c/F5e closure remains open.
