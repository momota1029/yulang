## Candidate and exact obligations

Let `F_C` denote that original residual. A candidate solver takes its finite
source-owned syntax and authentic finite input assumptions `Gamma`; it returns
a finite checked derivation of `F_C` with original-scoped joint evidence, or a
checked derivation of its falsity. Any symbolic input has an independent
interpretation contract covering the actual fixed imports/worlds. An opaque
predicate name, validity flag or oracle is not such a contract.

The candidate uses symbolic variables for universal inputs. An existential
witness can be a finite term or finite proof-directed recipe using only
earlier inputs/witnesses at its original scope. Such a term denotes an
operation on arbitrary admitted objects; the compiler need not enumerate or
encode each object. Its interpretation, legality and effective construction
must themselves be proved. A recipe that inspects a semantic parameter still
needs an effective justified inspection operation; writing it symbolically
does not make it computable.

Four obligations suffice for this proposed route:

1. **Actual input correspondence.** Source generation supplies `F_C`, `Gamma`
   and their independent meanings, preserving all emitted observers and
   original dependencies. Each actual semantic-stage input fits the effective
   interface; a conditional input supplier is not a construction.
2. **Sound joint proof rules.** Every rewrite, theory rule and proof constructor
   is independently sound on complete original tuples under every legal
   interpretation of `Gamma`. Universal rules quantify over the full original
   domain; recursive/future rules use the selected fixed-point meaning.
   Shared existential evidence cannot be independently selected for conjuncts.
   Rewrites preserve the original witness fiber or supply explicit lawful
   forward/backward transport when exact solving needs both directions.
3. **Effective complete search on actual emitted residuals.** A terminating
   algorithm finds the required decision and evidence for every input in the
   governing admitted source envelope. The finite derivation language must
   express a lawful effective strategy whenever this contract requires one.
   Completeness is joint, including interacting structural/effect/profile/
   guard/admission predicates; separate pointwise atom checks do not imply it.
   Bounded proof search can return Unknown, but that is not this decision law.
4. **Result interpretation.** Checking/replaying the finite result constructs
   one coherent original-scope witness family, preserves alias/provider/
   carrier/scope/K,D incidence, and establishes every active obligation at the
   actual semantic result. Production consumption and atomic publication
   remain separate correspondence/lifecycle obligations.

These premises require no finite prefix-state carrier, prefix surjection,
computable semantic quotient, or concrete universal-challenge coding. They do
require an effective finite representation of the *returned proof/recipe*.
This is different from encoding every admitted input or every possible
semantic strategy. Merely retaining an unsolved `F_C` is exact residual
presentation, already distinguished from satisfiability decision by open
factorization §4; it does not close JOINT_DEC.

## Conditional derivation and discriminating case

**Claim class: unreviewed conditional theorem.** Obligations 1, 2 and 4 imply
accepted-result safety at this semantic seam; adding obligation 3 gives an
effective complete solver in its exact stated envelope. No premise is proved
for full Yulang here.

Induct on a returned finite proof. Each primitive step uses obligation 2 on
the same whole tuple. Boolean steps preserve the common earlier environment;
conjunction composes evidence with its original sharing. At `forall x`, the
eigenvariable rule proves the body under the independent legality hypothesis
for arbitrary `x`, then discharges that hypothesis over every admitted `x`.
At `exists y`, a lawful witness recipe scoped only over the earlier environment
proves the body at that original `y`. Any branch test is likewise defined only
on that earlier environment. Thus an earlier witness cannot depend on a later
challenge. Fixed-point rules require their independent semantic soundness
lemma; finite proof syntax does not supply it. Obligation 4 interprets the
checked proof/evidence at the actual inputs. Obligation 3, rather than proof
checking alone, supplies termination and the required successful search.

The discrimination is the generic equality sequent

```text
forall x in A. exists y in A. y = x
```

for an arbitrary independently interpreted domain `A`. A finite derivation
introduces arbitrary `x`, chooses `y=x`, and uses reflexivity. The recipe is
the identity operation, covers every admitted object, keeps the original
`forall`–`exists` dependency and handles empty `A` by universal vacuity. No
classifier or concrete-input encoding is needed. This demonstrates an
algorithmic proof shape, not a Yulang source theorem or a refutation of EPR.
Adding an original leaf `G(x,y)` leaves `G(x,x)` to prove; reflexivity proves
no arbitrary `G`. The exact premise is a source-independent, uniformly valid
law for that actual leaf on its complete tuple. This derivation therefore
does not repeat the reviewed halting or Unit/Bool quotient attacks.

The accepted noncomputable-response example in the constructive attempt
§3.1 still applies to unrestricted strategy extraction: a direct solver cannot
emit a computable response to `H(n)` merely by putting `H` in term syntax.
That prior result is cited, not reconstructed. No Yulang undecidability or
impossibility of a direct route follows.

## Source evidence, classification and stopping point

The inspected source records supply finite joint syntax and scoped exact
factorization under their stated premises. They do not supply exhaustive
primitive meanings or an effective complete symbolic calculus. The
[admission audit](2026-10-05-residual-admission-source-premise-audit.md),
“Result” and “Minimum missing source rule,” identifies varying-endpoint
Guard/Phi/K,D and derived-context laws as unsupplied in its cited sources.
[Context closure](../design/2026-10-03-source-context-finite-closure.md) §§2–3
has supplied-template premises; it is not a decision law for future/admission
leaves. This is bounded documentary evidence, not an exhaustive compiler or
all-source primitive inventory.

**A:** complete-tuple rule soundness, lawful original dependency/evidence,
accepted-result reflection and safe production consumption. **B:** effective
complete search and representable successful recipes for the actual admitted
source residuals required by the language. **C:** completeness for unrelated
arbitrary predicate theories, or representation of every semantic strategy
regardless of active observations. Arbitrary tuples of *actual active*
predicates remain A/B; they are not reclassified as C. Genuine retained
construction evidence can avoid D reconstruction debt but cannot manufacture
a missing primitive law. No canonical gate changes class or status here.

This method removes the quotient's cell/prefix-classification obligations;
it relocates the essential semantic work to sound, effective and complete
joint symbolic rules. The precise current blocker is the missing actual
primitive rule/interpretation supplier and its joint completeness argument,
not finite state counts. Another evaluator that assumes those rules would
leave the same premise untouched. Stop this lane at that cut.

One recommended next action: using the primary's actual-emitted observer
inventory, select one active non-name admission/future predicate and construct
its proof-producing symbolic rule at the owning independent definition,
including all operands and original binder dependencies; establish its
uniform soundness and exact success/failure completeness before extending the
direct solver. No production implementation follows from this recommendation.
