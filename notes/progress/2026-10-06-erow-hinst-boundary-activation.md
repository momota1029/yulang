   This is §10's invocation schematic instantiated to the receiving function,

## Minimal missing premise and concrete obligation

For the single original g/p targeted here, the missing certificate can be
stated without presupposing b or its transported incidence:

```text
ResolvedSourceUse_xi(delta_use, beta, Slots(beta), F_cb)
CurrentReceiverEntry(delta_use, r)
CompletedOriginalProfile_xi(beta, p, g, occurrence, scope)
------------------------------------------------------------------ [OPEN producer]
SourceBoundaryBinding_xi(delta_use, r, a, Gamma, source_view, endpoints)

required output facts:
  a realizes original beta in this actual r;
  Gamma(p) retains g, occurrence, scope and original predicate identities;
  source_view/endpoints belong to this use and the source domain of M;
  original K,D remain jointly scoped under this same nu;
  no premise or selected completion depends on pending comparison Q.
```

The displayed predicates name proof obligations over existing objects. They
are candidate notation, not approved typing rules, runtime records, solver
atoms or a new evidence carrier. CurrentReceiverEntry identifies an actual
source entry; it is not defined by an arbitrary r being active. The output
is the factorization's activation certificate specialized to this g/p.

The concrete next proof obligation is to provide a derivation for that
horizontal line from resolution and typed elaboration of one known
callback-receiving application. It must show where the static-to-dynamic
binding is generated and preserved before Receive, with the profile
attached to the same original use/map domain. Full inventory and arbitrary
scheme instantiation are separate larger obligations. Naming
`establish source boundary instances`, or defining “resolved use” to include
the output certificate, supplies rather than proves this line.

Conditionally, this certificate plus §6 introduction gives fresh b and its
original profile recording; the supplied M then gives this tagged source's
target incidence by relational image with K unchanged and its D incidences
transported. A multi-source target can also contain other sources' images;
no equality with this source's image alone is required. Those are Hinst's
conjuncts. This consequence is already the factorization's result, not a new
source theorem. Event Observe, matching receipt/Path, current activity and
handler ownership remain additional Grant premises.
