
The following are **candidate operational hypotheses**, not authenticated

1. Ordinary original admissions have stored B(s,+,x) and B(s,-,A(v)). Their
   replay child `x <= A(v)` is genuinely queued, but has not dispatched when

   x has no other upper. All contexts in this minimized discriminator are

   the incoming source s in this one map. The compared receiver and source
   Values do not qualify these parent/copy pairs for intrusion. No negative
   occurrence or other selected bound introduces another upper on x'.
3. After extrusion, the actually queued original `x <= A(v)` finishes through
   ordinary dispatch and the typed drain succeeds. Subsequently a fresh
   unmatched Contribution q, with effect(q) outside J, is admitted **directly
   to x'** through the ordinary typed Effect owner. No intervening admission
   touches s' with a new upper, merges these source rows, fresh-restores the
   copied graph, or otherwise installs an upper on x'. That later drain also
   completes successfully.

## Strongest alternative routes and exact conditions

The obstruction deliberately distinguishes original admission from lower
forwarding, and remapping from reuse:

| Alternative | Why it succeeds, or why the conditional trace does not invoke it |
| --- | --- |
| Original symbolic cut registered before g scan | The (g,x,v) incidence reconstructs B(x',-,A(v')); ordinary later q on x' replays it. This defeats the candidate. |
| x reused at/below target | Then x'=x. Completing the delayed original cut installs the executable upper on that same row; later q reaches it. This defeats the candidate. |
| q admitted to original x after the scan | It meets the original cut and/or the actual x-to-x' link. Original unmatched forwarding to g meets B(g,-,g') through ordinary admission. This defeats the candidate; the candidate requires direct use of genuinely copied x'. |
| Original unmatched q already forwarded before g snapshot | Positive g copying selects that physical residual into g'. This defeats the old saturated-atom witness. Here no q exists before the copy. |

| Later upper admitted ordinarily on s' | It replays the stored x' lower; if it is the copied cut, x' gains the required registration. This defeats the candidate. Bare IncomingAllowance initialization did not invoke that owner. |
| Actual qualified equality or fresh-bound restoration | These have explicit Cartesian/replay owners and can repair the pair. Their actual occurrence must be included in a source witness, never excluded by changing the selected SCC rule. They are absent only by the conditional history's hypothesis. |
| Reverse use of s'-lower x' or original x-to-x' link | Insertion writes one physical owner-side bound (`candidate_extrusion.rs:409–570`). Replay reads the admitted owner's opposite side. ## Ordered context and typed-drain limits

IDENTITY minimizes the conditional obstruction; it is not an assumption that
all original source protection is identity. Successful replay uses exact
ordered ContextExpr::Replay(lower,upper), except the explicit identity/identity
shortcut (`candidate_context.rs:3131–3213`). Copy transport carries the
original bound fiber's post-check context and source association
(3403–3416); a source/copy link has the actual extrusion processing fiber.
The positive path through x-to-x' followed by the copied cut therefore forms
nested ordered Replay nodes. An equality between that operation and a direct
pair cannot be inferred from endpoint support or dependency ancestry.

Ordinary atomic forwarding calls enqueue_task; its child context is admitted
from the child pair while Derived retains ancestry separately
(`candidate_context.rs:2754–2776`, `lib.rs:12884–12920`). This does not by
itself transport an arbitrary parent operation. The detached evaluator's mix
law is not an independent proof of live source/consumer correspondence.

The actual live context checker accepts a restricted zero-word fragment:
`candidate_zero_word_filters` at 2833–2923 validates prefix weights, replay
children and directed residuals, and rejects nonzero words, unsupported forms,
or mixed replay-filter/directed-residual cases. An arbitrary ContextExpr with
the proposed shape cannot simply be declared successfully processed. The
conditional identity trace therefore establishes no nonidentity failure or
all-context repair theorem. For an extension with nonidentity context, both
its authentic constructor and successful live checker path remain necessary.

Replay children transfer with acknowledgements into the actual FIFO typed
worklist (`candidate_context.rs:3281–3344`, `lib.rs:12837–12845,12894–12920`).
Transport and Context precede Effect dispatch (`lib.rs:12072–12119`);
acknowledgements and diagnostics finish before normal drain return. This
ensures completion of emitted original-cut work and the later q admission
under the hypotheses; it does not create the absent x'-cut comparison.
Memo checks use the exact admitted relation and current generation
(`lib.rs:11513–11525`); no endpoint-only suppression is used as a failure
premise. Suspension, abnormal paths and all-history memo correctness are open.
