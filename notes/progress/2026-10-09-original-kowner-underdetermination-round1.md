## Exact cut and logical distinction

Use the original source tree, symbolic shared root `R`, upper occurrence
`u_f,U`, original `X` and one shared `xi`. The core source correspondence is:

```text

  result(lambda(x, call(result(name f), result(name x)))),
```

```text

```

This supplies no equation identifying an original slot with an output path or
an original contribution with this relation. Let `H_cut` denote the actual
premises of round-2 K-Owner, including original O0 and seed-at-exposure; those
last premises are not inferred from annotation absence or endpoint identity.
The target is:

```text
H_cut
  => exists s,o. s in Slots_orig((d_f,R))
       and Own_orig((d_f,R),s,u_f,p0,o;X).
```

There are three different questions:

1. Is this sequent derivable from a concrete original source judgment?
2. Does its original kernel admit multiple owner proofs or slots here?
3. Do two complete formation interpretations yield observably distinct,
   semantically legal results, while preserving every original witness?

The target's existential quantifier is not a uniqueness assertion. Conversely,
the lack of a displayed uniqueness proof supplies neither a second owner nor
two models. Even two distinct valid proof terms can denote one complete
contract, and a complete formation relation could retain both alternatives.
That possibility does not discharge the required principality result either.

Fixing only a symbolic kernel parameter is sufficient for a conditional
transport theorem. A countermodel claim needs actual interpretations satisfying
the premises, together with a discriminator not erased by permitted typed
renaming. The inspected documents provide no independently realized original
slot domain or ownership judgment at this cut with which to certify that.

## Conditional discriminator and exact blocker

A genuinely discriminating pair would need the following independently
established data, before any toy checker or label construction:

```text
K_1,K_2 : complete original interpretations at the fixed source cut
  with original X, scope tree and the same shared xi retained;
W_1,W_2 : full original witness relations with a certified correspondence
  preserving every arm, provider license and joint incidence dependency;
G_1,G_2 : Q-independent source introductions satisfying the retained
  formation, admission, callback B, Option 2 and required theorem contracts;
t_i     : actual source-derived O0/O1 output of G_i, in its original sorts;
D       : a stated owner/path/incidence predicate invariant under every
  permitted original typed transport, with D(t_1) and not D(t_2).
```

Conditionally, these premises would give a real difference: invariance rules
out dismissing it as a legal renaming, and the certified correspondence rules
out manufacturing it by dropping or recombining original witness arms. This
is just the criterion a proposed pair must meet, not an existence theorem for
`K_i`, `G_i` or `t_i`. Agreement of `Pi(W_1)` and `Pi(W_2)` is insufficient;
their joint witness/typed-incidence obligations must be checked directly.

The first unavailable data are the concrete O0/O1 outputs and their original
source introduction semantics. Granting independently complete Call typing
leaves precisely that gap; even supplying one original owner does not supply
a second invariant-distinct complete interpretation. Moving on to incidence
would additionally need C1/J0 and the complete uniform-family law, rather
than a pointwise witness or an observation-only comparison.

Recommended next action: obtain one pinned original source-formation judgment
whose conclusion supplies O0/O1 at this capture, with its original sorts and
full evidence preservation stated; then assess its admissible alternatives
before considering a model pair. This is a request for the missing semantic
interface through the primary, not a proposal to adopt a slot shape or change
the selected source meaning. No further equivalent toy probe is useful here.
