## Objective and result

The predecessor seed-elimination derivation already distinguishes existential
from universal Value-tag triggering with two uses. Repeating that record would
leave its eligibility premise untouched. This pass instead attacks the proposed
inference from such a trigger to a completed **shared relation**.

The bounded result is that “retain alternatives jointly” is a preservation
requirement, not a third trigger predicate. It can coexist with either an
existential or universal discharge clause. A three-way comparison of completed
source outcomes therefore needs the missing source clauses, including what
eligible evidence asserts about the full relation. The approved singleton
example and the exact nested-block interpretation do not provide those clauses.

A two-use, two-coordinate symbolic witness conditionally falsifies the shortcut
“one Value-tagged use permits restricting the completed relation to non-Handler
formal alternatives while preserving its solutions.” It does **not** falsify
the approved example or exhibit a valid Yulang program. The exact premise
missing from a safe restriction is identified below.

## Why two tags do not discriminate three completed meanings

Let `b` be an unannotated provisional formal, `F_b` its one shared inferred
interface, and `U_b={c_v,c_t}` a supplied complete use record. Both calls
resolve to `b`. Conditional core synthesis supplies a Value-tagged ordinary
name at `c_v` and a retained Computation-tagged name at `c_t`. Neither their
source acceptance nor satisfiable complete Function constraints are asserted.

Assume, only to examine aggregation, that `c_v` qualifies as discharge evidence
and `c_t` does not. Then `exists eligible` is true and `all eligible` is false.
This is exactly the predecessor's trigger distinction. Joint retention adds no
third truth value: it demands that the complete relation, including correlations
with other source uses, survive whatever discharge clause is chosen.

Failure of an all-use clause to fire does not assert a final Handler result,
program rejection, or absence of another non-Handler derivation. Likewise,
joint retention alone cannot tell which alternatives are source-admissible.
Calling those three phrases complete competing language meanings would add
decisions absent from the authority. In particular, an all-use clause can
retain a joint relation whose other constraints already determine non-Handler.

## Minimal correlated witness and conditional theorem

Use two finite metatheoretic coordinates, not proposed compiler fields:

```text
n in {0,1}    whether a completed candidate has NonHandlerFormal(b,F_b)
k in {0,1}    one correlated original source-fiber choice
R = {(0,0), (1,1)}
S0: k=0
S1: k=1
R0 = R intersect S0 = {(0,0)}
R1 = R intersect S1 = {(1,1)}
```

`n=0` records absence of that formal conclusion; it is not an actual Handler
role assertion or an irreversible equation for the provisional seed. `k`
stands for a shared source constraint coordinate, not an invented runtime
carrier. Keep source use identities, annotation absence, protection, actual
callable role/entry, and all other incidence coordinates fixed. Keep `Q`
absent. Attach the same mixed tag record to either row set. The relation and
the restrictions are **candidate assumptions**, not authority-derived source
constraints.

Here `Ri` is hypothesized to contain the completed source solutions after all
independently justified source constraints. It is not the provisional seed's
raw candidate set. Inference may legitimately narrow a provisional set when it
adds a source-entailed constraint; this calculation does not require preserving
every provisional alternative. If discharge itself supplies a missing source
constraint or a different seed-to-final interpretation, its justification must
be established before calling `Ri` the completed relation.

Consider the proposed restriction `Discharge(Ri)=Ri intersect {n=1}` solely
because `c_v` is Value-tagged. Direct finite set calculation gives:

| Original joint relation | Nonempty original solutions | After discharge restriction | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `R0={(0,0)}` | one | empty | sole solution lost |
| `R1={(1,1)}` | one | unchanged | non-Handler already entailed |
| `R` | two correlated rows | `{(1,1)}` | one original alternative lost |

**Conditional theorem.** For any supplied relation `Phi`, restricting it to
`n=1` preserves its complete solution set iff `Phi subseteq {n=1}`. This is
the elementary equality condition `Phi intersect N = Phi iff Phi subseteq N`.
It establishes the exact premise a solution-preserving restriction needs;
it supplies no source rule establishing that premise.

Thus the tag-only shortcut is refuted under the candidate `R0` premise. A
proponent can exclude `R0` by deriving that eligible evidence already imposes
`n=1` on every relevant original fiber, or by specifying a sound seed-to-final
interpretation where deleting provisional alternatives is justified. Those
are the missing source judgments. The approved example requires their outcome
for its singleton shape; it does not validate arbitrary `R0`, nor establish
their extension to the mixed record. Eligibility may itself depend on the
whole component, so it must not be assumed independent of `S0`.

Two uses are minimal for the some/all distinction on a nonempty use record.
Two values of `n` are necessary to expose alternative elimination. Two
coordinates and two correlated rows are minimal to demonstrate dependence on
an additional shared fiber coordinate. A one-coordinate example could expose
loss but would leave joint source correlation untested. No recursive component,
effect operation, callback literal or annotation change is needed for this
symbolic discriminator. These are minimalities of this method, not of valid
Yulang programs.

Mutations considered analytically: discard `S0` and choose the locally suitable
`(1,1)` row; independently select `n=1` and `k=0`; or choose a row because `Q`
succeeds. The first drops source constraints, the second invents a tuple absent
from `R`, and the third violates the independently stated formation boundary.
Actual callable preservation alone does not detect any of these mutations.
No executable mutation suite was run.
