## 4. Dependency cycles and stopping boundary

1. **Code-Call inversion:** constructing Code-Call requires rho_arg and
   Carrier-Delay. Obtaining rho_arg by inversion of that desired Code-Call
   assumes the missing premise. O0/O1 also require an emitted record, so using
   its completed Call image to obtain this pre-emission origin repeats the
   same dependency cycle.
2. **Interface insertion:** IF-Insert requires original registration;
   S-Delay starts at an actual origin. Their output cannot justify their own
   original-registration premise.
3. **Successful-inlet supplier:** an attachment or license justified only by
   WholeArgCompatible, CarrierMem, successful Q or the eventual Call receipt
   depends on downstream typing. None is the independent supplier required.
4. **Domain defined by its source image:** replacing the independent Delay
   domain by literal executions makes the required total input map automatic
   by changing its premise. It omits admitted alternatives and cannot prove
   the selected contract.
5. **Old-preservation placement:** candidate §4 correctly stages P_plus before
   requiring H_oldext and keeps that certificate out of the staged entry.
   This removes a self-referential data definition. It still leaves the
   actual old-active preservation theorem as an external obligation.

The candidate has a noncircular proposed premise order. No selected target
rule application was found in the bounded source set. Previous registration
factoring attempts already left the original supplier unresolved; this audit
adds the selected IF-Insert and immutable-extension ownership distinctions,
rather than another model assuming those suppliers. The precise stop is the
three outputs `Rule_orig`, A and H_oldext at j. No third toy probe is warranted.
