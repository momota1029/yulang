## Exact shortcut and its missing premise

Fix a known original `beta`, effect position `p`, original scoped clause `g`,
and jointly well-formed `xi=(nu,K,D)`. The audited Hinst premise is:

```text
b is introduced from this original beta/profile at xi;
Gamma_b contains this same scoped g and predicate identity at p under nu;
chi_original(p,b);
M is source-certified, maps p to p0, and transports original evidence at xi.
```

The invalid inference form replaces some or all of that conjunction with
one of the observations below. Its invalidity is an explicit proof boundary,
not a newly discovered accepted execution.

| Shortcut | Exact missing evidence and explicit exclusion |

A label alone supplies neither the introduction derivation nor the instantiated clause/path certificate. If “same beta” already includes that certificate, Hinst has been assumed rather than derived. |
| Same family or undecorated profile shape implies the same clause/predicate and map | Typed-boundary §6 attaches evidence to typed views, preserves decorated annotation occurrences/depth, and generates no correspondence from family equality. Transport preserves original predicate identity; it does not reconstruct predicates from shape. Equal operation acceptance at one instance would also fail to establish predicate identity. |
| Same receiver implies the original local clause | `Grant` separately requires `b.receiver=owner(h)` and explicit `Gamma_b` admission at the incident position. Wildcard/omitted profiles protect without a concrete grant. Receiver identity discharges only its ownership conjunct. |
| `Receive(u,slot,view,M)` implies that view came from this beta/g | §6 says receipt records ownership of the use and creates no boundary or contract. `Path` separately joins the original profile through matching typed flow to the observed position of that same received view.
