### Recursive providers: K, KP and KV

For the exact source `my f x = g; my g y = f`, Theorem K constructs the actual
finite lexical closure graph before selecting or validating member contracts.
Source lambda heads supply Value tags; the selected literal/parameter laws
supply actual Pure roles and Value entry. Resolved edges connect each closure
to that same other provider, including after return and later use. No
validated simultaneous environment or solved recursive type equation is an
input to the construction.

Theorem KP pairs finite operational prefixes on that graph under independently
paired argument/context primitives. Value entry, suspension, divergence,
current-state raw resumption, pending suffixes and future calls retain the
same providers and receipts. This establishes structural execution
correspondence, not typed world or admission validity.

Theorem KV constructs a Q-independent semantic contract-realization predicate
by retaining the independently interpreted complete membership obligations at
the original roots of those actual providers. Its solutions exactly realize
the proposed contracts and original local obligations on the fixed graph and
one original assignment. Satisfaction, nonemptiness and deciding the predicate
are not premises of schema construction. This positive result must not be
erased by calling it merely an unimplemented solver.

The reviews also preserve KV's limit: an occurrence of `CompleteMem` is not a
proof that it is an effectively presented primitive, and the conjunction's
exactness is not an independently selected recursive source-typing theorem.
Open-body soundness gives conditional implications; mutually assumed member
validity does not prove those assumptions. An independent guarded complete
descriptor/admission/history characterization and its source correspondence
remain the reduced validation target. No greatest fixed point or recursive
equality rule is selected.

The source/core discriminator is valid: even when `f`'s Name/Result body emits
no request, a declaration-derived request in its admitted whole argument can
execute during Value entry. Thus its pure body-result skeleton is not a
complete request-free invocation contract. The construction covers inert
constructor-guarded immutable components, not unguarded alias initialization,
effectful initialization or arbitrary recursive local Bind.

### Projection endpoint families: PG-1

This is conditional source-to-coordinate and source-kernel reconstruction.
The actual Generalize boundary, complete descriptor/admission validity, all

certificate or bypass every-derived-comparison level guards.

graphs. Their candidate brace spellings have no proved source realization.

Distinct runtime inputs by themselves do not prove under-generalization or
