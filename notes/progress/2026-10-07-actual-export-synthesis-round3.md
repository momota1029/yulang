composition node, while all three prerequisites remain open.

hypotheses are exactly the current DAG obligations, with their already
stated ordinary-use/evidence clauses. The proof constructs the admissible

## 3. Strong conditional theorem for the existing PRINCIPAL node

### 3.1 Objects and quantifier order

Fix supported source S, its original scope tree T, rigid imports and the
chosen independent conservative abstraction. Once, before choosing any
public view, SOURCE_ADEQUACY supplies the complete generated original
relation and its interpretation. PROJECTION computes the one finite legal
exported scheme P_S for the complete common-extended relation; its actual
designated export is the represented `B_common`, including its query and
evidence alternatives. ALL_VIEW includes COMMON_TOTAL in its existing
prerequisite cone, so this common-extended relation is the appropriate
presentation, rather than one selected by a successful public query.

Let V be any independently valid finite public scheme with public solution
predicate C_V and actual target root R_V. Its validity is established against
that same declared independent semantics, not defined as instancehood of P_S.

For explanatory notation write

```text
F_S = J_S and K_G and Q_common
U_S,V = F_S and Direct(B_common,R_V).
```

These are complete scoped relations. World, admission, histories, source
profiles and all correlated providers are retained. `Scoped_T` denotes the
original binder tree with its allowed witness dependencies, not existential
prenex closure. The completed ALL_VIEW obligation says

```text
for every independent V, original xi and public solution v:
  C_V(v;xi) => Scoped_T U_S,V(v;xi).
```

For a scheme with public quantifier alternation, a public solution means its
permitted public witness strategy, interpreted at the original positions.
The proof below acts under those binders; it does not select a convenient
world/history or alter the dependency of a hidden witness on public inputs.

The desired order is

```text
for each S, one computed P_S;
for every independent finite V, one finite ordinary map graph m_S,V;
for every original xi and public solution/strategy v,
  m_S,V has a compatible original-scoped solution/strategy.
```

The graph is fixed before v, challenges and histories. Its assignments and
legal local witnesses may depend on v and on earlier original binders.
No one ground substitution for all V, and no graph per runtime challenge,
is introduced.

### 3.2 Construct the ordinary map before selecting any witness

Use certified-use §5.1 on P_S and V:

1. Choose one injective capture-avoiding fresh-copy map for all eligible
   P_S identities, its complete residual, incidences and binder tree,
   fixing imports. This map depends only on the two finite input graphs.
2. Retain the joint public constraint C_V.
3. Append the one ordinary complete query at the actual exported fresh
   root `R_P^fresh <: R_V`, including all resolution/evidence alternatives.
4. Keep every copied original-scoped residual binder in the instance.

No descriptor graft is required by this construction: the source roots may
remain separate from the client roots and be connected by the ordinary query.
If the actual admissible use requires a fixed finite graft, use the one
provided by PROJECTION's ordinary-use factorization and transport it
uniformly; do not select a new syntax graft per public assignment. A semantic
linking witness in K_G is retained as a joint constraint at its original
scope, rather than converted into independent endpoint substitutions.

Call this finite graph m_S,V. Its size is bounded by the two finite graph
sizes plus any supplied linking entries and a constant query node. Registered
recursive edges are copied without unfolding. This proves the existence of
one ordinary admissible graph for each V independently of whether any
particular public assignment has yet been solved.

### 3.3 Lift the ALL_VIEW strategy through the computed interface

Take arbitrary original xi and a public v satisfying C_V. Apply ALL_VIEW
once to obtain its compatible original-scoped strategy for the **entire**
joined relation U_S,V. That strategy supplies the common coordinate a jointly
with source/profile/provider/world witnesses and the actual query evidence.
Never replace its source witness by an unrelated witness from SOURCE_ADEQUACY
or its a by an independently chosen COMMON_TOTAL witness. The existing
ALL_VIEW obligation already requires their compatibility.

PROJECTION's ordinary-use factorization transports this whole satisfying
strategy, including the submitted root operands and actual query evidence,
to the computed finite interface P_S and its ordinary use against V. Apply
the fixed fresh-copy map to all incident operands and evidence together.
Consequently the same public v has a scoped extension satisfying P_S's
copied residual and `Direct(R_P^fresh,R_V)`.

Here is the binder argument, rather than an exchange of quantifiers. Traverse
T from outer binders to inner binders. At an original universal binder,
retain its arbitrary environment and apply the correspondence under that
same environment. At an eligible existential binder, transport the witness
chosen by the supplied strategy using only the data available before that
binder. Any eligible hidden coordinate remains bound once around all its
incident source, admission, query and evidence predicates. Fixed imports
are unchanged. Recursion is a finite registered root transport, not an
extra choice for each unfolding. Thus a witness before a later universal
binder cannot acquire a dependence on that later variable.

For example, `exists shared. forall kappa. exists body` remains in that
order. Transport the one shared witness first, then transport each permitted
body witness under its current kappa. The proof never treats this as
`forall kappa. exists shared,body`. This is exactly the witness-strategy
preservation required by the current PROJECTION node; pointwise truth or
separate root marginals would not support this step.

Let Ext_P(v;xi) denote that ordinary-use extension predicate. We have proved

```text
C_V(v;xi) => Ext_P(v;xi)
```

for every public solution, with the one graph m_S,V already fixed.
No successful comparisons are composed; the sole public Direct proof is the
ALL_VIEW proof at B_common, transported as evidence through the actual
ordinary-use interface supplied by PROJECTION.

### 3.4 Derive exact public factorization

Certified-use Theorem 3 identifies the public projection of m_S,V with

```text
C_V(v;xi) and Ext_P(v;xi).
```

The implication just proved makes this exactly C_V. Conversely every use
solution satisfies C_V because its complete client relation is retained
conjunctively. Therefore the map loses no independently valid public solution
and adds none. Original source and public correlations, scope-dependent
strategies and evidence are preserved by the same joined transport.

This proves universal ordinary-use factorization through the one computed
P_S for **every** independent V in the full declared class. It does not
restrict V to allocation derivations, identical value interfaces, equal
admission domains, fixed source profiles, or matched local abstraction
forms. Any such restrictions in the actual still-open proofs belong to
ALL_VIEW/PROJECTION/SOURCE_ADEQUACY and prevent their completion; they are
not extra premises of this final composition theorem.

SOURCE_ADEQUACY also identifies the complete source/typed interpretation
used on both sides, so the inferred relation is not a scheme over a
convenient unrelated model. This implication does not close SOUND or
PROD_CONFORMANCE: their separate production containment obligations remain.

## 5. Smaller actual-root rule head: remove unnecessary semantic premises

The independent complete-Function law requires only

```text
D_V subset D_common
for every h in D_V: M_common(h,-) subset M_V(h,-),
```

where M includes ordinary DescMem, residuals and every production alternative,
and both relations keep one original assignment, tuple and projection.
Consequently the smallest finite-clause-shaped candidate head is

```text
Le_Gamma(D_V, D_common)
Le_Gamma(D_V(h) and M_common(h,o), M_V(h,o))
actual submitted complete roots; one original scoped incidence map
---------------------------------------------------------------
Direct(B_common,R_V; the supplied finite proofs).
```

Root legality, source-authorized query sorts and the actual role/entry/
consumer meanings remain checked in the complete operands. The displayed
rule is a candidate sufficient semantic introduction, **not** a current
accepted resolver clause. In particular merely naming semantic Le assertions
is insufficient: the proofs must be finite and checked by an applicable
actual evidence rule.

Proof of soundness: fix h admitted by V. The first implication makes it
admitted by common. For each common membership witness at h, conjoin this
same h's admission witness and apply the second implication to obtain V
membership with the same original dependencies. The same final projection
yields observation containment. This argument preserves every compatible
punctured context and finite future history; no successful-Q transitivity
appears.

Equal domains are unnecessary. In an abstract complete contract algebra,
let D_common={h0,h1}, D_V={h0}, M_common(h0)={good},
M_common(h1)={bad}, and M_V(h0)={good}. The semantic law holds, but neither
Eq(D_common,D_V) nor unguarded global membership inclusion holds. This
example isolates precisely why target-domain guarding is necessary.
Identical non-coverage interfaces are likewise unnecessary if their actual
complete implications have finite proofs. The head does not assume profiles
are identical; it retains their original predicates in the complete relation.
Dropping those predicates would invalidate the argument.

For paired §3.7 Option 2 grammars, work at the fixed xi,h of that construction.
At h in D_V, finite-derivation induction carries the guarded source-base and
hard-envelope inclusions through every matched Z leaf and W_abs step.
Unanchored extras are included. At h outside D_V no inclusion is required.
This uses the grammar's fixed-h interpretation, not an abstraction that can
rewrite a challenge from outside D_V to inside it. Such a rewrite would
need a separate domain-dependent rule: checking only guarded base inclusion
would fail to account for its crossing predecessor.

```text

```

with complete original decorations delta_o, plus literal introduction and
complete descriptor-constructor transport at the actual root. It is not a
claim about row support or a kernel Int/Top tag alone.

The literal's retained Integer/effect facts identify the source occurrence
and its ordinary endpoint constraints. They do not specify the complete
independently interpreted MemValue predicates or derive the required source
profile, typed path, lineage and ambient joint constraints. Source-contracts
§2.2 explicitly supplies DescMem independently; §3.5 assumes local descriptor
typing lemmas. Typed-core §7 uses VIncl as a semantic premise and states that
its effective sound/principal presentation remains open. No inspected clause
introduces this nonidentity decorated primitive proof.

Even a granted semantic VIncl leaf would need a complete constructor law to
transport the same witness through Return and the actual Function descriptor,
including target admission and Option 2 hard envelopes. The source-contracts
§5.3 primitive rule provides Eq only for matching relation identity/operands;
its parent Function rule still requires matching non-coverage interfaces.
Thus the new head has a precisely identified next source/evidence predicate,
while adoption/conformance is a separate seam. It is not a full certificate
for `my f x = 1`, and a permissive abstract Top definition would only assume
the missing interpretation.

A semantic containment proof cannot force actual resolver acceptance:
in a finite contract algebra, an equality-only resolver can reject the
strict-domain example while remaining sound; a resolver recognizing the
proved inclusion can accept it. The approved interpretation/admission policy
does not by itself give a completeness theorem selecting one outcome. This
is an abstract underdetermination witness, not two complete competing Yulang
semantics on the same admitted source. The latter would require the omitted
complete descriptor/source/admission clauses and actual source derivation.
