## 4. A discriminating field derivation and its limit

Typed-boundary §6 defines, for the same event q and candidate h,

```text
Inc_C(q,h,b,p) iff Path(q,owner(h),b,p)
  and Active(h,C) and Active(owner(h),C) and Active(b.receiver,C).
```

Assume the original Path holds and all three activity conjuncts hold at C.
If a lawful source expiry yields C' with `not Active(h,C')`, while retaining
the same raw Path/profile/predicate evidence, then `Inc_C` holds and
`Inc_C'` is false. This follows by conjunction elimination/introduction from
the displayed definition. It discriminates packet retention from live
incidence preservation even with an identity packet map. It is conditional
on that lawful source expiry; it is not a complete source trace, a refutation
of j0, or an assertion that j_e itself becomes false. A valid later world
may correctly record the absence of the expired permission.

Conversely, at the **same** C and same exact h/receiver references, relational
image commutes with the live filter (`M_* live_C^h = live_C^h M_*`, typed-boundary
§6). Its proof tests the same activity predicates on both sides. Substituting
C' for one side would change that premise. Thus neither immutable IDs nor
this commutation law proves compatibility of a transition.

No independent executable oracle is used. The tables and derivation share
the cited source contracts as assumptions; they establish documentary
ownership and a consequence of the explicit incidence formula, not independent
semantic adequacy. No seed/range, mutation search or executable coverage is
claimed. The inspected declaration inventory is not a closed inventory of
foreign histories, State laws or private guard fields.
