## Objective and result

Test the candidate route at the fixed captured ordinary Call in exactly
`my apply f = { my step x = f x; step }`, after the round-3 conditional
PRINCIPAL composition result. The attack grants source identity and, for
isolation, independently complete operand/Call typing. It asks whether
individually typed original slots, output paths and complete contributions,
even with every pairwise compatibility fact, supply the required original
joint incidence.

**Result:** they do not in general. A three-sort binary parity relation has
every possible pairwise projection, yet only half the jointly licensed
tuples. Joining its pairwise projections retains every original licensed
witness but adds unlicensed tuples. A separate indistinguishability argument
proves that pairwise data cannot determine the original ternary relation.
This is a conditional mathematical obstruction to a weakened constructor
route, not a counterexample to the stated round-2 lifting theorem or the
open kernel contract. Those candidates expressly require the missing joint
original law and witness-linked maps.

No complete SEM_JOINT interpretation, admitted Yulang source counterexample,
empty original fiber, or impossibility of an original constructor is proved.
In particular the finite relation below has original incidences at `p0`;
the attack concerns invalid reconstruction of a particular incidence, not
failure of the ORIGINAL_ASSOC existential target. ORIGINAL_ASSOC stays open.

## Sorted witness and derivation

Use three disjoint illustrative original sorts:

```text
S = {s0,s1}          original static slot candidates in Slots(beta)
P = {p0,p1}          distinct original typed output-path candidates
C = {c0,c1}          independently typed complete contribution candidates
```

The displayed cardinalities are fixtures, not a slot-count or path-count
claim for the selected source. Both path descriptors can have the same
extensional type while their original typed locations remain distinct.
Neither path is identified with a slot. The selected query still fixes
the original `p0`; it does not rename it to make a tuple succeed.

Fix the source owner `d_f`, shared root, exposure `u`, actual provider and
receiver, actual role/entry/consumer, scopes, `X` and `xi` as parameters.
The source owner is not replaced by the actual receiver. No role bit is
one of the three varying coordinates. Suppose the illustrative typed
original incidence relation has exactly these four licensed witnesses:

| Witness | Slot | Typed path | Complete contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a000 | s0 | p0 | c0 |
| a011 | s0 | p1 | c1 |
| a101 | s1 | p0 | c1 |
| a110 | s1 | p1 | c0 |

Write `I_even` for that whole relation, with its four distinct license
witnesses retained. Equivalently its indices satisfy `i xor j xor k = 0`.
Typing, source ownership and any complete contribution coverage in this
fixture are independent stipulated certificates, not consequences of
parity. No license outside these four is inferred.

Every binary projection is the full product:

```text
proj_SP(I_even) = S x P
proj_SC(I_even) = S x C
proj_PC(I_even) = P x C.
```

For each fixed pair, exactly one third coordinate completes its parity.
Thus these equalities follow by the four rows, without an executable oracle.
Unary projections are full as well.

Consider the tuple `(s0,p0,c1)`. Its owner/slot/path pair is witnessed by
`a000`, its slot/contribution pair by `a011`, and its path/contribution pair
by `a101`. All three witnesses use the same fixed `X/xi`; this is not
per-port choice of new `nu,K,D`. Nevertheless **no single original witness**
has the desired tuple. Pairwise agreement permits incompatible choices of
the retained joint incidence evidence, even when every ordinary type matches.

The natural join of those three projection tables is `S x P x C`, containing
eight tuples. It adds `(s0,p0,c1)`, `(s0,p1,c0)`, `(s1,p0,c0)` and
`(s1,p1,c1)`. Every actual fixture witness a000/a011/a101/a110 survives;
the failure is fabricated incidence, not loss of an existing license.
Keeping an opaque bag of those four license IDs beside the tables does
not license a new joined tuple.

If the pair tables retain the **same original witness ID as an active join
coordinate**, the phantom tuple cannot be produced: its three pair
witnesses have different IDs. This is precisely a joint evidence condition.
The attack therefore does not apply to a representation retaining the full
incidence relation, a shared witness-linked decomposition, or a separately
proved lossless join law.

### Information-loss theorem and bounded minimality

Let `I_odd` consist of the four complementary tuples whose indices satisfy
`i xor j xor k = 1`. All unary and binary projections of `I_odd` equal those
of `I_even`. Therefore any reconstruction that depends only on these
projections and the same fixed source/type/role/admission parameters receives
identical input for the two relations. It returns the same relation for both,
so it cannot equal both distinct originals. QED.

This second relation is an auxiliary logical comparison, not another
interpretation asserted for the same admitted Yulang program. The single
`I_even` join attack above preserves all of its license witnesses unchanged.
The theorem distinguishes missing information from a chosen false predicate:
even complete knowledge of every projected pair is insufficient.

For this specific higher-order indistinguishability method, three coordinates
are necessary: with two coordinates their binary projection is the whole
relation. Each sort must have at least two elements; a singleton coordinate
reduces to a retained binary relation. On the 2x2x2 fixture, realizing every
pair requires at least four rows, and the displayed parity relation uses
four. This is minimality of the **projection attack**, not of a source program,
the ORIGINAL_ASSOC gate, or the eventual original semantic constructor.

## Candidate audit and precise blocker

The weakened implication refuted is:

```text
H_gen + H_typed + independently typed original objects
  + all pairwise incidence projections + retention of all old license IDs
  => a sound original joint incidence for any compatible projected tuple.
```

The actual candidate does not assert that implication. K-Image requires
compatible **joint** evidence; K-Incidence has an original semantic
introduction/coverage law; OC-Call-Intro requires a preimage in the original
contribution domain and its joint source license. Granting those full laws
blocks the attack. The open candidate also never declares that source labels,
typed endpoints or a relation certificate are original contributions.

The minimum missing information for this reduced argument is one original
joint incidence certificate, or an independently proved witness-linked
lossless decomposition. At the selected source, that certificate must be
obtained from the actual source-owner and complete invocation-image
constructor; choosing a parity table does not supply it. This is not a
proposal for a new ternary runtime carrier or an exact arity requirement for
Yulang. An original kernel may encode the same joint information differently.

**Precise blocker:** H_gen and granted independent descriptor/Call typing
do not provide the original contribution interpretation's preimage at the
complete invocation image, nor the joint owner/path/contribution license
for that preimage. Source contracts §2.1 takes that kernel as input;
C-realization and the §5.3 congruences preserve already interpreted whole
operands. They cannot reconstruct it from its proper projections. The
bounded reads do not prove that an original law cannot exist elsewhere.

## Option 2, independence and omitted cases

independent exhaustive grammar and complete hard envelope. Such observations
can be parameters of c0/c1 without changing the join proof. Their presence
does not admit any of the four phantom original incidences: a conservative
observation license is not an arbitrary slot/path/contribution license.
A source-only filter would omit licensed extras; a port-only guard would
permit the phantom tuple. The candidate's full `G` and kernel evidence must
retain whichever original incidence constraints actually apply.

No Oracle, reference implementation or checker was used. The proof shares
the selected source decisions and stipulated typing/kernel fixture assumptions
with its comparison; it independently establishes only the displayed finite
relation facts. Implementing parity or supplied transition rules would add
consistency evidence, not derive those rules from Yulang's source contracts.

Seeds/ranges: none. Logical domain: three disjoint two-element sorts; four
original fixture rows and eight possible joined tuples, all accounted for
by the derivation. Mutation analyzed, not executed: forget the joint
incidence/witness links and join the complete binary projections. Recovery
condition: active common witness IDs, retained full joint predicate, or a
proved lossless decomposition. A representation already carrying one of
these conditions is outside the mutation's premise.

Omitted/unverified: actual slot/path/contribution cardinalities and meanings;
full admission/descriptor/world/response/resumption clauses and inhabitance;
actual licensing grammar; complete production extras; source adequacy;
recursive/mixed/generalized components; annotation realization; proof that
the original contribution domain admits the required complete image.
