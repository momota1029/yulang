Captured side is retained at freshening; induced comparisons drain before the next captured bound (`candidate_scheme.rs:552–593,1140–1181`; `candidate_extrusion.rs:643–690`).

Freshening maps such a still-generic local row to a new row at `use_level`; it shares rows only in the alternate branch (`:927–939`). This applies to Y just as it applies to q and X.
4. Assume distinct canonical generic q, X, Y; each is above capture boundary B; representatives and metadata stay fixed; no other callback supplies the missing old-parent edge.
