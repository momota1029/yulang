## Objective and method

Audit whether the selected constructors independently form
`V_0 = VP(I_q[Int;Delta],Int,Int,IF)+Fixed(J_0)` with initial restriction H,
at the **same original native id source/scheme telescope and IF incidences**.
The method is inversion of the selected registration, extraction, decode and
capture constructors. The candidate note supplies only the proposed operands
and unresolved C1; its assertion of C1 is not a premise here.

Result: the selected rules supply H's *kind* of initial restriction and a
constructible ground public J_0. They do not supply the required original
Fixed capture incidence at the unchanged import-free id IF. The first missing
premise is a well-formed target registration containing that original J_0
capture port and current-world binding restriction, related lawfully to the
original id telescope. This is a missing derivation, not a universal theorem
that independently constructed client Fixed targets are invalid.

The approved `successor-generalize-root-policy/q1/a1`, decisions 1–5, and its
receipt select the transformed/displayable export direction. They do not
select a new target-capture formation rule or establish its sufficiency.

## Conditional derivation and first gap

The retained-telescope argument has these explicit hypotheses:

1. The source component is the selected bare native id with no captures.
2. Its original I/IF/telescope is retained as required by candidate C1.
3. Extraction/decoding and legal whole transport are the selected PE operations,
   which preserve retained incidence owners and only substitute raw aliases.
4. Fixed means phase §7's original-capture specialization, including its
   current-world binding restriction; it is not a new result-equality-only law.

Under these hypotheses, PE-ID supplies no operation that adds a captured-z
port to IF_id. PICK-J supplies Fixed only when the original port and authentic
capture certificate already exist. Therefore attempting to instantiate that
constructor at IF_id reaches an unsupplied premise before either result
equality or Function comparison is considered. H's conjunction rule operates
on an already registered initial carrier coordinate and does not supply the
missing port, binding or hereditary capture restriction.

The precise remaining constructive obligation is:

```text
independently registered V_0 at the actual check
  with J_0's actual installed binding/world/port and complete hereditary contract
  + an original capture-incidence certificate for Fixed at IF_id
  + legal scope availability of all rigid/shared J_0 operands
  + an independent same-source derivation and original kernel/path alignment
  => the complete C1/Valid_V formation premise.
```

The selected rules read here do not derive the second conjunct. The third and
fourth conjuncts must also be checked after a proposed owner construction;
they cannot be replaced by semantic membership of the actual id callable.
This note supplies neither a full counterexample nor proof of their universal
impossibility. A target with its own separately valid IF and client environment
may be a different case; equating it to IF_id needs an actual lawful map.
