## 2. Accepted finding and repair

Both reviewers independently identified the same **major coverage gap** in
the first source-view submission. Its concrete result rebind required an
actual Return, but its whole-row theorem quantified over every independently
typed invocation. The governing admission includes divergent carriers and
suspended prefixes whose entry has not returned. Such a row has no actual
rebound formal value, no captured `step` environment, and no executed read.

The producer repaired the theorem over the original prefix/resumption relation:

1. Actual receipt introduces the prospective fragment at
   `argument.result.(u,p0)` and retains the original packet and pending suffix.
2. Running prefixes, Request and independently admitted resumptions retain
   that packet, original handle composition and receiver reference. They do
   not construct a rebound value or replay receipt/Force.
3. Actual Return retains its actual provider/result evidence; the original
   rebinding transition alone creates the formal `SourceViewInst`.
4. Capture/read certificates require their actual Lambda/Name transitions.
   Every finite divergent or indefinitely suspended prefix remains covered.

This is a Pending/Rebound **proof certificate sum**, not a new computation
outcome, a new recursive carrier, or a restriction to returning arguments.
An administrative cut between Return and rebind retains the actual result
without installing an unperformed binding. Repeated resumptions keep one
original receipt and row while indexing their distinct actual result events.
Fresh execution ownership does not revive the original boundary receiver.

Both independent delta reviewers verified the repaired hash and closed the
major finding. Neither reported any remaining blocking, major or minor finding
within the repair's direct dependency scope. They checked the complete Pending
prefix/resumption account, original raw handle, current resumed state,
Return-before-rebind cuts, actual provider/result evidence, repeated results,
original receiver and conditional capture/read transitions. This acceptance
does not cover raw-source formation, initial admission or production behavior.

### SC-normalization

Both reviewers accepted the exact **complete-source singleton** equality
rewrite. The approved `apply f x = f x` outcome entails the inferred formal
coordinate `rho_f=NonHandlerFormal`. Substitute this proved constant into
every original active primitive at its original binder, keeping the entire
original `xi=(nu,K,D),w`, all seed/upper/lower evidence, actual callable role

For recursion, the equality must remain in the paired operator interface or
the proof works on its fixed selected fiber; eliminating a coordinate first

an incomplete registration prefix already has the completed source's solution
