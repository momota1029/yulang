## Result and claim boundary

For exactly `my apply f = { my step x = f x; step }`, the selected meaning
fixes resolution, sequential binding, non-invoking return and retained capture.
It does not supply the first rule introducing the captured provider's original
symbolic contract/profile association. Call-view §2 explicitly leaves its
exact generating judgments open.

Two partial input protocols remain possible in the inspected interfaces:
introduce an association obligation from the resolved component, or introduce
it after independently generating the ordinary endpoint/call skeleton.
They differ in prerequisite certificates and the first available output,
rather than merely renaming a provider. Neither is a complete registration
producer. Both leave precisely the same source contract/profile/origin/scope
constructor unproved. This is **bounded source-input/proof-staging
underdetermination**, not demonstrated ambiguity in the selected language.

I did not construct two certified, materially different complete `Reg`
conclusions consistent with all authority, and did not prove that no such
pair exists. Producing such a pair would require independently completing
the very formation premise that the inspected interfaces omit. No accepted-
source, semantic, soundness or principality counterexample is claimed.

Claim classes:

- Established requirements: the approved lexical meaning, position-plus-
  contract slot identity, one shared source relation and joint `nu,K,D`,
  comparison-independent admission, and preservation boundaries below.
- Bounded characterization: the named sources distinguish lexical input,
  ordinary skeleton, decorated source envelope, completed association and
  transport. They provide no applicable complete introducing rule at the
  first two inputs.
- Candidate assumptions: the two partial protocols and any eventual
  completion judgment. They are research choices, not selected rules.
- Conditional theorem: the two partial protocols have identical completed
  outputs if the same independently proved completion relation connects
  their inputs as specified below. That hypothesis is not discharged here.

## Smallest retained source component and explicit hypotheses

Use labels, not proposed compiler fields:

```text

b_x       inner step formal

l_step    returned closure's source occurrence
L(C)      resolve(u_f)=b_f; resolve(u_x)=b_x;
          c belongs to l_step; l_step retains that b_f capture;
          final step returns the closure without calling it
S6(C,e)   the independently generated ordinary endpoint/call skeleton
Reg(C,J)  the missing completed original symbolic association certificate
```

`L(C)` follows from the selected source meaning. For the following ordinary
skeleton only, assume the approved structure is represented as the finite
ordinary graph to which typed-core §6 applies. That representation is not
current production acceptance. Generate unannotated parameter endpoints
`A_f,A_x`; retain the outer binding identity in the inner environment; generate
the whole-argument/complete-call obligations without claiming them solved.
Then §6 supplies

```text
P_f=Value(A_f); P_x=Value(A_x)
Gamma_inner(b_f)=Value(A_f); Gamma_inner(b_x)=Value(A_x)
I(u_f)=Value(A_f); I(u_x)=Value(A_x)
I(c)=Computation(E_c,A_c), with callable/path/contract obligations
```

This `Gamma` contains ordinary value endpoints, not a completed Function
interface, slot inventory or profile. The stored callable's actual role/entry
is not inferred from the ordinary binding tag. No solution of the call
obligations or seed discharge is assumed.

The required `Reg(C,J)` must associate the original provider retained by
this closure with one source-generated role-indexed contract, its original
position-plus-contract `beta,Slots(beta)`, typed capture/use incidences,
annotation scope, and one original jointly scoped `nu,K,D`. It may retain
unsolved endpoint constraints. Original source-origin classifications,
guard obligations and binder-environment requirements must be proved or
carried as unresolved obligations; lexical nesting does not decide them.
Later receiver activation is a separate certificate.

One captured formal, its one call occurrence, and one returned closure
suffice for this audit. A second outer activation, request, effect family,
annotation or recursive group adds no discriminator at this cut. Removing
the capture or the original-association target removes the assigned seam.
This is minimization of proof coordinates, not an accepted-program witness.

## Two partial protocols and their exact residual

Write `Pending(C,J,Obl)` for a candidate association *obligation*. It does
not mean `Reg(C,J)` holds. `J` is proof notation for an unsolved relation
root; introducing its name proves none of its semantic properties.

**P-L: resolved-component input.** From `L(C)` and the retained source
occurrences/annotation absence, introduce one pending `J` for this shared
component. Its obligations include ordinary endpoint generation, source
contract/profile formation, typed capture/use incidence, original logical
scope, source origin/guards, joint constraints and independent admission.
No `beta` value or complete `Slots` inventory is asserted.

**P-S: ordinary-skeleton input.** From `L(C)` and an independently generated
`S6(C,e)`, introduce one pending `J` connected to the already named ordinary
endpoints and call obligations. All contract/profile, typed-incidence,
origin/guard/scope, joint-constraint and admission obligations remain.
The argument's Value evidence is recorded without performing the separately
unproved formal/use seed discharge.

The distinction is material to a proof packet: P-L accepts only resolved
source input at its first step; P-S additionally requires a generation
certificate and can immediately identify symbolic endpoint references.
It is not yet a difference in complete association or observable behavior.
Neither protocol uses §3.1's full decorated envelope as a hidden input.
Neither permits a pending marker to authorize admission, transport,
dynamic boundary introduction or an authoritative profile.

The exact common blocker is a new proved judgment

```text
Finish(C,e,J,source_origin_and_scope_obligations,...)
    => Reg(C,J)
```

whose leaves derive the contract/provider/profile incidence from this source
component instead of supplying it inside a completed `Gamma`, decorated
input or transport certificate. Merely adding `Q` to neither protocol's
signature does not prove that `Finish` exists or that admission is independent.

**Conditional output equality.** Hypothesize one independently justified
relation `Finish(C,e,J)` that consumes the same endpoint skeleton, original
source associations and jointly scoped constraints in both protocols; P-L
later obtains exactly P-S's skeleton, up to one permitted joint renaming,
and adds no further acceptance condition. Then their completed-output sets
are both `{J | exists e. S6(C,e) and Finish(C,e,J)}`, at the same original
source coordinates. This follows by substituting the two premise sets;
no witness is chosen independently per port. The equality proves neither
`Finish`, nonemptiness, uniqueness/principality, B-equivalence of an actual
scheduling optimization, nor source adequacy. Consequently these protocols
cannot be advertised as two different complete language rules.

## Preservation, independence, mutations and failure conditions

Both candidate protocols must carry the original source occurrences,
annotation presence/scope and source obligations. They cannot identify
`beta` with a lexical key, split `nu,K,D`, independently freshen a captured
provider per use, weaken recursive origins/guards, select handler eligibility,
revive an ended receiver, or treat capture as dynamic authority.
Callback B remains normative. No callback-literal scheduling change is
proposed; any subsequent optimization still owes B-equivalence. Existing
Pure callable values retain their actual role and entry. No annotation is
introduced into this exact source; scoped permissions on other sources are
neither widened nor realized by this audit.

There is no executable oracle, numerical seed, enumeration range or runtime
trace. The independent external constraints are the accepted source decisions;
the conditional packages share their supplied source typing, original
profile, binder and joint-constraint assumptions. The conditional equality
above deliberately shares `Finish`; it is not independent validation of
that source rule. A checker executing P-L/P-S/Finish would assume the
missing transitions and would not prove them.

Two static mutations delimit the claim: promote `Pending` to `Reg` without
an introducing proof, or require supplied `Reg` as P-S's skeleton premise.
The first has an unproved conclusion; the second moves the desired
conclusion into an input. These were inspected symbolically, not executed.
They are proof-packet mutations, not alternative accepted semantics.

The prior two attacks already left the same formation premise untouched.
This audit changes method to compare input contracts and explicitly stops
without another equivalent toy probe. A complete nonuniqueness witness
would need two independently justified `Finish` clauses differing beyond
joint renaming/staging. No such pair was found in the inspected interfaces.
No exhaustive search of possible inference systems is claimed.

This result needs reassessment if an overlooked governing clause introduces
the complete association from `L(C)`/`S6(C,e)`, if a reviewed clause supplies
either completion, or if a direct semantic dependency changes. A staging
optimization might have B/residual consequences outside this audit; the
conditional equality does not cover those absent certificates.
