## Inventory and telescope

→ receipt/Force/rebind → immutable Name → pure/invocation Return.
Before challenges Parameter introduces one ordinary Desc `a` at `sigma`;
formal, body and outward payload share it. Delta contains only already
introduced incident operands. The bare source has no annotation, literal,
captured import, operation bound or additional Function/effect demand.
Build §4 generates the local clauses of this chain; it cannot synthesize a
new fixed Unit operand merely because Unit is useful to a later quotient test.

## Smallest discriminating derivation

Assume the counterexample's genuine independent ground laws: pure Unit and
Bool carriers have complete certificates, identity inclusion is available,
and Unit has no same-value inclusion into Bool. Preserve all other supplied
bare-id guards and the same fixed native provider U_g.

At prefix `A=Unit`, the supplied `J_U.payload=Unit` has identity mu_result;
the selected bare-id projection derivation supplies Delta evidence. At prefix
`A=Bool`, that **same** J_U cannot supply mu_result, because it would require
`Unit -> Bool`. Thus this J_U challenge is admitted at the first prefix and
excluded at the second. The Bool prefix separately admits its genuine J_B
with identity inclusion. These are two alternative prefixes and two supplied
ground challenges, with no search or assertion about all other challenges.

This conditional source derivation establishes a real source-owned
description-dependent admission boundary without H3. It does not reproduce
the earlier H3 mutation attack: a quotient might map J_U and J_B to one
observer-respecting abstract extension and lift it differently at each parent.
Different concrete challenge sets alone do not refute such a quotient.
Conversely, the absence of H3 does not prove that erasing A is valid: the
quotient still owes reflection of mu_result, whole J/context clauses and every
actual retained client predicate at each original prefix.
