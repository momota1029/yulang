## 3. First unsupported rule: initial interpretation, before receipt

The most favorable route is to retain an original declared `I` alongside
these native fields, then show that both presentations interpret the same
designated execution. The first required readout is already at zero steps.
Let `d` be an independently assembled complete challenge of the original
entry interface, at its actual event `e=(sigma,h)`. Let `o0` be the actual
initial observation of the original invocation: no argument result has been
installed and no receipt has yet been established. The needed bridge has the
following shape, under the same original source and independent guard inputs:

```text
original declared I at P; native pair (a,epsilon_entry) at P;
d with its original independent context/entry guards;
o0 at the actual C_e,w_e
---------------------------------------------------------------- Init bridge
an interpretation of the native pair's designated entry whose initial
observation is this same o0, preserving its original phase/path/world guards
and original witness, with the corresponding action under legal sigma.
```

This is a requirement to interpret and transport independently supplied
evidence. It does not ask syntax, an effect row, or a source label to create
runtime guard truth. The native constructors above do not define the target
initial-observation predicate or its typed guard action. Entry-Value supplies
ordered static ports; source-interface insertion supplies contextual incidence;
neither is this interpretation law. This is the first unsupported rule in
the attempted bridge, rather than a later missing raw-handle field.

The semantic side genuinely supplies a route once its independent input is
present. Semantic input realization §2 fixes the compatible context and its
receipt/path preconditions independently of membership. Its §3.1 includes
zero-step observations in the complete observation obligation. In the exact
selected two-closure constructor, simultaneous immutable introduction §4's
“Admission/initial prefixes” paragraph derives the initial immediate guards
from independent context guards plus original inert registration evidence,
and its simultaneously constructed world. Section 2 of that constructor
starts with independently interpreted `I_x,I_y`; constructor inversion
recovers those stored interfaces. This is an available local constructor
argument, but it does not supply the broader native-row interpretation above.
The native recursive-interface selection copies the entire selected
presentation, including background worlds and guards; it does not identify
that mathematical copy with solver Function4 merely by the word “native.”

Two tempting eliminations fail to add the missing premise. Eliminating
ContextualFunction membership gives all actual observations only **after**
membership at an already interpreted complete descriptor is established.
Using that result to introduce the same complete interface at `P` would be
circular. Eliminating IF-Use recovers the whole instantiated interface only
from an originally registered complete telescope and legal substitution;
`candidate_call::observe` authenticates native provenance but does not supply
that original telescope or its guard interpretation. No successful Function
comparison is used as either premise.

## 4. Same-sigma and later phases: exact conditional continuation

Call-input §4 proves whole-substitution for its source certificates and
explicitly leaves semantic substitution to the original interpretation's
lawful action. Source-interface construction §2 likewise lists original
lawful whole-map action as an independent input. Substituting all native
handles therefore supplies syntactic coherence, not the missing Init bridge.

For a proposed bridge `b` over the complete original telescope, the joint law
would be a dependent naturality equation
`b[sigma](sigma(x)) = sigma(b(x))` at each legal scope, including each field's
type. In particular a future interface `J_new(p_new)` must move as
`(sigma J_new)(sigma p_new)`, with that arm's own license and scope; it cannot
be replaced by an independently chosen interface having a matching native
Result. Captured imports remain fixed where required. The current event's
`C_e` is used at that history, rather than a maker-world snapshot. The native
scope and child checks establish source ownership but do not establish this
semantic equation. This records the required test without claiming its proof.

Conditionally on a complete entry interpretation and its lawful initial and
carrier actions, semantic input realization §4.3 and the selected local
constructor identify the next argument: unfold the admitted whole carrier's
universal Force-observation obligation. Its pending case must preserve the
same request, typed response, original raw handle and ordered suffix at the
actual resumed `C'`; ordinary Bind does not prepend receipt on resumption.
These are later obligations, not results of this source challenge: the
attempt stopped before the initial bridge. No request/response experiment or
source-qualified pending counterexample was run.

Noncallable alternatives retain their own original membership and incidence.
Every genuine same-provider `Act(v,U,r)` remains quantified; no projection
selects a provider. All output-dependent futures and original whole-Call,
callee, receiver and outside-arm alternatives remain in the parent. Consumed,
protected, unmatched, outside-arm and resumed contributions remain distinct.
This analysis neither removes them nor claims their preservation theorem.
