## Candidate T interface

Use proof notation retaining original identities:

```text
T_xi(S,ell,omega,sigma,delta_ann,delta_comp)
  => tau : OriginalPosition(beta,p,original_profile_entry,
                            omega,sigma,ell,
                            local_realization,predecessor_evidence)
```

This is an obligation signature, not an implication proved by its inputs.
Its constructor must come from source formation over S. delta_ann checks
and exports a target; delta_comp contains positions. Neither alone derives
their occurrence correspondence. Necessary conditions on tau are:

1. p is an original effect-observation position of this beta/G/profile and
   retains source occurrence and lexical scope.
2. Local realization and predecessor evidence survive. Boundary successes
   cannot be composed into an original-to-final concrete comparison.
3. Profile/dependency incidences retain the same xi and original predicate
   identities; shared abstract/concrete occurrences remain joint.
4. Complete call, body result and latent result positions remain distinct.
   Typed direction is derived after p is known and cannot replace identity.
5. Q is no premise. The approved local annotation-boundary comparison can
   occur in delta_ann; that is distinct from pending Function comparison Q.

This signature is relational: it does not assume unique-position solving
for arbitrary decorated sharing. For this clause the eventual source rule
must determine the applicable original incidence to the extent H2 needs.
If several completions are possible, characterize them jointly at xi and
prove the required uniqueness/principality. Choosing separate completions
per port is invalid. No selection algorithm follows from this interface.
