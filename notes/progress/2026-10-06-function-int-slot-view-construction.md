environment value and no ambient handler. This is the predecessor's typed

```text

c_arg = result(literal(1))
C[-]  = call(result(H), c_arg)
```

`literal(1)`, its `result`, `result(H)`, and `call`. Delay is the whole-carrier
translation of the argument, not an extra source call. For this chosen
literal-argument route there is no removable node: the result constructors

removes the inlet challenge. This is syntactic minimization within this
route, not a claim that every possible admitted context has this size.

## 5. Failed routes and stopping point

**Candidate empty decoration.** Choose all slot/CallView profiles empty,
no inherited boundary roots, and an empty initial receipt graph. This gives
a finite mathematical packet and makes profile transport empty. It leaves
unproved the source declaration that no callback protection applies to the
complete slot/view inventory. Typed-boundary §6 says omitted/wildcard
capture annotation supplies protection at applicable callback positions;
empty effects do not identify that set. This candidate is an assumption,
not a constructed source witness. The Int carrier's computation port and
the callable's observation port cannot be erased as a substitute.

**Use Invoke to create the missing profile.** §10 says to establish source
boundary instances, then record receipt and execute entry. It does not
derive those instances' signature profile from the call skeleton. Receipt
creates ownership of a use, no boundary or contract. Using `H` as callee
does not make its own invocation a receiver of its public callee view.
Therefore Invoke cannot certify the missing profile by its own receipt.

These two routes leave the same formation premise untouched. The lane
stops here. Theorem C §3's Unit example lists a known slot/view as a datum;
it provides no general slot/profile formation clause to specialize at Int.
Typed-core §6 retains admitted annotation/typed-flow premises, and
typed-boundary §6 explicitly leaves source profile derivation to elaboration.
Within these exact governing sections there is no earlier rule discharging
`Form_Int`. This is a bounded absence finding, not an exhaustive search of
all historical repository material or a reason to reopen selected semantics.
