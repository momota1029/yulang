
Conditional on independently typed actual carriers, the existing entry/body


different computations. q at the entry prefix is not an event from the

complete f invocation, including its actual entry and consumers; it is not
the whole step invocation. Equating the two merely because their eventual
body endpoints coincide would drop the original carrier/entry dependencies.

correspondence, the f packet stays at f's typed paths. At the displayed entry

discriminator inside the inspected kernel; it does not prove absence of all

The second branch cannot use only transport of p0 as its last rule. Capture
identity retains the f port; returning step does not expose its private f

immediate `call.effect` onto a result-latent effect simply by returning a
closure. Nor does Dir-Protect supply the seed/exposure/contribution premises
for a new latent introduction. A separately justified original signature


portwise completion is allowed.

For a single candidate h with no other protective incidence, ordinary
coverage and no concrete grant, typed-boundary §6 gives the conditional

live incidence at pS could change candidate eligibility where the f/p0
capture route alone does not. Actual differing handler selection or output

Those are proof obligations, not supplied observations.

Receiver expiry is a separate failure condition. If the original boundary
receiver is inactive at Cq, its candidate incidence is false in both branches,
even if the static profile survives in step. One cannot revive that receiver
or silently replace it with step's receiver. The approved body only returns
step; it supplies no invocation of step inside its own block. Neither the
mere returned closure nor a diagram of a surrounding consumer establishes
the required admitted live-receiver query.

**Precise blocker:** source attachment/licensing of cS to a beta-owned pS
covering the external step carrier, and complete exclusion/inversion for the
other branch, are missing before a pair can be formed. Independent whole-row
admission and activation remain further obligations. Turning these missing
judgments into Boolean inputs would characterize chosen assumptions, not
produce an Authority-consistent source countermodel. The attempt stops here.
