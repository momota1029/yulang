## Independence, omitted cases and stopping condition

No Oracle, executable checker, compiler test/build, random seed, numeric
enumeration or mutation execution was used. The two constructor expansions
are an analytical calculation over one finite fixture, not independent source
semantics validation. Their shared assumptions are the supplied constructor
law and admitted typed request/response interfaces. A checker implementing the
same equation would add replay evidence; it would not prove the source law,
admission, original contribution preimage, owner introduction or coverage.

Option 2 may admit a reversed observation through a separately licensed extra.
That does not certify changing this original source witness's ordered map.
Conversely, this note does not reject all reversed observations from production
membership or define production bounds by exact source traces. It tests the
source-base correspondence and original contribution provenance only.

Failure conditions: the candidate retains a certified active ordered map;
suffixes are not independently admitted or typed; no original returning
development exists; requests are observationally indistinguishable even in
the required complete evidence; the proposed observation projection forgets
the distinguishing information and only projected equality is at issue; or
the coarse view itself includes the active semantic interpretation. Each
invalidates the stated discriminator or its applicability, rather than being
silently treated as a passing case.

Unverified: actual original a/c and slot/path inhabitance; the independently
admitted h; entire all-world/future-use family; exhaustive licensing; production
membership/extra rules; recursive/generalized/mixed cases; any implementation
fault. No whole-repository absence or complete semantic model is claimed.

This method connects one omitted semantic coordinate to a supplied constructor
law, but it leaves the same original OC-Owned-Image introduction untouched.
Stop here; another permutation/metadata checker would not reduce that premise.
Recommended next action: require the source producer to return and justify the
original ordered source-to-contribution map together with O0/O1/C1/J0, then
use this two-suffix mutation only if a separately authorized checker has that
independently justified input.
