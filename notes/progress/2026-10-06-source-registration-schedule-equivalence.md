## Objective and result

Compare component-wide preallocation of pending source associations with
demand/call-triggered association that subsequently joins the same source
component. The ambiguity audit's P-L/P-S protocols specify different first
inputs, not complete inference rules. Demand-triggered allocation is a further
candidate completion/scheduling hypothesis; it is not established by P-S.

Under the explicit hypotheses H1–H8 below, both schedules produce the same
completed symbolic fact set up to one joint scope-preserving renaming. The
same independently interpreted constraint set consequently has the same
solution set. This conditional theorem does not construct a source rule,
prove existence or principality of a solution, or establish B-equivalence.

Joining the same component alone is insufficient: the three-atom trace below
loses an enabled constraint when late registration does not replay an earlier
fact. This is an abstract protocol countermodel. It becomes evidence about
Yulang only if its transition rules are independently connected to approved
source rules or an authorized implementation. No such connection is proved.

## Exact graph and worklist hypotheses

Fix one source component C, an independently generated ordinary skeleton e,
and the approved source meaning. The following are sufficient, not claimed
necessary, conditions.

H1. **Common generation contract.** Both protocols ultimately materialize
exactly the same independently justified source-generation rules and input
facts. Their only difference is when pending roots become available. A
pending marker does not certify `Reg`, a completed profile, admission or a
dynamic receiver. Neither protocol invents facts from comparison Q, public
type shape, pointer equality or allocation order. This is precisely the
currently unproved rule correspondence; naming it does not discharge it.

H2. **One fixed graph and original coordinates.** Relevant declarations,
definitions, uses and recursive references are fixed in C. Both schedules
refer to the same node identities and original logical binder environments,
with recursive SCCs represented by shared nodes, not repeated unfolding.
No generalization freezes an SCC before all its source obligations close.
If roots are allocated in different orders, one injective joint renaming
transports every dependent coordinate; original scopes and rigid inputs
remain fixed. Lexical nesting alone does not choose the logical binder tree.

H3. **Finite monotone presentation.** There is a fixed finite universe U of
symbolic facts, including pending markers R, and finitely many positive rules
`premises subset X => add conclusions`. Facts retain unresolved constraints;
adding constraints can shrink their solutions without deleting facts.
Rules are monotone and inflationary under inclusion. More generally a
finite-height lattice suffices, but the proof below uses finite sets. No
absence test, first-observed role choice, destructive profile replacement,
fresh allocation on each recursive revisit or unbounded shape invention is
hidden inside a rule. Provisional Handler obligations may only be encoded
monotonically if an independently proved discharge certificate and final
interpretation justify that encoding; this note provides no such rule.

H4. **Complete demand coverage.** Before declaring inference complete, the
lazy protocol registers all roots R that the eager protocol's rules can
require. A trigger expands to the whole same relevant component, including
all recursive SCCs and retained captures. Relevant components without a
trigger must also be visited, or separately proved irrelevant to every
published contract and acceptance condition. In particular, returning a
captured closure without invoking it is not proof that its original source
contract can be omitted. A pure unseeded registration cycle is not a demand
coverage proof. A demand may defer allocation; it may not discard constraints.

H5. **Fair late replay and actual closure.** Registration schedules every
newly enabled rule, including rules whose other premises were seen earlier;
each productive enabled rule eventually runs. Source-edge/SCC additions
likewise replay affected existing facts. Work deduplication is finite, and
termination means all required roots exist and every productive rule is
closed, not merely that an event queue is empty. The exact replay mechanism
is not selected. Zero productive work may remain at publication.

H6. **Preserved entire source relation.** Productive facts preserve
position-plus-contract `beta` and complete `Slots(beta)` incidence, original
annotation presence/protection and typed permission, typed name/capture/use
paths, owners/receipts and dependent provider roots, source origin and guard
obligations, and one original jointly scoped `nu,K,D`. Constraints on the
whole relation remain active at their original typed incidences. They are
not independent port witnesses or independently hidden/freshened fragments.
Actual providers from separate outer activations are not equated merely by a
common static binder label. Capture does not create a live receiver.

H7. **Publication and solving after closure.** The completed symbolic output
is a common function of the closed set, modulo the joint renaming, without
allocation/event history. The same independently defined whole-relation
constraint interpretation determines solutions and admission. Generalization
and use preserve sharing through their separately justified certificates.
No premature successful comparison, unsatisfiable partial branch, seed guess
or incomplete slot inventory commits a result. If a solver chooses one
method/adapter or evidence witness among alternatives, an additional common
selection/equivalence argument is required; equality of solution sets does
not supply one.

H8. **Evidence and B boundary.** All evidence is retained with its original
incidence and source execution order, or a separately proved equivalence
justifies normalization. Normative B still delivers its known callback
boundary before body generation, synthesizes independent endpoints, and
checks the one completed inequality. Registration can schedule administratively
only within that preserved dependency order. No evidence-bearing force,
receipt, boundary introduction or annotation check moves in execution.
Diagnostic presentation is a common function of the final evidence/constraint
object, or its order equivalence is separately proved. The fact-set theorem
below uses H1–H7; H8 names obligations needed beyond that theorem.

## Finite closure derivation

Let S be the common source/skeleton input facts, R the finite set of
administrative pending markers, and T the common finite positive productive
rule family. Define

```text
F(X) = X union S union R union
       { conclusions(t) | t in T and premises(t) subset X }.
L = least fixed point of F on subsets of U.
```

This definition does not assert that a marker in R proves an original
association. A registration rule's semantic consequences remain in T and
must have independent formation proofs under H1.

Eager starts with `S union R` and fairly saturates T. Lazy starts with S and
some subset of R, materializes the remaining markers on demand under H4,
and fairly runs the same T under H5. Generation can equivalently reveal
rules gradually if no unrevealed rule is omitted at completion and every
new rule replays its already present premises.

1. F is monotone and inflationary. Starting at the empty set, iteration
   reaches L after at most `|U|` strict fact additions; finite deduplicated
   scheduling gives termination. This bounds distinct additions only, not
   a particular implementation's rule evaluations, CPU time or allocations.
2. Every eager/lazy intermediate fact set is a subset of L: seeds and
   introduced markers belong to L, and each productive step applies a rule
   whose premises are already in L. Induct on the actual step sequence.
3. At completed termination, H4 puts S and all R in the final set X; H5
   makes X closed under T. Thus `F(X)=X`. Leastness gives `L subset X`,
   and step 2 gives `X subset L`. Both final sets equal L.
4. Projecting away administrative markers gives identical symbolic source
   relation/constraint outputs. H2/H6 compare all dependent coordinates by
   one joint renaming. H7 then gives identical full solution sets under
   the same interpretation, including identical inconsistency if empty.

Recursive SCCs introduce no extra induction assumption: shared nodes keep U
fixed. Each generated fact has a finite derivation from S/R. A cycle without
a base derivation generates nothing in least closure; neither coinductive
registration nor recursive unfolding is inserted to make it succeed.

The theorem is established within the proposed finite model. Its application
to the source protocols is conditional. It does not derive T from source,
prove the required finite presentation, or show that a least fact set is a
principal type. It also does not validate a solver by composing successful
concrete inequalities.

## Smallest explicit missed-replay countermodel

Remove H5's late replay while retaining identical rules and complete
registration of the same one-root component. Use exactly three atoms:

```text
d = one already available source call/skeleton fact (also the demand)
r = one pending component association marker
k = one generated symbolic constraint

S = {d}; R = {r}; U = {d,r,k}
trigger: d => register r
productive rule t: d and r => k
published output: whether k is present (r is administrative)
```

Treat d as a supplied static fact, not runtime execution or Q success.
Registration creates no role/profile/receiver. In both runs t has the same
premises and conclusion. The event implementation below checks t while
processing d; the lazy variant then installs r without replaying d or t.

| Step | Eager facts X | Eager queue | Lazy facts X | Lazy queue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial | `{d,r}` | `[d]` | `{d}` | `[d]` |
| Pop d; check t | `{d,r,k}` | `[]` | `{d}` (t disabled) | `[]` |
| Complete demand registration | `{d,r,k}` (r already exists) | `[]` | `{d,r}` (install r; no replay) | `[]` |
| Publish on empty queue | k present | `[]` | k absent | `[]` |

At the last lazy row, t is enabled. Therefore `F({d,r})={d,r,k}`: the
empty queue is not a fixed point. Both protocols registered exactly the
same component, yet the lazy reported result differs. If k denotes a
retained constraint `a=0` on a symbolic endpoint, eager's completed solutions
exclude `a=1`, while the defective publication omits that constraint. That
interpretation only illustrates possible downstream influence; it is not a
Yulang typing or acceptance claim.

This witness has one demand, one marker and one non-administrative output,
the minimum distinct coordinates for this explicit late-marker mechanism.
Removing d removes the early event; removing r removes late registration;
removing k removes the projected difference. No source-program minimization
or exhaustive enumeration is claimed.

Repairing the abstract mechanism by enqueueing t (or replaying d) on r
insertion gives `{d,r,k}` in the lazy run. This repair is a model implication,
not authorized compiler implementation.

## Evidence order, independence and failure boundaries

Final set equality alone cannot establish diagnostic sequence equality.
For example, two enabled diagnostic facts a and b can be accumulated in
either order by fair runs of the same positive rules. Their final set is
`{a,b}`, while “first emitted diagnostic” can differ. Such an emitter is
outside H7's publication condition. Canonical source ordering or a proved
presentation equivalence is an additional obligation, not an inference
from commutativity of set union. Likewise an effect row set cannot replace
ordered receipt/Force/body or annotation-boundary evidence. A proof of H8
and of B's acceptance, principal-solution, method/adapter and residual/evidence
equivalence remains separate.

No executable oracle, random seed, range or search was used. The independent
constraints are the approved source decisions and exact governing sections.
Both abstract schedules intentionally share S, R, T and the final constraint
interpretation. Their equality is not an independent validation of those
source rules. A checker implementing these assumed transitions would prove
only model consistency, not the missing introducing judgment.

The missed-replay mutation attacks H5 and is worked out completely above.
Other explicit failure boundaries are demand coverage omitting a relevant
root; moving a binder or splitting a joint witness; a nonmonotone rule
choosing from absence; seed deletion without proved refinement; freezing a
recursive SCC/generalization early; changing protection from annotation
absence; or publishing the first evidence event. These were premise checks,
not executed additional probes or accepted language counterexamples.

The prior registration attacks left original formation unproved. This note
uses a different method to isolate a sufficient scheduling theorem and a
failure of an incomplete replay mechanism; it stops at H1/H3/H6. Filling
T with guessed contract, origin, guard, role-discharge or annotation rules
would encode new semantics and is outside the assignment. No further toy
variants are needed to address that unchanged formation premise.
