## 3. Objects, acceptance and quantifier order

Fix one supported source snapshot S, original source occurrence graph and
binder tree T, declared abstraction, and rigid outer imports/captures rho.
RAW_SOURCE supplies the finite complete original relation J_S with all
source roots, profile/provider coordinates, origins, permissions, worlds,
method/role alternatives, recursive labels and live K/D incidence.
SOURCE_ADEQUACY interprets it against the actual source machine. The
original witness positions are those of T, not a prenex existential block.

Fix the whole relation returned by exact joint solving. PROJECTION computes
its legal finite exported scheme P_S, including designated exported roots,
complete residual, original binder/identity incidence and evidence. Completed
HIR_WIRING makes this the actual publication represented by the pipeline;
completed RESOURCE and FRESH_LIFE exclude partial or mixed-attempt results.
The publication is fixed before a client use graph or its assignments are
chosen. Multiple boundary roots from one simultaneous interface remain one
joint object, rather than independently computed root marginals.

Let U be any finite ordinary client use graph accepted inside the exact
resource/source envelope. U may contain several distinct incoming source
instantiation events, aliases of a single event, internal SCC uses and
arbitrary cross-use client constraints. Let C_U contain its complete client
relation and let Q_U contain its actual ordinary queries with their original
submitted operands, guard contexts and resolution evidence alternatives.
U is fixed before public assignments, challenges and histories.

For each genuinely distinct incoming instantiation event u, FRESH_LIFE gives
one coherent map iota_u on eligible local identities, fixing rho, with
pairwise disjoint eligible ranges. Every type, effect, recursive and evidence
occurrence of one identity uses that same map. Aliases of one event use its
same copy; internal SCC uses retain the live joint roots. Client and original
rigid identities remain fixed. These are actual maps, not hypothetical maps
selected to repair an accepted result.

An accepted residual is not treated as proof that every assignment satisfies
it. In the residual route the statement applies to every admitted scoped
solution/strategy of the published accepted-use residual. The residual
continues to impose its obligations. If executable-use acceptance claims a
successful solved query, HIR_WIRING identifies that result with JOINT_DEC's
exact successful result and evidence. Thus there is no rule "accepted
syntax implies every pending Direct succeeds".

The theorem's order is:

```text
for each supported S and completed publication P_S,
for each finite actual ordinary-use graph U,
for every admitted original outer environment and every satisfying
  public/client strategy of the actual accepted-use result,
there exists a compatible original-scoped joint source/use strategy,
  reflecting that same public/client strategy and evidence.
```

The reflected relation contains the appropriately indexed original source
relation for every authorized fresh instance, the one shared non-generic
outer assignment, internal live relations, C_U and Q_U. It is one strategy
for their complete conjunction. This does **not** force two legitimate
independent polymorphic uses to choose the same eligible local value. It
does force all incidents of a shared original coordinate, and every alias
of one use, to agree. Distinct copies are copies of the same original
source template, not independently invented source semantics.

## 4. Construct the reflected joint strategy

### 4.1 Reflect the actual successful result to the complete use graph

Start with an arbitrary actual admitted result strategy theta. The solver
part of HIR_WIRING identifies the actual submitted finite graph and result
with the specified complete solver inputs/results. JOINT_DEC's reflection
therefore reconstructs a strategy for that entire graph. Every active
source, client, guard, permission, admission and evidence predicate remains
in the graph. An exact residual route reconstructs the strategy after its
retained residual is satisfied; it cannot drop an unresolved conjunct.

This step uses solver reflection on the complete joined input. It does not
solve the uses independently and combine arbitrarily chosen witnesses.
The actual query evidence is part of the reflected result, not inferred
from semantically compatible printed endpoints or comparison transitivity.

### 4.2 Undo actual freshening on the joined graph

Use the actual maps from FRESH_LIFE. On incoming-use local namespaces take
the inverse iota_u; on shared outer/client coordinates take identity; on
internal live roots retain their current source association. The resulting
product map exists because eligible ranges for independent uses are
disjoint and all rigid/shared coordinates have one designated meaning.
Transport every incident predicate and evidence operand together.

The inverse is indexed by the use event: `iota_u(x)` is transported to
`(u,x)`, not to one unindexed template coordinate x shared by every use.
Thus two legitimate independent polymorphic instances remain two separate
copies of the original template's eligible local coordinates. Aliases of
one event retain the same u, and genuinely shared rigid coordinates have
no new use index. The product action never merges independent instances.

The existing [joint-use reindexing theorem](../design/2026-10-01-coupled-effect-interface-core-draft.md),
"Joint uses under a shared receiver context", reflects

```text
(and_u K_u^copy) and C_U and Q_U
```

as a whole formula even when C_U or Q_U correlates different copies.
Its covariance and identity-observer hypotheses are supplied by the actual
FRESH_LIFE/IFACE_EQUIV cone, rather than derived from equality of printed
schemes. The [CI_USE result](2026-10-07-successor-recursive-synthesis.md)
likewise concerns arbitrary finite jointly constrained fresh uses and
unchanged Direct queries. No client conjunct is discarded by this pullback.

### 4.3 Reflect the whole projected use back to the original relation

PROJECTION's exact original-fiber projection and ordinary-use/evidence
factorization now apply to the actual represented projected scheme, by
HIR_WIRING's generalizer/instantiator/publisher correspondence. Reflect the
projected residual and its whole ordinary use to the same original solved
relation, keeping the client's public assignments, query operands and
evidence. Hidden coordinates are reconstructed at their original eligible
binder positions, bound once around all their incidents.

The [certified ordinary-use construction](../design/2026-10-04-certified-callback-and-constrained-use.md)
§§5.1–5.2 requires the copied residual to be the actual original residual
under permitted transport and retains complete Direct evidence. It explains
why this use is the ordinary complete-copy/client-conjunction/query graph.
Theorem 3's all-view extension direction is unnecessary here: there already
is an accepted use strategy to reflect. Neither ALL_VIEW nor a new public
query succeeds as a consequence of this argument.

For finitely many independent copies, restore the hidden coordinate family
for each copy under its original binder tree. Distinct eligible local
families are disjoint, so their extensions combine; the outer assignment
and client coordinates are already the same fixed joined strategy. Any
coordinate with another incident use/provider/admission/query dependency
is retained or bound once jointly by the full projection contract, so it
cannot be chosen anew per fragment. With aliases or simultaneous member
roots apply the whole-interface reflection once, rather than per root.

Traverse the original binder tree explicitly. At a universal position,
keep its arbitrary current environment and reflect under that environment.
At an existential position, reconstruct its permitted witness using only
the preceding environment and the supplied projected strategy. At an
event-local position, retain the event's allowed extension and all sibling
compatibility conditions. For `exists shared. forall kappa. exists body`,
first reconstruct shared; then reflect body under each arbitrary kappa.
Do not change this to `forall kappa. exists shared,body`. This yields one
permitted strategy for the complete original source/use conjunction.

The witness reconstruction is a composition of the upstream exactness and
transport witnesses; it introduces no semantic candidate choices outside
those proved correspondences. It is not a promise that the compiler emits
concrete worlds or runtime Skolem functions. Effective finite publication
and solver construction are the existing PROJECTION/JOINT_DEC tasks.

### 5.1 No lifecycle assumption of SOUND or PRINCIPAL

The [conditional rebuild lifecycle theorem](2026-10-05-inference-lifecycle-interface-conditional.md)
§2 explicitly assumes a valid, sound/principal rebuild in its first
hypothesis. Its REUSE conclusion cannot establish that hypothesis, and this
proof does not import it to prove SOUND. In particular, no sound/principal
initial rebuild or SOUND/PRINCIPAL induction hypothesis justifies the
reverse-pipeline reflection in §4.

First apply §§4–5 to a fresh complete publication at each source snapshot,
using HIR_WIRING's actual stage correspondence, JOINT_DEC, PROJECTION and
the independent source/production interpretation. FRESH_LIFE contributes
only its actual maps, sharing, reference/dependency validity and atomic
publication correspondence at this step. Its REUSE dependency is not
invoked as a semantic proof of fresh snapshot validity. The actual maps and
barrier are required outcomes of the full existing FRESH_LIFE contract,
not inferred from the lifecycle theorem's conditional validity premise.

Only after complete snapshots have separately received this semantic
soundness argument may lifecycle correspondence transport their results
through unchanged complete interfaces, valid references and atomic reuse.
For a reused result, its originating complete publication must therefore
be covered by the same reverse-pipeline proof; equality or a cache hit
alone does not certify it. If the REUSE theorem is used with its additional
principality hypothesis, that hypothesis must be established separately
by the PRINCIPAL route; the SOUND composition neither assumes nor derives
it. This ordering prevents the conditional lifecycle result from becoming
a circular premise for sound inference.

## 6. Adversarial weakenings

These tests explain which existing clause prevents a failure. They are
mathematical contract weakenings, not compiler defects or countermodels to
the full sufficient route.

1. **Topology without stage-result correspondence.** Keep the correct
   solver for original public fiber `{0}` and route its result to a publisher
   that emits `{0,1}`. Retain all graph connections and fields. SOURCE_ADEQUACY,
   JOINT_DEC and PROD_CONFORMANCE can hold, but the accepted `1` has no
   original source extension. This preserves weaker plumbing, while it
   violates complete publisher correspondence to the full PROJECTION
   result. It is not a counterexample satisfying HIR_WIRING in §2's reading.
2. **Marginal projection without ordinary evidence reflection.** Retain
   the correct public residual `{0}` but let the projected-use evidence
   accept a guard/evidence alternative absent from the original query.
   Public marginal equality remains true. The joined original use cannot
   be reconstructed. Full PROJECTION's evidence/use clause rules this out.
3. **Pointwise hiding with a binder exchange.** The original formula
   `exists b in {0,1}. forall k in {0,1}. b=k` has no strategy. The weakened
   `forall k. exists b. b=k` does. Pointwise reconstruction per k is
   insufficient; exact original-binder projection and JOINT_DEC strategy
   reflection retain the first formula's failure.
4. **Shared-non-generic identity duplicated across uses.** Let one outer
   Boolean b be shared and let a joint client demand b=0 through one use and
   b=1 through another. Independent per-use satisfiability holds. A faulty
   instantiator freshening b accepts both together. The original joint
   source/use relation has no strategy. Full FRESH_LIFE fixes that rigid
   coordinate, and JOINT_DEC reflects the joint conjunction, so the route
   rejects it. Distinct eligible polymorphic locals are different and may
   legitimately take these different values.
5. **Independent observation/domain marginals.** Hide an admission-live
   witness and admit a challenge via one value while producing an extra
   observation via another inadmissible value. Root marginals can look
   adequate while the source/production squares fail on the original
   fiber. Full projection preserves that dependency, and PROD_CONFORMANCE
   applies its nonredundant containment quantifiers at the same original
   tuple, rather than independently existentially projected marginals.
6. **Partial or stale publication.** Expose the new residual with old
   evidence/roots before the SCC's new complete result is ready. Correct
   abstract projection and solving remain possible, but the actual accepted
   result is a mixed object. Full FRESH_LIFE/RESOURCE and complete publisher
   correspondence forbid it. A conditional theorem about coherent maps
   alone would not establish that the actual barrier exists.
