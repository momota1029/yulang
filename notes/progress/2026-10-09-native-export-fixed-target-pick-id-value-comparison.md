## Same-provider carrier route: what it would and would not prove

There is a concrete source-directed candidate for the carrier, rather than a

```text

```

The selected immutable Name/Return/Delay construction offers the following
conditional instantiation. Assume the original reify license and lawful

compatible live event. Resolved Name then supplies the same `(0,p_0)` and

its reify license, exact carrier port and dependent scope pairing are not

also have a different provider and would not substitute for `p_0`.

Even if these operands construct the complete `t_0` certificate and
`mu_adm` is supplied, replaying id on this single carrier would establish a
pointwise source behavior at that challenge. To derive the requested Value
term by that route one additionally needs an original-interface law stating

designated-Force behavior. Choosing `t_0`, proving `H` for one challenge, or
strengthening the target by `H` does not supply that universal premise.

This separates three claims: own-head target validity is supplied under the
independent-pick source hypotheses; same-source id validity has no supplied

still needs the finite whole-tuple implication at the two actual roots.

challenge premise untouched. This note therefore does not propose another
VP-only probe of `E_U` as progress on the requested certificate.
