## Proposed smallest coupled internal seam

This is a proposal under the selected internal gate, pending the separate
constructive invariant and authorization for exact compiler/test paths.

1. Add an internal negative result-effect construction at the existing paired
   effect-port owner. Supply its annotation/scope, exact `row.position`,
   resolved members/ordinals, composed polarity, row level, original symbolic
   binder when present, and source occurrence. Reuse
   `candidate_formal_effect_variable` for that binder; do not allocate an
   unrelated coordinate and infer equality later. Retain one local atom-set
   identity for the paired operations. Closed and symbolic-tail branches need
   their own stated constructor equations; do not assume the symbolic equation
   also determines the closed boundary.
2. Give `candidate_context` executable attachment payloads and exact operation
   construction for the paired view, preserving negative filter versus PUSH
   versus scoped POP. Keep the actual call/output owner connected to that
   identity: `candidate_source` Apply formation and `shadow_apply` source
   admissions currently provide four-port demands, but retain no parameter
   subtraction predicate or corresponding lambda-output operation. Inert
   `InferredEntryOrigin` is not a certificate authorizing `BothFromRight`.
   Do not inject POP at formal formation or globally clear the enclosing row.
3. Place the consumer at the row-to-annotation-upper relation boundary reached
   by `candidate_context_execute_relation` and `candidate_apply_effect`, before
   ordinary endpoint equality/self omission. Retain each constructor's lineage,
   source coordinate, concrete heads, exact residual context, gamma coordinate,
   attachment and origins. Emit **both** the source-to-retained-head/gamma
   feedback and exact gamma-to-original-tail projection through existing typed
   relation/bound/work owners. The proposal does not require every branch to
   allocate gamma: empty-retained, filter-only and genuinely discharged cases
   need separately justified transitions.
4. Index retained recipes by canonical source coordinate without using that
   coordinate, nominal family or evaluated residual key as recipe identity.
   Register all current source lowers at construction and fan future admitted
   lowers to every recipe; include gamma upper feedback. Preserve both parents
   and exact context of each emitted obligation. Complete a child before
   acknowledging it; existing replay cursors distinguish queue transfer from
   drain completion.

These four responsibilities are the minimum coherent construction/consumption
cut. An internal harness can supply resolved source data to it while public
private-source preflight and general formal admission remain disabled. It must
not bypass guards to claim the source infers. No new source restriction or
approximation follows from using a bounded harness.

## Current/future lowers and transport lifecycle

Current allowance behavior already uses an ordinary negative upper bound as
the retained check. `candidate_apply_effect` inserts then
`candidate_replay_bound` meets opposite physical bounds.
`candidate_context_execute_relation` reconnects an already registered
Allowance to each new occurrence and replays it; discharge preserves a directed
post-check context. `candidate_context_begin_replay` produces ordered lower ×
upper fiber dependencies, retains shared context children and schedules only
new quadrants for ordinary replay. Incoming scheme uses repeat diagnostic
delivery with their own origin. This machinery supplies reusable delivery
owners, **not** the absent per-lineage watcher theorem.

Material exception: `candidate_insert_bound` is storage/evidence insertion,
not a general guarantee of initial queue activation. Signature construction
and extrusion use direct insertion. Existing source-recursion findings record
that the direct extrusion insertion does not enqueue its initial contextual
replay. The new recipe producer must explicitly own initial replay and later
delivery at every insertion path; watching only `candidate_apply_effect`
would miss this seam.

`candidate_extrusion.rs` transports views/tails through level/polarity maps,
including incoming Allowance capture incidence. `candidate_effect` retains
`CaptureIncidence { source, view, next }`, registers it for mixed Allowance
bounds, and splices buckets on equality. This is capture reachability metadata;
it is not an applicable-lower residual watcher.

`candidate_scheme.rs::capture_candidate_graph` reaches row bounds and their
exact contexts, captures context-only payload views and tails, and retains
bundle/evidence references. Freshening uses a per-use view remap, weight remap
and `rename_fresh_contexts`, preserving shared nodes inside the use while
creating fresh local weight identities. Its remap key includes original view
and mapped tail. The new attachment's operation nodes, recipe heads/source/
gamma/tail, registrations and watcher incidences must join this same closure;
capturing only a visible output port loses latent feedback. Existing freshening
cannot be claimed to cover these absent records.

`candidate_intrusion.rs` records actual extrusion parent/copy pairs and merges
only SCC-qualified pairs. Its merge appends both bound sides, transfers origins,
splices capture incidence, changes the representative, canonicalizes third-owner
bound fibers, and replays combined lowers. New recipe buckets must move with
the representative without merging lineage identities. A recorded qualifying
gamma pair remains eligible; gamma is not exempt by its role.

Existing context transport preserves post-check contexts and explicit
`TransportWitness` reasons for extrusion, parent-copy, equality and fresh use.
One visible evidence limitation remains:
`candidate_effect.rs::candidate_transfer_bound_origins` says its origin table
lacks origin-to-fiber association and explicitly records
`CoverageGap::MissingOriginAssociation`. Thus endpoint/origin presence cannot
authenticate the exact new recipe emission. Keep its source relation/parent
certificate at construction rather than reconstructing that association.

`candidate_context::Checkpoint`, effect-algebra checkpoints and the route
journal retain contexts, relations, bounds, origins, discharges, replay cursors
and ownership state. New recipe/watch/registration state must participate in
successful continuation and invalidation. `State::retained_input` still returns
Incomplete, including producer-readiness and dependent-observation gaps;
synthetic source-support registrations are not production cycle certificates.
The exact two-cycle acceleration is therefore no blanket general admission
argument. Under top-level principles, an abnormal route may stop/discard safely;
full capacity restoration/retry is not an extra requirement without an owning
semantic proof. No inconsistent graph/result or stale certificate observation
may escape.

Current construction clones the concrete vector into each View and LocalWeight
and allocates a member-ordinal vector: O(m) payload storage per attachment plus
scope/source handles. Detached materialization clones the family again but is
not a live hot-path cost. Context interning is structural, preserving child IDs;
it does not prove termination or semantic context equivalence. Existing replay
already pays for the admitted lower × upper fiber product. Recipe fan-out must
at least pay O(k) emissions for k applicable distinct lineages per new lower;
record per-recipe/per-input progress instead of rescanning historical events.
These are structural accounting obligations, not measured timings or a new
approved layout. Exact context growth for mixed cycles remains unresolved.

A source-row recipe index with O(k) reachable iteration is a proposal. Avoid
whole-state scans on every lower; include actual allocated capacities, context
payload storage, indexes, dependent observations, retained child batches and
temporary rename maps in existing resource sampling. Sharing immutable resolved
sets might reduce repeated O(m) copies, but changing storage is an implementation
choice requiring its preserved-identity invariant. No benchmark was run.
