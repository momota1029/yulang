## 1. Result and exact remaining boundary

There is a terminating exact observation algorithm for **arbitrary finite,
possibly branching recursive one-ID left-only grammars**, followed by any
fixed finite mixed operation tree. The recursive grammar permits PUSH and POP
prefixes, left-only replay of independent recursive children, and left-only
constants. Its weights are the full pairs `(p,n,0)`; **p and n may both be
positive and may both be unbounded**. Recursive PUSH mass is unrestricted.
The continuation permits actual bracketed replay, swap, both-from-right,
prefixes, right suffixes, arbitrary finite constants, and finitely repeated
shared or independent inputs. Its observations include exact count thresholds,
identity, both pending-presence predicates, active/filter violations, and the
local compact left-ID entry predicate at their actual continuation nodes.

The algorithm does not cap counts or assume a finite recursive PUSH budget.
It reduces a query to point reachability in a finite pushdown vector addition
system with states (PVASS). A published terminating PVASS reachability
algorithm supplies the final decision step. This is a decision reduction,
not the claim that constructing a finite grammar itself decides its queries.
No small resource bound or suitable production implementation is claimed.

This is strictly beyond the earlier debt-grammar/fixed-PUSH-continuation
interface: recursive PUSHes can produce unbounded active counts, and the
algorithm retains the **joint** leading-debt/active relation. It is also beyond
an active-only regular-path theorem: recursive binary replay is allowed, the
left word language can be context-free rather than regular, and finite mixed
queries may compare correlated exact counts.

The result does **not** decide unrestricted recursively mixed grammars.
Recursive swap/both/right debt, and recursively shared child copying, are
excluded from this construction. It also does not derive their absence from
source. Any source application must establish precisely this left-only,
independent-child grammar interface or provide a separate bridge. A source
separation into debt-only recursion is not assumed or proved here.

## 2. Exact supplied algebra and bracketing

Use natural counts for one authentic identity, with fixed active family S.
Write `W=(p,n,r)` for left `POP^p PUSH^n` and right `POP^r`. The supplied
operation equations are inherited from the reviewed companion theorem and
its pinned owners, not independently re-audited against source in this lane:
`constraints/mod.rs:3566–3612` and
`constraints/directed_weight.rs:12–39,137–176,399–420`.

Left composition is

```
p0 = p1 + max(p2-n1,0)
n0 = n2 + max(n1-p2,0).
```

Replay uses this composition and `r0=r1+r2`, then the actual directed mix.
If either left word or right debt is empty, mix leaves that single-sided
weight alone. When both are nonempty, append the right POPs to the left word.
If an active PUSH remains, retain the resulting left word and set r to zero.
Otherwise move the pure residual debt to the right, including exact identity
when everything cancels. Thus in the participating case:

```
if n0>r0:  (p0,n0-r0,0)
else:       (0,0,p0+r0-n0).
```

The other operators are

```
swap(p,n,r) = (r,0,p)
both(p,n,r) = (r,0,r)
prefix(a,b)(p,n,r) =
    (a+max(p-b,0), n+max(b-p,0), r)
suffix(c)(p,n,r) = (p,n,r+c).
```

All mixed continuation nodes retain their original syntax and guards. No
mixed replay reassociation occurs. The left-only grammar has `r=0` at every
node. In that restricted subalgebra mix's single-sided case always applies,
so replay really is ordinary left-word composition. The associativity used
in the next section is proved for that subalgebra alone; it is not imported
into mixed replay.

Filters have a finite exact decoration domain generated by the finitely
specified constant filters under intersection. Active families are fixed S
in the recursive grammar. Finite continuation family changes, compatibility
checks and filter resets may be represented as finite decorations and tested
at their actual nodes. Arbitrary parameterized families, source endpoint
registration and exact residual-key allocation are outside the theorem.

## 3. Recursive grammar interface, including the full pair

A left-only grammar is a finite set of nonterminals and finite productions.
Every leaf is a finite `(p,n,0)` constant. Internal recursive operations are
left prefix by a fixed word and replay of left-only children. A replay child
may be another expression, so this includes arbitrary finite nesting and
arbitrary cycles. Independent occurrences of the same nonterminal choose
independent finite child derivations. All derivations are finite trees.

No production binds a recursive derivation once and uses that chosen child
repeatedly. Such recursively shared copying is a different language class
and is not silently replaced by independent occurrences. Sharing of a chosen
whole grammar result **in the fixed continuation** is supported exactly.

Let `U` denote one PUSH and `D` one POP. Reduce literal words by deleting
adjacent `UD`. There is no overlap of two redexes, so the terminating deletion
system is confluent. Its unique irreducible words have the form `D^p U^n`.
Concatenating two such normal forms and reducing gives exactly the displayed
left composition. Consequently normalization is a monoid homomorphism from
literal concatenation to left-only replay. In particular, original left-only
bracketings give the same pair without changing any mixed bracketing.

Replace a constant `(p,n,0)` by the literal word `D^p U^n`. Replace replay by
concatenation in the same order; replace prefix by its fixed literal word
followed by its child expression. This effective finite translation produces
an ordinary context-free grammar `G_T` for every requested nonterminal T.
Induction on derivation trees proves

```
Values(T) = { (p,n,0) | some w in L(G_T) reduces to D^p U^n }.
```

The equality is exact even for `(p,n)=(1,1)`. Collapsing to the signed integer
`n-p` would identify this value with identity and lose both activity and
pending debt. For example, the recursive production

```
T -> I | prefix(D, replay(T,U))
```

has exactly `(k,k,0)` for k>=0. It has unbounded recursively introduced
PUSHes and leading POPs together. The theorem also permits added productions
`prefix(U^2,T)`, `replay(T,D)`, and `replay(T,T)` with independent children;
these introduce actual cancellation and branching recursion. No finite
recursive PUSH mass is asserted for any of these examples.

Finite filter decoration can be incorporated by refining nonterminals by
its exact possible result. The operations on filters are specified finite
operations, so this refinement is a finite grammar construction. This does
not add a legality guard to derivation semantics: observations of violations
are separate queries.

## 4. Global-minimum marker lemma

For a word w of length l, let h(j) be the PUSH-minus-POP balance of its first
j symbols, with h(0)=0. Literal normalization gives

```
p = -min_{0<=j<=l} h(j)
n = h(l)+p.
```

Proof: while reading w, a PUSH increases active n; a POP decreases active n
if positive, and otherwise increases unmatched leading p. Therefore the
accumulated leading p is precisely the largest prefix deficit, and final
balance equals n-p. This proves the formulas for every finite word.

Here is an equivalent relation designed for a PVASS. Nondeterministically
choose a natural p and a split `w=w_before w_after`.

1. Start a counter a at p. Execute the +/-1 updates of w_before, requiring
   ordinary counter nonnegativity throughout.
2. Freeze a, and start a fresh counter b at zero. Execute the +/-1 updates
   of w_after with the same ordinary nonnegativity requirement.
3. Require frozen a=0 at the final point target. The retained p and final b
   are the reported leading-debt and active counts.

The target condition a=0 is essential. It is **not** a new guard on grammar
steps: a remains untouched after the split and its value is checked only as
part of ordinary point reachability.

Soundness: a=0 means h(j)=-p at the split. Nonnegativity before the split
means h(t)>=-p for all earlier positions. Nonnegativity after the split means
h(t)-h(j)>=0 for all later positions. Hence h(j) is a global minimum. The
formula above gives exactly leading p and active b.

Completeness: choose p from the formula and any position attaining the global
minimum. Both nonnegative runs exist and frozen a=0. Repeated global minima
may give different markers, but all give the same `(p,n)`; no overapproximation
is introduced.

If the frozen-zero requirement were omitted, word `U`, split before U, and
guessed p=1 would falsely report `(1,1)` instead of `(0,1)`. This is why
nonnegative generation alone does not suffice.

## 5. Compiling an exact residual generator into a PVASS

A PVASS has finite control, one ordinary pushdown stack, and finitely many
natural counters. Transitions change counters by fixed integer vectors and
are enabled precisely when the new vector remains nonnegative. There are no
arbitrary semantic tests in the construction.

For each independently selected grammar input allocate three counters:
retained debt d, prefix-run counter a, and suffix-run counter b. Initially
all are zero. In an initialization control state repeat a transition that
adds one to both d and a, then nondeterministically stop. This guesses p and
stores it in d while a starts at p.

Use the standard grammar-to-pushdown generator of L(G_T): expand a nonterminal
on the pushdown by a production and process terminals in their written order.
Finite control carries a before/after phase. Exactly once, at any terminal
boundary, an epsilon transition changes before to after. A U or D terminal
adds respectively +1 or -1 to a in the before phase, or to b in the after
phase. The pushdown generator must finish the complete grammar word, the
marker must have occurred, and its generation stack must be empty.

Counter a is never changed after the phase switch. Counter d is never changed
after initialization until the final query gadget. The final target requires
a=0. By the marker lemma, the pairs `(d,b)` available to that target are
**exactly** the full normal-form residuals of finite derivations of T.

For k independently chosen grammar results, run k such generators in sequence,
reusing the single generation pushdown but retaining separate triples of
counters. Earlier generators' a counters stay frozen. Each generator's
nonterminal and finite filter decoration are part of finite control. The
counter dimension is 3k, with no unbounded number of query inputs because
the continuation is finite.

For one shared choice used several times in the continuation, generate its
pair once, and use the same two variables in every occurrence of the observer
formula. For independent choices, use independent generators. Equal
nonterminal names do not imply sharing.

## 6. Finite mixed observer graphs are effectively Presburger

Every displayed primitive operation has a graph definable using natural
addition, equality, order and Boolean cases. For example, the definition of
`z=max(x-y,0)` is

```
(x>=y and z+y=x) or (x<y and z=0).
```

Constants are finite natural numerals; swap and both are linear equations.
Replay is represented by the left-composition equations, right addition,
then the actual mix cases from section 2. Introduce three fresh variables per
actual observer node and conjoin its graph relation. Raw unary intermediates
with both active PUSHes and pending right debt are retained; they are not
pre-normalized or silently mixed at a different point.

A fixed threshold observation is `n>=c`, `n=c`, or the requested finite Boolean
combination of count comparisons. Active and pending predicates are `n>0`,
`p>0`, and `r>0`. Compact left-ID entry presence is `p+n>0`. Identity is
`p=n=r=0`. A fixed-family active/filter violation is `n>0` together with the
finite decoration fact `S` not included in the actual F. Finite terminal
head subtraction changes the family decoration, leaves counts unchanged,
and can precede a following check in the same formula. These definitions
preserve actual check placement.

After existentially eliminating internal observer variables, the accepted
input pairs form an effectively Presburger-definable set

```
Q subset N^(2k).
```

Equivalently Q has an effective finite union of linear-set representations
`v0 + sum_j h_j*v_j` with nonnegative natural vectors and h_j>=0. Computing
this representation uses the ordinary effective equivalence of Presburger
sets and semilinear sets. It does **not** assert the grammar's full residual
set is semilinear.

There is a concrete sharing discriminator. With T having only P_1 and L_1,
let `C(t)=replay(t,swap(t))`. Shared P_1 gives P_1; shared L_1 gives R_2.
Neither is identity. Independent choices t1=P_1,t2=L_1 in
`replay(t1,swap(t2))` give identity. The query formula for the shared case
reuses its `(p,n)` variables; the independent case has two generators.

## 7. Semilinear target gadget and termination

For each linear component of Q, nondeterministically select that component,
subtract its base v0 from the retained counter vector `(d_i,b_i)`, and loop
subtracting any of its period vectors v_j any finite number of times. Other
counters, including the frozen a_i, stay untouched. After these subtractions
enter a distinguished final control state and require the **whole** counter
vector to be zero with empty generation stack.

The subtraction phase succeeds exactly when its entry vector belongs to that
linear set. If a vector equals a base plus a nonnegative combination of
periods, subtracting the base and the chosen periods cannot underflow: after
each subtraction the remaining vector is the sum of the remaining nonnegative
periods. Conversely, a run ending at zero proves precisely that equality.
Taking finite union covers Q. No zero-test transition is introduced; final
zero is the point target. For Q empty there is no target path.

The entire construction is a finite PVASS point-reachability instance.
Soundness of a reaching run follows successively from grammar generation,
the marker lemma, exact observer graph, and the target gadget. Completeness
uses actual finite derivations, a global-minimum marker for each generated
word, the exact continuation evaluation, and its corresponding linear-set
decomposition. Thus point reachability is equivalent to the original
existential observation question. A universal pass query is the absence of
an existential violating choice. Empty grammar denotation is also decidable
by using Q equal to the whole input space.

The terminating external decision theorem is Guttenberg, Keskin and Meyer,
**PVASS Reachability Is Decidable**, LICS 2026, Article 53, DOI
[10.4230/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.53](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.53).
The official [paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol380-lics2026/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.53/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.53.pdf)
proves decidability in arbitrary counter dimension. This reduction invokes
that published algorithm; it does not reprove it or present a practical
implementation. The paper reports Hyper-Ackermann complexity. No claim of
cheap solver computation follows from the mathematical termination result.

All preceding translations are finite effective constructions and use no
iteration cutoff. Their composition with this point-reachability algorithm
is therefore a terminating exact decision algorithm for the displayed
recursive grammar/observer interface.

## 8. Observations at recursive production nodes without changing semantics

The theorem does not add guard-enabled derivations. Grammar values above
include every finite derivation allowed by its unguarded productions.

Nevertheless, existence of a violation at **some** recursively repeated
production node can be decided when each production has a fixed finite local
observation template over its own child results and constants. First remove
unproductive nonterminals and productions with unproductive required children.
Compute which remaining productions can occur in a finite derivation of the
requested root; this is ordinary finite grammar usefulness. For each such
production, independently generate the residuals of its required child
nonterminals and apply its local finite observation tree at the actual node.
There are finitely many production templates, so finitely many queries suffice.

Every violating local choice embeds in a root derivation because the selected
production is useful and its independent siblings are productive. Conversely
any violating occurrence in an actual root derivation appears among these
queries. This proves existential bad-node detection, and therefore universal
legality of all generated derivations, without moving checks after replay or
pruning derivations by a newly invented guard.

This is **not** an algorithm for existence of an entirely legal derivation
under semantics that prunes or conditions each recursive production on its
checks. Such conditioned semantics needs its own correspondence and theorem.
It is also not an algorithm for jointly observing an arbitrary recursive
node and its root with correlated ancestry; only the stated local templates
and finite continuation queries are proved.

## 9. Precise obstruction to extending this construction

The left-word CFG translation fails as soon as a recursive operation introduces
right debt, copies a child, or applies swap/both to a recursive residual. This
is an exact failed premise, not a generic impossibility assertion.

The companion obstruction's debt grammar
`T -> R_1 | replay(I,both(T))` has residuals R_(2^k). Even its exact unary output
language is not context-free, so the CFG-generator step in section 3 cannot
silently handle that cycle. The reviewed debt-only theorem handles its finite
PUSH-budget observers by another method; that theorem cannot supply the
unbounded recursive PUSH budget needed here.

Full mixed replay also lacks the left-only homomorphism used to flatten words.
For example, with P_1=(0,1,0) and R_1=(0,0,1):

```
replay(replay(P_1,R_1),L_1) = L_1
replay(P_1,replay(R_1,L_1)) = R_1.
```

The weights distinguish pending side even when active count agrees. Therefore
no normal-form-word flattening is claimed across those brackets.

The PVASS construction freezes a global-minimum certificate until its final
zero target. It supports a finite number of such certificates, one per query
input. A recursively mixed replay tree would require compositional certificates
for unboundedly many internal mixed/copying nodes. No reduction of that tree
to this finite PVASS has been proved. Merely labeling the tree a finite grammar
would leave its decision problem unsolved.

No unrestricted impossibility or undecidability claim follows. The exact
remaining work is to construct an observation algorithm closed under recursive
swap, both, raw right debt and shared children, **or** to derive from actual
source owners the left-only/debt-only separation that makes the established
subsystem algorithms sufficient. Finiteness of IDs or endpoint names alone
establishes neither premise.
