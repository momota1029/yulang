## Name classes and identity reads

A presentation name is distinguished from its assigned semantic object.
Relabelling the bound coordinate for A and pulling back its assignment can
leave the same descriptor A as semantic operand. Moving an actual registered
descriptor root instead changes an operand and needs that root's own action
and readout law. Generalize's fresh declaration keys do not identify these
two operations. Likewise, renaming an event-binder variable does not replace
the actual runtime event, its occurrence or its parent lineage.

## Conditional whole-image derivation and exact blocker

Let rho be a bijection on the permitted presentation names. Required hypotheses:

1. rho fixes all selected source/provenance/registered/runtime identity readers,
   preserves sorts, binder order, owners and every dependent telescope, and
   acts once on the complete incidence graph.
2. Every independent input of gamma, its J member/challenge/history domains
   and hereditary evidence has a typed action with inverse rho^-1. Both
   directions preserve its full judgment, not merely scalar payload membership.
3. Each active registered local rule (including continuations, primitive
   guarantees and equation pairings) has the corresponding two-way covariance
   law on all original operands and evidence; all alternatives are retained.

Then constructor inversion transports each gamma field. Induction on the
actual `delta_check` tree transports the exact-conjunct/equality/logical cases;
hypothesis 3 supplies the genuine leaves and scoped continuation cases. At
Return, hypothesis 2 transports the same hereditary V evidence through
mu_result; at Request/prefix it transports the complete unchanged-kind J
fields and each original continuation subtree. At future result ports it
uses that same transported hereditary certificate. Hence

```text
rho(Image_m_gamma(h)) = Image_m_(rho gamma)(rho h)
```

as equality of whole images with a bijection on their full dependent witness
records. If m is written as a map on records, its input must include the
selected local output/intermediate evidence; covariance does not manufacture
a canonical witness selector for a relation. Reflected admissibility follows
by the identical induction for rho^-1. Lemma S is compatible with this square:
it eliminates the same carrier and whole-image proof and never selects a
different observation. It does not itself supply hypotheses 2–3.

Claim class: conditional theorem, not established complete nominal action.
The restricted unchanged-semantic-operand identity cases discharge the relevant
hypotheses locally. Generalize §5.2 allocates schema keys and scope routing;
it expressly excludes an alpha-transport theorem. Source-contracts §2.1 names
the capture-avoiding action, §3.4 requires transformation certificates and §5.3
allows primitive equality only under a **supplied legal** renaming/graft with
matching full operands. None proves hypotheses 2–3 by successful checking.
CI §5.1 explicitly requires independent covariance for every used operation.

Candidate assumption, not an adopted result: every actual independent leaf
observes names only through the enumerated operands and those operands admit
the proposed action. L1–L5 require genuine full local laws and telescopes;
they do not state this nominal law for every such leaf. A model with an opaque
name-sensitive leaf therefore illustrates non-entailment only until its actual
source declaration is exhibited. This research does not select that model or
reopen the ordinary source meaning.
