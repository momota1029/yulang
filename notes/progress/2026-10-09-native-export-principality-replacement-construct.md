## 1. Fixed objects and original-scope judgments

Fix genuine formation inputs Γ, original binder/event tree T, fixed fields Ω,
and authentic source S. Keep all independently interpreted local laws, original
world/authority/registration guards, providers, complete carriers, proof slots,
and source Generalize premises required by PE. Nothing here constructs those
ambient premises from the source string alone.

Let

```text
G = Generalize_source(S,b)
P = Extract(G)                       -- chosen once, before a client view
u_i = Decode(P,i,A_i,whole_action_i)  -- at each actual original new frame
```

The actual source-selected result is the complete synthesis root for this bare
definition. PE §4.2 installs active ordinary equations on u_i, including its
whole inlet, A_i result, EntryValue dependency, admission, VP+Echo production
and ordinary certificate slots. Aliases reuse the same frame; different real
new frames have independently owned eligible images. Ω and Shared fields stay
fixed according to T. There is no identification of `B_common` with u_i and no
use of `Q_common` in this construction.

Use `Sol_T(K;W)` for the complete retained strategy-and-observation fiber of
an independently formed joint grammar K, including W. A strategy assigns a
binder only from fields in its original telescope. It is a strategy over all
independently admitted challenges, histories and finite futures in K, not a
separate satisfying tuple chosen after each challenge. All nondefinitional
proof choices are coordinates of the fiber. This notation names existing
semantics; it introduces no changed admission or witness relation.

Use `IndependentValid_S(V;ν,η)` for the governing independent validity judgment
for a finite public scheme V about the same actual source/provider, at its
original scheme assignment ν and complete strategy η. Its definition must come
from the required consumer contract. This note does not choose between a
source-derivable contract domain and a larger semantic-valid domain. The latter
is the explicit target when attempting the canonical PRINCIPAL obligation.
Denote its retained fiber by `Fib_T(V)`. V and its validity are defined without
P, extraction, public Direct or inference success.

## 2. What PE-ID gives, with quantifiers visible

For the fixed genuine inputs, PE provides one finite legal selected native P.
For every independently source-lawful finite grammar d and well-scoped W,

```text
∃ finite U_d, F_d, G_d, formed from d before assignments/challenges/histories:
  F_d : Sol_T(d;W) ≅ Sol_T(U_d(P);W) : G_d.
```

Here U_d is the whole public allocation/check/run/join/Bind grammar, not a
collection of marginal queries. F/G eliminate/restore only forced private raw
aliases. They preserve Ω, original worlds, ports, proof choices, event scopes
and correlations. The production coordinate is preserved by equality of the
independently selected VP+Echo clauses, not by source-execution inversion.

At every ordinary checking occurrence of d, the same registered finite local
L term, with simultaneous operand substitution, is recognized by PE §6.1 at
the actual u_i and actual target. Value, Computation and complete Function are
separate original proof constructors. An executed conversion remains executed
and uses its resulting handle. Thus no argument may replace the original
target by a convenient synthesized root or force Any/union into Function.

The existential U_d precedes assignment, challenge and history choices. PE
does not instead assert `∀ semantic V ∃ d`; that is the missing implication.

## 3. Smallest sufficient presentation bridge

The following judgment is a **candidate proof obligation**, abbreviated VPRES.
It changes no source or public rule:

```text
VPRES_T(S,V) = (d_V, ρ_V, E_V, C_V)
```

Its fields must have these independently justified meanings:

1. d_V is a finite native source-lawful joint use grammar formed independently
   of P and public Direct. It contains the actual V scheme/use obligations,
   with their original operands, binding tree and correlations. It uses the
   same source/provider and retains every required annotation, conversion,
   check, domain restriction, run, Bind and future-use branch.
2. ρ_V projects its retained complete fiber onto the actual V fiber. C_V is a
   proof that `ρ_V(Sol_T(d_V)) ⊆ Fib_T(V)`. Keeping V's genuine clauses inside
   the same graph can establish this direction, provided their semantics and
   projection are proved; a copied display string is insufficient.
3. E_V is a scope-respecting right inverse for coverage:

   ```text
   E_V : Fib_T(V) -> Sol_T(d_V)
   ρ_V(E_V(η)) = η.
   ```

   It supplies one coherent original-scope extension for the whole strategy.
   Every retained V proof choice and all shared/fixed fields are unchanged.
   Existence of independently satisfiable frame witnesses is insufficient.
4. Each checking node of d_V has a genuine finite L proof at the original
   source operands. For a Function node this includes the full independent
   domain implication and complete-observation implication, including native
   Option 2 members; source-only execution inclusion is insufficient. Value
   and Computation retain their own full decorated premises. A conversion is
   represented by its original executed rule and result handle.
5. Its construction and finite graph/telescope maps are fixed from S and V
   before ν, η, challenges or histories. E_V may depend on η only through
   dependencies allowed by the original telescopes; it cannot let an earlier
   type choice inspect a later challenge or replay an executed prefix.

The universal bridge has quantifier order

```text
∀ independently valid finite schemes V in the required domain,
  ∃ finite VPRES_T(S,V), before assignments/challenges/histories,
    ∀ η ∈ Fib_T(V), E_V(η) ∈ Sol_T(d_V), coherently at T.
```

P is already fixed before the initial universal V. For a client's W, use the
same independently well-scoped W on retained coordinates, with PE's explicit
total raw-alias substitution. Universal W cannot be replaced by equality of
individual endpoint marginals.

VPRES deliberately requires no public Direct certificate. It instead exposes
the exact independent source/local-law completeness premise needed for PE to
construct one. This is a reduction to a different, source-directed method; it
is not evidence that the new premise is easier or true for all semantic views.
If item 4 can only be met by assuming every true inclusion already has an L
term, that is an unproved completeness theorem, not a certificate constructor.

## 4. Conditional composition and exact factorization

**Theorem candidate N-Factor.** Under the fixed genuine native formation inputs,
PE-ID, and VPRES for V, there is a finite whole public use grammar U_V, chosen
before assignments/challenges/histories, whose retained V fiber is exactly
`Fib_T(V)`. Every ordinary query introduced by d_V is checked at its actual
decoded u_i or its original conversion-result handle.

Derivation:

1. Instantiate PE-ID with d_V and W. Let U_V = U_dV and obtain F/G at T.
   Their construction does not inspect a later satisfying assignment.
2. Define the exported projection `π_V = ρ_V ∘ G`. For every public strategy
   τ, PE reflection gives G(τ) in `Sol_T(d_V)`. C_V therefore gives
   `π_V(τ) ∈ Fib_T(V)`. This is the no-additional-fiber inclusion.
3. For every η in `Fib_T(V)`, define the lifting `H_V(η) = F(E_V(η))`.
   VPRES gives a single original-scope source strategy; PE preserves its
   whole joint evidence and scopes. Then

   ```text
   π_V(H_V(η)) = ρ_V(G(F(E_V(η)))) = ρ_V(E_V(η)) = η.
   ```

   Thus `π_V(Sol_T(U_V(P))) = Fib_T(V)`, with an explicit right inverse.
   This equality does not identify distinct internal proof witnesses; π only
   forgets the additional coordinates specified by the independently proved
   presentation. PE F/G themselves preserve all retained evidence choices.
4. By item 4 and PE §7's proof-constructor induction, each local L term is
   translated simultaneously and recognized by PE §6.1. The produced query is
   `Direct(u_i,R_V; translated_term)` where that actual occurrence calls for
   such a check. The native rules inspect u_i's active ordinary equations.
   No Direct is a premise of E_V, and none is supplied by silently changing a
   complete Function inlet, target, world or provider.

Every step is a conditional composition of the displayed maps. This is a
useful extension of PE-ID's statement only if VPRES is proved independently for
a genuinely larger target domain. Merely renaming a supplied d as a semantic
view leaves the premise untouched.

For a whole finite scheme, d_V and U_V must include all correlated uses of
that scheme at once. Single-root membership inclusion or pointwise Direct is
not the exact ordinary-use/evidence factorization above. General effective
elimination of those fibers into a principal solver output remains a separate
projection/algorithm obligation.

There are two distinct unmatched components inside VPRES: source realization
of an independently valid V at the same assignments/origin, and production-
aware finite local checking completeness. Neither follows from the other.
For example, authentic native id execution can return the original value while
an ordinary admitted production observation contains an independent alternative;
a target law proved only for the executed return cannot certify the required
whole Function implication. This is a premise discriminator, not a new legal
counterexample or an experiment.

The exact COMMON-TOTAL obligation is still `∀ original s ∃ jointly compatible a:
Q_common(s,a)`, preserving the original source/admission relation. This route
does not prove it. The exact ALL-VIEW entry still requires original-scoped
`J_S ∧ K_G ∧ Q_common ∧ Direct(B_common,R_V)` for every independently valid V.
N-Factor does not imply that literal conclusion: it uses a distinct, selected
actual export and omits a common-allowance coordinate. It is a possible
replacement sufficient route for its native consumer, not a proof of ALL-VIEW.

Likewise the existing conditional PRINCIPAL theorem explicitly uses the actual
designated B_common and its unchanged upstream contracts. It cannot be invoked
by substituting the name u_i for B_common. A new native composition statement
can use N-Factor at P once the target domain, adequacy, full projection and
effective output obligations are established. It then proves a scoped native
principality result, not all-language PRINCIPAL. Covering every source form,
annotation/conversion/common-root case and the complete required scheme domain
needs further constructor-specific proofs and primary adjudication.

## 6. Architecture consequence and stop boundary

Architecture §7 permits F4 independent source-solution factorization as a
replacement route while retaining all legal source refinements, annotations,
actual export and coherent dependent evidence. In the native finite source-lawful
domain PE already supplies that constructive factorization. This establishes
no new all-view result merely by placing F4's name on the theorem.

For arbitrary independently semantic-valid views, VPRES is still open. If
semantic validity is exactly the legality criterion for every public contract,
proving universal VPRES requires semantic-to-source/local-proof completeness;
architecture §7.1 explicitly warns this may reproduce the old all-view burden.
No useful unconditional extension to that domain is justified by the inspected
sources. The specific gain is the factorization identity and the removal of
common-allowance construction from this *conditional native* proof route.

No selected meaning was changed to make VPRES true. Restricting independent
validity to supplied finite L terms, dropping production-only members, replacing
the actual target, collapsing proof-choice fibers, or allowing later challenges
to choose an earlier template would change or weaken the target; those are
failure conditions, not permitted repairs. No second equivalent toy probe was
attempted. Static analysis stops at the same two explicit premises above.

Recommended next action: assign an independent source/semantic correspondence
proof for VPRES on the required native view domain, with its first task to
decide whether the existing validity judgment supplies finite L proofs and
same-origin source realizations. Do not schedule another alias-bijection or
certificate-recognition proof; PE already supplies those parts.
