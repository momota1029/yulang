## The clause-grounded discriminator

### What registered recursion actually proves

Source-contracts §5.3 requires paired registered predicate equations and a
finite local `Le` proof between their defining operators. It permits only
assumptions `X_r <= Y_pair(r)` at the registered recursive references, accounts
for every source alternative, and requires positive child uses. Its proof is
induction on finite derivation height for the finitary grammars; the more
general argument is inclusion of least fixed points of monotone operators.
The roots retain their original source/guard/binder labels and incident
operands. Universal source binders stay at their original external positions.

Its conclusion has the form

```text
registered operator comparison certificate
  => Left_least_relation <= Right_least_relation.
```

It has no conclusion `DescMem(R_g,v_g,...)`. Source-contracts §2.1's positive
recursion describes the rule graph's relations, while §2.2 keeps ordinary
`DescMem` independently interpreted and jointly active. Declaring its
descriptor conjunct another registered least root is an additional premise,
not a consequence of the word “recursive” in the source grammar. The actual
clause/binder correspondence and independently sound descriptor introduction
would first need to justify that move.

Even if complete descriptor roots eventually expose suitable operators, a
comparison proof still needs a left-hand membership witness to transport:

```text
L <= D  and  L(y)  => D(y).
```

Here `y` must be the same full provider/observation tuple at its original
incidence. Using §3.5's realization theorem to supply a source-base bridge
requires its local descriptor typing premise; it does not construct that
premise. A separately supplied raw source membership witness would still
need the complete `L <= D` certificate and descriptor-root correspondence.
For `L` equal to the proposed certificate relation, independent acceptance
of that certificate by the ordinary descriptor remains the disputed premise.
None of these bridges is manufactured by self-comparison.

### Smallest logical witness for the invalid inference

Take one point `y` and one predicate `D` with `D(y)=false`. Then `D <= D`
holds, but `D(y)` does not. This is the smallest discriminator for the shortcut

```text
Eq(D,D), or Direct(R,R), therefore the actual value inhabits R.
```

The predicate can be the empty least relation of a positive operator; no swap
or nonmonotone recursion is needed. The example tests the conclusion sort of
the actual comparison rule. It is **not** a complete interpretation of this
Yulang source: no source realization, nonempty admitted inlet, complete
descriptor grammar, world validity or Option A constraint satisfaction is
certified by this one-point interpretation. It is consequently an abstract
rule-entailment witness, not an admitted-source counterexample.

The same separation follows directly from §5.3's **Whole Function
introduction**. That rule proves `Direct(A,B)` from complete domain equality,
complete membership inclusion, matched role/entry/consumer/non-coverage
interface, and one original incidence map. Its membership roots already
include ordinary descriptor membership. Reflexivity of those roots can
support a comparison certificate even when they have no inhabitants. The
rule does not add a value-typing premise/conclusion that validates `v_f,v_g`.
The whole calculus remains an explicit resolution-conformance hypothesis.

### Exact conditional transport theorem

If independently defined complete descriptor clauses expose a registered
least-root presentation `D`, if a registered left relation `L` admits the
same original tuple `y` independently of the target descriptor fact, and if
the complete §5.3 comparison certificate proves `L <= D` without losing any
original incidence/constraint, then `D(y)` follows. Apply relation inclusion
to that very witness. This conditional theorem introduces no coinductive
meaning and moves no quantifier. Its decisive missing premises here are the
actual presentation/descriptor correspondence and independent left witness.
The theorem does not imply that every ordinary descriptor has that form.
