## 1. Objective, method and result

The assigned question is whether the conditional unequal-grammar derivation in
[the all-view extension attempt](2026-10-05-all-view-extension-proof-attempt.md)
§7 can be instantiated by one independently licensed source/common/view pair.
The target must differ in an abstraction `Z` or `W` clause while retaining both
complete membership grammars, original source allocation, independent admission,
every original joint scope/incidence, provider future uses, and the actual common
export. The method is inspection of the supplied governing clauses, rather than
an executable model that assumes their missing source rules.

**Result:** no such pair is derived from the inspected clauses. The exact missing
premise is an independently justified formation/interpretation contract for an
actual unequal abstraction pair, including its exhaustive membership alternatives
and abstract-provider admission/future-use rules. Source-contract §3.7 explicitly
leaves these primitive contracts unselected. This result is a bounded account of
what the supplied clauses justify. It is neither impossibility of such a pair nor
a counterexample to the conditional extension or unrestricted principality.

## 3. Exact unsupported premise

For a single source component `S`, original binder tree, fixed whole fiber, and
actual common presentation `P`, the preceding conditional derivation needs:

```text
H_i = lfp X. G_i and
      (R_i or Z_i or exists x,z. X(x) and W_i(x,y,z)), i = P,V

IndependentSourceLicense(S,P,V)
ExhaustiveMembership(H_P) and ExhaustiveMembership(H_V)
IndependentAdmissionCertificate(P,V)
Le_Gamma(R_P,R_V), Le_Gamma(G_P,G_V)
Le_Gamma(Z_P,Z_V), Le_Gamma(W_P,W_V)
```

Every relation carries the complete old tuple, original scope tree, source paths,
provider incidences and parameter interface. The unequal clause must concern
`Z` or the abstraction relation `W`, rather than merely the public allowance
`W_public`. At least one abstractor must differ under the legal correspondence.
The local query-conformance hypothesis remains conditional even if these finite
proof DAGs can be constructed.

The obstacle occurs before the last two certificate obligations can be applied
to an actual source pair:

1. Source-contract §3.7, lines 304–331, specifies a sufficient positive grammar
   shape and requires independently interpreted whole-tuple abstraction
   relations. It explicitly says the relations are not selected there and
   leaves their validity/certification as a primitive-design obligation.
2. The same section, lines 367–380, requires complete alternative accounting,
   matched abstraction parameters, and independently supplied future-use and
   admission rules. Positivity cannot supply the admission certificate.
3. Section 6.1's finite constructor table generates conservative *allocation*
   obligations. Section 6.3 reconstructs original source endpoints and the
   matching non-coverage kernel. Neither gives a new root-extra or abstract
   rewrite formation judgment.
4. Section 7, lines 915–944, explicitly restricts its Option 2 corollary to
   paired `Z,W` contracts. It excludes unmatched abstractors and does not assert
   that every independently valid view belongs to that paired class.

Consequently, the inspected sources do not provide an actual judgment that can
fill `IndependentSourceLicense(S,P,V)` for a concrete unequal pair. The positive
grammar is an assumed semantic interface, not a source-formation derivation.
This identifies the missing premise without changing the admitted source class.

## 4. Two nearby derivations and their discriminating failures

### 4.1 Source-authorized public allowance widening

Section 6.3 explicitly allows the original endpoint `E_out = Read` and a separate
public allowance `W_public = Read,Write`, given its original scoped coverage
certificate. Section 7 then chooses only the fresh common coordinate
`a := W_public`, retains the old endpoint and provider contracts, and derives
same-provider absorption and local guarantees. This is a genuine clause-level
derivation already available under the package's conditional source contracts.

It does not produce the requested unequal abstractor. The allowance named
`W_public` is expressly distinguished from the abstraction relation `W` in
§7, lines 924–926. Differing base/envelope coverage leaves still lift through
the *same* declared abstraction grammar. Reclassifying an allowance widening
as an abstract rewrite would introduce the missing formation judgment rather
than derive it.

### 4.2 A local guarantee proof cannot create a root-extra contract

The following conditional proof fragment is available in §5.3:

```text
Cov_Gamma(E,A)
---------------------------------------- Guarantee
Le_Gamma(Bound_j(E,u), Bound_j(A,u))
```

Its hypotheses require exactly the same complete provider operand `u`, fixed
non-coverage envelope, full A/G inventory, original scopes, future domains and
invariant fields. Thus a proposed abstract clause that already exposed these
actual leaves could receive local inclusion evidence.

The unsupported inference is to take that proof fragment and *declare* its two
leaves to be the common/view `Z` clauses. No inspected source rule licenses this
placement, identifies all their membership alternatives, or establishes their
abstract-provider future-use and independent admission contracts. A `W` clause
would additionally require its predecessor/output/witness interface and a rule
for every changed coordinate. The `Guarantee` rule proves a relation comparison
under its operands; it is not a constructor for new independently valid source
abstractions.

There is a concrete nearby provider contract: source-contracts §4, “Bound
definition” and “Lemma 1: provider-bound monotonicity” (lines 434–478), defines
`Bound_j(E,u)` over a whole typed provider, including finite future uses, and
proves guarantee monotonicity. This grounds a provider operand for a candidate
local leaf; it still gives no rule placing `Bound_j`
into an exhaustive `Z/W` grammar or supplying that grammar's other alternatives
and independent admission. The candidate therefore has a lawful local
comparison premise but no source formation judgment for an unequal pair.

Changing an opaque primitive identifier also has no identity certificate:
§5.3, lines 604–607, requires identical relation identifiers, typed operands and
scopes under one legal transport. Replacing a primitive by an arbitrary safe
widening cannot bypass this requirement. These are failure conditions for
proposed witnesses, not minimized counterexamples to the extension theorem.
