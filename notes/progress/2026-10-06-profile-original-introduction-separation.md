## 1. Result and its exact boundary

Two **total candidate local source-introduction producers** can be constructed
over the exact resolved NoAnnotation formal and Call. They agree on the
existing `Gen-Call-0` source origin, role records, policy, original joint
valuation and every inherited packet. They differ by one result-indexed
dependent introduction schema whose origin is the **source formal plus its
resolved Call**, emitted before a result descriptor is solved. Both respect
the inspected selected source/transport/policy clauses at this local interface.

At the fixed descriptor `F_c.result = Thunk(empty,Integer)`, their original-position
certificates differ at `p_1=(beta,result.latent.effect)`. The second producer
has a fresh original introduction witness there, protected with no grant;
the first has none from this source origin. This is a persistent static
certificate difference before typed capture, actual receipt or receiver
activation. It is not obtained by copying `p_0`, discovering an effect row,
or importing a supplied original profile.

This proves **relative local clause non-entailment**, not two complete Yulang
languages, full source-valid admission separation, or main-gate closure.
In particular it does not prove that the intended original source relation
accepts either candidate completion. It constructs two expansions of the
selected *partial local contract*, rather than presupposing an exhaustive
original-introduction theorem. No all-world model is needed for this limited
claim. No missing-original-relation assumption is then used to certify the
expansions as the intended language.

The reduced design seam is concrete: **does the implicit unannotated formal
contract introduce a separately source-owned result-profile default in
addition to its use-generated immediate invocation position?** The selected
clauses do not answer this seam. Adopting either alternative as the original
rule requires a design completion/approval unless further existing authority
supplies that rule. This note itself selects neither alternative.

row. No additional `K` predicate or `D` truth condition is introduced. The
original `K,D` ledger is carried as before. The profile/source-introduction
records are dependent *outputs* of the local producer, not independently
chosen proof coordinates to join later.

**Totality.** On every resolved exact component, S0 emits its fixed schema
and S1 emits its fixed pair of schemas. On every well-formed base realization
of their dependent signature, the displayed set comprehensions have a
defined meaning. Empty `Eff(A_c)` yields an empty result-default realization;
nonempty `Eff(A_c)` yields the corresponding original positions. Failure of
the underlying Call constraints yields no satisfying Call solution; it does
not prevent either source schema from being emitted. Neither alternative
changes that failure or solves those constraints by profile generation.

**Counts and identities.** S0 has one introduction schema. S1 has two,
regardless of how many result positions are eventually realized. On a
result endpoint with `n` effect positions their generated inventories have

one profile position. Both use the same `beta`; the distinct source witness
`o_r` establishes the extra origin. Inherited packets can have arbitrary
profiles, even coincident static beta names, and retain their own provenance.
Their positions do not contribute to either generated count.

## 4. Conservative extension over the available static constructors

Extend either producer over the selected finite core tree by the same rules:

| Constructor | Treatment of the generated original introductions |
| --- | --- |

both inert closures and the sequential binding. It does not manufacture

Conservativity has two precise senses here. First, erasing just the candidate
original-introduction records yields the identical existing source skeleton,
operands, actual-role/entry facts, raw `xi`, Call constraint and inherited
packets. The canonical lift and erasure are inverse on the chosen producer's
output records, so neither introduces an extra restriction at that static
projection. Second, source transport clauses are unchanged: for each supplied
typed correspondence, apply `chi_out=union_i M_i*chi_i`, carry `D` by the same
indexed image, retain predicate identities in `K`, and retain source/runtime
lineage. Their composition/no-grant/depth proofs work for both outputs.

This is **not** conservation of every final semantic observation after
changing a profile. Active constrained membership and future visibility may
depend on the added profile; precisely that interpretation would give the
clause semantic force. No Call execution, independent import interpretation
or all-context admission is redefined or declared equivalent by this static
extension. Available primitive/decorated ambient contracts retain their own
profiles and are untouched. Arbitrary annotation or recursive-component
source-introduction rules have not been completed.

## 7. Decision consequence and remaining work

The two local expansions agree on every checked retained constraint but
return incompatible original introduction inventories at the **same**
descriptor valuation. Thus no proof using only those constraints can derive
both the complete original singleton and the absence of `o_r`. That absence
is a substantive source-production clause, rather than another instance of
transport algebra or a missing-rule inventory without candidate meanings.

The smallest proposed decision is:

> At this unannotated formal's shared inferred contract, does the formal+Call
> source introduce a result-indexed protection schema independently of
> immediate Call elimination? The result-default alternative marks matching
> effect positions of its result endpoint, with full protection and no grant;
> the elimination-only alternative introduces none there from this origin.

S0 answers no and S1 supplies a concrete yes completion. These options affect
original source certificates even before a runtime difference has been
constructed. Selecting “yes” still requires specifying/reviewing its exact
dependent domain for a broader language; selecting “no” must be recorded as
the local negative introduction clause, rather than assumed as a theorem.
Neither selection alone closes contribution completeness, actual receipt/
capture, same-root full-solution preservation, A, principality or production
Option A/2 correspondence.

Recommended next action: independently audit the two local clauses and the
source-origin versus dependent-address distinction, then present this narrow
source-default decision if no additional governing rule selects a producer.
Do not claim complete-language independence or promote the generated
singleton to the original singleton before that adjudication.
