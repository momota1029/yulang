## Result

The existing notes already cover the strongest generic construction obtainable
from supplied complete component views: extend one complete joint witness by
its derived port views, then forget only the new defining coordinates. Both
directions are proved for that operation. Existing notes also define the
same-assignment complete handler image. Repackaging these results as a new
mixed-row proof would duplicate them while leaving the same source premise
unproved. This lane therefore stops at the duplication boundary.

The missing premise is an independently derived source clause interpreting
the mixed annotation at its selected role, typed port and polarity, with its
joint component constraints and attachments. It is not an additional carrier.
The approved polarity-sensitive example constrains that clause but does not
supply it.

## Conditional endpoint and quantifiers

Fix an independently supplied receiver role, its separate actual entry mode,
an original typed effect-port path `p`, polarity `epsilon`, and one joint
`xi = (nu,K,D)`. Fix the original residual and binder tree. Let
`J(E;xi,h)` name the **already independently interpreted** complete joint
witness relation for the supplied descriptor `E` and independently admitted
challenge `h`. This notation names the corresponding existing `Rel_C` view;
it does not define mixed membership by stipulation.

If a normalized display `n_p^epsilon(w)` is a supplied total definition on
those witnesses, the existing definitional-extension result gives

```text
N(E;xi,h) = { (w, n_p^epsilon(w)) | w in J(E;xi,h) }
forget_new(N(E;xi,h)) = J(E;xi,h).
```

Extension keeps the same old `w`; forgetting keeps every old premise. These
are the two justified directions. The endpoint is equality of the retained
complete relation and its definitional extension, hence equality after the
same final whole-observation projection. It is not an inversion of a handler
image, a total subtraction rule, or a successful concrete Function query.

This law holds for each fixed `xi` satisfying its supplied source/evidence
premises and each `h` in its independently supplied domain. A witness shared
by components is bound once, at its original scope. Rigid binders and witness
strategies retain their original dependencies; no `forall h. exists w` is
turned into `exists w. forall h`. Initial joint component admissibility must
be established separately. An empty observation set, including one caused by
divergence, does not establish annotation acceptance or component consistency.

For covariance, the conditional union-of-occurrences support calculation is
already in compatibility. Canonical flattening does not establish that its
premises hold for the source annotation. The abstract contribution can be a
correlated complete view with multiple family points, and a concrete component
can cover multiple occurrences. Equal support points retain distinct original
terms and incidences.

For contravariance, the existing relational image gives each output an input
and a source-transition witness by its definition. The opposite assertion,
that every represented input yields an output, needs the separate nonempty
concretization and transition/observation totality premises recorded in the
coupled core. Recovering the input from output support has no such proof:
aborting handlers and different continuations can have the same support.
No reverse step follows merely from the displayed row or a handled event.

## Precise unprovided source premise

The next source derivation must provide the following evidence before a
pending comparison is decided:

1. A legal descriptor occurrence at the role-selected typed path, with the
   abstract occurrence denoting its correlated complete view under the same
   `nu`, and the concrete occurrence interpreted at that same port. Existing
   operation declarations instantiate requests after their occurrence is
   supplied; they do not derive this annotation-to-port clause.
2. Its independent admissibility and complete observation clauses, including
   how both components combine and retain original `K,D`, dependent roots,
   continuations and occurrence incidence. The clauses must establish the
   joint relation `J` above rather than reconstruct it from component marginals
   or define it by the pending query's success.
3. For the approved contravariant/deep fragment, source-derived compatibility
   and attachment evidence connecting the designated concrete contribution
   to the actual complete expansion/image. The example `int <: 'a` supplies
   no universal family-argument variance rule. Actual typed `Flow`, receipt,
   `Observe`, visibility and operation compatibility premises must be retained.

The approved [answer d1](../../questions/2026-10-05-function-effect-row-denotation/approved-answer.md)
does not supply these clauses. Its covariant allowance shares `'e` with the
contravariant occurrence; its deep output concerns targeted removal. Full
output absence of a family point additionally requires that no output path
in the complete image exposes that point, including selector/arm requests,
re-emission, latent results and later use. Consuming one attached event is
insufficient. An event's callback eligibility does not require its origin to
equal the row item's origin once the selected typed contract and incidence
hold; origin nevertheless remains in the complete evidence.

There is an important envelope boundary: source-indexed realization §2
excludes offered handler-image nodes. Its linked Pure-value theorem therefore
cannot supply the missing mixed deep-handler membership clause. Extending
that reference envelope would require its own source correspondence proof.
Production realization §7 also permits independently specified conservative
extras; constructor inversion for `P_ref` cannot be imposed on all production
members. This audit establishes neither extension.

All coordinates needed for the conditional construction are existing complete
relation witnesses or views of them. No source fact has been shown
inexpressible by those carriers. Missing derivation is not carrier
insufficiency, and adding a parallel attachment/provenance map would not
discharge the identified premise.
