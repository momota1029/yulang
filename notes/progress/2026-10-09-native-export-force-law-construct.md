## 3. Conditional rule, with a concrete supplier premise

Fix one original immutable assignment, `xi=(nu,K,D)`, scope tree, joint
witness strategy and actual `Act(v,U,r)`. Let rho be the invocation/raw
operation witness; alpha the original designated Force incidence; delta the
independently admitted demand at its live event; and o the Force component
of the actual observation O. The symbols are projections of that original
dependent record, not newly chosen matching endpoints.

The needed supplier premise E_U is an independently justified **raw actual
operation expansion and observation-inversion law**, at U's registered
entry implementation:

```text
ExecuteCallable(U,h) at rho
  has its actual entry prefix
    Receipt_IF(U,h);
    Force(h.wholeCarrier,delta) >>= S_U

where the raw dependent observation/Bind constructors identify the
designated component (alpha,o) of O with that very Force execution,
and S_U retains only the original unfinished rebind/body/return suffix.
```

This is a conditional premise about the actual registered operation, not
an equation imposed on U merely because it realizes a VP/Strict descriptor.
Its full signature must retain the original world action, receipt, carrier
port, Force incidence, raw continuation and proof operands. A bare operation
name, ValueEntry shape, or P-membership proof is insufficient.

Under E_U the following derived local inference is sound:

```text
Act(v,U,r); raw InvObs(U,h,O;rho)
E_U at the SAME registered operation/entry/incidence tuple
O's retained designated component (alpha,delta,o)
H: h.wholeCarrier = t at the ORIGINAL registered carrier field
-----------------------------------------------------------------
raw ExecObs(Force(t,delta),o;rho|alpha)
at the SAME current-event/world/incidence and dependent proof tuple
```

Here `rho|alpha` means the existing suboperation witness supplied by E_U's
dependent inversion. It is not a new invocation witness or a fresh effect
operation instance. At a pure prefix there need be no Request instance q
or raw handle k. Such fields are required only when an actual Request exists.

**Derivation.** Invert the raw observation through its registered receipt
and Bind constructors, using E_U to identify the first executing leg.
Project its original suboperation witness, demand, world and result fields.
Rewrite the actual carrier operand once by H on that whole dependent tuple.
Equality substitution now gives the displayed actual Force(t,delta)
observation. Neither Car elimination nor VP production inversion is used to
infer that execution witness.

The derivation has a finite schema: registered-operation expansion, raw
receipt/Bind inversion and total equality substitution. A particular finite
observation/development requires finitely many such inversions. It does not
claim one finite proof of an infinite execution or a termination theorem.
PE §6.1 could consume this schema only if the genuine independent operation
law and its full scoped constructor are actually available in L. SRC §3.2
permits further independently established constructors **when adopted**;
that permission does not register E_U or its inference.

## 6. Independence, mutations and omitted coverage

This is one documentary construction attempt. It uses no executable oracle,
transition checker, enumeration, seed/range or sampled process. Its shared
assumptions are the selected contextual V/Car/world meanings, native VP/Echo
clauses and raw source-constructor laws in the inspected owner passages.
It treats their explicitly selected scopes as inputs rather than independently
proving those definitions. Production membership remains logically separate
from raw execution provenance.

The following are logical premise mutations, not executed mutations:

- Remove E_U: VP-Force still types an independent T_I record, but the raw
  Force(t,delta) conclusion lacks a producer. C2 cannot be applied to it.
- Replace U with the source id: its source proof supplies entry provenance,
  but the arbitrary same-provider antecedent has been changed unless a
  genuine original anchor/equality proof licenses that substitution.
- Replace full H by equality of displayed endpoints or erased carrier IDs:
  the actual carrier/root/provider and shared evidence substitution fails.
- Replace the retained Force k/current world by a production continuation or
  saved activation: pending development no longer has the original raw
  subexecution; the conclusion's provenance/current authority fails.
- Require Request fields at every Force prefix: ordinary pure divergence and
  zero-step observations are omitted.
- Infer outer result equality for nested future returns: Echo/Fixed's actual
  outer-provider scope has been exceeded.

Unverified scope: uniform E_U and its adopted L constructor; opaque/primitive
operation coverage; complete actual descriptor-role correspondence at those
operations; C1; concrete C2; domain/result-demand maps; exact return/evidence
transport beyond the stated owner actions; arbitrary Valid_V coverage;
foreign kernels, State, broader recursion and production implementation.
There is no impossibility result, minimized counterexample, principality
theorem or gate closure. Another VP-only consistency probe would leave E_U
untouched and is not recommended.
