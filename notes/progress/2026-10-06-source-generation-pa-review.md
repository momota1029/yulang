```text

  -> Generated(c,beta,p_0)
  -> policy(Generated) = FullProtection, AnnotationGrant=None
  -> contribution schema at the complete output p_out(c).

```

Its contribution interpretation observes the complete invocation before
handler selection, including entry, body and designated consumer. It is not
the outward support of the body. It becomes an actual incidence only with the
matching typed realization, receipt and current activity. Source generation
does not manufacture those runtime facts.

The transport normal form is a tagged union of images of generated or inherited
leaves. It composes matching typed correspondences and retains the same ledger,
origins, owner references and dependent incidences. Inherited actual-provider,
actual-result and independently present callee-result packets are not discarded,
even if static beta labels coincide across instances. No `call.effect` image
is projected onto `result.latent.effect`, and a public returned closure view
does not expose its private captured fields.
