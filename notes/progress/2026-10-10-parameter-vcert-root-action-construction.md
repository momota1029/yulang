## 1. Result and exact scope

An explicitly supplied bijection on registered descriptor roots does not by
itself act on `VCert(A,v,p,e)`. The selected native clauses treat those
certificate domains and their independent primitive/descriptor laws as inputs.
The first missing supplier is a typed action at descriptor formation and
value-certificate ownership, before interpreting a moved Identity proof.

There is nevertheless a smaller conditional construction than covariance of
the entire local registry L. Given independently formed target descriptor
domains and scope-respecting inverse maps on their **full evidence objects**,
Identity and Compose transport by their selected equality/composition clauses.
The construction retains both tags, the complete tree, every intermediate
certificate and all sharing. No additional equivariance premise for these two
checking constructors is needed. This conclusion does not construct the
supplied evidence maps.

For a proof containing only Identity and Compose, typing forces every endpoint
to be the same descriptor. Thus a source-admitted `mu_result` in this smallest
fragment requires `J.payload=A`. Moving A coherently necessarily moves that
payload occurrence too. Keeping J's entire actual contract fixed while changing
only the target occurrence cannot preserve this proof's Identity tag. A target
evidence bijection alone cannot repair an unchanged source endpoint.

Hereditary transport is conditional on restriction commutation for the complete
retained record, including separately transported fields and binder domains.
Original event restriction keeps the same root; it does not supply movement
between two registered roots. No arbitrary checking tree, whole gamma/image,
Function-domain action, CI_USE or aggregate IFACE_EQUIV result closes here.

## 3. Hypotheses before a checking proof can move

Fix one actual source checking occurrence and its original dependent telescope
T. Write u for its full nonmoving decorated operands, including `(v,p,e)`,
current-world/consumer data and any independently supplied preceding fields.
Use the abbreviation

```text
E_r(u) = the actual original VCert(r,v,p,e; remaining original operands in u).
```

This is notation for the selected domain, not a newly defined membership
relation. Multiple objects of E_r remain distinct. In particular the evidence
family is not the truth value of value membership.

Let h be the single supplied sort/scope-respecting bijection on the moving
registered root support. It fixes every rigid identity and acts on every
occurrence of a moving root, including checking endpoints and intermediate
incidences. h changes an actual root r to h(r), not a spelling denoting r.
No group action for arbitrary bijections is assumed or proved: this assignment
uses this one h and its inverse.

The minimum independent hypotheses for the local construction are:

1. **Target formation.** Each h(r) is an independently valid descriptor/root
   at the same allowed scope with its actual registered interpretation. All
   changed formation/incidence operands are well typed. If original formation
   witnesses are included in the retained proof package, their full typed
   forward/inverse actions must also be supplied. A name in h's codomain is
   not a formation derivation. Defining the target interpretation to be the
   desired image would assume this hypothesis rather than prove it.
2. **Dependent evidence action.** For every required original prefix of T,

   ```text
   B_r,u : E_r(u) -> E_h(r)(u)
   C_r,u : E_h(r)(u) -> E_r(u)
   C_r,u(B_r,u(theta)) = theta
   B_r,u(C_r,u(theta')) = theta'.
   ```

   These are typed maps of the complete independently interpreted evidence
   objects. They preserve the same raw value/provider/event and every rigid
   reference. They use only permitted preceding dependencies of that field;
   no future observation chooses an earlier proof. If h also changes a
   non-root dependent operand, the displayed target u must instead be its
   independently supplied whole typed image, with its own inverse. That
   larger action is not supplied in this note.
3. **Coherent incidence.** A repeated original root/evidence occurrence uses
   the same h/B map. Shared evidence is mapped once and referenced thereafter.
   The source/target occurrences of an Identity rule both move. Every
   intermediate root of Compose and all its incident operands move together.
   In this minimal scope raw world/event/provider objects and other auxiliary
   fields stay identical, and their typing must permit that identity action.
   A moved root nested in their typing instead requires its corresponding
   independently typed action; it cannot be ignored as auxiliary data.

Hypothesis 2 is substantially stronger than pointwise inclusion, equal printed
types or extensional equivalence of membership predicates. It preserves proof
objects and reflection, not only successful tuples. It cannot be inferred
from the native `Identity(r)` constructor, which only acts within E_r.

## 4. Direct Identity/Compose construction

Consider the exact finite checking-term fragment

```text
d ::= Identity(r) | Compose(d1,d2; retained intermediate record).
```

It contains no registered equation reference, structural inclusion,
conversion, Any rule or other primitive. At the original checking occurrence,
all input/output and intermediate certificates are explicit proof data as
required by native §3. Denote a typed instance by
`Apply_L(d; theta_in; theta_out, auxiliaries)` using that source notation.

Define H on a whole typed proof record, not on the term's tag alone:

```text
Identity(r;theta_in,theta_out) with theta_in=theta_out
  |-> Identity(h(r); B_r(theta_in),B_r(theta_out))
      with B_r(theta_in)=B_r(theta_out)

Compose(d1,d2; r_mid,theta_mid,full retained intermediates)
  |-> Compose(H(d1),H(d2);
              h(r_mid),B_r_mid(theta_mid),mapped full intermediates).
```

The subscript u at each B is its original field telescope, suppressed only
for readability. For rigid auxiliary fields the mapped intermediate is the
same object. No intermediate is projected away or reselected. The displayed
equalities are mathematical side conditions on retained objects, as in the
selected Identity table; they do not erase a separately stored equality-proof
choice. If an authentic instance stores such an additional choice with changed
typed operands, hypothesis 3 requires its typed inverse action too.

**Identity derivation.** The selected rule requires the same decorated tuple
at r and equality of input/output evidence. Applying the same function B_r to
both sides preserves that equality. Hypotheses 1–2 make both mapped objects
genuinely typed at h(r); the selected Identity schema is now applicable there.
For reflection, apply C_r to the mapped equality and use both inverse laws.
The Identity constructor tag remains Identity. No claim equates it with a
Compose tag or an independently chosen canonical term.

**Compose derivation.** Its first subproof ends at the original intermediate
interface and evidence that its second subproof consumes. By induction, H(d1)
ends at h(r_mid) with B_r_mid(theta_mid), and H(d2) consumes that exact same
object at that exact interface. Their original nonexecuting decorated tuple,
world/event evidence and allowed dependencies agree by hypothesis 3. Apply the
selected Compose clause to these two genuine local laws. Retain both child
proofs and every intermediate/auxiliary field. This uses no L entry beyond
the two selected clauses.

Apply the identical construction with C and h inverse for the inverse map K.
Structural induction gives `K(H(record))=record` and `H(K(record'))=record'`
on the entire typed fragment. Identity uses C(B(theta))=theta; Compose uses
the two induction hypotheses and that same equation for every stored
intermediate. No witness is existentially erased, so the inverse recovers the
whole original record rather than just its proposition. A client inspecting
the Identity/Compose tree or comparing shared proof choices sees the
corresponding tree/choices; arbitrary root-sensitive client truth needs its
own law or a fixed rigid root under successor §5.1.

**Conditional conclusion.** Hypotheses 1–3 yield a typed forward/inverse
action on this exact native proof-record fragment. Registry equivariance for
Identity and Compose has been derived from their clauses and these typed
inputs. Equivariance of other L entries has neither been assumed here nor
proved. The formation/domain hypotheses remain external.

## 5. What this actually says about gamma's mu_result

Uniform-inlet §4.2 types mu_result from the actual J payload to the prechosen A
with the same decorated result and every required hereditary restriction.
This is an input-side checking proof, not a body-result safety oracle.

In the two-constructor fragment above, a simple typing induction proves:

```text
Every well-typed d has the same root at every input/output/intermediate
endpoint, and Identity/Compose equality forces every endpoint certificate
to be the same object at the given decorated tuple.
```

For Identity this is its declared signature and equality. For Compose both
children satisfy the induction hypothesis; their shared intermediate equates
the roots and certificates across the children. This is a consequence of
this fragment only; no such statement holds for arbitrary inclusion rules.
Different Identity/Compose **proof terms** at that one certificate still
remain distinct.

Consequently a native gamma with such a mu_result has J.payload=A as actual
typed endpoints. The smallest source-admitted example is one Identity node;
the next is `Compose(Identity(A),Identity(A))` with its retained theta_mid.
H changes these to the same respective trees at h(A), provided the incoming
payload occurrence also becomes h(A) with the same typed B_A action.

The one-node typing obstruction is exact: if J stays literally fixed with
payload A, but the output endpoint changes to distinct h(A), then
`Identity(h(A))` expects an E_h(A) input; gamma still supplies E_A. The original
`Identity(A)` produces E_A, not the required E_h(A). Neither is a typed
tag-preserving mu_result for these endpoints. Inserting B_A as a new registry
checking leaf or composing a new inclusion bridge would change the original
proof program and requires independently selected rules. This observation
is not a counterexample to a coherent whole-root action; it explains why
target-only movement is not that action.

Moving J.payload can affect J's complete contract, formation/provenance,
carrier evidence, future ports and whole observation image. The derivation
here does not transport those gamma fields. If a different mu_result uses an
actual nonidentity inclusion from an unchanged payload P to A, its finite
tree has other genuine L leaves; transporting those leaves requires their
own selected laws and endpoint domains. Compose alone cannot supply them.

## 6. Hereditary boundary and first missing supplier

At an independently compatible event extension chi:e->e', the selected
certificate supplies a restriction

```text
R_r,chi : E_r(v,p,e;...) -> E_r(v,p,e';...).
```

Native §4.2 requires the same actual binding/root/provider and keeps chi's
live authority/scope/lifetime data. This is restriction at r, not a map to
h(r). Even two genuine eventwise bijections B_r,e and B_r,e' do not, from
their typing or inverse equations alone, imply

```text
B_r,e'(R_r,chi(theta)) = R_h(r),chi(B_r,e(theta)).       (N)
```

If the action on chi changes any actual context or domain, that compatible
action must first be independently supplied too; this smallest theorem keeps
chi and the current-event anchors rigid. Equation N preserves the full
restricted evidence object, not just membership of the same raw value.

N covers the VCert field only. It does not cover a separately retained
formation witness, intermediate auxiliary, equality-proof choice or any other
field permitted by §§3–4. An invertible action on such a field can fail to
commute with its restriction even when N holds. The hereditary conclusion
therefore requires the following premise on the **complete retained record**,
including those fields and their original binder domains.

Write P_e for that record at e, H_e for its full action from §4, and R_chi for
its independently supplied whole restriction. Write R_chi^h for restriction
of the target record at the same rigid chi. The required complete-record square
is

```text
forall original admissible e,chi:e->e',P_e.
  H_e'(R_chi(P_e)) = R_chi^h(H_e(P_e)).               (N-record)
```

It is sufficient to supply the following exact field and binder squares,
then derive N-record by dependent record and checking-tree induction. Let f
range over **every retained field occurrence** in its original telescope,
including source/target/intermediate VCerts, formation/incidence witnesses,
world evidence, extra auxiliaries and stored equality-proof choices. At that
position let u be the complete permitted preceding tuple, including any
enclosing original binder assignments. Let F_f,e(u) be the actual field
domain; A_f,e,u its typed inverse root action; rho_f,chi,u its supplied
restriction. Let rho_<f,chi act on u, and H_<f,e be its already constructed
action. Require

```text
forall f, original admissible e,chi:e->e',u.
forall x in F_f,e(u).
  A_f,e',rho_<f,chi(u)(rho_f,chi,u(x))
    = rho^h_f,chi,H_<f,e(u)(A_f,e,u(x)).              (N-field)
```

The two sides have the same dependent target type only after the preceding
tuple square is established. The record induction proves that prefix square
first; it cannot apply A_f at a differently chosen prefix. For a VCert field,
N-field specializes to N with its complete original operands. A rigid field
can use the identity action only when its domain and owner restriction justify
N-field. For a static field whose restrictions are identity, this premise
requires its action to agree at e and e'; independent eventwise bijections
alone do not imply that agreement.

For every original subordinate binder telescope beta, let D_beta,e(u) be its
independently admitted domain, S_beta,chi,u its supplied binder restriction,
and H_beta,e,u its typed inverse root action. These domains/actions must exist;
the square is not permission to define a new domain. Require

```text
forall beta, original admissible e,chi:e->e',u.
forall d in D_beta,e(u).
  H_beta,e',rho_<beta,chi(u)(S_beta,chi,u(d))
    = S^h_beta,chi,H_<beta,e(u)(H_beta,e,u(d)).        (N-binder)
```

Here `<beta` denotes the permitted prefix before that binder. Apply N-binder
in the original binder order, then N-field under the resulting corresponding
u,d assignments. Repeat under every original response/raw-resumption/latent/
future binder in the claimed record. A future-only field is quantified at its
own later occurrence; this does not fabricate it before that binder. No
quantifier is moved outside its original dependency telescope. If the owner
does not supply a particular binder or field restriction, no hereditary
commutation is claimed at that occurrence.

Given N-field and N-binder exhaustively, dependent prefix induction establishes
N-record; Identity/Compose induction keeps the same checking tree while every
retained field agrees by its own square. Inverse commutation follows by applying
the full inverse actions to N-record, using their two inverse equations at
each corresponding prefix. The construction supplies no later authority from
origin. Full mu_result requires these premises on its complete original
hereditary record, not merely on its VCert projection.

The first missing supplier is target descriptor formation plus the full
VCert-domain action in §3, before Identity's target input can be typed. The
complete restriction laws N-record/N-field/N-binder are a later independent
seam. Selected Function membership
provides same-provider realization under independently fixed complete
admission/observation domains. It provides no changed-root bijections of those
domains; other descriptor meanings remain independent parameters. Source
Renaming and matching-operand congruence do not fill either missing supplier.
This is bounded clause analysis, not an exhaustive absence theorem over every
repository file or a finding of two accepted language meanings.

## 7. Evidence, omissions and recommended method

Method: dependent typing and constructor induction. No executable oracle,
search, seed/range or random mutation was used. The derivation shares the
selected Identity/Compose laws and the supplied independent domain maps;
it proves their consequence, not those source laws or the existence of the
maps. A checker encoding B and the complete restriction squares as transitions
would test consistency of
those premises and could not establish them. The target-only endpoint
mutation in §5 is a typing discriminator, not a coherent-action falsification.

Failure conditions: an unformed target root; a noninvertible or merely
propositional evidence map; an untransported dependent/auxiliary field;
independent mapping of shared intermediates; changed provider/event/consumer;
canonicalization of proof tags; a moved root hidden in a supposedly rigid
world or J field; future-dependent selection of an earlier proof; failure of
any retained field or binder square; an unlisted active identity observer.
Any of these invalidates the stated
conditional action.

This attempt reduces a whole-registry premise to the actual typed domain
supplier for two native checking constructors. It does not add another toy
model of the same missing action. Under the proof-economy rule, preserving
scope/typing through an actual compiler fresh-root use is an A/B obligation;
recovering construction incidence after discarding it is D. Retain the owner
formation/incidence package where it is known. Evidence-domain transport
cannot be reconstructed from that package without its genuine local laws.
Arbitrary-root all-model characterization is broader C work; no existing gate
or production dependency is reclassified by this note.

Recommended next action: give the descriptor/VCert owner one authentic
formation case and one actual permitted h; derive or refute B on its full
evidence and hereditary fields there. Identity/Compose syntax needs no third
probe. If the owner only supplies opaque primitive laws, request that exact
