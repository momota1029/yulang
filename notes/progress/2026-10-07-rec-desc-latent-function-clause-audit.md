## The actual latent clause and its binder information

The selected real clause is source-indexed realization §3.2:

> A returned closure or delay retains the source label and roots used to
> justify a later interaction. A certified future use unfolds the corresponding
> constructor.

This is a source-reference clause. The associated §3.3 root rule is exactly

```text
Der_ref(e,h;xi,w) and Obar = Pi_xi(Obs(w))
------------------------------------------------
Obar in P_ref(e,h;xi).
```

Here `e` is the original endpoint occurrence, `h` its challenge, `xi` the
original fiber, and `w` the full finite derivation witness. The accompanying
text excludes additional arbitrary-latent root rules and says every reference
membership has a constructor witness. Thus inversion of **this reference
membership** recovers a finite reference derivation. It recovers neither an
ordinary `DescMem` clause nor a descriptor-failure witness.

The actual binder information must be retained rather than completed by
guessing:

## H1–H5 at the real clause

The H1–H5 route fixes the actual provider, original descriptor, scopes and
old `(xi,w)` before extracting a compatible witness fiber. The following
findings apply to independent `DescMem`, not to a fabricated descriptor.

| Hypothesis | Finding | Precise unsupplied premise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1: exhaustive finitary factorization | **Cannot instantiate; earliest blocker.** The reference clause is available, the independent ordinary inversion is not stated in these windows. | A latent Function descriptor clause with its actual original binder tree, complete obligation domain and all static/latent conditions; then a proof identifying the total extension fiber `E_o` and finite checks `A_p` without omitting a condition or moving a binder. |
| H2: independent coverage | **Only reference coverage is supplied.** Source-indexed §4 and source-contracts §3.3 list future-use/response/resume cases independent of Q. | The actual independent clause's domain must be covered by the approved all-compatible production admission, including relevant licensed extras, at the same provider/port/current world. Finite source-client certificates are not automatically exhaustive for that domain. |
| H3: exact FH local judgment / raw extension | **Uninstantiated.** FH fixes old assignments and compatible event extensions but supplies no concrete full latent clause judgment. | The complete local judgment and its original binders, plus its equivalence to nonemptiness of the actual `A_p`. Any passing partial assignment must extend to `E_o` when that equivalence requires it. A bad chosen extension is insufficient. |
| H4: finite conjunction coverage | **Not established by the four case names.** FH allows sibling joining only under explicit compatibility. | A single independently admitted finite presentation carrying each required finite joint family at the original shared coordinates. The exact family/presentation grammar is missing; mutually exclusive histories cannot simply be concatenated. |
| H5: finite refutability | **No instance yet; no failure proved.** Finite reference derivations are derivations of positive reference membership. | For the independently extracted fixed fiber, prove empty total intersection has an empty finite subfamily, or derive a coherent-witness route that avoids this factorization. No topology, finite witness domain, or allowed compactness principle is supplied for that fiber. |

FH's actual statement is pointwise preservation through every independently
admitted transition, stronger than choosing a fresh old `w` for each longer
history. If the eventual complete local judgment contains existential event
extensions, reflection must negate that entire judgment. The inspected
sources do not instantiate it as exactly `exists e. Check(h,e)`; further
universal binders must also remain in position. No toy refutation of the
weaker `forall h exists e` statement is used here.

The smallest useful next item is **one independent latent Function clause
instance at the original returned `v_g` and its descriptor**, with its local
constructor rule and future-use inversion written at their actual original
binder positions. This item must identify:

1. immediate/root obligations discharged independently at Return or by `S`;
2. the exact independently admitted future-use operands on this retained

   dependencies, without requiring the membership being proved;
3. the whole local satisfaction judgment, including every scoped witness,
   and its link to the independent descriptor clause.

This is a requested specification/proof interface under the already selected
meaning, not a proposed replacement equation for `DescMem`. If that actual
clause quantifies only over finite eliminations, its precise classical
negation and independent admission/readout correspondence may supply the
one-clause reflection theorem directly. If it instead hides a shared witness
across an unbounded family, then derive its coherent extension or H1–H5 using
the actual fiber. The audit cannot choose between these routes before the
clause is available. One-clause success would still not close exhaustive
REC-DESC or simultaneous CompleteMem.

The discriminating falsifier for this audit's localization is a pinned
approved/Authoritative/current reviewed source section that already states
that independent clause, its complete binder tree and corresponding full
local judgment. Returning only the reference constructor, contextual
simulation, hard guard `G`, four port types or a supplied toy transition
system does not falsify this specific finding. Such a section should cause
this audit to be revised and H1–H5 to be analyzed there.

Recommended next action: assign that single latent `v_g` source-clause
instance to the descriptor-clause producer; require its independent inversion
and complete binder/readout data before another finite-fiber probe.
