## Actual source seam and immutable input `b`

The mathematical body is an outer call whose inert whole argument carrier

```text

  original typed RebindResultPath(D_g,r,C1);
```

`D_g` is notation for the existing carrier, not a new source parameter or
descriptor constructor. This is an expansion under the supplied entry
certificate, not a claim that the approved `apply f x` example determines

resolution approved for `apply f x` cannot be generalized into
Value-entry-implies-Pure or used to rewrite actual supplied callable roles.

The required incidence inventory is:

| Original object | Same-witness incidence retained |

| `D_g` and inner return `r` | Whole inner computation, original Force, returned descriptor/provider, current state and typed result rebind into `f`. |

If `a` is Function-valued, `r`'s latent provider contract is retained and
passed to `f` on the original result path. If `d` is Function-valued, `y`'s

reselected from a displayed value/effect shape. In contrast to `higher`,
this source does not invoke `r` as its second callee: the outer receiver is
the original `u_f`. Returning either provider does not execute its latent
body. The same incidence discipline applies to any provider inside a value.

For a request from `Force(D_g)`, the retained observation includes its
operation-instance witness, origin, response port, raw continuation, live

resumed state without replaying receipt. A nonreturning inner computation
requires no invented `r` or executed body witness. Admission includes the
original initial, response, same-handle resumption and returned-provider
future-use clauses at their actual scopes.

All original relations and client constraints are retained as complete
factors, including internal binders and every free operand. No stage marginal
or new projection reconstruction is used. Whole-witness composition applies
before the approved observation projection; no projection/bind congruence is
assumed. A body-local rigid identity cannot be hoisted into the public `c`
