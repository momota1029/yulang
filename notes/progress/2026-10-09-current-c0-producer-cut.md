its full local relation, the original output-dependent hard conjuncts at the

including pending/raw-resumed branches with the original unfinished suffix.
That mathematical receiver theorem is not an implementation correspondence
certificate for the current compiler.

## Pending branches and the receiver distinction

The selected complete operation's exact requirements are in
`notes/theory/2026-10-07-owned-call-contribution-construction.md:128,182,253,272,344,351`.
Callee-pending membership retains the raw callee continuation followed by the
unreached receiver suffix. Receiver-argument pending membership retains the
already entered receiver/receipt and its remaining rebind/body/consumer/return
suffix. Resumption uses the current world and never repeats receipt. At
return, future evidence is indexed by the actual output provider; opaque
whole-Call alternatives retain their own original witnesses. C0 supplies
descriptor/world/guarantee/authority typing on that same whole tuple.

None of the inspected Apply/recipe/pending-row outputs carries these fields.
The candidate's explicit unresolved list at
`crates/yu-solver/src/shadow_apply.rs:8,29` includes application typing, complete
invocation image, whole-argument/provider compatibility, source admission,
role/entry/protection, whole-tuple scope transport, complete Function
interpretation and event-output correspondence. Those duties remain after
successful solving. Receiver VP membership would still be a separate,
smaller operation than full Call; the currently emitted scalar Function is
also not a producer of that complete receiver theorem.
