# Operation supplier boundary and snapshot validator readiness

This sprint tested whether the exact adopted Operation/Call clauses supplied
the same-w incidence `eta_op` and native correspondence `realization_op`. They
did not. The adopted Call and IF clauses require a genuine, independently
typed original primitive, declaration insertion/reference and complete
independent interface premises; they do not construct the Operation
contribution at the native signature seed. The scoped Operation instance
package retains dependent coordinates but is not that missing constructor.

The historical reviewed Call construction selected by the authority document
was not available as exact bytes in the accessible history. The current later
construction and the unadopted primitive-image candidate were excluded. The
proof result is therefore an evidence boundary, not a theorem, impossibility
claim or source-qualified counterexample. `CHOOSE-SOURCE-2 / RECEIVE-NEGATIVE-PAIR`
remains OPEN; `At(D,w,delta)`, source admission, transport, receive consumption,
subtraction and production remain open.

The readiness lane froze a one-shot read-only structural-validator Draft over
one fixed successful live snapshot. Its temporary certificate is tied to the
borrow and expires with it; later lowers require a separate live contract. The
validator would check existing evidence, preserve alternatives and both
ordered Replay premises, and report structural incidence only. It has no code,
source-admission, `At`, subtraction or production authority.

Fresh architecture and specification reviews found no blocking or major
conformance defect in retaining the Draft. Both identified a major
implementation prerequisite: no owner-local interface has yet been shown to
provide stable whole-state borrowing and complete enumeration of the relevant
evidence. They also require a later reviewed contract to define qualifying
evidence, shared witness traversal and deterministic repeated-scan/work/storage
limits. These are gates before any compiler lease, not defects that authorize
code under the current Draft.
