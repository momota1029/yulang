## 3. What determines Delta_a

Source construction §3.3 defines `Delta_a` by the actual Parameter
introduction: it is that ordinary declaration's original free dependency
telescope at `sigma_a`. Uniform §4.1 separately defines complete `Delta` as
the finite tuple of already introduced operands incident to the entry schema.
It enumerates operand categories and inlet fields, rather than equating the
two telescopes.

The derivable scope facts are:

1. The Parameter declaration is introduced before challenge `h`.
2. Its dependency telescope therefore cannot acquire the later challenge's
   argument contract, actual receipt, carrier observation, returned value,
   response/raw handle or future-event operands.
3. Complete inlet evidence may depend on such later operands at their own
   checking/event scopes. Those dependencies stay in that evidence's
   telescope; they do not move into `Delta_a`.
4. Static/initial `Delta` predicates remain in `delta_static`; predicates
   with observation/response/resumption/future/current-event operands are
   discharged in `delta_check` under their original binders (Uniform §4.3).
   This partitions evidence for the full entry constraints. It does not
   compute the declaration's free dependency telescope.
5. The raw generic inlet's bound presentation has no free `a`. This explains
   why fixing `U_g` need not fix `d_a`; it does not imply that `Delta_a` or
   complete `Delta` is empty.

Thus none of the following is justified: `Delta_a=[]` from import-free input;
`Delta_a=Delta`; `Delta_a=Intrinsic`; or `Delta_a=all in-scope variables`.
The first discards possible genuine dependencies, the next two confuse distinct
record roles, and the last fabricates incidences without an owning rule.
The selected texts supply no operation that chooses one of these assignments.

**Minimum missing premise:** an authentic Parameter formation instance for
this source, exposing `(sigma_a, ordered Delta_a, typed dependency targets,
original parent scope)` and the local clauses using those dependencies. Its
certificate must explain which already introduced operands are actual free
dependencies of the declaration and which merely occur in the complete inlet
schema or its later checking evidence. This is a supplier of the selected
meaning, not permission to introduce a new field or select a representation.

The first complete-owner enumeration stops here. A later full invocation table
cannot repair a missing Parameter telescope. In particular, repeating the
retain/fix proof or executing a checker with a guessed telescope leaves this
same supplier premise untouched.

## 5. Conditional Generalize conclusion and smallest rejection witness

Under H-source, retain the entire `N_id=Build(S_id,T_id)`, `R_id`, component
and original records. Fix raw `U_g`, Intrinsic/IF0 and authentic fixed base
fields. Generic formation binds its presentation internally, captures are
empty and H-source excludes another fixed free dependency on `a`. Follow
`Description(C_id,q)` and `Internal(C_id,r)` through the complete component
in retain mode. The only ordinary `d_a` is reachable and not fixed, so the
selected walk yields `{d_a at sigma_a}`. Body/outward references do not add
ordinary declarations. This conclusion is conditional even though exact
`Delta_a` operands have not been materialized: it requires their authentic
retention and H-source, and does not authorize deleting them.

No false semantic claim in the frozen draft is demonstrated. Its §§2, 3.1
and 4.1 expressly require authentic suppliers and acknowledge schema
incompleteness. The claim that cannot presently be derived is **a complete
owner-produced SourceBuild package for this HIR input**.

The smallest rejection witness is the single authentic Parameter record
obligation `Desc(C_id,q,ValueEndpoint,sigma_a,Delta_a)` with no supplied
`Delta_a` operand manifest. This one record prevents verification of complete
dependencies and therefore prevents freeze under the draft's own eligibility
contract. It is a minimized missing-premise witness, not a running source
counterexample or a proof that no supplier can exist. Returning one opaque
`ParameterValid`/`H-source` flag does not supply the telescope. A complete
authentic Parameter schema is a direct falsifier of this *current extraction
blocker*; the remaining Lambda/invocation supplier obligations would then be
the next distinct work.
