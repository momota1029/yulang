rule, exact port/guard catalogue and telescope arities, complete IF0, Parameter
image, anchor selection, future/event actions, actual invocation, source
publication, foreign/State/recursive cases, implementation/resource/failure

port/scope introduction at an authentic caller insertion site**, with its
