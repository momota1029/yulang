## Native replay lemma, with explicit hypotheses

For state `s`, let `c_s` be current representative canonicalization. Write
`Q_s(o,p) = D_s(o,p) ++ E_s(o,p)` for the direct-row and exact-nonvariable
opposite vectors of canonical owner `o`, retaining physical duplicates.
Let `F_s(K)` be the actual newest-first linked RelationId list at literal
BoundKey `K`. This notation describes existing storage and adds no carrier or
compiler relation.

Consider a valid successful native incoming-use restoration. It inserts one
accepted bound, stores `N = |Q_s(c_s(o),p)|`, and retains the restoration
operation. At index `n < N`, the current code re-canonicalizes **both** saved
owner and saved bound, and reads the current opposite index:

```text
o_n = c_sn(o)
b_n = c_sn(b)
a_n = c_sn(Q_sn(o_n,p)[n])
I_n = BoundKey(o_n,p,b_n)
J_n = BoundKey(o_n,opposite(p),a_n)
(L_n,U_n) = (I_n,J_n) for positive p; (J_n,I_n) for negative p
```

`candidate_context_begin_replay(..., incoming_use=true)` takes the actual
heads and sets previous heads to `(None,None)`. Its first rectangle enumerates
exactly `F_sn(L_n) × F_sn(U_n)`; the second is empty. Each ordered pair retains
the directed Replay context and both parent identities even if several pairs
intern the same child RelationId. Incoming mode bypasses existing-dependency
suppression. The child batch is owned before any child drain begins.

**Bounded established operational fact, pending independent review:** on valid
successful calls, each pair in those literal-head snapshots is generated and
passed to the native typed drain with the incoming occurrence/cause. A child
is marked consumed only after that drain and its diagnostic completion
succeed. If one restore's first `N` positions, canonical keys and applicable
ordered fibers remain unchanged, every initial product is consumed by that
restore. Proof is integer induction on `n`, then on the owned child's
`consumed` cursor. The loop advances only after driving its active replay;
successful drains acknowledge the exact queued child index.

This is a local conditional source-to-execution bridge. It does not prove
that memo/self omission is semantically justified, that all diagnostic
ancestry is complete, or that an arbitrary required omega lies in those
snapshot heads. Stored recipes alone prove neither activation nor consumption.

Two older obstructions must not be carried forward unchanged. The historical
restore-product note's saved-row-bound `b0` case is superseded for this code:
`candidate_drive_bound_restoration` explicitly re-canonicalizes `bound`
before constructing its literal key. Also, generated replay progress is now
distinct from consumed progress. Neither repair proves future fiber coverage,
incoming diagnostic accessibility, lifecycle invalidation or source closure.

## Conditional trace theorem and exact failed induction step

Fix any authentic execution in the original universal domain and any genuine
required incoming-use omega. The witness consists of its original ordered
lower/upper relations, literal bound keys, directed context/shared inputs,
attachment/provider lineage and incoming occurrence/cause. Transport changes
the witness only along that execution's actual evidence transforms.

The following are **unproved source/consumer hypotheses**, not adopted
invariants:

1. **Authentic requirement coverage.** Every required omega is represented at
   its actual generation/activation point by the correct source-owned bound
   inputs or typed relation; capture/freshening does not silently omit it. At
   that point, ownership is explicitly partitioned: omega is selected by a
   guaranteed future restore, retained as a pending replay/typed consumer, or
   already completed with valid dependencies and the incoming occurrence/cause.
   Mere endpoint or relation representation does not seed this partition.
2. **Mutation preservation.** At every actual mutation before consumption,
   each required omega either keeps a guaranteed future restore selection
   with the correct current keys and fibers, or an actual replay/typed child
   owns its transformed ordered obligation. An already completed observation
   counts only while its dependencies remain valid. Dirty completion is
   withdrawn and its exact recomputation precedes the saved continuation.
3. **Consumer closure.** Successful typed completion covers the required
   semantic child obligations and makes every applicable checking/reporting
   result reachable from this incoming occurrence/cause. Reusing an ordinary
   old dependency requires this same-use diagnostic bridge; dependency
   existence or a reverse edge alone is insufficient.
4. **Terminal and transaction closure.** A terminal completed source/use
   result has no unconsumed required omega, including one that was represented
   but never selected or scheduled, and no pending required selection, replay
   child, recomputation or diagnostic. Publication observes that condition.
   Failure restores the same requirement/selection/completion ownership;
   successful retry proves its own coverage from the restored generation.

**Conditional theorem:** under 1–4, every required omega in every *completed*
authentic source/use execution is checked/reported before affected publication.
Consequently no completed published execution witnesses the full A event.
If, additionally, the original target's remaining retained/suspended schedules
are shown either unable to construct the full event or to reach the required
actual consumers, this establishes original Alternative B. That additional
source premise is not established here. Suspension and an unchanged wait set
provide preservation, not eventual rescue or all-source impossibility.

Derivation: induct on the length of the actual retained transition trace,
not on SCC size, context count or failed-search count. At activation, hypothesis
1 supplies the witness. At a restore read, the native replay lemma transfers
the selected snapshot product to its owned batch. A mutation uses hypothesis
2 to preserve pending ownership or invalidate/recompute consumed ownership.
A successful drain uses hypothesis 3. Rollback and retry use hypothesis 4.
At completed publication hypothesis 4 excludes unfinished ownership, leaving
valid consumed checking/reporting. No termination theorem for arbitrary
mixed contexts follows from this finite-trace induction.

The attempt stops at hypothesis 2 on the **unchanged third-owner** case:

```text
An authentic callback changes a literal bound key/fiber on restoring owner S
through a qualified parent/copy merge elsewhere, after the relevant incoming
product heads were frozen. For every original required omega affected by that
change, either a remaining actual S restore reads its exact transformed pair,
or an actual consumer completes that pair with the incoming use's cause.
```

`candidate_context_canonicalize_bounds` scans and transports those third-owner
fibers. `candidate_context_transport_witness_with_evidence` attaches a child
and records its transform but does not start replay. The merge replays its
own parent. Physical slots may survive and later repeated S slots may rescue
the pair; transport alone proves neither loss nor that rescue. This is the
precise missing source/operational bridge, conditional on an authentic
required pair and mutation. For B, either every such source event has the
stated actual rescue, or the full unrescued event is source-impossible. A
failed original-Y/tail construction proves neither disjunct.

There are also unclosed preservation cases for direct-row append shifting an
old exact suffix, fibers added after heads freeze, and diagnostics of suppressed
ordinary replay. Bounds append within lanes; concatenated direct/exact order
need not append. Existing new-bound replay and full merged-owner replay do
not entail same-use coverage of each displaced old pair. Furthermore,
`candidate_transfer_bound_origins` explicitly records
`CoverageGap::MissingOriginAssociation` (`candidate_effect.rs:479–497`);
the origin table cannot choose an exact marginal fiber from endpoints.
No independent witness selection is permitted to fill that gap.
