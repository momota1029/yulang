## 1. Result and predecessor delta

A direct simultaneous certificate can bypass **finite-failure reflection as
a proof method**, provided the independently interpreted ordinary descriptor
has a proved **certificate absorption law** for this exact knot. The actual
two providers and their pending invocation suffixes supply a concrete
candidate relation; target member validity is not its input. Every external
carrier/world/admission and immediate descriptor condition remains a separate
premise. Neither the concrete relation nor even a complete monotone clause
equation proves absorption.

The new exact obstruction is algebraic. On the two latent member obligations,
after every independent local guard has been supplied, the knot contributes
the positive dependency operator

```text
F(Z)_f = Z_g
F(Z)_g = Z_f.
```

Both the empty and full relations satisfy its unfolding equation. The full
actual-knot certificate is postfixed, but cannot establish membership in an
independently interpreted fixed point solely by unfolding that fixed point.
A general absorption law for an exhaustive positive operator is equivalent
to identifying its greatest fixed point. It is an independent semantic
theorem to derive, not something licensed by graph guardedness, operational
Return, or the current positive finite source grammar. Restricting absorption
to this particular certificate narrows that requirement, but does not prove it.

This is **not a Yulang countermodel**. The two-element operator is a projection
of the proposed proof method's recursive dependencies, with independent
guards explicitly suppressed for the algebraic test. No interpretation
satisfying every actual DESC_CLAUSES / ADMISSION_CLAUSES / SEM_JOINT clause
and refuting membership has been constructed. The rigorous result is that
unfolding plus a postfixed certificate does not validate the proposed
inference, even for the exact positive cycle.

Compared with FH and the 2026-10-08 coherent-path attempt, this method does
not negate descriptor failure, require finite refutability/compactness,
construct a countable union of event assignments, or exchange path witnesses.
It exposes a different proof cut: independently derive a knot-restricted
coinduction rule for the actual ordinary interpretation. That rule could make
finite-failure reflection unnecessary for the selected component. Current
clauses do not instantiate it. REC_DESC, REC_LOCAL, INIT_VALID and
MEMBER_DISCHARGE remain open; their current DAG dependencies are not edited.

## 3. The actual local relation to which a coinduction rule would apply

The candidate is a relation on **whole obligation records**, before any
projection. A record retains its descriptor occurrence, actual provider,
source path, world/configuration, original witness and any authorized
event-local extension. A carrier record additionally retains the received
whole carrier and its designated one-layer Force; a pending record retains
the exact argument continuation and invocation suffix. A nominal member name
alone is not the relation's carrier.

Let `j(f)=g` and `j(g)=f`. For a call of member `i` with carrier `t`, write
its actual saved suffix as

```text
S_i(a,C) = Rebind(formal_i,a,C);
           Return(v_j(i),C_body);
           original ReturnFromInvocation.
```

`C_body` is the actual configuration obtained from receipt/entry/rebind;
its store and inherited active context are threaded, not replaced by an
initial snapshot. The original body is `result(name j(i))`. Name lookup
returns the captured `v_j(i)` inertly. `S_i` contains no latent force or new
call of that returned handle.

For every supplied independently valid current-world tuple and authorized
compatible extension `e`, form the following **joint certificate scheme I**:

1. root obligations `d_f(C,e)` and `d_g(C,e)` on both original member
   descriptors and actual providers, with their simultaneous captured edges;
2. an invocation obligation `p_i(t,C,e)` for each independently admitted
   whole carrier/context at the actual typed port;
3. a pending obligation `p_i(k >>= S_i,C',e')` at every independently admitted
   request suspension/response/raw resumption, retaining that original `k`
   and suffix;
4. the returned obligation `d_j(i)(C_ret,e')` on the **same** actual returned
   provider, at its original latent port and original scoped tuple;
5. every required jointly scoped world/environment/local check attached to
   these records. If a check binds one shared witness around both members or
   multiple uses, it is one joint record, not two projected records.

This scheme is explicit even when its semantic premises have no inhabitants:
it denotes the set of records satisfying the supplied independent operands.
It does not assert existence of an initial world, prove any unlisted local
guard, or assume `DescMem(R_i,v_i)` to include a member record. Candidate
membership means “listed in this proof relation”; it is not ordinary descriptor
membership.

The actual invocation equations furnish its constructor cases:

```text
Invoke_i(t,C) = Receipt_i(t,C);
                Force(t) >>= S_i

Return(a,C') >>= S_i = S_i(a,C')
Request(q,C',k) >>= S_i
  = Request(q,C',lambda(r,C''). k(r,C'') >>= S_i).
```

For the Return case, the independent entry/rebind checks must validate `a`
at the original formal port. The body selects exactly `v_j(i)`; its recursive
latent obligation is looked up in I, together with **all immediate nonrecursive
return guards**. For Request, independent packet/response/carrier checks
validate the current transition; the residual record stores the same suffix.
For raw resumption, use the same raw handle, current resumed state and retained
operation witness. For future use, an independently admitted invocation
starts at the previously returned `v_j(i)` and is another I case. A diverging
carrier supplies pending/progress cases only; no completed body Return is
invented.

Thus actual control closure of this scheme follows from K/KP and the displayed
ordinary equations under the already retained external argument/context
correspondence. Its **typed** postfixed closure is conditional on every local
guard in §4. This explicitly distinguishes the actual suffix construction
from an unproved typing rule that would move latent descriptor checks into I.

## 5. A conditional direct theorem, without finite-failure reflection

Suppose the actual independent package supplies a set `J` of whole obligation
records and an exhaustive local operator `F_A` on subsets of J. `A` here is
the full independently interpreted admission/domain family, held fixed as
an operand. Every recursive occurrence in the operator is positive; all
independent local guards and original quantifiers remain in the operator.
It is not a source-image definition. Let `D` be the restriction of the
already independently justified ordinary judgment to J.

Required premises of the direct theorem are:

- **C1, exact factorization:** the displayed operator represents every actual
  relevant ordinary clause at these incidences, with immediate and latent
  conditions distinguished and no binder movement or lost constraint;
- **C2, monotonicity:** `Z <= Z'` implies `F_A(Z) <= F_A(Z')`, with A,
  original scopes, external primitive interpretations and all tuple operands
  fixed. This must follow from the real clauses, not from Function syntax;
- **C3, actual local certificate:** §3's joint I satisfies `I <= F_A(I)`.
  The recursive I references are allowed only at the clause positions certified
  by C1. Every independent guard in §4 is independently discharged;
- **C4, restricted absorption:** for this admissible, jointly scoped certificate
  class, `Z <= F_A(Z)` implies `Z <= D`, proved from the independently
  justified ordinary meaning;
- **C5, realization and completion:** actual initial source tuples are included
  by independent INIT_VALID certificates, and all non-DescMem CompleteMem/KV
  conjuncts hold jointly at those same tuples.

**Conditional direct-discharge theorem.** C1–C5 establish both original
member judgments and every retained complete validator conjunct on the
actual K graph, using one original `xi,w` and compatible authorized extensions.
No finite-failure reflection premise is needed.

**Proof.** C3 makes I an admissible postfixed certificate. C4 gives `I <= D`
directly, so every root and returned-handle record in I has ordinary descriptor
membership. C5 places both actual roots in the joint I realization and supplies
the independent non-descriptor conjuncts at that same tuple. Conjoin these
original predicates to obtain KV satisfaction. I has not projected witnesses
or reselected a provider, so the result uses the original joint assignment.
No contradiction, descriptor-failure inversion, compactness, or countable
witness union occurs. QED, conditional on the explicit premises.

C4 is a **semantic theorem requirement**, not adopted authority. In particular,
this conditional proof does not implement or silently choose greatest-fixed-
point membership. C1–C3 are not currently instantiated by the missing ordinary
latent constructor clauses, and C4 is not derived by the sources inspected.
C5 remains open even if C1–C4 were supplied.

## 6. Construct the certificate interpretation, and expose what it cannot prove

For any supplied monotone `F_A`, its mathematical certificate relation can be
constructed explicitly:

```text
U_A = union { Z subset J | Z subset F_A(Z) }.
```

This is a candidate **certificate interpretation** on the declared J; it is
not named `DescMem`, `M_E`, `WorldMem` or `CompleteMem`. It checks precisely
the chosen operator's clauses, not unknown ordinary clauses.

**Certificate saturation lemma.** `U_A = F_A(U_A)`.

For each postfixed Z, monotonicity gives
`Z <= F_A(Z) <= F_A(U_A)`. Taking their union gives
`U_A <= F_A(U_A)`. Applying monotonicity again gives
`F_A(U_A) <= F_A(F_A(U_A))`, so `F_A(U_A)` is itself postfixed and is
one of the union's contributors. Hence `F_A(U_A) <= U_A`. QED.

Every certificate I from §3 satisfying C3 is included in this explicit U_A.
If the actual independently interpreted D satisfies the same unfolding
equation, that still proves only `D <= U_A`, since D is a postfixed contributor.
The direction needed for validation is `I <= D`, or sufficiently
`U_A <= D`. The latter holds exactly when every postfixed certificate is
absorbed; with `D = F_A(D)`, it identifies D with this greatest fixed point.

The exact two-handle projection makes the missing direction concrete. For
`F(Z)=swap(Z)` on `{d_f,d_g}`, both `D=empty` and `D={d_f,d_g}` satisfy
unfolding, and `I={d_f,d_g} <= F(I)`. Choosing the former algebraic fixed point
does not contradict unfolding but defeats `I <= D`. This proves **failure of
the proposed inference rule from its algebraic premises**, not an alternative
complete Yulang meaning. A returned handle being inert guards execution;
it does not make a static recursive predicate obligation strictly decrease.

Moreover, an independent immediate local guard cannot be eliminated by
coinduction. With guard bits `s_f,s_g`, the projected operator is

```text
F_s(Z)_f = s_f and Z_g
F_s(Z)_g = s_g and Z_f.
```

Its maximal certificate is full only if both guards hold; otherwise it is
empty. The bounded checker tests all four guard masks. This attacks the
shortcut that constructs the reference cycle and treats its independent
Return/root constraints as automatically satisfied.

The source-contract positive recursion clause concerns a different relation:
`M_E` and the source/reference emission grammar have least, finite-derivation
interpretations. On the projected pure cycle the least relation is empty,
whereas U_A is full. Replacing that finite grammar by certificate coinduction
would change its meaning. Thus the candidate must retain ordinary descriptor
typing as separate from positive source generation and Option 2 abstraction;
there is no simultaneous replacement of all predicates by U_A.

## 7. Adversarial variance, projection, quantifiers and latent handles

### 7.1 Freezing admission is a substantive premise

The usual Function obligation has the form

```text
forall h. A(h) implies Check(Z,h).
```

It is positive in Z when every recursive Check occurrence is positive and A
is fixed. It is negative in A under ordinary subset order: enlarging the
domain can invalidate an obligation. The finite witness has an empty domain
passing at `Z=empty`, and one admitted challenge requiring `d_f` failing there.
This is a check of variance, not an admitted source counterexample.

Consequently comparison independence of admission does not establish
monotonicity of a product operator varying descriptor, carrier, world and
admission predicates simultaneously. Reversing the domain order would require
checking every other clause under that mixed order; independently generated
admission may have its own positive descriptor/world dependencies. No such
signed operator, order or exhaustive clause proof is supplied by the current
package. The direct theorem freezes admission as an already independently
interpreted full domain and exposes that factorization as C1–C2. It cannot
solve SEM_JOINT by presuming the same domain has already been soundly separated.

The exact `x,y` arguments are unused only by the bodies. Both actual entries
force their carriers. Whole-carrier inlet, request and latent returned-value
typing therefore remain active. Pure introduction or an empty body effect
does not make the Function obligation positive in a changing inlet domain.

### 7.2 Existential projection does not certify a shared witness

Existential relational image is monotone, but it need not preserve the joint
certificate inference. If the full f certificate requires old `w=0` and the
full g certificate requires old `w=1`, both separate projections are inhabited
while their shared-witness conjunction is empty. This smallest two-value
pattern is tested. It does not supply actual Yulang constraints; it falsifies
the algebraic shortcut

```text
(exists w. I_f(w)) and (exists w. I_g(w))
  => exists w. I_f(w) and I_g(w).
```

The construction therefore fixes w first and conjoins both members before
any allowed hiding. A postfixed projected provider relation can lose the
incidence needed for C1. Its saturation theorem alone does not repair that
loss or prove scope-preserving lifting back to whole records.

### 7.3 No uniform witness is selected by pointwise coinduction

The diagonal two-history check matrix
`((true,false),(false,true))` satisfies `forall h exists e. Check(h,e)` but
fails `exists e forall h. Check(h,e)`. All sixteen Boolean matrices are checked,
including the exact reflected negation of the pointwise existential.
With an old coordinate already chosen, this diagonal fails FH's pointwise
preservation; with genuinely fresh branch-local coordinates, a single global
e is the wrong binder. Neither reading licenses moving the actual descriptor
existential. C1 must retain the original binder tree, and C3 must supply a
joint compatible extension wherever that tree demands one. Merely taking a
union of certificate states does not assemble a globally compatible strategy.

### 7.4 Returned handles are original operands

`f` returns `v_g`, and `g` returns `v_f`, at every body completion. The future
obligation is at that actual provider and latent typed port under the caller's
then-current configuration. Candidate records cannot substitute a fresh copy,
change the descriptor annotation, hide a provider separately per history,
or certify the callee itself in place of the returned provider. The checker
rejects these identity mutations as operand substitutions. This check shares
K's supplied return mapping; it is not independent runtime validation.

At inert Return a candidate reference addresses the *latent recursive*
obligation only if the ordinary constructor rule admits that coinductive
certificate position. Immediate conditions stay independent. If the rule
requires full existing ordinary DescMem at Return, §3's cycle cannot be
postfixed without assuming the result. If it accepts a certified recursive
reference, C4 must prove soundness of that acceptance. This is the precise
