## 1. Requested result and actual result

The user requested complete proofs against the actual bottleneck, followed by
reviewed GitHub publication, rather than another localization of missing
premises. The [construction artifact](../theory/2026-10-07-call-occurrence-construction-proof.md)
therefore specifies the proposed position and occurrence judgments completely

properties. It includes a semantic interpretation of the exact root, using

The historical original-sort bottleneck is **not discharged**. The new
dependent families do not become the preexisting original judgments by
renaming, and the proof supplies no embedding into an independently fixed
original interpretation. This limitation is part of the result, not a hidden
premise of a claimed O0 closure. The user's requested destruction of that
historical bottleneck has not been achieved by this packet.

The complete theorem is instead: for every actual emitted Gen-Call-0 record
in the frozen well-scoped input graph, the explicitly proposed `T_call`
formation cases construct its typed immediate-invocation/source incidence
without an H_eff or source-map premise. This includes the approved nested
singleton. The result is total even when the old generated constraints have
no satisfying assignment. Calls without such a record retain their old node
and child evidence; generic record generation remains an independent target.
