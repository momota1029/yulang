## Exact premise and method

Write `B(o,s,i)` for an exact stored `BoundKey`, `C(B)` for endpoint canonicalization and `F(B)` for the linked list of attached RelationIds. A physical row slot stores an item independently of that list. The attacked shortcut is `C(B)=C(B')` implying `F(B)=F(B')`, or implying equal direct admission/emission association. The source does not implement either implication.

The actual local copy rule is narrower: on a successful `Work::Bound`, enumerate **every member of `F(Braw)`**, transport it to the copied bound with an explicit witness, then transfer diagnostic origins separately. The remaining coverage question is whether those exact raw members supply every context required by that physical copying occurrence. An arbitrary member of `F(C(Braw))` is not automatically an additional required occurrence at this slot.

## Evidence association is a separate seam

The canonical transport creates/attaches `r'` and records a FiberTransform, but does not call `evidence_admit` or emit a new third-owner physical slot. Assuming no earlier direct admission at `(B(z,-,p),r')`, `evidence.snapshot(B(z,-,p),Some(r'))` has no direct group/admission/emission (`candidate_context.rs:688–706`). Transporting that canonical relation onward can therefore retain `MissingOriginAssociation` (`transform`, `791–806`). This is a conditional direct-association gap, not absence of the executable attached relation. The older raw admission remains available at its own exact key. Transform provenance must not be silently identified with a new direct physical admission.

Likewise, `origins[B]` stores diagnostic pair keys without an origin-to-fiber association. Scoped copied-bound initialization imports a snapshot with `MissingOriginAssociation` and admits metadata with `relation=None` (`candidate_extrusion.rs:416–430`; `candidate_effect.rs:537–545`). The subsequent first transport loop owns executable fibers. The origin loop explicitly retains that gap; no identity alternative may be manufactured to fill it.

## Other candidate attacks and bounded exclusion

- **Later canonical admission:** after the merge, a new ordinary `z <: p` admission can enlarge `F(B(z,-,p))` while the old raw list stays unchanged. It also appends a physical `p` slot at `z`. A later extrusion processes both physical slots. Extra canonical members alone do not show a missing required context at the earlier `c` slot; the proposed counterexample has no such missing member witness.
- **Merged owner:** merge copies each physical selected item from the representative copy row before forest mutation; insertion canonicalizes the destination and then transfer uses the source's exact raw item. Global canonicalization follows. No raw-key erasure was found in that route. This bounded reading does not prove induction over every mutation owner.
- **Fresh-use restoration:** `begin_candidate_fresh_bound` (`candidate_scheme.rs:1858–1906`) reconstructs a captured relation and `candidate_context_fresh_transport_scoped` attaches at canonical destination coordinates in scoped mode. `candidate_begin_bound_restoration` immediately admits/emits through the same physical constructor. Scoped FreshUse without a mapped relation returns `ReceiptMismatch`; it does not silently accept a required absent relation. Unscoped admission instead constructs its ordinary relation; no equality of its provenance with scoped reconstruction is claimed.
- **Empty lists:** `bound_cursor=None` is legal evidence absence, not automatically a defect. Scheme capture explicitly records one `relation=None` bound when no fiber is attached (`candidate_scheme.rs:1017–1049`). Scoped copy bootstrap may preserve an empty executable list and association gaps. A falsifier must identify the independently required fiber and its owning constructor; mere emptiness is insufficient.
- **Legacy append bypass:** ordinary value/effect direct vector append sites in `lib.rs` are behind candidate early returns in `apply_value_task`/`apply_effect_task` (`13933ff`, `11825ff`). Those sites do not by themselves provide a candidate-mode uninstrumented-slot witness. This is not a complete inventory of all mutations.
