## 4. Derivation and exact closure boundary

Hypotheses H1–H4 are selected-source premises, not results of this audit:

1. H1: authentic native id formation and its actual source Generalize output,
   original fixed fields, scope map and allowed A_i assignment.
2. H2: the selected decoder's active ordinary equations at u_i and complete
   membership interpretation, with independent source/phase/guard laws intact.
3. H3: the genuine hereditary Top law at the actual decorated tuple,
   original guards and full evidence telescope of `(u_i,v_Any)`.
4. H4: the finite submitted Top term uses those exact operands/scopes; no
   additional W, primitive, conversion, Identity/Compose wrapper or invocation.

Under H1–H4, PE §6.1's Value case validates the finite Top term and emits
`Direct(u_i,v_Any;Value(Top))`. Top derives the same-value membership
implication; the native Value consumer checks that term against actual
readouts. This is a conditional derivation from genuine selected local laws.
It does not establish H1–H3 from typing, nor prove source rules independently.

Positive established source facts: bare Delta has the finite projection/
equality/conjunction/original-binder derivation schema described above; the
four elementary CE laws have selected immutable record meanings; hereditary
Top has its selected independent same-tuple meaning; exact Value-certificate
recognition is defined. Their finite syntactic **support** suffices for this
one fixed Top query, relative to the original supplied operand interpretations.

The full finite local-law catalogue is **not closed** by these sources.
Certificate §3 explicitly says an open authentic local catalogue remains an
independently typed catalogue parameter. Section 4.5's exhaustive *newly
defined native grammar* still has Local/Ref and Check's actual L law. PE §6.1
requires the full genuine law of every used primitive and says a bare name
does not supply it. Finite slot count, finite tagged grammar and a fixed Top
term do not enumerate all imported evidence fields, J alternatives, equation
laws, or well-scoped W observations. SD §6.3 excludes active proof-tag W from
its decision theorem; its structural completeness argument cannot fill this
boundary. No stronger all-Delta or full PE-ID local-law closure is claimed.

The precise missing supplier, if a downstream gate requires a closed
operand-indexed catalogue or renaming law, is the owner of each *actually
changed/observed* independent record field or primitive call: its full operand
and evidence telescope, semantic identity observers, and preservation/
reflection law for the proposed action. For this fixed query no such changing
action is performed. Source formation/registry ownership, not another model
of Top, must supply that stronger fact.
