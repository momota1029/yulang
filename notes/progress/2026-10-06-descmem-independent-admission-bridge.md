## 5. Minimum missing judgment and conditional consequence

For this lane the smallest sufficient missing production premise is an
admission embedding at the actual retained G incidence:

```text

  Adm_ref(actual_inlet,e,h;xi,w_h)
    implies A_A(G,e,h;xi)
```

The conclusion must use the same original scopes and h, with a witness map
preserving carrier/provider identity, request and response incidence,

authority and all shared K,D. The embedding must be obtained from an
independently interpreted production punctured-context rule. Merely defining

semantics rather than derive the independently interpreted production rule;
this audit has no such selection authority. No premise may cite `Q_pending`
or whole-bound containment. Its four closure
clauses must account for the cases in §4, including the returned-provider
incidence after the original slot expires.

**Conditional theorem.** Suppose that independent embedding is supplied for
every reference challenge at the actual inlet, at the original fiber and
scopes. For the exact linked pair covered by Theorem C, every
`h in D_checked^ref` lies in `D_actual^prod`.

**Derivation.** Source-indexed §5.2 maps h to the same actual-inlet reference
certificate. Apply `EmbedAdm_G` to that certificate without changing h.
This gives `A_A(G,e,h;xi)`, which is precisely membership of the proposed
production actual challenge domain. No Function comparison or production
observation membership is used. This conditional theorem covers source-base
challenges only; it says nothing about additional production checked
challenges or production-only observations. It applies to a provider and its
source-generated linked checked view, not a comparison of Q against R.

The premise is not derivable from the inspected clauses:

   not define them for Option A.
5. Both approved handoffs require independent rules and explicitly leave their
   completion open. They do not promote the reference rules into exhaustive

Thus the absent object is an independent rule interpreting a punctured
