## 2. The exact first missing primitive

Give the required initial judgment the proof name `PCInit`:

```text
PCInit(Gamma; H:F at beta; kappa; eta; h0; xi)
```

It means that the source context `kappa`, with the call position occupied by
the declared callable hole H, admits the empty-history challenge `h0` under
its independently typed environment `eta`, original slot, whole carrier,
result/rebind path, source owners and joint constraints. This is notation
for the missing interpretation, not a new presentation field or selected
typing rule. The tested filling is absent from `eta`. H may be used through
its declared interface F; no proof that a filling satisfies F or that
`Q_pending` succeeds is a premise.

The first unavailable step is not raw resumption or a difficult recursive
membership inversion. It is construction of `PCInit` from independently
interpreted source-context typing/environment rules. In particular, the
existing ordinary name rule copies `Gamma(H)`; it does not state a callable
hole assumption rule whose environment requires no satisfaction proof for
H. Using an ordinary semantic environment containing a filling certified
at F would reintroduce precisely the circularity the coupled-core two-hole
candidate excludes.

Typed-core §6 also leaves the application's whole-carrier endpoint/path
translation open. Therefore a conditional call derivation cannot silently
replace the hole assumption by the completed test
`Result(I_arg) <: Parameter(F)`. Even if the exact translation and its local
direct evidence are supplied, its sufficiency as an initial production
context-admission rule remains an additional obligation. A local argument
query may be independent of `Q_pending`; such independence alone proves
neither context closure nor exhaustive production admission.

Two separate missing links must stay visible:

```text
independent hole/context/environment rules -> PCInit
PCInit and legal history extensions -> Adm_F(F,h;xi)
```

The second link must interpret the actual constrained Function root at its
recorded incidence. Source-contracts §2.2 assumes it is semantically active;
the assumption is not a constructor deriving either link. The earlier
`EmbedAdm_G` premise is their combined source-reference embedding. This
attempt exposes the initial primitive that an attempted proof of that
embedding would consume.

## 3. Conditional puncturing lemma and complete obligations

The following is a conditional proof obligation, not an adopted source rule.
Suppose a finite decorated source derivation is supplied together with all
of these hypotheses:

1. A callable hole assumption is independently interpreted at declared F and
   static beta without requiring the filling's F-membership. The semantic
   environment certifies only other free variables. Scope, annotation presence
   and all recorded typed incidences are retained.
2. Source constructor rules extend parametrically to that hole assumption.
   In particular the exact whole-carrier/path/contract translation for Call is
   supplied independently, with local source constraints checked at xi.
3. There is a separability certificate: context rules use the filling only
   through H's declared interface and the retained boundary references. They
   do not use its private body proof, a filling-derived authority grant or
   `Q_pending`. Deleting the filling derivation leaves all other premises
   justified. This is stronger than retaining matching four Function children.
4. The resulting context-typing/environment judgment has an initial rule
   producing `PCInit` with the whole carrier and original compatible current
   context. Static slot identity does not require an already executed receipt.
5. Production interprets this judgment at the actual Function root, and has
   independently justified closure rules for response, same-handle resumption
   and future returned-provider call/force. For each case it uses the original
   request/provider incidence, continuation and current context; xi and its
   scopes are shared. These rules are not inferred from membership positivity.

**Conditional lemma.** Under 1–4, deleting the certified filling subtree and
marking its use as H gives `PCInit` without `Q_pending`. Under 5 as well, every
finite challenge obtained by the four supplied admission rules gives
`Adm_F(F,h;xi)` at the same retained root/incidence.

**Derivation.** Induct on the finite context derivation outside the deleted
subtree. At H, use hypothesis 1. Literals/declarations use their original
local certificates; a name keeps its original lexical root; closure/reify
keeps its captured roots inertly. Bind keeps the shared result witness and
ordered suffix. Call keeps the callee-hole assumption, independently typed
whole argument, exact translation from 2 and source boundary references.
No constructor can acquire a private filling premise by 3. Registered
recursive references retain their original labels and interfaces; this is
a finite proof-graph replacement, not unbounded unfolding. Hypothesis 4
then produces the empty-history certificate.

For the second conclusion induct on the finite history certificate. Initial
uses that certificate and its independently supplied production incidence
rule. Response uses the exposed request tuple in this history and checks the
response provider at the same operation witness/current context. Repeated
resume uses this request's retained raw handle. Future use keeps the actually
returned provider and original typed port, with the next independently typed
provider. Provider reuse is permitted; no freshness condition is added.
In the request case the supplied bind equation

```text
Request(q,C,k) >>= S
  = Request(q,C, lambda(response,C'). k(response,C') >>= S)
```

retains the original witness/handle and pending suffix, threads C', and does
not replay receipt or revive expired authority. Hypothesis 5 supplies the
production rule at each step. All witnesses stay at their original binders.
This proves the implication conditional on the displayed premises; it does
not prove those source or production rules. In particular, hypothesis 4 is
the first unprovided judgment and hypothesis 5 is the remaining production
embedding/closure contract, rather than a hidden conclusion of the proof.

The lemma quantifies only over supplied, separable decorated derivations and
their finite certified histories. Deleting one reached filling does not
enumerate all independent clients or counterfactual typed contexts. An
exhaustive production domain must separately account for those contexts and
history-provider rules applicable to Option 2 observations without a source
constructor witness. Nothing here forces such observations into `P_ref`.

## 5. Smallest inspected shortcut discriminator

```text

F has Value(Unit) entry and Unit result; known static beta
eta is empty; ambient handler context is empty; history is empty
```

Retain the same original slot/profile, parameter/result paths, source roots
and xi. The context can be typed with declared H before filling it; its
initial reference challenge has no invocation's executed `Receive` event,
no request Observe, no raw handle and no returned provider yet. Filling can
later be the separately generated Pure Unit identity, but its comparison
success is not part of the initial reference certificate.

This distinguishes the shortcut “admission requires an actual observed
receipt/reached call” from the already stated reference admission rule.
There is one call, two result nodes, one hole and one Unit literal. No bind,
operation, closure construction, capture, adapter or request history is
needed. It is minimal within this displayed closed ordinary-value call
grammar; no global grammar-minimization search was performed.

This is an established reference instance reused as a failure control, not
a novel production counterexample. Production admission at this root remains
uninterpreted. Pre-call and post-receipt records differ in their dynamic
receipt evidence, so they are not two identical complete retained-evidence
tuples with different approved meanings. No such equal-evidence witness was
found. The instance therefore shows the direction error in receipt inversion
and does not establish loss of information or carrier insufficiency.

## 6. Independence, failed routes, limits and next action

No executable checker or external oracle was used. Reference generation and
reference admission deliberately share decorated source relations, owner
kernel, witnesses and rule inventory. Their agreement cannot independently
validate production semantics. The conditional lemma similarly shares the
explicit hole/context rules with its conclusion; its value is the complete
dependency list and first-premise localization. A checker populated with
those rules would only test their consequences.

Routes stopped at named premises: inversion of actual Call/Receive stops at
independent hole/context typing; ordinary Gamma lookup stops at a hole
assumption independent of filling satisfaction; using the local argument
inequality stops at its endpoint/path translation and admission sufficiency;
reference embedding stops at independent active production interpretation;
guarantee weakening stops at the already supplied original challenge domain.
The quiet/captured-provider execution probe was not repeated. No second toy
attempt can remove these premises by increasing its cases.

Coverage is the inspected constructor/admission rules and all finite histories
only under the conditional hypotheses; no numeric range, seed, enumerated
sample or mutation campaign applies. Some broad exploratory command output
was truncated; targeted section retrieval supplied the governing excerpts.
No exhaustive code, source-context or semantic-alternative search is claimed.
Failure conditions include nonseparable private filling evidence, a tested
value hidden in eta, Q-derived path/authority, altered original binder scope,
independently chosen port witnesses, discarded active provider predicates,
cross-history response/handle substitution, replayed receipt or revived
expired protection. An initial rule requiring source productivity would also
miss Theorem C's admitted divergent carriers.

Unverified: exhaustive production admission and satisfaction, source raw-role
and profile inference, non-source-witnessed provider histories, descriptor
constructor typing, every-client context closure, unknown conversions,
mutable State/imports, handler-image extension, generalization of the missing
judgment, effective solving and principality. No source rejection or new user
semantic choice is established.

Recommended next action: formalize or locate the independent initial
hole/context/environment rule at the retained Function incidence, with the
exact Call endpoint/path translation and the empty Unit instance as its first
obligation; then review the production interpretation and three history
closure clauses. This is narrower than another whole-bound inclusion probe.
