3. Clause meanings are those stipulated in source-contract §2.1: conjunction

   There are no additional unaccounted primitive rules.

**Conditional theorem.** Every relation formed by that common graph has the
same extension in the two interpretations, including any licensing relation
that is actually presented by the graph.

For nonrecursive nodes, induct on the expression. Equal primitive relations
give the base. Conjunction and union preserve equality pointwise. An indexed
image has the form `exists z. R(z) and M(z,y)`; equal R and M give the same
output witnesses. Uniform renaming is the same image. Original scoped binding
uses the same domain and body, so its quantified predicate agrees. An
independently interpreted constructor applies the same extensional operator
to equal inputs. These steps preserve source indices rather than quotienting
by endpoint equality.

For positive recursion, translate a finite derivation rule by rule. A leaf
uses the same primitive witness; each rule has the same premises/operator and
translated finite subderivations. This gives both inclusions. It does not
assume that semantic worlds or the full relation are finite.

Thus a successful pair in this fragment must differ in a primitive relation,
constructor/operator, certificate/eligibility predicate or clause inventory.
This identifies where a difference must be justified; it does not certify
any differing primitive interpretation. The hypotheses include a completed
original kernel, so this theorem cannot construct that kernel or prove the
source rules that would supply it.

Applied conditionally to the requested cases: inherited evidence cannot
separate under identical original provider inputs and indexed images;
generalized evidence cannot separate under identical eligibility certificates
and whole transport. A mixed-use case needs a distinct, justified original
aggregation clause; the earlier tag probe provides none. Annotation presence
can be a primitive source input, but FVIEW §4 expressly leaves its exact
contribution association open. These are consequences of the displayed
hypotheses, not all-source uniqueness claims.
