## Where the premise is actual

For the selected native unannotated Value inlet, every admitted checked
challenge has

```text
gamma = (J, kappa_J, carrier_J, mu_result, original_port_map,
         delta_static, delta_check, current_guards).
```

Uniform inlet §4.2(2) explicitly requires `mu_result`; its type is a finite
same-decorated-value inclusion proof at the original argument-result
incidence, with all required hereditary restrictions. A precedes the Lambda
challenge at Parameter's type scope. J is the independently typed argument's
complete contract at its actual root, supplied with the challenge. Neither J
nor A may be replaced with a convenient contract chosen after the Return.
The proof is applied on a J Return to its actual V(J.payload) evidence. A
pending/divergent prefix has no completed result evidence, while gamma still
contains the required inclusion proof. The same proof action is available at
the original future result telescopes.

This proves that the premise is mandatory throughout this **selected checked
Value-entry** domain. It does not make `mu_result` the universal name or field
of every typed check. Source §3.2 includes independent annotation, structural,
same-computation and complete Function checks. The ordinary native consumer
§6.1 dispatches Value(d_V), Computation(d_T) or Function(d_D,d_P) according to
the actual proof constructor. A callable checked against Any can use the Value
case without a fictitious target Function inlet. Retained Computation entry,
annotated/fixed/foreign inlets and executed conversions need their own rules.

Native bare `my id x=x` forms one Desc a, its repeated payload/result
incidences and a proof **slot**. It does not form a particular argument J or
an arbitrary mu_result in advance. Uniform inlet §4.3 gives its fixed finite
Delta projection/image proof schema with no additional operation bound;
that result is reused here, not independently reproved. Whole J/Car/context
validity is still an input premise.

## Conditional derivation: local recognition without inhabiting the context

Fix the following hypotheses, explicitly stronger than the Rust facts below:

1. P=J.payload and A have faithful finite complete interface/telescope codes;
   their identity, reference and scope checks are effective.
2. L is a supplied independently sound local registry with effective node
   signature/side-condition checks. Each used primitive has its genuine law.
   Recursive references use a supplied justified equation rule and its full
   domain/variance conditions, rather than unrestricted circular proof.
3. A submitted finite proof d and all retained auxiliaries are well scoped at
   the original checking incidence. Symbolic variables stand for the complete
   original value/provider/world/evidence tuple, with hereditary binders;
   all free assumptions are explicit in Gamma.

Define the documentary recognition judgment

```text
Gamma |- d : P => A
```

as checking those original local signatures and premise subtrees. This is
notation for the selected finite registry interpretation, not a new semantic
relation or implemented checker. For an acyclic finite term, recurse over its
finite tree; for a registered finite graph, check the finite graph plus the
genuine equation rule. Effective checks in hypotheses 1–2 give terminating
recognition on this supplied syntax. This does not assert an implementation
of those effective side conditions for the complete Yulang catalogue.

**Conditional local soundness.** For every lawful interpretation of Gamma
and every original hereditary decorated tuple z, a recognized d transports
the supplied V(P) evidence at z to V(A) evidence at that same z, retaining
its complete output/auxiliary proof data.

**Derivation.** Identity transports the exact evidence required by its rule.
Compose applies its first child to z, keeps the intermediate certificate,
then applies its second child to that same z. Record, union and intersection
cases use their actual selected fields/alternatives and child induction
hypotheses, preserving shared operands. Top/Any and authentic primitive
leaves use the independent laws from hypothesis 2. Complete Function and
hereditary binder nodes apply child induction hypotheses under the original
challenge/future telescope, with their actual domain/equality conditions.
Registered graph cases use precisely the assumed justified equation rule.
Every case preserves source value/provider identity and retains rather than
erases proof alternatives. These are the selected rule laws, not properties
proved independently by a checker which assumes those laws.

The smallest witness is the one-node term `Identity(A)` with P=A. Its local
signature can be recognized with a bound symbolic V(A) input. Recognizing
this term does not construct any actual carrier t, J, Car(J,t), compatible
context, world or global strategy; the interpretation theorem is universal
over lawful supplied inputs. Even an uninhabited Gamma can have a valid
conditional identity derivation. This is a derivation separating the two
obligations, not a source counterexample or a witness that Gamma is lawful.

Thus a **local** proof consumer can receive symbolic endpoint/telescope codes,
typed references and finite d without constructing arbitrary J/context
witnesses. An **actual gamma admission** consumer must additionally receive
faithful J/kappa/Car/port/Delta/current-guard evidence, or a uniform justified
symbolic interpretation of those inputs. Local recognition neither supplies
that package nor reflects its inhabitance at a fixed jointly scoped prefix.
Proof search, exact negative answers and JOINT_DEC remain open.
