# Flexible actual trace scope

At.refresh accepts a disjunction of view equality and a finite syntactic nominal-only condition over the currently translated queries of every registry declaration. No semantic Instance/Evidence conclusion is an action guard. Refresh still supplies the original actual certificate plus Unknown-to-Known source progress. The new Step is embedded in actual Step, and the previous 174 Step embeds in the new one.

The recorded-current invariant is generated from InitialSource and empty journal. Ordinary installation gets current_instance from derived SourceFormed; preserved instances carry their own registry membership to select the declaration-fragment premise. A single final model is backtransported along steps. Arbitrarily many finite refreshes may be interleaved with ordinary steps.

The proof does not cover descriptor replacement, carrier changes, all possible actual executions, existence of a selected dictionary, or general B4 completion. Refines preserves old descriptors, even while nominal-only refresh may change the normalized view.
