# Final cycle 181 support review

Read-only review of ModeLedger181, FramedExtension181, ModeExtension181 and ModeFrameExample181 found no stronger theorem claim than the proved scope.

- ModeLedger181 keeps rawSources unchanged, preserves captureSources and all centered site centers, and replaces schema footprints with the mode-sensitive ones. A binder omitted from one endpoint-use schema can still occur in captureSources, an actual head use, another schema, a child site or the raw journal. No global absence or strict aggregate reduction follows from the singleton example.
- FramedExtension181 states its frame premise openly and retains linked endpoint/root support plus four semantic conditions. ModeExtension181 correctly discharges that premise with the proved full centered frame; it does not discharge the four conditions.
- ModeFrameExample181 proves an inhabited singleton upper-use schema survives a top-to-bottom binder change, with old-frame disagreement. It does not prove the entire plan or journal survives that mutation. Its comments preserve this distinction.
