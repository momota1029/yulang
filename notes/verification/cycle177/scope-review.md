## Remaining general obligation

The general exact G EvidenceNode field is **not completed**. The new producers cover explicitly delimited scalar and checked terminal nominal fragments. The current candidate's `∃ model, packet.Holds model` does not supply a common model of the complete centered journal and `plan.Holds`, which also contains center equations. Stability only rules out an installation after its support and feasibility guards are available; it cannot manufacture those missing guards. The general novel branch therefore still needs a noncircular producer (or a genuine stable reachable counterexample).

No new declaration changes the language semantics. No source reviewed here establishes a stable reachable counterexample to general EvidenceNode. ScalarIntervals has been separately source-reviewed: its endpoint ordering genuinely follows from `valid.2.1 plan.input candidate.centered_recorded` and transitivity through the center. This uses the stronger centered Models predicate, not merely erased-state Models; accordingly no erased Committed conclusion is claimed. Candidate carries Prepared inspection and registration, but no pre-assumed Instance or universal same-model packet truth. Its existential branch witness is not used in candidate_holds. Final compilation and audit of ScalarIntervals remain the implementing thread's responsibility.

Final fixture review: RequestsExample177 is nonvacuous (`inhabited` is constructed from `source_models`, not assumed). Its positive test uses an actual nonempty named-output request; `branch` constructs the existential Candidate witness independently, then the new consumer produces Evidence. The two negative tests are deliberately certificate-level checks and should not be reported as general semantic rejection theorems.
