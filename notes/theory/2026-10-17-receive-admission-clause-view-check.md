would exclude a tuple. Conversely treating a required conjunction as an
alternative could include a tuple missing a genuine prerequisite. If the
proposal means to retain the original graph rather than flatten it, this
discriminator is avoided, but its phase recursion remains unspecified.

For original joint hiding, take a shared witness b in `{0,1}` with
`R(b) := b=0` and `S(b) := b=1`. Each marginal existential is inhabited,
whereas `exists b. R(b) and S(b)` is empty. The original same-b conjunction
therefore cannot be reconstructed from separately witnessed phase records.

K/D witnesses; no actual such b/R/S occurrence is asserted here.

A future-dependent certificate may legitimately be stored initially as a

asserting an actual future result or live grant before the event. The selected
Bind/Scope laws preserve this distinction. Merely labeling a clause A, C or P
