## 2. Exact fragment and explicit hypotheses

Fix one original binder tree, jointly scoped `xi=(nu,K,D)`, and one legal
whole assignment/incidence map `rho`. Let `u` be the ordinary descriptor
actually returned by the proposed public decoder, and `F` the actual ordinary
use descriptor. Write `D_u,D_F` for complete independently presented admission
clauses, and `Complete_u,Complete_F` for complete whole-tuple membership
clauses before the same final projection `Pi_xi`. The tuple includes history,
observation and original scoped witnesses. `D` inside `xi` and the admission
root notation `D_u` are different objects.

Here `Complete` includes independent ordinary descriptor membership, original
residuals, authority and scope predicates, every source-base alternative and
every licensed production alternative. D0 §5 calls the guarded exhaustive
grammar `Complete_u`; a source-base `R_u` alone cannot fill this operand.
This notation does not define descriptor membership by source execution.

H1–H7 are premises. The proof rules available *relative to those premises*
are the §5.3 finite `Cov`, provider `Guarantee`/`Absorb`, `Eq`, positive
constructor congruence, registered positive recursion, and Function rules.
Their availability in the actual resolver is H7, not a consequence of source
construction. D0 does not yet supply H1–H4 or H7 as established facts.

The exact sufficient query fragment is:

```text
H1, H2, H5, H6, H7
finite Eq_Gamma(D_u,D_F)
finite Le_Gamma(Complete_u,Complete_F)
one original scope-preserving assignment/incidence map rho throughout
------------------------------------------------------------------ Function
Direct(u,F; that finite proof DAG)
```

`Gamma` contains the supplied original scoped clauses and certificates, not
the desired query as a premise. H3 and H4 ensure that the two proof operands
really are complete. Identity of endpoint IDs or four printed children does
not establish H2 or either finite proof. No successful child Function query
is composed into this parent introduction.

Soundness within this conditional fragment follows directly: `Eq` gives equal
complete challenge domains, `Le` gives complete whole-observation inclusion
on each admitted fiber, and one common `Pi_xi` preserves inclusion. Original
shared witnesses are transported together before projection. This proves
the sufficient complete Function containment used by the rule, relative to
the independent meanings and licenses in H1–H6. It does not prove these
meanings, licenses, actual provider acceptance, or H7.

## 3. Smallest same-interface conditional proof

Suppose `F_0` is the supplied legal whole transported copy of `u`'s complete
interface, with unchanged clauses, all alternatives and scoped primitive
operands. Identity is the smallest case (`F_0=u`, `rho=id`). For a distinct
use root, finite clause matching under `rho` supplies the same argument:

```text
Eq-id / matched primitive leaves / Eq-C / registered Eq:
  Eq_Gamma(D_u,D_F0)
  Eq_Gamma(Complete_u,Complete_F0)

Eq-as-Le:
  Le_Gamma(Complete_u,Complete_F0)

Function at the actual roots:
  Direct(u,F_0)
```

For a registered equation, the Eq certificate covers both directions and
every alternative. Fixed binders, actual receipt, whole argument, ordered
Bind suffix/current state and designated consumer remain at their original
positions. An arbitrary extensional equal view lacking finite certificates
does not satisfy this derivation. Even the reflexive case requires H7; a
pure semantic identity theorem does not produce ordinary query evidence.

For `id`, the supplied freshening substitutes the local binder once through
formal/body/result ports and their incident clauses. For captured `pick`, it
substitutes the unused input family once while leaving the fixed outer result
provider, its public import contract and transitive dependencies unchanged.
Both uses retain Value entry's receipt/Force/rebind and actual result
consumer. This proof does not create capture admission or show either
generalization's eligibility. It does not select D0's unresolved S/T result
guard; whichever independent policy is supplied must match on both sides.

## 4. One coverage-widening conditional proof

Give this additional explicit subcase of H4, at fixed original scopes and
non-coverage interface. Let `p` be the **same whole provider operand** on both
sides and let its complete fixed envelope be `N_j`. Supply an existing flat
allowance union `A=E or T`; finite union injection gives
`Cov_Gamma(E,A)`. No new operation/capture grant is inferred from this union.
The complete provider A/G inventory, future input domain, fixed guarantee
equations and all non-coverage fields remain identical.

Supply identical source-free template base `R`, independently licensed `W,Z`
and complete independent admission `D_*` at both roots. The only changed
membership leaf is the displayed genuine provider guarantee:

```text
G_E(y) = G_fixed(y) and Bound_j(E,p)(y)
G_A(y) = G_fixed(y) and Bound_j(A,p)(y)

Complete_u = H_GE(R),       Complete_Fplus = H_GA(R)
D_u = D_* = D_Fplus

H_G(R) = least X.
  G(y) and (R(y) or Z(y) or exists x,z. X(x) and W(x,y,z)).
```

Here `G_fixed` includes the active independent descriptor, result policy,
original residuals and every other guard conjunct. Any coverage-dependent
primitive requires its own parameter-inclusion certificate; it is outside
this simplest identical-parameter subcase. If an extra changes a
history/provider coordinate live in admission, supply an explicit
unchanged-domain certificate as well. Identical grammar text is insufficient
to derive that certificate from positivity.

The finite certificate is:

1. Union injection yields `Cov(E,A)`; `Guarantee` yields
   `Le(Bound_j(E,p),Bound_j(A,p))`. Matched fixed leaves yield `Eq` and hence
   `Le(G_fixed,G_fixed)`. Positive conjunction yields `Le(G_E,G_A)`.
2. Pair the two registered `H` equations. Match `R`, the unanchored `Z` arm,
   `W` and all typed parameters exactly. At recursive references use only
   the registered premise `X_u <= X_Fplus`. Positive image, union and
   conjunction congruence provide the finite operator comparison. The
   registered recursion rule yields `Le(Complete_u,Complete_Fplus)`.
3. Exact independent admission matching yields `Eq(D_u,D_Fplus)`.
   Apply Function once at `u,Fplus` to obtain `Direct(u,Fplus)`.

For the membership step's semantic justification, induction on a finite
derivation preserves a source leaf through matched `R`, a `Z` leaf through
its matched license, and a `W` step through the same full witness plus the
translated predecessor. Every final guard uses the proved `Le(G_E,G_A)`.
Thus unanchored extras and every finite abstract chain are covered. Original
rigid universal binders are fixed external parameters, checked pointwise;
no universal binder over growing `X` or unsupported nonpositive child is
introduced. The domain equation remains independently certified.

**Strictness is a separate conditional premise.** To call this a strictly
wider complete view, supply one original-scope whole tuple `y_*` at a common
admitted history with an independently licensed `Z(y_*)`, `G_A(y_*)` and
`not G_E(y_*)`. The `Z` arm derives right membership; the mandatory final
guard excludes left membership. One such tuple is sufficient. Observable
strictness after `Pi_xi` additionally requires that its projected observation
has no left preimage; guard separation alone need not survive projection.
The inspected proposal supplies no actual licensed `y_*`, so strictness is
a conditional theorem, not an established source witness. This specifies
one strictly wider subcase without inventing a license. Reversing the query
requires a new `Le(Complete_Fplus,Complete_u)` certificate; Cov injection
provides none. This is a local guarantee widening, not arbitrary arrow
subtyping or arbitrary public-view completeness.

root has an independently licensed unanchored extra whose final guard holds,

and the registered recursion certificate fail. The claimed `Direct` proof
must be rejected even if its source-base induction and matched admission

the claimed complete containment is itself false. This is an exact audit
condition and logical mutation, not an executed or source-realized witness.
It attacks exhaustive input accounting rather than repeating the existing
import collision or hidden-root toy probe.

Independently, deleting Function introduction at `u` defeats H7 while leaving
