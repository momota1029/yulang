## 1. Indexed operands and scope discipline

Fix one original interpretation S, original binder tree B, original X and
xi=(nu,K,D). S itself is an input, not constructed by this note. Fix a legal
event e=(eta0,h), its actual current C_e, and formal holes
H=(H_f:T_checked,H_a:I_argument). Neither actual filling is supplied.

An incidence region A contains the supplied original binding/use/root
incidences and all their required reference operands. It is not a set of
closed provider identities. References carry their licensed original origins;
two incidences may denote one provider without becoming one source binding.
Latent captures, raw handles and ordered saved suffixes remain operands even
when unreached. No primitive heap or new runtime carrier is assumed.

Write `T_A^H` for the original operand/witness telescope restricted to A.
Restriction includes dependencies and original binders, not merely fields
whose names mention A. Let `U_A:T_A^H` denote its complete data, with `w_A`
the witness coordinates at their original positions. Writing U_A and w_A
separately is projection notation, not permission to choose them separately.

The actual importing incidence is

```text
i = (original import declaration/use, receiving root/binding incidence,
     original provider/interface reference, importer scope in B, e, xi).
```

An independent semantic import specification kappa fixes a new incidence
region N and a shared boundary L. Original source/import evidence supplies
inclusions j_A:L->A and j_N:L->N and the identification of references on L.
The combined incidence region is P=A union_L N, with inclusions a:A->P and
n:N->P. This union identifies only licensed shared operands; distinct Use,
Name and binding incidences stay distinct. For a cyclic region not admitting
the telescope construction below, the candidate is unavailable until its
own independently sound recursive justification is supplied.

The boundary contains every genuinely shared dependent coordinate, including
provider/reference identity, descriptors, family/operation witnesses, original
K/D incidences, current configuration, owners/receipts, paths, scopes, handles,
and suffix references when present. Overlap determined from equal printed
types or endpoint IDs is invalid. Unshared supplier identities remain rigid.

## 2. Dependent amalgamation, not independently chosen worlds

Candidate structural input `Amalg(B,A,N,L)` specifies dependency-closed
restriction maps r_A and r_N to T_L, embeddings into the same original B,
and a constructor on compatible telescope data:

```text
glue : (U_A:T_A^H) -> (U_N:T_N^H)
    -> Eq_L(r_A U_A, r_N U_N) -> U_P:T_P^H

a* (glue(U_A,U_N,q)) = U_A
n* (glue(U_A,U_N,q)) = U_N
j_A* U_A = j_N* U_N                         [including dependent witnesses].
```

`Eq_L` includes dependent equality/transport where coordinate types depend on
earlier shared data. Matching provider tokens alone is insufficient. The
construction does not infer this agreement from separate satisfiability.
An overlap certificate q must establish all displayed equalities in their
actual telescope fibers.

**Structural lemma, conditional on a supplied Amalg.** For acyclic dependent
telescopes whose dependency and binder embeddings satisfy this signature,
compatible data glue by original binder order. At each coordinate, copy the
unique side's field, or use q for a shared field; prior coordinate equality
justifies the required dependent type transport. Restriction recovers each
input by the same coordinate induction. At an original universal binder the
construction remains inside that binder, pointwise; a later existential is
constructed there. It never changes `forall x. exists w(x)` into
`exists w. forall x`, prenexes witnesses, or identifies distinct source scopes.

This is a theorem about the stated telescope input, not proof that every
original semantic world has such an Amalg. Infinite future histories may be
parameters at original universal positions. No finite-state or finite-history
restriction follows. Nonlocal dependencies, incompatible binder orders or an
unresolved recursive witness knot are explicit failure conditions.

## 3. Exact clause frontier and independent import evidence

To eliminate a hidden completed-world premise, the owner must expose an
exhaustive clause inventory for the regional world judgments. Let J_P be
the original clause occurrences demanded on P, retaining every operand and
binder, and partition them as

```text
J_P = J_old disjoint-union J_import disjoint-union J_cross.
```

J_old must be exactly clause instances available from the old world on A,
with a proved restriction equation `p_P(glue U_A U_N q)=p_A(U_A)`.
J_import consists of the independent local kappa clauses at i, including
open descriptor/provider conditions and any local current-world conditions.
J_cross contains every other new obligation: cross-boundary identities,
joint authority/path/continuation constraints, global consistency conditions,
and any previously checked clause whose meaning changes upon extension.

Support names alone do not establish the partition. A clause that quantifies
over all installed roots, or reads a whole-world condition, goes into J_cross
unless the owner proves its restriction equation. Dependencies must include
negative tests, absence conditions and hidden guard/admission parameters.
The inventory may be infinite if the original semantics requires it; no
claim of a finite effective checker follows. Its exhaustiveness and agreement
with the fixed original predicates are unresolved semantic obligations.

The independent primitive at the importer has signature

```text
P_kappa^H(i,e,xi; U_N,w_N) : Prop

cross(i,e,xi; U_P,w_P)
    = conjunction, at original B, of p(U_P,w_P) for p in J_cross.
```

P_kappa is independently specified before the pending comparison. It exposes
its complete local operand/guard clauses, original role/entry, rigid references,
and dependencies on formal H. It is not defined as `EnvStore(P)`, target
DescMem, Q success, source-image membership, or validity at every closed
filling. It can license an opaque semantic provider with no source body.
Ordinary independent validity of external values remains required in its own
clauses. A raw export witness without a justified importer embedding is not
P_kappa(i,...); exporter-to-importer correspondence remains its supplier's
separate proof duty.

The remaining identification obligations are written explicitly:

```text
ID-world(S,kappa,i):
  (EnvStore_S^H(P,U_P,w_P) and JointWF_S^H(P,U_P,w_P))
  iff [old_S(A,U_A,w_A) and P_kappa^H(i,U_N,w_N)
       and Eq_L(r_A U_A,r_N U_N) and cross(i,U_P,w_P)]

ID-import(S,kappa,i):
  P_kappa^H(i,U_N,w_N) and Eq_L(...) and cross(i,U_P,w_P)
  -> Imp_Delta,S^H(i,P,U_P,w_P).
```

Event/xi arguments suppressed on these two lines remain the same throughout.
`old_S` is only EnvStore/JointWF on A, before installation, and does not
contain the new imported root or require membership of either actual hole.
ID-world also supplies the conclusion's new open root/descriptor obligations;
it cannot discard them into an unchecked descriptor conjunct. These IDs are
unproved correspondence obligations for fixed S, not definitions of existing
predicates by this display. Even the forward direction required by the rule
is missing; the reverse direction is a stronger exact characterization.

## 4. Candidate zero-step importer rule and conditional derivation

Here is the single proposed rule, with all its supplier requirements exposed:

```text
[S,B,X,e,xi,H,A,N,L,i,kappa fixed as above]
Amalg(B,A,N,L)          q : Eq_L(r_A U_A,r_N U_N)
old_S(A,U_A,w_A)        p : P_kappa^H(i,e,xi;U_N,w_N)
d : cross(i,e,xi;glue(U_A,U_N,q),w_P)
ID-world-forward(S,kappa,i)     ID-import(S,kappa,i)
---------------------------------------------------------------- [OpenImportExt-0]
U_P = glue(U_A,U_N,q)
Imp_Delta,S^H(i,P,U_P,w_P)
EnvStore_S^H(P,U_P,w_P) and JointWF_S^H(P,U_P,w_P)
a*U_P=U_A; n*U_P=U_N; all old scoped incidence evidence restricts unchanged
```

IDs quantify over the displayed regional operands; they are owner-level
clause correspondences, not an assumed complete validity witness for this P.
To construct d, supply actual proofs of each frontier clause on this glued
tuple. Naming `Compatible` without those clauses merely recreates the missing
premise and does not instantiate the rule. Independent semantic membership
under its own external contract may be a primitive premise; completed
membership of the installed importer root, target membership of the callable
under comparison, and a completed own-root world are not premises.

**Conditional regional assembly theorem.** Given precisely these premises,
Amalg constructs U_P at B. Every J_old proof transports by its restriction
equation; p supplies every J_import proof under its fixed interpretation;
d supplies every J_cross proof on the same U_P. Exhaustiveness yields the
whole clause conjunction. ID-world-forward identifies it with the original
EnvStore/JointWF; ID-import supplies the actual original Imp_Delta judgment
at i. The two restriction equations retain the old certificates. This proves
only this regional instance, conditional on the IDs and primitive proofs.

The extension is inert and takes zero local source execution steps. C_e is
preserved; it is not reset to the configuration of eta0, and h need not be
empty. No argument Delay execution, callee Return, receipt, activation,
result/rebind value or actual carrier filling is introduced. Structural root
installation does not mint live authority. Static callback views retain the
provider's actual role/entry. Name/Bind aliases may subsequently use their
independent preservation rules; they do not justify this installation backward.

## 5. Lawful uniform action and restriction laws

Each independently legal whole action theta needs its own certificate; its
legality is not inferred from a uniform-looking renaming. The maps act on
incidences, complete operands and dependent telescope witnesses together:

```text
S_theta,A : T_A^H -> T_theta(A)^theta(H)
S_theta,N : T_N^H -> T_theta(N)^theta(H)
S_theta,L : T_L^H -> T_theta(L)^theta(H)

r_theta(A) S_theta,A = S_theta,L r_A
r_theta(N) S_theta,N = S_theta,L r_N
S_theta,P glue(U_A,U_N,q)
  = glue(S_theta,A U_A, S_theta,N U_N, S_theta,L q).
```

The action preserves original binder placement, actual role/entry, rigid
imports and licensed provider identities. Any legally movable xi/K/D
coordinates and every incident predicate move jointly; rigid coordinates
remain fixed. Repeated references to H_f/H_a move together at their original
origins, even when one closed filling accidentally equals a rigid provider.
Identity/composition equations hold only for identity and compositions declared
legal by the independent clauses. Event-witness extensions stay at their
authorized binders.

Additionally the owner supplies semantic preservation certificates for
old_S, P_kappa, every frontier predicate and both IDs in the fixed target S.
For example `P_kappa^H(i,U_N,w_N)` transports to that same independently
specified primitive's transported instance at theta(i), rather than merely
to the relational image of an arbitrary predicate. The clause partition
must also correspond under theta; otherwise a fresh whole frontier proof
is required. These preservation certificates are unproved here.

With them, transporting each rule premise and using the commuting equations
proves naturality of the constructed extension. It does not prove the
certificates, independence of admission, or checked-to-actual domain inclusion.
Actual plugging requires independently justified actual callable/carrier
contracts and joint domain evidence; no rule checks an actual filling at
T_checked as a prerequisite to its own comparison.

## 6. Smallest instance and discriminating boundaries

A minimal target instance for this method has old region A containing the
formal H_f and current C_e; one external semantic root r at i in N; and one
recorded reference from r to H_f in overlap L. Supply P_kappa for that open
reference at i, without a source body, and the complete frontier witness d.
The rule constructs r's installation while leaving H_f formal and C_e fixed.
Removing r removes installation; removing its reference removes hole
dependence; omitting q loses shared dependency identity. Minimality is only
for one root with one hole-dependent boundary, not globally for Yulang source.
This is a candidate instance schema, not an admitted concrete program.

The previous two-incidence closed-identity obstruction is reused only as a
boundary: incidence-aware action here retains the data that its shortcut
discarded. No second proof of that counterexample or Boolean world probe is
claimed. The new method discriminates whether an alleged supplier actually
provides the missing fixed-semantic frontier and identification.

Logical mutations, not executed experiments:

| Mutation | Exact failure |
| --- | --- |
| Supply compatible provider IDs but different dependent operation/family witnesses on L | Eq_L is absent; glue is ill-typed or loses the original joint witness. |
| Put a whole-world absence/expiry clause in J_old without its restriction proof | New roots may invalidate it; clause exhaustiveness cannot yield ID-world-forward. |
| Supply only local P_kappa and omit a cross-root authority/path constraint | d lacks that frontier clause; no joint importer judgment follows. |
| Replace either ID with generic relation-image transport | Fails identification with the fixed original importer interpretation. |
| Use EnvStore(P) or installed-r membership as the import primitive | Assumes the extension conclusion; no independent introduction remains. |
| Require a source body or closed-source reachability for P_kappa | Excludes the selected independently compatible semantic import domain. |
| Require actual DescMem(T_checked,f), Q success, or a returned argument | Circular admission or loss of permitted divergent/suspended carriers. |
| Change source binding identities, entry role, or expired authority during glue | Violates the original incidence/current-world premises. |

There is no independent executable oracle. The assembly proof shares S,
primitive clauses, Amalg and IDs with its reference; a checker assuming them
would prove only its own clause partition/action consistency. Seeds, ranges,
sample counts and executable mutation coverage: none. No complete competing
source models, complete admitted counterexample or repository-wide absence
theorem is provided.
