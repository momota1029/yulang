## 5. The derivation and what it actually removes

**Descriptor normalization.** Under the selected constructors, at the same
original scopes,

```text
R_c := ReadInvoke(F_c,D_c,IF_c)
F_step := Strict(I_x,x:A_x,R_c,IF_step)
```

are total typed constructor expressions. Substitute the first into the second
and substitute every fresh name/projection of these expressions in all
dependent admission, membership, constraint and evidence fields. Interface
projections equal the §4 telescopes by selected IF formation, retaining every
original slot and map. No semantic primitive is eliminated by this step.

Given a witness before substitution, forgetting the fresh aliases yields the
normalized witness. Conversely evaluate the constructor expressions at that
same binder node to extend it uniquely on those aliases. Both maps fix all
old coordinates, strategies and guards. They preserve every occurrence because
substitution is simultaneous across every consumer. This is the reviewed
total-definition theorem; it needs neither hidden-witness uniformity nor new
quantifier order. This proves descriptor equality, and equality of relations
that differ **only by those total definitions**. It does not justify removing
an old admission predicate or source-image conjunct.

**Producer introduction.** Step constructs the actual v_step and installed
world at F_step under H-local, without assuming step membership. Descriptor
normalization transfers that conclusion to the displayed Strict/ReadInvoke
schema. The producer can retain an introduction certificate whose conclusion
is membership at that schema for the same actual closure/root/event. Ordinary
use needs its conclusion and lawful restrictions, not its source proof tree.
An independently fixed A_step still needs its actual same-value comparison;
the displayed schema cannot overwrite that endpoint.

**Raw structural phases.** For this fixed source pattern, selected source
prefix rules expand Name/Result into same binding/Return, Delay into inert
formation, Call into the callee Bind and actual-operation image, and local
entry into Force/rebind/body/return. Their inversions identify those same
operands and finite raw witnesses. Replacing derivation-node aliases by these
fixed constructor schemas preserves each given raw structural derivation,
including its original pending suffix and result/future maps. Reconstruction
of a source-base proof is producer-side use of those same selected rules.
It is not inversion of arbitrary DescMem into a source execution.

This produces a fixed **source structural schema** for the actual captured
closure and open admitted carrier interface. It still has the raw actual
operation, original formation/guard predicates and incidence tokens. It is
not an independently exhaustive production membership grammar. In particular,
the selected ReadInvoke theorem proves `M_source -> DescMem` on its scoped
structural branch; it does not prove the converse, nor identify W/Z with that
branch. Claiming complete-root equality by deleting M_source at this point
would leave the old bridge premise untouched.
