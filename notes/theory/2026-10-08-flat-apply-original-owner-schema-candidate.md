## 3. Opaque complete primitive contract and the conditional discharge

Let j retain the entire original dependent tuple: B,X,xi=(nu,K,D), telescope,
scopes, original binding and route, c/u_f/a, endpoint variables, all source
ports and proof dependencies. Fixed imports remain fixed. The required
primitive-owner output at a can be opaque but must have these fields:

```text
PrimWhole(a;j) =
  actual original primitive declaration and source incidence at a;
  complete parameter/whole-tuple/evidence telescopes P_a,T_a;
  original relation Prim_a(h,y;xi) with its complete witness family;
  original license, scope, hard constraints, provider/future and domain laws;
  typed Data interface Value(Int), actual inert value 1;
  projections to every field in T_a and independently specified completeness;
  lawful action on P_a,T_a,relation,witnesses,licenses,domains and incidences.
```

Completeness here means that T_a exposes every field of this declared contract
required by a consumer and that the retained opaque relation is the **whole
original primitive relation**, including genuine alternatives and proof
choices. It does not mean every allowed tuple satisfies the relation. A bare
endpoint `Int`, an existentially chosen pleasant witness or an unknown
predicate with no original declaration/contract supplies none of this claim.
The law must preserve the original witness family, rather than replace it by
one canonical witness. No finite cardinality is imposed on that family.

For one admissible sorted whole map theta, demand the primitive's actual
dependent transport on all these components, with identity/composition and
relation/evidence compatibility. Noninjective endpoint substitution does not
identify declaration/reference occurrences or erase proof multiplicity.
Reflection is required only when a consumer requires it and the primitive
supplies the corresponding invertible-renaming or inverse-on-image law.
Syntactic Normalize transport is not this semantic law.

**Conditional opaque-interface calculation.** Given PrimWhole, IF-Insert at
its actual declared root and IF-Use at its actual typed child reference retain
the whole T_a under the same scoped substitution. IF's primitive induction
case treats this evidence atomically, including infinite indexed fields.
Code-Result then requires the separate original Result port; Carrier-Delay
additionally requires Call-owned ReifyOrigin. Their construction preserves
the primitive contract rather than enumerating its internals.

**Derivation.** Apply source-interface §2/IF proof §§2–3 to the independently
typed declaration, then the actual source-reference introduction. Apply the
Value branch of typed-core §6 to obtain `result(literal(1))` and
`Comp(empty,Int)`. Supply the original Result port to Code-Result and original
Call-owned Reify origin to Carrier-Delay. Each conclusion retains its child
evidence. No step requests an enumeration of Prim_a's observations. Every step
still needs its actual typed premise; this calculation does not introduce a
missing Literal rule into Theorem S's displayed Name grammar.

Thus internal finite primitive expansion is not justified as a prerequisite
for this interface route. Authentic primitive completeness/action and the
separate Result/Reify registrations remain necessary supplied contracts.

## 5. Exact failed premise and stopped derivation

An exhaustive interface can contain opaque complete subcontracts. It must
nevertheless identify their actual original insertion/reference positions and
all other Application-owned clauses. Opacity supplies no rule deciding that
five displayed reference heads, or their union with G.cert, are this owner
presentation. L.receive and G.cert may have a genuine refinement/attachment
relationship; its typed rule and preservation laws are absent from the
inspected inputs. No inequality or alternative semantics is inferred from
that absence.

relation and ReceiveSchema. Source generation §5 factors a supplied decorated
rule envelope and returns its supplied certificate. Code-Call §3.4 takes the
existing Application inventory and only projects operand origins. IF-Insert
§2 takes an actual original clause; IF-Use takes its genuine reference. Theorem
IF likewise takes actual emitted inventory and authentic Q_e. None of these
output judgments is AppOwnerDecl with the required exhaustive original
identification. Inverting these constructors returns their supplied original
premises; it does not remove those premises from the construction. This is a
bounded positive-constructor calculation, not a model-based non-entailment
proof or repository-wide absence claim.

One could now **define** a fresh declaration with exactly L's five heads,
declare a tagged emitted family and implement lookup in that presentation.
Its own occurrence inversion would follow by constructor cases. The comparison

it would not be justified by that definition. Alternatively declaring L and
G.cert independent conjuncts would impose an unproved simultaneous semantic
condition and potentially duplicate evidence. Neither construction is selected
here. No complete competing language meanings are established, so this note
does not conclude that a genuine user decision is necessarily required.

tags, scopes, occurrences, alternatives and evidence.

Whole action must move the entire dependent j, all child contracts,
registrations, source origins, occurrences, original rule entries and their
lookup derivations together. Its identity/composition derives from component
laws only after AppOwnerDecl/E-flat provide their laws. O0's eight projections
retain the whole original record; equality of endpoints is insufficient.
Neither a free syntax action nor the opaque primitive action proves the
missing owner's semantic action.

Keep the gates separate:

- **R-flat:** authentic original port registration, granted for isolation.
- **E-flat:** original owner declaration/rule, literal-fiber installation,
  actual membership and complete inversion; remains unfilled.
- **SeedExposure:** source upper-exposure protection may be constructed before
  emission; exact packaging at e_c for paired O1 still needs its actual record
  and owner-retained formation attachment. Literal Int is not a seed.
- **O0/O1:** O0 consumes actual emitted e_c; Reg/Route/O0 gives the selected
  slot; paired O1 additionally consumes the separate exact seed. Their selected
  local meanings are unchanged.
- **CallInitial/I0/K0/P0/action:** complete independent initial context/profile/
  world and phase/action inputs remain downstream; source occurrence formation
  is not admission.
- **Solving/admission/export/principality/cutover:** generated propositions may
  be inconsistent. No successful comparison, semantic assignment, actual
  provider acceptance, transformed export or implementation is constructed.

This research operation has no production state to roll back. A future owner
operation must withhold its entire output on incompatible old registration,
ill-scoped child/primitive, invalid whole-map contract, missing owner rule,
partial occurrence/alternative coverage or failed typed insertion/lookup.
It must retain old entries and publish no partial new package. Unsatisfiable
generated constraints are not such a static formation failure. These are
candidate construction requirements, not new runtime/compiler policy.

Stop on any dependency change affecting these clauses; opaque predicates
without complete original contracts; proof-fiber erasure; treating IF-Insert
