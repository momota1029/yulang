## 1. Objective, dependencies and exact premise

Attempt the uniform RT.2 obligation in
[outer introduction](2026-10-08-outer-apply-closure-introduction.md) §§2–6,
especially §3, for the selected source

```yu
my apply f = { my step x = f x; step }
```

The governing selected cases are
[contextual Function membership](../design/2026-10-08-contextual-function-membership-definition.md)
§§2–3 and
[captured closure constructors](../design/2026-10-08-captured-closure-constructor-definition.md)
§§2–4. Their constructive arguments are
[input realization](2026-10-08-call-semantic-input-realization.md) §§2–4,
and [captured introduction](2026-10-08-captured-call-closure-introduction.md)
§§3–6. No different source meaning or new comparison rule is selected.

Keep the same positive operator and fixed independent domains:

```text
M    = nu Phi
B_as = nu Omega_as
Omega_as(Y).V = Phi(Y).V union H_a union H_s
Omega_as(Y).W/T/Car = Phi(Y).W/T/Car.
```

The holes are the exact actual apply and designated step tuples, at their
specified descriptors, roots, incidences and lawful restrictions. They grant
no world, carrier, continuation, admission or current-authority fact.
RT.2 asks, at every admitted outer Return and subsequent actual inner use,
for independently justified **non-hole** evidence

```text
Phi(B_as).V(F_c,v_f,r_f,e),
```

including every retained actual decomposition, captures, ActualAdm and the
complete `P_F_c[B_as]` observation/development/future family. A relative
membership assumption or final-model inclusion is insufficient.

This attempt grants RT.1 and RT.3 only conditionally to isolate RT.2; it does
not prove their whole-domain coverage. All source operations, true immediate
guards, original dependent maps, entry interfaces and arm contracts remain
independent inputs. A false or missing one blocks the construction.

## 2. Established ordinary route and the first missing inference

The selected Step proof establishes the following route for independently
ordinary returned f. It applies the supplied original same-value comparison
to an ordinary certificate **before** embedding:

```text
M.V(A_f,f)
  -- ordinary VIncl action --> M.V(F_c,f)
  -- M = Phi(M) -----------> Phi(M).V(F_c,f)
  -- monotonicity M <= B_as -> Phi(B_as).V(F_c,f).
```

Roots, provider alternatives, live events, restrictions and all joint fields
are unchanged. This is precisely captured introduction §6's body-Call case;
the attempt does not reopen it or extend its input premise.

For a relative Return, the corresponding derivation stops here:

```text
B_as.Car(I_f,t)
  -- actual Force Return/restriction --> B_as.V(A_f,f)
  -- unfold outside exact holes -----> Phi(B_as).V(A_f,f)
  -- missing comparison action ------/ Phi(B_as).V(F_c,f).
```

The second unfolding is available when the **complete indexed A_f tuple**
is outside `H_a union H_s`, even if f's runtime identity happens to be apply.
It does not make that tuple an M certificate. Its captures, returned values
and future fields may depend on the relative assumptions. Applying ordinary
VIncl at this point silently assumes its extension to this background.

For complete callable endpoints, input realization §3.2 exposes exactly
what an evidence-producing extension would require. In addition to the
whole challenge inclusion, it needs a whole observation action

```text
for every d in D_F_c:
    P_A_f[B_as](d) -> P_F_c[B_as](d)
```

at the same tuple, preserving every hereditary field, provider and guard.
The corresponding ordinary action over `P_A_f[M]` and `P_F_c[M]` gives no
action on the larger relative input family. Positivity maps a front at a
**fixed descriptor** along relation inclusion; it does not change A_f into
F_c. A union-valued A_f likewise needs its full original decorated-value
action over this relative family, rather than selecting one callable arm.

This is the precise output-level gap for arbitrary non-hole f. It is a gap
in this constructor derivation, not a claimed impossibility theorem for
every possible future construction of the original comparison contract.
The candidate shared-clause identity route already appears in
[Shared-NonHole](2026-10-08-apply-shared-formal-interface-construction.md#5-conditional-non-hole-rt2-derivation).
Its conditional lemma is reused as prior work: no common A_f/F_c clause
identity or uniformly lifted action is established here.

## 3. Conditional transformer sufficient for the non-hole branch

Name the missing supplier without adopting it. A candidate constructor
transformer would provide, at every original actual use tuple,

```text
N_Z : Phi(Z).V(A_f,f) -> Phi(Z).V(F_c,f)
```

for the relation backgrounds Z required by the source proof, including B_as
and an enlarged postfixed candidate S. Its proof must preserve the entire
independently fixed challenge/history domain and all dependent fields.
Literal equality of the **complete clauses** is one sufficient special
case; endpoint spelling, shared IDs and equality of a printed skeleton are
not such evidence. For complete descriptions, whole domain and observation
actions constructed uniformly in Z are another sufficient case.

Conditional consequence: given this supplier at `Z=B_as`, a relative
returned A_f tuple outside the holes unfolds to its ordinary Phi(B_as)
constructor and N_B_as produces exactly the requested RT.2 front. This is
the existing Shared-NonHole argument with the missing hypothesis displayed.
No new theorem beyond its conditional scope is claimed.

## 4. The apply-hole case: a different constructor plan

For an exact H_a tuple, unfolding yields an assumption rather than a Function
front. Rewriting its descriptor, even by genuine identity, preserves that
assumption. The smallest circular dependency in the two-stage outer proof is

```text
construct step capturing actual apply
  -> inner Call needs apply's complete Phi(B_as) Function front
  -> current outer proof obtains that front through RelativeBlock
  -> RelativeBlock needs the same RT.2 apply front.
```

This is a minimized proof-dependency witness, not an asserted executable
source counterexample. It uses one admitted coincident f/apply demand; no
effects, pending histories or extra arms are needed to expose the dependency.
Those cases remain in the full obligation and cannot be discarded.

A different postfixed construction can avoid first proving each returned
step in B_a. Its candidate S contains B_as, the actual apply value and **all**
actual steps allocated by admitted apply invocations, their lawful
restrictions, and their original construction/invocation/result worlds,
carriers and complete finite observations. This family must be closed under
the actual retained operations and future demands. Restricting it to the
one designated H_s would leave fresh steps made by later apply invocations
without an available candidate value field.

The reason to try this enlargement is visible at the outer constructors:

1. Actual outer closure inversion supplies the same stored provider,
   ValueEntry(I_f), source/capture guards and carrier-contract equality.
2. Independent challenge admission supplies ActualAdm. Carrier progress
   unfolds its full relative input fields; pending Bind retains the original
   raw continuation and unfinished suffix.
3. At an actual Force Return, PhiW can construct the parameter world using
   the exact returned f's S.V tuple and all other bindings. No F_c readout
   is needed merely to bind f.
4. Lambda formation is inert. The created t can be placed in S.V(F_step,t)
   with all lawful restrictions. PureReturn and ordered Bind can use that
   **candidate value premise** at the RHS and final result ports, with the
   actual root and current world. The outer body does not invoke t or f.
5. Strict composes the original Force/rebind/body/invocation-return ports.
   Its hereditary recursive fields use S. Later latent demands must retain
   their full S fields and actual current-event guards.

Thus the outer *immediate* source composition can use returned step tuples
as candidate recursive premises instead of assuming final step membership.
This identifies a way to construct the apply source front within a larger
postfixed proof. It is not itself a completed `Phi(S)` front or a proof of
postfixedness: the whole result/future fields, every retained decomposition
and extra arm, and all added step/observation/world/carrier cases still need
their complete constructor derivations. Adding tuples to S supplies premises
inside a readout; it does not validate those tuples by itself.

If those complete derivations establish

```text
S <= Omega_as(S),
all exact H_a/H_s tuples have separately constructed Phi(S).V fronts,
```

greatest-fixed-point introduction gives `S <= B_as`. Positivity then maps
the **constructed** fronts to `Phi(B_as).V`, discharging the exact hole
readout obligation without invoking final apply membership. This is a
conditional inference from a genuine postfixed construction, not permission
to assume either displayed premise.

The outstanding step cases still read captured arbitrary f. Outside the
holes they need the missing N_Z action of §§2–3. An exact captured hole needs
its separately constructed source front; S.V membership alone cannot be
eliminated as Function realization. Mutual source-front equations or
construction induction must justify those uses, not rename them as a
supplier. No complete solution of that system is provided here. Consequently
the enlarged coalgebra establishes neither exact RT.2 nor OuterNat closure.

## 5. Stopping condition, evidence and recommended action

The ordinary unfold/embedding route and the relative non-hole unfolding
route both reach the same unsupported comparison premise when used for
arbitrary relative f. Further identity rewrites or a larger trace table would
leave that premise untouched. This attempt stops at the explicit N_Z
supplier and preserves the separate postfixed source-construction plan for
holes. It does not propose another equivalent executable probe.

Under compiler-engineering's proof-economy rules, the selected uniform
relative theorem is stronger than an ordinary independently supplied-f
Step theorem. Its canonical production relevance is unchanged. The
source-owned comparison certificate may be evidence retention or semantic
transport work; this artifact does not establish which facts that owner
already possesses. No source restriction, annotation requirement, new
language distinction, semantic weakening or gate reclassification follows.

Method: documentary constructor elimination and a minimized proof-dependency
witness. There is no executable oracle, no bounded semantic enumeration,
seed/range, mutation or performance experiment. The argument shares the
selected Phi, source rules, independent domains and guard contracts with its
dependencies; it does not independently prove those rules. Integrity checks
only inspect bytes, links and scope. Self inspection is not independent review.

Unverified scope: N_Z construction and uniform coverage; complete mutual
source fronts/postfixedness; RT.1 and RT.3; foreign interfaces/host embedding;
general recursion/State; production source acceptance, inference/principality,
full C0/CompleteMem/KV and cutover. No omitted challenge is claimed impossible.
The conditional argument fails on a changed background meaning, missing
provider/arm contract, invalid immediate guard, nonpositive hereditary clause,
lost dependent coordinate or a domain filtered by successful typing.

Recommended next action: require an evidence-producing source-owned
comparison derivation at the relative background, beginning with its complete
`P_A_f[B_as] -> P_F_c[B_as]` action. Keep the apply-hole source construction
as a distinct conditional method until that non-hole supplier is justified.
