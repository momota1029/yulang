## 4. The precise additional source/consumer equation

A faithful local construction needs a law at the actual source operation and
Row-demand consumer determining which head occurrences cover which fibers.
The required discriminator is:

```text
a head E in J covers f for this recipe's local subtraction
  only when the actual source annotation/call/output construction
  establishes f's participation in this exact attachment and route.
```

The association must be constructed, not assumed as a Boolean license.
An AnnotationMember tag alone is insufficient: operation views also produce
that endpoint variant. A family match, canonical row, present stack ID, or
independent choice of bound origin/context is insufficient.

If gamma is the designated surviving residual for the independent fiber,
the countermodel forces an additional consumer consequence:

```text
E in J and f is independent on this route:
    retain the same q_f -> g obligation with this fiber's exact context,
    authentic parents and recipe lineage, despite nominal head membership
```

Equivalently, a recipe-specific head decision would use both membership and
the established source coverage. For an unlicensed head match, it must supply
that surviving consequence or another proved live output route. This is a
necessary handoff condition, not an adopted replacement for ordinary Allowance
semantics and not a proved complete recipe rule.

Such a gamma emission would then be replayed to P using the actual ordered
inputs: first the source fiber and F context, then its gamma lower and P's
projection context. Native `candidate_context_begin_replay:2911–2982`
retains those children/parents. They must not be commuted, flattened by an
unproved context law, or chosen independently. The new constructor must also
justify that K's payload transformation and this delivery preserve the
same unlicensed contribution through the resulting contextual projection.
That semantic bridge is not supplied by merely enqueuing q_f-to-gamma.

Neither choice follows just from the operator formula.

This does not require another user vote on local subtraction policy. It
