rules supply their required form, but do not supply the arms or their checks.

## Conditional exclusion theorem

Fix one original assignment, whole scope tree, admitted history and incidence
map. Let `y` retain the entire observation/provider/witness tuple before `Pi`.
Assume the actual complete production grammar is exactly

```text
X = least S. { y | G(y) and
                 (R(y) or Z(y) or exists x,z. S(x) and W(x,y,z)) }.
```

Hypotheses:

1. This is the exhaustive finite-derivation grammar of §3.7, including all
   alternatives and rigid operands. No branch is hidden outside it.
2. `G` includes the independently meaningful ordinary descriptor and all
   original authority, scope, dependency, guarantee and provider constraints.
3. `I(y)` is a specified whole-tuple predicate, fixed independently of a
   membership query or the desired exclusion result. It retains the original
   typed transports. It is not raw endpoint-ID equality.
4. The following independent judgments hold on those same original fibers:

   ```text
   G(y) and R(y)                       => I(y)          Base
   G(y) and Z(y)                       => I(y)          Extra
   X(x) and I(x) and W(x,y,z) and G(y) => I(y)          Step
   ```

The `Step` premise uses only reachable predecessors. A practical local
certificate may strengthen it to all legal `I(x)` predecessors, thereby
avoiding reconstruction of reachability; that strengthening is not selected
semantics and is not necessary for the theorem.

**Conclusion:** `X subset I`. Induct on a finite membership derivation. A
source leaf uses Base; an unanchored leaf uses Extra; a rewrite uses the
inductively invariant predecessor and Step. Original fixed binders remain
at their original positions throughout. Since `G` already includes the final
descriptor, an additional descriptor conjunct cannot enlarge `X`.

**First-loss inversion:** suppose Base holds but a finite derivation reaches
`y_bad` outside `I`. Along its predecessor chain select the first node outside
`I`. It cannot be a source leaf. It is either a guarded `Z` leaf outside `I`,
or a guarded `W` target outside `I` whose predecessor satisfies `I`. Thus an
actual licensed counterexample must expose one concrete failing Extra or
Step instance. This is inversion of a supplied derivation, not an existence
theorem for such an instance.

For direct package exclusion, take `I_pkg` to require the returned package to
be the received package through the legal identity transport. The selected
actual Return gives the source-base case where those source laws apply.
No supplied governing section proves Extra or Step for `I_pkg`.

For the discriminator's future observation `1`, `I_pkg` alone is inadequate:
an abstract continuation may preserve the returned `zero` package while
adding a future observation under its wider complete contract. Excluding the
projected `1` requires an observation invariant `I_obs` and independent
judgments that every `I_obs` tuple fails to project to that result, including
all original-scoped witnesses and the fixed caller conjunction. That stronger
invariant cannot be obtained from provider identity alone.

Admission is absent from this induction because it is a fixed external
premise. The theorem proves no `Reach/Future` law and no equality of decoded
challenge domains. A provider change still needs the §3.7 domain certificate.

## Two exclusion shortcuts fail at their exact premise

**Same actual callable.** The selected contextual definition has the direction

```text
for every admitted challenge h of the same actual U,
  every actual complete/pending development of U satisfies Contract(h).
```

Schematically this is actual-development inclusion in the contract, with the
actual decomposition and all contextual indices fixed. It does not quantify
every contract alternative as an actual development of U. Inferring that
every production-bound result must be U's actual returned package would invert
this inclusion. Source `id` remains diagonal; an independently licensed
production extra would remain an abstract observation of its bound. This
distinction is explicitly preserved by the selected definition §4 and Option 2.
No change to the meaning of actual Function data is proposed here.

**Repeated universally instantiated q.** A schematic complete-value premise
has the form

```text
for every legal instantiation q and admitted input package p,
  actual-result membership is required in the same Val_q interface.
```

To derive identity from matching interface membership at a single use would
require the additional implication

```text
Val_q(p) and Val_q(r) => identity-transport(p,r).
```

No selected rule states this injectivity property. A universal scheme also
does not supply relational parametricity, naturality of observations across
different q assignments, or an admissible separating interface for every
unequal pair. The universe of legal complete descriptors is not declared to
contain arbitrary singleton value/provider predicates.

Even an independently available narrow instantiation would constrain its
own original fiber. Moving its exclusion to a different, wider q fiber needs
an actual cross-instantiation law on complete observations and provider
dependencies. Capture-avoiding transport of the same original assignment is
not that law. Thus neither universal notation nor repeated spelling supplies
Extra or Step at a broad common interface. This is an audit of missing
premises, not a countermodel assigning new meanings to q.
