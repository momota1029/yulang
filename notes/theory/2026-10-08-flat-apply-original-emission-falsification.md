## 1. Objective and result

Test the proposed inference from a genuine literal child, the complete
candidate Call package, conservative argument representation, original port
registration and lawful whole action to **actual original emitted membership**.
The inference fails in the bounded documentary fragment below, even after
granting a literal-capable argument coordinate. The missing premise is an
original source-owner introduction at that exact dependent fiber. O0/O1
consume its output and cannot supply it.

This attacks a stronger shortcut than the existing Name/Literal inversion
witness. The [argument-origin extension](2026-10-08-literal-call-argument-origin-extension.md)
already separates the two source constructors and proves the old-image
injection/retraction. Here the literal coordinate is granted, so neither
failure of a Name premise nor failure of that retraction causes the result.
The candidate package and its representation survive unchanged in the
counterinterpretation; only their alleged implication to original membership
fails. No defect in the correctly conditional extension is claimed.

Claim class: bounded non-entailment for the explicitly inspected rule fragment.
This is neither an admitted-program counterexample nor a complete alternative
Yulang semantics. It proves no global impossibility of original literal
emission and makes no claim that the displayed Name/Name constructor exhausts
all original constructors.

## 4. Why preservation and action do not close the cut

Projection equality is compatible with the table: a literal shape can have
every original index, demand, atom and registered port without having an
original introduction derivation. The failure is not a missing field value.
Retaining the child proof rules out replacing a literal by a Name; it does
not change the conclusion judgment of the candidate Call rule.

For any granted legal group of coherent source renamings, leave the complete
literal orbit without an original emitted derivation. Its complement can
retain all old orbits. The empty membership fiber is action-closed:

```text
Emitted(e) -> Emitted(theta(e))
```

is satisfied there because there is no such `e`. Equivariance and identity/
composition laws regulate supplied members; they do not establish existence.
This abstract orbit argument is conditional on granted lawful actions. It
does not compute or prove the missing source action laws.

Likewise Keep/Add preserves old record identity and stores all candidate
literal data, but neither constructor has an actual original emitted
conclusion. Rebranding `Add(p_L)` as that conclusion is precisely an original
family completion, not a consequence of its injection equations. H-E(ii)'s
authentic dependent output membership would rule out M-minus directly;
H-E(i)'s properly typed original installation and the rest of the embedding/
inventory laws are still needed for the complete proposed package. Merely
storing a candidate schema under an original-looking label is insufficient.

No reduced original membership was produced by this lane. The precise
blocker is the missing original source-owner introduction/interpretation at
`(j_L,LiteralOrigin(actual primitive))`, together with authentic installation,
all original attachments/atoms and lawful whole transport. Once supplied,
the selected O0 root/occurrence construction may consume its actual emitted
output; selected O1 additionally needs the independently justified flat seed.

The candidate-package repair and representation extension both leave this
premise untouched. A further probe that assumes literal membership would
evaluate the assumed bridge rather than establish it. Recommended different
method: construct and adjudicate the actual original introduction at its
owning source phase, then check its dependent conclusion and inventory.
