## 1. Result

The selected authority does **not** yet construct a sufficient transformed
public export. It does support a useful partial construction: remove total
derived coordinates by scoped substitution, retain the remaining joint
boundary dependencies, and freshen that boundary coherently at each incoming
use. This removes genuine construction detail without recovering it from F5
ordinals or from printed ports. A finite certified replacement of the complete
root would finish this route, subject to independent eligible-view coverage.

The missing supplier is a **source-derived finite abstraction certificate at
the actual designated transformed root**. For the selected captured local
`step`, it must replace the source-specific complete constructor presentation
by a reusable interpreted boundary schema, discharge its admission/membership
and ordinary-query obligations, and justify which remaining parameters can
freshen. The selected Step/Block/OuterTrace theorems supply actual closure
membership; they do not supply that abstraction or its instance coverage.

The earlier lossless export candidate cannot discharge this supplier: it
deliberately retains the entire source graph. PG-1's retained-root Direct
certificate also cannot establish a query at a changed root. This packet uses
the boundary-elimination method instead of another lossless-copy proof or
another experiment assuming a Generalize rule.

## 3. Precisely indexed inputs

Fix one finite component constructor presentation with original member and
export events, binder tree `B`, source roots `r_m`, resolved environment links,
and complete scoped relation `J`. `J` includes constraints and the evidence
relations actually needed by later consumers. If it is only a source-reference
relation, it is insufficient input for a production proof: the independent
production admission/membership clauses and Option 2 alternatives must also
be supplied at the roots being compared.

The following are hypotheses, not newly defined source inference rules:

1. **H-source.** The emitted relation and its independently interpreted
   constructor/primitive contracts are complete for the specified envelope.
   Every clause keeps its original source occurrence, ordered operands,
   provider/owner/receipt/path incidence, original indices and binder position.
   Original source roots remain available during construction and proof.
2. **H-boundary.** The actual Generalize event supplies its semantic eligible
   identities `Omega`, fixed anchors `F`, legal scope placement and original
   public operands. Lexical locality can derive a capture reference but cannot
   itself establish this partition. `F` contains the shared transitive
   environment/capture/import dependencies, including admission-live ones.
3. **H-cut.** Each elimination/rewrite used below has its finite local proof,
   complete free dependency list and admissible original scope. A changed
   complete root has a separately interpreted target, full admission and
   membership clauses, and an ordinary-query certificate at that target.
4. **H-use.** Independently legal uses retain their actual enclosing context,
   client constraints and source query/evidence observations. They do not
   obtain validity from the proposed export's query success. Any graft is
   uniformly legal at the supplied source sorts and scopes.

For a schematic existential block write `J(F,Omega,p,h)`, where `p` includes
all boundary operands/readable evidence and `h` is a proposed internal block.
This notation abbreviates the **original binder tree**. It does not license
`exists F`, hoisting dependent `h`, moving an outer choice below a universal
challenge, or binding every component-local coordinate together. Registered
recursive references retain their selected interpretation. Source roots and
source identity references are distinct from the transformed public root.

## 4. The finite partial construction

### 4.1 Find a cut using actual consumer incidences

Start at designated export operands and at every actual use-time admission,
membership, residual and evidence read. Follow their finite declared free
dependencies, including provider/future, world, profile and typed-path
references. Preserve a shared coordinate once over its whole incidence.

This gives a conservative retained set. A dependency can leave that set only
after a local elimination proof also updates every dependent consumer.
Unknown primitive dependencies are a missing input, not evidence of absence.
There is no claim that graph reachability finds a minimum interface or that
the currently inspected consumers cover all future language uses.

Original introduction evidence can remain producer-side proof material.
An ordinary use may retain stable source identity tokens and a derived
certificate it actually reads. It must not follow a token back into the
function definition to redo inference or reconstruct complete source clauses.
A pointer to the entire `J` consulted at every use fails q1/a1's target.

### 4.2 Remove a total derived coordinate

Suppose a supplied constructor extension, at one legal binder node, is

```text
J0(F,Omega,p,z) and d = t(F,Omega,p,z)
```

where `d` is fresh, `t` is a total well-typed expression at that node, and all
admission, membership and evidence occurrences of `d` denote this definition.
Substitute `t` at every occurrence and remove the fresh binder for `d`.
Totality is relative to the independently specified original sorts; a failed
partial computation is not a total definition.

**Derivation.** Given an original scoped witness, evaluate `t` there to extend
it uniquely on `d`; given an extended witness, forget `d`. Each dependent
consumer reads the same expression before and after substitution. These maps
preserve original evidence and every old dependency, and commute with legal
whole freshening/grafting. Induct over a finite acyclic sequence of definitions.
Recursive source proofs use their finite derivations and registered back
references, as source-joins §2 specifies; a cyclic defining equation alone
does not establish totality. No uniformity across impossible alternative
values of `d` is required.

This is an established scoped elimination method reused here. It removes
allocation/alias coordinates, not an arbitrary old hidden witness or the
code denotation of an unknown body. Inlining a source body into `t` can retain
its full size and therefore does not prove compactness.

### 4.3 Other internal witnesses and the public root

A genuinely old shared `h` may be hidden jointly at its original eligible
existential position only with the required admission certificate. Absence
from transitive admission dependencies is one sufficient check. The reviewed
linked-pair Coverage theorem is another route within its exact hypotheses.
These certificates cannot be replaced by independent membership/domain
marginals or by success at a retained hidden root.

After certified elimination let `K_P` denote the resulting boundary residual
and required evidence schemas. A candidate output is mathematically

```text
P = (displayable scheme, K_P, boundary dependencies/incidences,
     eligible/fixed scope data, designated transformed roots).
```

This is an obligation list, not an API, storage format or selected public
grammar. Dependencies shared between actual member exports stay joint. The
scope/eligible data is per actual export event; storing one component does
not force a new semantic binder block across all its members.

At each designated root `g`, H-cut must supply its **own** independently
interpreted complete admission/membership, with actual role, entry, consumer,
provider/future contract and Option 2 alternatives. A sufficient restricted
route is a finite equality of the complete admission clauses and the
appropriate complete membership comparison, plus the source-contracts §5.3
local Function rule. An exact complete-clause rewrite also supplies reflection.
General over-approximation needs its independent soundness and view-coverage
arguments; one-way source membership is insufficient.

The original `r_m` and `g_m` are distinct indexed proof operands. Retain their
correspondence in construction evidence. Every ordinary incoming query uses
`g_m`, never substitutes the old `r_m` as its actual operand. A constructor
certificate can later be discarded from the public result only after its
necessary conclusions have been compiled into `P`'s interpreted schemas.

Merely writing `K_P = exists h. J` is not this last step. It proves a logical
projection but can retain the full source relation verbatim. Displayability
and q1/a1 sufficiency require a finite boundary schema whose interpreted
operations and retained operands are justified independently, with no use-time
source traversal. Neither existence nor a size bound for that schema follows
from the projection notation.

## 5. Conditional use theorem and its exact premise

Let `A` be a finite sequence of the certified operations in §4. Let `J_u`
be the original legal instance relation under the supplied Generalize/use
interpretation and `K_u` the transformed one. Include actual whole-root query
and evidence relations in both, at their respective designated roots. Suppose
the cut certificate establishes their original-scope boundary relation
equivalence for those complete consumers. This last premise is H-cut; this
packet does not obtain it from a printed scheme.

For each distinct incoming event `u`, use one injective action

```text
theta_u(o) = tagged(u,o) for o in Omega
theta_u(f) = f           for f in F.
```

Apply it through every eligible declaration, residual, root operand, incidence
and dependent evidence field, keeping binder kind/order/dependency positions.
Repeated occurrences within one use have one image. Different use images
are disjoint. Fixed outer/world/capture inputs remain shared, and original
source anchors retain their same resolved meaning. Static use allocation
does not allocate a receiver activation, operation event, receipt or seed.

**Conditional theorem.** For any finite list of these independently supplied
uses, any legal uniform grafts and any unchanged joint client relation `H`
over their retained boundary operands, replacing every `J_u` by `K_u`
preserves the projected joint solution/evidence relation at the original
scopes. The public checker needs only the supplied `P` and client context.

**Proof.** Each certified local operation carries extension/forgetting or
rewrite maps over the same original tuple and binder tree. Their composition
preserves the complete consumers by H-cut. Whole transport gives the same
maps after `theta_u` and each uniform graft. Compose them for all uses while
fixing the common `F`. The maps preserve all retained boundary values, so
they preserve `H`. Shared hidden coordinates remain bound once over their
entire original incidence. Per-use owned witnesses have distinct images.
Apply the same approved observation/evidence projection to the resulting
joint relation. Neither direction chooses independent port witnesses or
changes a witness after inspecting a later challenge. QED conditionally.

The theorem proves preservation of **supplied** legal instances. It does not
derive H-boundary or show that every independently valid view is such an
instance. Natural inference/principality separately requires

```text
for every independently valid V and public v satisfying C_V:
  exists witnesses at their original binder positions:
    K_P^fresh(v,w) and Direct(g_P^fresh(v,w), R_V(v)).
```

The finite graph is fixed before public assignments and histories are chosen.
This is certified-use §5.2's actual-export extension clause; it is not a
definition of valid views. Identity isolation does not imply that separately
satisfiable uses remain satisfiable under arbitrary joint client constraints.
There is no theorem here that all permitted substitutions must succeed.
