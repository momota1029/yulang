## Why this cannot discharge independent-fiber hygiene

The all-entry transformation receives weight, removed nominal families and
parents. It preserves entry IDs and counts but receives no separately
established association of original lower fiber b with attachment i, actual
provider U, receipt r and output route. Its common-payload intersection is
not an intersection of authenticated contribution licenses. Row feedback and
exact projection arithmetic therefore do not establish clause 3 above.

The missing information has a small direct obstruction. Let a and b be
distinct fibers at the same family E under the same outer call/scope indices.
An operation receiving only their nominal image {E} and grant image {E}
cannot distinguish the indexed case where a is licensed and b independent
from the case where b is licensed and a independent: its inputs agree, but
the required surviving fibers are respectively {b} and {a}. A deterministic
function of those nominal inputs has one output and cannot realize both
indexed conclusions. This is an information-sufficiency refutation, not a
claim that either case is reached by the actual source, that Oracle deletes
b, or that every possible Y3 representation forgets b. Nominal outputs in
both cases may still print {E}; such equality cannot certify witness survival.

Preserving actual operand IDs and their joint source/route evidence can avoid
that obstruction. The current Y3 Contribution and AnnotationMember operands
already distinguish provenance at the checking boundary; their existence
does not establish the missing license or residual transformation. A family
set, equal type row, support view or balanced weight is insufficient evidence
for this indexed conclusion.
