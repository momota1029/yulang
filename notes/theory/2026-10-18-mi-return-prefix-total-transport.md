projection h factors as `(h o E) o L`; dependent projections agree after the
displayed transport along the round-trip equality. Consequently a coordinate-only map
that identifies distinct original whole witnesses cannot have this round-trip.
This elementary necessity applies directly to the requested transport; it
neither supplies `P_f` nor assumes its absent map.
