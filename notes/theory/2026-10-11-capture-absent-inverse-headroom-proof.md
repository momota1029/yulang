## Logical inverse classification

Proceed backwards through completed logical steps, with later steps already
restored. This induction hypothesis is stated explicitly; it does not assume
the still-open storage conclusion for an arbitrary whole history.

For `A(r)`, the post-step map contains `r`. Its saved `Some` snapshot therefore
restores an occupied key; a saved `None` snapshot removes the new key.

For `S(f,t)`, the post-step map contains `t` and lacks `f`. Undo visits the
destination snapshot first. A saved `Some` destination is occupied; a saved
`None` removes `t`. Since `f != t`, either operation leaves `f` absent. The
next snapshot restores the exact saved source at absent `f`. Later admissions
or splices do not change this classification after their own inverses finish.
Physical reserve-only steps preserve it as well. The inverse uses saved raw
u32 keys, without recanonicalizing them through the still-live forest.

Consequently the only absent saved-`Some` entries under this history grammar
are splice sources, exactly one per completed nontrivial splice. At every
occupied saved-`Some` position, `candidate_effect.rs:272` uses `get_mut` and
assigns the copied bucket. Hashbrown's lookup at `map.rs:1452` does not reserve
or grow the table. That branch is allocation-free under the stated scalar
hash/value premises. If condition (I) holds at every absent-source position,
the induction advances normally and proves the local capture-map conclusion.
The classification alone does not prove (I).

## Pinned library premise and cumulative result

Read-only metadata confirms rustc 1.95.0, commit
`59807616e1fa2540724bfbac14d7976d7e4a3860`, host
`x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`. Matching installed std source and lockfile hashes,
hashbrown 0.16.1 archive/source hashes match the reviewed
[exact library reduction](../progress/2026-10-11-capture-map-inverse-capacity-proof.md).
This pins the source semantics, not the linkage/features of every solver binary.

Write `n=items`, `g=growth_left`, and `c=full_capacity` of the allocated raw
table; public capacity is `n+g`, not generally `c`. HashMap insert invokes
`reserve(1)` before searching (`map.rs:1841`, `raw/mod.rs:1161`). At `g=0`,
`reserve_rehash_inner` (`:2781`) rehashes without backing allocation only when
`n+1 <= floor(c/2)`; otherwise it requests a resize allocation. This gives (I)
at the surviving absent-source insertion.

Removal increments `g` by `b in {0,1}` depending on EMPTY versus DELETED
(`raw/mod.rs:3249`). Insertion of an absent key decreases `g` by
`e in {0,1}` depending on the reused control tag (`:2499`). Occupied assignment
through `get_mut` changes neither `n` nor `g`.

The preliminary cumulative lemma derived before the scope amendment is:

```text
If g >= P at rollback entry, where P counts pending splice-source inverses,
then every absent-source inverse uses the positive-credit arm of (I).
```

Proof: destination/new-admission removals never lower `g`; occupied assignments
leave it unchanged. At a source inverse, `P >= 1`, hence `g >= 1` before
HashMap's reserve. Its insertion consumes at most one credit, while that
completed source inverse reduces `P` by one. Therefore `g' >= P'` holds.
This proves conditional sufficiency over the actual pinned collection
transitions; it is not a native invariant or an adopted reserve strategy.

For the forward owners, after their successful reserve, the exact credit
equations are `g_after = g_before - e` for admission and
`g_after = g_before + b - e` for splice. A completed splice adds one pending
source inverse. Current owners reserve one additional map entry; they establish
only `g_before >= 1`, without tracking pending splice inverses. They do not
establish `g >= P`. This observation neither disproves (I) nor makes that
stronger invariant necessary: actual removals, tombstone reuse or in-place
rehash may allow (I) even when `g < P`. Further reserve-count design is deferred
under the primary's amended packet; no implementation proposal follows.

The earlier reviewed conditional control-layout example still exposes the
surviving absent branch: one absent-destination splice may consume the last
credit; reversing its destination removal can leave a tombstone with no
refund; the source is absent and therefore still takes `insert`. The occupied
repair does not change that branch. No native row/order/RandomState/control
history realizing this layout in a qualifying route is established here.
The preceding occupied-key counterexample is not rerun or repurposed as an
absent-key witness.
