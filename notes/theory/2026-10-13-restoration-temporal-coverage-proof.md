## Frozen original obligation and bounded claim

The parent remains: for every finite successful native construction history,
each newly formed opposite-bound incidence pair has owned processing or an
independently justified completed/cache/transport disposition, preserving its
actual owner, both original admissions/fibers, ordered contexts and dependencies.
Original lexical scopes, binders, attachment/provider/witness identities,
correlated use maps and residual lineages remain fixed. Availability success
does not imply sound typing, publication readiness or discharged constraints.

This packet changes method from tracing one shifted restoration to accounting
for the complete captured Value-row block. Its **bounded operational claim** is:

For every finite authentic current capture G and successful completed FreshUse
restoration execution satisfying S below, every pair of captured lower and upper
records for the same Value owner, each carrying an actual original RelationId,
has at least one actual incoming-use Replay child joining their mapped fibers.
That child reaches the typed context/dispatch consumer and its completion
receipt, including when dispatch uses its current-relation cache. The joint
Replay dependency retains ordered lower/upper mapped relations and BoundKeys;
each mapped relation retains its original relation, source BoundKey and this
same use identity through FreshUse transport. The original snapshot/admission
references remain retained by the actual FreshUse admission causes.

S is a source-checkable constructor specialization, not a proposed restriction:

1. G is obtained by the current immediate capture route, not an arbitrary or
   delayed Graph. Every captured row is a local Value row. There are no shared
   nongeneric/older coordinates, Effect rows, Allowance/Support operands or live
   effect payloads; Function effect fields use the collected bottom/empty leaves.
2. At preparation entry the candidate has no retained extrusion parents and an
   empty typed worklist. The captured fibers and source contexts of the mapped
   callback closure are identity; no child-local source weight is registered
   there. There is no interleaved external source action or unrelated mutation.
3. One actual prepared FreshUse map is used at one use level k, with its authentic
   imported evidence. Execution of the entire restoration prefix succeeds and
   finishes. The claim does not prove termination or resource availability.

The all-local condition is checked from the actual captured `Row.local` fields;
the level equality and absence of callback-created lower direct prefixes below
are **derived**, not assumed. All hypotheses concern the native constructor
interface. A parsed source family satisfying them is not established here.
Pairs of physical duplicates deduplicated by capture are outside this bounded
record-level statement, while remaining in the original parent obligation.

This is not a theorem that the complete original admissions are authenticated,
that Replay is a sound complete Call judgment, or that all original contextual
dispositions are valid. Those premises remain open even in this subcase.
Broader shared-row, merge, nonidentity, Effect, source, soundness, principality
and F5 gates retain their original scopes and statuses.

## 2. Assign each captured pair to an actual later restoration

Let l and u be arbitrary opposite captured records of one owner, and let j be
the physical index of the mapped earlier endpoint when the later record is
inserted. This index exists because that earlier record has already emitted its
physical bound; duplicate endpoints do not erase the appended slot.

* If l is DL, l precedes every upper record. In u's restoration its direct lower
  slot has a fixed index j. Appending exact lowers or any upper bound cannot
  move it; no direct lower append occurs in callbacks. Saved N includes j.
* If l is EL and u is DU, u precedes l. In l's restoration, u's direct upper slot
  has fixed index j even when callbacks append other direct uppers. Saved N
  includes j. An exact-upper shift can occur elsewhere in this same restoration
  without affecting this selected direct upper.
* If l is EL and u is EU, l precedes u. During u's restoration the lower direct
  length D is constant, while existing exact-lower order is preserved. Thus
  l's index is the fixed value D + its exact-lower index j0 < N. Appended exact
  lowers come after l. This restoration selects l despite any upper-prefix
  shift that occurred during the earlier restoration of l.

These three cases exhaust captured Value lower/upper lane combinations. Every
restoration advances one index only after its child replay returns, and retires
only at its saved N (`candidate_extrusion.rs:697–759`). Therefore the indicated
lookup occurs. This assignment requires no preservation of every initial
opposite by **each** restoration: the previous authentic `[r',f1',f1']` trace
still omits f2 from the lower restoration and is covered by the later EU case.

## 3. The lookup really joins and consumes the two mapped fibers

Before physical admission of either captured record,
`begin_candidate_fresh_bound` (`candidate_scheme.rs:1221–1266`) creates its
mapped relation through this same prepared use. Fresh transport at
`candidate_context.rs:3192–3201` attaches the mapped relation to its mapped

admission additionally retains its imported snapshot and mapped relation in
its cause; `candidate_effect.rs:420–475` does not replace the mapped fiber.

At the assigned lookup the two mapped fibers are therefore present together.
Restoration forms the correctly ordered lower/upper BoundKeys and calls Replay
with `incoming_use=true` (`candidate_extrusion.rs:739–759`). This resets old heads
to empty, so the product includes this pair regardless of ordinary replay's
prior progress (`candidate_context.rs:2966–3019`). Each child records
`Dependency::Replay { child, lower, upper, lower_input, upper_input }`. Identity
context simplification retains that ordered dependency. If relation interning
coincides, both FreshUse transport dependencies and admission snapshots remain;
coincidence is not a proof that original admissions are identical.

Incoming-use replay does not skip an existing dependency at `:3015–3017`.
Its owned child is driven synchronously through the typed drain
(`:3055–3152`), reaching Transport, Context, Dispatch in that order
(`lib.rs:12259–12283`). Memo reuse requires the actual processing relation to
be completed at current intrusion generation (`lib.rs:11463–11474`); endpoint

and the terminal publishes the retained return receipt before popping the drain
(`lib.rs:12613–12633`). Restoration cannot advance before that return. This
establishes actual pair activation and consumer delivery, not just stored
