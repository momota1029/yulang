## 4. Finite owner-cut compression: conditional mathematical generalization

The following finite graph argument explains the rescue without requiring a
fixed chain length or an arbitrary lexical-level bound. It does not assume
higher source level always remains the owner after canonical merging.

Take a finite directed path of ordinary row inequalities between provider
interface rows. Each physical inequality is stored at one endpoint: upper v
owns positive lower u for u<=v, or lower u owns negative upper v. Assume all
path rows relevant to this lemma are local at Capture, representatives are
fixed for the argument, and every needed owner cut has **actually materialized
its valid derived constraint with both ordered source parents** (or a reflexive
step). This final assumption is not established universally below.

Let R be actual Capture reachability from the interface rows, expanding each
reached local row's stored lanes. If a maximal subpath has internal rows outside
R, its first edge is owned by its first outside row; otherwise expansion would
reach that row. Its last edge is similarly owned by its last outside row. Along
the intervening edges, ownership therefore changes from the right endpoint to
the left endpoint. At some outside row v, the adjacent inequalities u<=v and
v<=w are both owned by v: exactly `(v,+,u)` and `(v,-,w)`. The assumed actual cut
materializes u<=w, or removes a reflexive detour. Replace the two path steps by
that real child and retain their ordered binary provenance tree. Path length
strictly decreases. Finite induction removes every outside segment. The
remaining path has rows in R and each owner-expanded edge is captured. Every
eliminated source contribution remains a leaf of the ordered derivation; it
is not renamed to an unrelated witness/provider.

Apply to the parent: this is a way to prove the needed boundary inequality
without demanding an actual FreshUse endpoint for every internal source Name.
For each actual i, a captured path under its one real map proves that instance's
boundary constraint. It supplies no map for deleted rows and no equality of
original and copied endpoints. All finite path lengths are covered by the
mathematical induction **under the stated cut premise**; the original universal
source theorem is not replaced by that conditional statement.

The worked trace above establishes its one relevant cut from the actual source
constructors. General cut materialization with context/residual/scoped payload,
current/reused children, canonical transport and stopped anchors is the
remaining source/operational obligation. A retained recipe or relation ID,
physical row endpoint equality, finite tested path or successful observation
is not that premise.
