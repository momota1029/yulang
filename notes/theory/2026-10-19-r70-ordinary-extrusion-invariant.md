## 1. Parent and actual result

The parent is the original type-inference/evaluator goal with unchanged source domain and independent quantifiers, including Simple-sub A/B. R70 asks only for successful entry-to-return behavior of `candidate_extrude` from `dispatch_live_item`'s two production branches under `shadow-apply-candidate`. Simple-sub A/B remain OPEN 0→0; R50 remains NOT ADOPTED.

Normative authority is top-level inference principles §§1–3: ordinary constraint generation/accumulation/propagation, levels, extrusion, qualifying intrusion and generalization stay intact. The effect-view/evidence/parent/replay assertions here describe current implementation, without silently adopting an additional semantic rule. No legacy bypass is restored.

**Conditional result.** Given the entry well-formedness predicate W below and successful checked helper operations, this operation terminates its local work, returns its root's kind-preserving map image, retains one actual same-kind level-L copy per visited deep `(endpoint,polarity,L)` key, retains the actual source links and visit-time selected bound copies, reconstructs all four Function ports with their variance, consistently maps views/tails, transports each observed source fiber, and records exactly its parent suffix/output group. Its final incoming-allowance pass creates retained ReplayOperations for the actual opposite-bound/fiber products and transfers their children through the typed worklist ownership protocol. It does not execute those children or acknowledge them.

W is **not independently established for every production-reachable entry by this packet**. The callers derive the envelope in §2, but do not themselves validate all reachable ordinal/term/view/incidence/evidence references. Thus the requested operation target remains OPEN; this note is a usable conditional lemma plus a precise smaller source-construction premise, not a theorem with that premise silently removed.

## 3. Mutable machine and invariant

Use kind-qualified `E`; `k=(E,p,L)`. Write R for `rows`, S for `structure`, V for `view_remap`, Wk for `work`, Pb for `pending_bounds`, and I for `initialized_allowances`. State sigma includes the mutable row bounds/levels, forest, arena, views, incidence, origins/fibers/evidence, Parents/groups/by_output, ReplayOperations and outer typed worklist. Ghost records below specify a simulation, not proposed production structures.

For each first deep-row Visit record `(k,copy,snapshot,link,parent_record)`. Snapshot contains the selected direct/atom prefixes **at that Visit**, preserving physical order and duplicate entries. For every scheduled Bound/IncomingAllowance record its exact original BoundKey, mapped source/key, and completion state. For each structural frame record original endpoint/terms and child keys. For every replay record its actual input keys, captured fiber heads/prior heads, ordered retained child vector, queue cursor q, consumed cursor c and ack owner.

The loop invariant is the conjunction:

1. **Stable identities, growing state.** Canonical representatives and existing row levels remain fixed throughout this call; fresh rows have level L and empty initial bounds. Existing bound slots/term nodes/views are not overwritten. Bounds, fibers, evidence and capture records may append. No typed dispatch, intrusion merge/settlement or graph reinitialization occurs inside the operation. Hence an earlier canonical key does not acquire a different representative. Forest extension in `retain_extrusion_parent` only appends singleton identities.
2. **Partial maps and sharing.** R and S have disjoint row/nonrow domains; each completed key has one image of the same kind. A row at level<=L maps identically, with no selected-bound traversal or parent allocation. A first row at level>L maps to one fresh same-kind row at exactly L, before scheduling descendants. Later Visits to that exact key are no-ops; opposite signs are distinct keys and may have distinct copies. No map survives this invocation.
3. **Mutable snapshot accounting.** Each deep row Visit snapshots selected list lengths before its own opposite-side source link. That link cannot modify the selected side. During the immediate scheduling loop helpers only reserve scratch; its prefixes remain stable, so every selected slot schedules one `Visit(child)` and one `Bound(copy,p,child,original BoundKey)`. Earlier opposite-sign source links may already lie in this snapshot and are retained. No claim replaces these snapshots by the entry graph.
4. **Continuation completeness.** Every unresolved discovered dependency is represented by a Visit, Function/EffectView continuation, or Bound/IncomingAllowance item. Wk is always popped before Pb. A Function/EffectView continuation lies below its structural child Visits on Wk; structural DAG descent finishes those children before the continuation. Row cycles are cut by immediate R insertion. Bound discovery waits in Pb; a popped Visit either installs a row image immediately or places its structural continuation on Wk, which finishes before the waiting Bound can pop. Therefore every popped non-Visit reads completed child mappings. Repeated completed keys skip safely.
5. **Physical bound/evidence correspondence.** Each completed Bound installs its selected p side on its mapped fresh owner and transfers actual original fibers with the exact Extrusion witness `(initial,polarity,L)`. Each IncomingAllowance resolves its positive source key and installs a Negative Allowance on that mapped source using V and the actual copied tail. It transfers the original incoming capture's fibers, then appends the canonical installed key to I. `candidate_insert_bound_with_evidence:499–671` reserves admission/emission capacity, appends the correct kind/lane, reads its actual slot back, checks equality, registers capture if applicable, marks dirty and emits the actual admission/physical-slot certificate. Physical duplicates are retained; no slot is removed or replaced.
6. **Evidence distinction.** `candidate_transfer_bound_origins:677–718` follows the captured source fiber cursor, retaining each parent via `candidate_context_transport_witness_with_evidence:4091–4105`: post-check context, exact sources, transported scoped payload, Transport dependency and FiberTransform. The source BoundKey in Work::Bound retains the raw selected bound (`candidate_extrusion:157–164`), while its child map key is canonical. `candidate_context_canonicalize_bounds:4005–4023` transports to canonical keys and retains raw originals. This invariant quantifies only fibers actually attached to the exact raw source key; equality or coverage of all canonical-key fibers after prior merges is **unproved**. It neither asserts loss nor reconstructs absent fibers from endpoint equality. Its separate origins loop admits diagnostic metadata with `MissingOriginAssociation`. In scoped mode copied bootstrap uses BoundTransport with relation None (`candidate_extrusion:416–430`, `candidate_effect:531–543`); only actual fiber transport supplies executable copied fibers. Unscoped bootstrap may create an OwnerEmission fiber. These cases are not conflated. A successful helper call does not make an explicitly missing association complete.
7. **View identity.** V keys `(old_view,mapped_tail)`; equal keys share exactly this operation's view, different old IDs are never equated by equal allowed lists. A view reconstruction preserves Support/Allowance tag and AnnotationMember ordinal and uses the mapped tail. Closed views return unchanged. `candidate_copy_effect_view:1099–1122` validates payload when present, retains owner/position/allowed/provenance/attachment source and reconstructs through `candidate_signature_view`. It does not preserve old view ID, or conflate a source attachment identity with a residual/provider/witness identity.
8. **Parents and output.** Every actual fresh row has one Parent for this occurrence, exact parent/copy row kinds, sign and target, with saved active sources/comparison. Qualified source events append its parent index to pending intrusion; parent provenance is deliberately absent from dependency adjacency. All earlier Parents remain. No settlement/merge occurs yet. `parent_start` fixes precisely this invocation's suffix. Output/group construction is deferred until both local stacks empty.
9. **Replay ownership.** Before the final pass no local helper executes replay children. At every replay transfer frontier, `0<=c<=q<=len(children)`; `[0,c)` is acknowledged, `[c,q)` is transferred and remains owned by queued/current/drain acknowledgement records, `[q,len)` remains solely in its retained ReplayOperation. Child identities and both input fibers/keys remain retained. The vector's existence, queue transfer, later execution and acknowledgement are four separate facts.

**Initial state.** Maps/stacks/I are empty, an occurrence is allocated, and root Visit is placed on Wk. The invariant's map/write/replay claims are vacuous. W and the caller envelope supply the identity/owner premises; they are not inferred from vacuity.

**Visit steps.** Completed-key reuse preserves the invariant. Anchors insert identity. A deep row uses `fresh_*_at_level`, retains Parent, inserts R, writes `(original,opposite(p),copy)` and schedules visit-time selected slots. For positive Effect rows only, it additionally traverses the original tail's actual capture bucket and schedules each incoming source Visit and IncomingAllowance. These are physical incidence records, not guessed Allowance membership. All writes have exactly the owners listed above.

**Function steps.** `candidate_extrusion:229–258` makes Argument and ArgumentEffect children at opposite(p), ResultEffect and Result children at p, in respective Value/Effect/Effect/Value kinds. On continuation (`343–370`), unchanged children reuse the original Function. Changed row/Function endpoints reify at each child's sign and the constructor selected by p validates all four children before interning. Atoms are never changed and therefore never require the intentionally partial `candidate_endpoint_term` (`393–409`). This proves port preservation without assuming term IDs remain distinct after interning.

**EffectView, Bound and IncomingAllowance steps.** Resolved children exist by item 4. EffectView requires a same-kind row tail, remaps through V and preserves endpoint tag/member. Bound installs selected side plus evidence and transports actual source fibers. IncomingAllowance installs/records only after its origin transfer succeeds. These steps preserve items 5–7, including duplicate slots and explicit gaps. None processes outer queued work.

## 4. Why source mutation does not invalidate this simulation

The valid statement is about visit-time snapshots. For a Positive Function whose argument and result name the same high Value row v, let both entry sides be empty. DFS first visits `(v,-,L)`, makes n and writes `n <: v`, placing n in v.lower. Later `(v,+,L)` snapshots that lower side, makes p and writes `v <: p`; its Bound installs `n <: p`. Omitting this synthetic snapshot entry would lose an actual generated obligation. This concrete trace was independently supplied by the complementary counter-reading lane and also follows directly from the four-port schedule/source-link code. It refutes an entry-only immutable snapshot proof, not the implementation.

There is nevertheless a finite discovery envelope under W. Each original row of level>L is expanded at most once per sign; at most two copies per such original row are born. Every generated row has level L, hence any later Visit is an anchor. The only writes to an original high row during local construction are its own opposite-side source links: Bound writes to fresh level-L copies; IncomingAllowance writes to a positive source image, either a level-L copy or an unchanged level<=L anchor. In particular an original high row's selected prefix may include its earlier opposite-sign source link, but cannot acquire arbitrary copied-bound mutations.

Every new capture record made by these constructors has a tail at level<=L: copied negative Allowances are remapped through tail Visits, and incoming Allowances explicitly use a copied tail. Source links are row-to-row, not Allowances. Thus no new record is appended to a high-tail bucket while the positive copying Visit traverses that bucket. Existing high-tail chains are stable and finite under W. Fresh views/functions are installed on copies/anchors or retained as output; they are not discovered by recursively traversing those anchors' bounds. Structural discovery therefore remains in the finite entry term/view closure plus generated row anchors. This is the source-mutation argument needed for map termination; an immutable-whole-state premise would be false.

## 5. Return and actual replay frontier

When both stacks are empty, item 4 gives root in R or S, same kind as initial. `finish_candidate_extrusion:368–386` is called before replay. If no Parents were appended it creates no group. Otherwise active comparison is Some(T.pair) on this route; it stores the exact `(comparison,output)` index and PairGroup interval `[parent_start,parents.len)`, occurrence and qualification-derived activation. Source-event qualification determines pending intrusion; this note does not prove that it corresponds to the intended source Value SCC criterion on every path.

The final loop (`candidate_extrusion:383–385`) visits every I entry after all selected copies and origin transfers complete. A fresh negative IncomingAllowance cut is paired with every physical positive opposite currently on that mapped source. On a fresh positive copy these include all completed selected lowers; on a reused anchor they include its existing lowers. Negative copies receive selected uppers only. Positive copies without incoming cuts receive lowers only. Source links target original high rows. Therefore every opposite pair involving the initialized copied bound sides that needs this final sparse pass has an IncomingAllowance upper; the pass does not claim to replay all original-high-row source links.

`candidate_replay_bound:867–909` snapshots opposite count, uses the actual ordered lower/upper BoundKeys and creates `candidate_context_begin_replay(...,false)`. No local operation subsequently drains the queue or changes row representatives/bound fibers during this final pass. The replay builder (`candidate_context:3773–3853`) snapshots fiber heads and old replay heads, enumerates new-lower × all-upper and old-lower × new-upper quadrants, retains exact ordered Replay dependency/inputs, unions their source sets, composes their scoped payloads, and interns the resulting relation/context. It rejects a lower demand or invalid scoped combination rather than silently dropping it. Equal resulting RelationIds do not merge distinct lower/upper dependency parents. Already-recorded exact dependencies can suppress new children; origin counts are not fiber counts.

The universal transfer invariant is the prefix statement in item 9. For the **successful complete call** here there is a stronger fact: a newly created nonempty operation starts q=c=0, and `candidate_context_drive_replay(None):3985–4002` repeatedly enqueues while a next child exists. `Queued` occurs only once no next queued child remains while c<len (`3963–3967`). A strict prefix q<len can occur at an intermediate transfer or propagated error, with the suffix still retained by the ReplayOperation; it is not successful exhaustion of this pinned whole drive. `enqueue_item:13000–13020` reserves evidence/queue/undo before `transfer_replay_child`, then uses infallible push_back. Queue records carry exact relation and `(operation,index)` acknowledgement; replay is not reconstructed from endpoints. Final replay children are admitted before the caller can enqueue its rewritten Value, but this is not a descendant-completion barrier. Actual later owners are `step_typed_drain:12369–12450`: pop retains current work and prepares acknowledgement, Transport/Context/Dispatch process it, empty queue can run `settle_candidate_intrusion` (`candidate_intrusion:568–596`), and diagnostics precede terminal acknowledgement. `candidate_step_typed_drain_terminal:12777–12797` calls `complete_replay_child` sequentially and restores enclosing processing only at ReturnReady. `complete_replay_child:2345–2352` checks exact consumed index; `acknowledge_replay:2298–2304` advances c and retires only at vector end. Finally `sample_f4_resources` succeeds, local containers are dropped and their scratch charge is removed (`candidate_extrusion:386–391`), then the mapped root returns. Returned endpoint retention does not erase Parents, evidence, views, operations or queued work.

## 6. Separate finite cost dimensions

All bounds are for this single successful local operation under W, not whole inference. Let H be the number of entry kind-qualified rows above L, R0 the total entry rows, B0 the total entry physical bound slots, T0 the finite structural entry term/view closure, and C0 the entry capture records in copied high-tail buckets. At most 2H fresh rows/Parents are allocated. Discovery sees at most the original rows plus those target-level anchors, two signs each; no more than 2B0 selected initial slots plus 2H synthetic source-link slots is a deliberately conservative bound. Each high-tail bucket is inspected once on its positive copy, giving at most C0 incoming items. Structural DAG work is memoized per key; four Function child edges and one view-tail edge bound local scheduling. Accordingly map/item **counts** are finite in R0,H,B0,T0,C0. These are not worst-case constant-time hash-map runtime bounds.

For sharper actual dimensions define K=completed row keys, F=Function continuations, Vt=view continuations, B=selected Bound items and A=IncomingAllowance items. Visit pushes are at most `1+4F+Vt+B+A`; non-Visit pushes at most `F+Vt+B+A`. Map entries and scratch are bounded by actual completed keys/items, including duplicates in I. V contains at most Vt+A entries; allowed-list copying costs the sum of lengths/payload sizes of actually new copied views, not O(1) per view.

Evidence transport has separate cost `E = sum f(source_i)` and `O = sum origins(source_i)` over all B+A transfers, using actual source fiber chains at each transfer. `attach` linearly scans an existing destination fiber list (`3155–3161`) before deduplication; dependency/context/source-set/scoped-payload/evidence interning and capacity/journal work add their actual costs. No unsupported O(E) total runtime follows merely from E loop iterations, and explicit MissingOriginAssociation metadata remains metadata.

For final replay let D be the sum of physical opposite counts over A I visits and Q the set of distinct ordered input BoundKey pairs so visited. Because the final pass adds no bound fibers, the fiber-product candidate count is at most `P = sum_(L,U in Q) f(L)*f(U)`; prior replay heads may reduce it. Duplicate physical visits still cost D and create up to D retained operations, including empty ones. Replay child construction/retention is at most P entries; actual typed transfer is at most that many and obeys the separate q frontier. Source unions, sorting/hashing, scope composition, evidence recipes and attachment lookup are separately charged; no linear/global complexity assertion is made.

Term work is at most four endpoint reifications and one Function constructor call per changed Function continuation. These can intern equal nodes. Arena allocation, page/directory growth, journals and interner hashing remain their owning costs (`term.rs:1062–1098`); identity preservation of nominal terms is not claimed. Later drain work is **unbounded by this lemma**: it may create more terms/rows/fibers/constraints and requires its own progress proof. Resource exhaustion is propagated; it does not license dropping obligations or returning an altered result.
