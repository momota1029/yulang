## 1. Objective and result class


from a source body/closure derivation to two already selected semantic
constructors with a joint operand/interface boundary. The Lambda, local Bind,
Name syntax tree and finite source observation proofs are producer evidence;
an ordinary instance must not revisit them.

There are three different claims.

1. **Established selected result:** Step/Block/OuterTrace introduce the actual
   captured step, its installed immutable world, and complete ordered entry
   observations under their stated independent inputs. No step membership is
   assumed. The selected Strict/ReadInvoke meanings include complete admission,
   pending prefixes and future-provider obligations.
2. **Conditional finite cut, derived below:** a finite source-independent
   presentation of the constructors' full operand dependencies and evidence
   actions gives complete descriptor correspondence at `g_step`, a finite
   complete-clause comparison, and matched ordinary use queries. The missing
   dependency/evidence supplier is stated in §3, not inferred from Step.
3. **Unproved:** an actual source Generalize rule emits that boundary with an
   independently justified eligible/fixed partition, all necessary residuals,
   and every independently legal view factors through it. Neither a compact
   scheme grammar nor a production resolver is selected here.

The candidate boundary is an obligation tuple, not a storage layout. It does
not establish that printing an arrow or serializing IF is sufficient. Its
finiteness is a finite schema graph, not a finite enumeration of worlds,
carriers, future clients or operation histories.

## 5. Construct the candidate cut at g_step

Under H-factor, instantiate the existing selected constructors using b:

```text
R_b = ReadInvoke(F_c,D_c,IF_c restricted to its supplied used maps)
g_step = Strict(I_x,x:A_x,R_b,IF_step restricted to its supplied used maps).
```

“Restricted” here means remove a field only with its complete dependency and
consumer elimination proof; it never narrows a challenge/observation domain.
With no such field elimination, use the same complete semantic IF operands.
The designated `g_step` is a new public descriptor root whose definition is
this constructor term. It is not a pointer to r_step or the original J.
This is a mathematical candidate at the assigned gate, not adoption of an
emission rule or proof that current compiler exports it.

The finite syntax consists of the two generic constructor applications,
their supplied finite interface/schema references and joint operand map.
Schema definitions may quantify universally over all original challenges and
histories. Cyclic contract references are registered once, not unfolded.
The local body code `call(result(name f),result(name x))` and its ME derivations
are unnecessary for invoking those generic interpretation clauses *if*
H-factor holds. If an IF/formation reference still requires source traversal,
this is an unfinished cut, irrespective of the printed size.

Do not define g_step membership as “the observations generated by this step”.
Its meaning is the selected Strict constructor for any Value-entry callable
realizing its full independently supplied interfaces. Its embedded ReadInvoke
is the selected interface over any immutable callable realizing F_c. The
candidate therefore reuses independent constructor meanings rather than
manufacturing a source-success denotation.

## 6. Complete clauses and evidence correspondence

Let D_S/M_S denote complete descriptor admission/membership at the selected
F_step, and D_g/M_g those at g_step. These are descriptor-root clauses, not
the complete source-emitted relation J. Under H-factor their correspondence
is derivable by the following finite cases.

### 6.1 Admission

Strict admission is its fixed complete I_x/IF_step challenge formation:
registered tested-hole data, valid other fields/context, I_x whole-carrier
admission and original receipt/path preconditions. It does not require the
tested value's membership, actual-provider acceptance or desired output safety.
D_g keeps precisely these predicates, operands and original binder positions.
The leaf correspondence is identity or the supplied evidence-preserving
factorization law. Constructor congruence gives `Eq(D_S,D_g)`.

At the later body phase, D_c is retained with the same independent punctured
context and whole carrier. This is a dependent suffix domain after actual
callee Return/carrier formation; it is not an early admission witness and is
not replaced by an execution image. Any changed admission-live coordinate
must use its separately supplied arm/domain law.

### 6.2 Ordered observation/member cases

The following list is finite proof syntax for the universal clause families.
Every row preserves the whole original tuple and its evidence.

| Actual phase/case | Correspondence at g_step |
| --- | --- |
| Initial, zero-step, administrative or pre-receipt | Original world/guards; suspended Force/rebind/body/return/future. No x, Delay or inner challenge asserted early. |
| Entry Force progress | Arbitrary independently admitted whole I_x carrier with every current-world, request, response and raw-continuation family. Its effects remain even when body read is pure. |
| Entry Request or divergent finite prefix | Actual raw handle and current state, with remaining suffix `rebind-x; ReadInvoke; invocation-return`. Resumption uses its live state and never replays receipt/completed entry. |
| Actual entry Return | Same x/root/provider at A_x, actual post-entry world and original typed rebind; only now instantiate body at that dependent tuple. |
| Early pure callee read | Same captured f and original binding/formation/path guards; suspended carrier/receiver suffix. No lookup forces a latent f. |
| Callee Return, then inert carrier formation | Same provider/root/current world; actual Delay(Name x) on the diagonal or original independent open filling with its own certificate; assemble D_c at that actual event. |
| Receiver entry, pending/body/native-consumer prefixes, raw Resume | Same original U_f and complete P_F_c(d_c), ordered IF_c maps and unfinished suffix. No second receipt/entry and no replacement world. |
| Body Request after x entry | Only unfinished body continuation and outer invocation-return remain; typed rebind-x and its Force are completed. |
| Final invocation Return | Same actual result/root/provider with its own dependent future contract; remove only the actual owned occurrence by the original return action. Do not restore exited apply. |
| FutureUse of step or returned latent value | Step uses its same capture at the later live event; a value returned by f uses that value's own future/provider field. Neither is executed merely by Return. |

For each row, unfold the selected Strict or ReadInvoke clause once. Their
non-child operands agree via b; their children agree by the matching complete
interface leaf or previously matched constructor. Reuse the original Bind,
Return/Request, typed rebind and consumer constructors in their original order.
Thus Eq-C builds `Eq(M_S,M_g)` and its evidence correspondence from the same
local leaves. Infinite finite-history families are universally quantified
schema parameters; no individual trace is enumerated by this finite proof.

Semantic correspondence of hereditary fields follows from equality of the
same selected complete descriptor constructor/parameters. It does not use
§5.3's least-finite source recursion rule as a proof of a greatest-fixed-point
hereditary interpretation. Conversely Step's coinduction proves actual value
membership, not arbitrary finite source-root equality. These are separate
arguments.

The maps are identity on actual provider/world/handle fields and the supplied
scope-preserving action on descriptor roots and static incidence fields.
Their inverse restores any total eliminated field by its original definition.
If a removed old witness has nonunique values, a separate original-scope
hiding/coverage certificate is required; total-coordinate inversion cannot
supply it. A failure of H-factor leaves that particular Eq/evidence leaf open.
Bare semantic assertions of equality are not §5.3 certificates.

Combining selected Step with descriptor correspondence yields actual step
membership at g_step and the same installed world, conditional on H-factor.
It does **not** yield `J equivalent K_g` without H-emission. A typed source
example can have two different complete source residual relations with the
same one realized closure; membership cannot distinguish them.

## 7. Ordinary query cases at the designated root

No incoming query substitutes the source Lambda/root r_step for g_step.
Use `g_step^theta <: T` with the original complete descriptor terms as inputs.
The available finite proof cases are exactly these restricted ones:

1. **Same complete constructor instance.** Under one legal scope map, T has
   the same Strict/ReadInvoke heads, role/entry/consumer, I_x and all complete
   non-child operands. Identity/factorization Eq leaves and Eq-C establish
   equal admission and complete membership; Eq used as Le gives §5.3 Function.
2. **Matched provider guarantee enlargement.** T has the same complete
   admission, invariant/consumer/future-input fields and whole provider. Only
   a supplied `Cov(E,A)` changes its allowed guarantee. The Guarantee/Absorb
   leaf, placed in the original positive constructor positions, supplies Le.
   A negative child or changed future domain needs Eq or a genuine separate
   law; a child success is never composed into a parent success.
3. **A different complete client view.** Form the ordinary actual-root query
   and retain its joint residual, but no success is claimed unless its own
   full equal-domain/member Le DAG satisfies Function or another independently
   justified ordinary resolver rule. Matching displayed input/output types
   is insufficient. Arbitrary result endpoints need same-value/whole-result
   comparison. Independent A_step/F_apply are not overwritten by g_step.

Even cases 1–2 require H-query to conclude ordinary Direct success. Without
it the packet is an exhibited semantic/certificate derivation, not production
query behavior. Neither query success nor formation of the use graph proves
all-view extension E_V. These cases supply a specific actual-root comparison
for the matched client envelope, not complete concrete-query decidability.

## 8. Fresh uses, internal roots and publication

For each supplied incoming use event v, take one legal injective action theta_v
on the whole eligible boundary and residual/evidence tuple, fixing F. Repeated
occurrences of A_x or an eligible contract port receive one image throughout
entry, body, result and future; different incoming uses have disjoint images.
Capture root/provider and non-generic outer dependencies remain shared.
Dynamic receipts, receiver activations and actual worlds are introduced by
execution, not by static type freshening.

An optional admissible descriptor graft acts uniformly at all occurrences
and retains binder kind, order and dependency position. The descriptor/leaf
Eq proof in §6 transports under that same action, hence the finite query proof
in §7 refers to g_step^theta_v. The complete joint instance residual and client
constraints remain conjunctive. Exact preservation of the emitted relation
requires H-emission; exact public-view coverage additionally requires
certified-use §5.2's E_V at this actual root for every legal view in the
claimed envelope.

An internal reference before component completion uses the existing live
member root. It does not receive an incoming-use fresh copy. The selected
sequential local Name step returns the just-bound same value; it is not a
second source definition or an independently chosen root. This note does not
infer a recursive component layout from the acyclic selected source.

For an enclosing component, construction/checking of g_step is provisional.
Every member's designated export and shared obligations must be certified
before any becomes visible; failure leaves none published. This is the
charter's barrier, not a proved production lifecycle implementation. A local
Step proof cannot license visibility while another member or an admission
leaf is unresolved.
