## Local facts applied to R70

**Aligned allocation.** A successful fresh-row call takes its ordinal from
the corresponding bounds length, reserves every parallel append, and appends
exactly one bounds/level/metadata entry (and its mark). Initialized rows are
constructed densely by kind before fact admission. Thus these two row owners
preserve alignment and produce in-range ordinals; changing source levels does
not change row-table lengths. This removes fresh allocation itself as a
missing supplier for R70's row-alignment component. It does not validate an
arbitrary `live_variable` ordinal or an unaudited component-position producer.

**Exact-key fiber integrity.** In non-test forward construction, `attach(k,r)`
either finds r already on k or appends at fresh index n with next equal to k's
previous head. All next indices are smaller; every link belongs to the same
key, hence traversal terminates and retains every earlier member. A replay
head saved from that chain remains a suffix after later attachment.
Canonicalization uses the same attachment constructor and does not unlink raw
members. Completed context rollback restores replay-head log entries and
removes the appended bound-key suffix in reverse, restoring prior head maps.
Test-only arbitrary chain mutations at context:1918–1950 are not constructors
in this domain. These facts supply R70's finite cursor/suffix sub-premise for
the actual attachment/rollback owners, subject to valid retained relation
inputs. They supply neither required-fiber generation nor a global rollback
or progress theorem; R142 handles its exact abort/ownership boundary.

**Capture-chain integrity.** Initially buckets/records are empty. Fresh record
append extends one bucket at its old tail and owns the fresh index uniquely.
With valid distinct forest roots, splice joins two disjoint bucket chains and
removes the old bucket; no record becomes reachable from both live buckets.
Recorded inverse links/buckets and record truncation restore this property on
completed rollback. This establishes the local finite incidence invariant
used by positive Effect Visit, under the source/view/tail validity premises.
It does not say that canonicalized incidence source has the needed raw fiber.

**v1 field members.** The actual lower-view lookup requires a closed Operation
view whose allowed slice has exactly one retained family, so Member(id,0)
has a valid member ordinal. The upper comes from the prepared q view retained
only after construction of the q row and successful constraint admission.
The final field action checks matching handler-argument coordinates and only
marks consumed after its actual typed constraint call. This removes an
unjustified blanket omission of operation-v1 from the constructor inventory;
its full preservation proof still depends on the shared open envelope.

These are partial applications to W. None removes the conjunction's remaining
clauses or promotes R70's conditional operation result to an unconditional
