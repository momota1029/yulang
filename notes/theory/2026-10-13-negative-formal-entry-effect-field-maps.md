from a written composed-negative Function effect selector to a dependent
field of the **actual formal callable's** complete invocation interface.

publication. An incorrect concrete lower requires its authentic check/error
and safe nonpublication. This note neither restricts H to candidate-admitted
programs nor proves H for the discriminator below.

Fix the actual outer source Lambda `f`, its formal binder `b`, the actual
resolved annotation `a`, a Function node in that annotation and its immediate
result-effect selector `h`. The simplest written shape is `b: (int -> [E] int)`;
this is a selector example, not an accepted test program. Retain its member
ordinal `m`, resolved concrete effect identity/arguments `E_m`, concrete set

to be `f` or `b` by type shape.

Keep the original whole indices and source telescope:


each retained actual decomposition `Act(v_b,U_b,r_b)`, not the enclosing `f`.

The required original conclusion would be a source-owned paired
checking/exposed correspondence for `a,h`, with its local inclusion and

authentic contribution route crossing the resulting governed field. Its

```text

  form the annotation's dependent field schema at its source type scope;

    inside their original receipt/Force/rebind event telescopes,
```

This is a schema even where no rebind, body or Return is reached. Every binder

## 3. Two proposed dependent field correspondences

For each later `Act(v_b,U_b,r_b)` and complete independently admitted
challenge `chi_b`, the actual producer interface has separate schemas:

```text
J_arg(U_b,chi_b)       same whole carrier t and its latent contract
J_entry(U_b,chi_b)     actual receipt, designated Force or retained binding
J_body(U_b,chi_b,...)  body under the actual rebound value/current world,
                      only under its original body-reach guard
J_native(U_b,chi_b,..) actual native return and its delimiter
J_cons(U_b,chi_b,...)  source-designated result consumer, where present
J_call(U_b,chi_b)      complete ExecuteCallable interface with all phases,
                      pending prefixes, returns and admitted developments
```

These are interface/field schemas, not independently chosen executions.
Their placements in the complete receiver interface are constructed by IF's
producer/entry rule. Body fields are dependent: a pending entry request need
not have a body observation. Operation native return and a closure body Result
also remain distinct. The actual complete source application containing this
invocation has additional callee, outer Bind, result and future coordinates;
that full Call is not `J_call` itself.

Let `j_body` name that actual producer's body-subinterface inclusion in
`J_call`, under its original reach guard. Let `effect_call` name the complete
invocation's actual typed effect-field catalogue, including each original
constituent tag and route. Neither name is a new semantic axiom: IF supplies
the phase inclusions from the original producer's actual interfaces; the
original local operator law supplies the typed effect fields. For an opaque
declaration those fields still require its authentic catalogue.

The two candidate associations for written `h` are:

```text
M_body(a,h):
  h -> j_body(effect fields of J_body(U_b,chi_b,...))
       under exactly the original ReachBody guard and rebind/body telescope.

M_call(a,h):
  h -> effect_call(J_call(U_b,chi_b))
       under exactly the original invocation/event guards and phase/history
       telescopes; an entry field is retained even before ReachBody.
```

Their codomain is always a **dependent field family inside the same complete
invocation record**, indexed by `v_b,U_b,r_b,chi_b,eta,Omega` and the actual
event fields. `M_body` does not replace the complete observation contract
`P_F(chi_b)` by the body projection. It leaves entry, native return, consumer,
pending, future, provider and hard-envelope obligations in `P_F`. It proposes
that this written permission governs the body subfamily of that contract.
`M_call` proposes that it governs the invocation effect subfamily. Neither
candidate asserts that all members of its catalogue are licensed contributions.

At formation these maps retain `h`'s original concrete-set identity and member
ordinal, `sigma,Delta,xi`, the syntax-directed sign, and the paired checking
and exposed written descriptions. As in the precursor, structural pairing is

```text
T+ = Function(A-,QA-,QR+,R+)
T- = Function(A+,QA+,QR-,R-).
```

The candidates concern the semantic correspondence of that written result
effect selector `QR`; the equations alone assign no original field map.
Argument/carrier and result-value correspondences keep their actual supplied
local laws. Neither map changes the actual role/entry, copies checked
endpoints into a synthesized literal, or equates input/output rows.

For either candidate, its checking map and exposed map must use the same
field family at the same indices. The negative concrete permission applies
only inside an authentic contribution derivation whose actual route crosses
that family. An entry contribution under `M_body` acquires no such permission
from this `h`; under `M_call` it is eligible only if its authentic injection,
attachment and local permission/realization law establish that crossing.
Common effect identity or row equality is insufficient in both cases.
Independent same-family constituents retain their own introduction tags and
receive no permission merely because they belong to the same complete tuple.

For **each** proposed complete annotation interpretation `F_j` (`j=body,call`)
the actual local checking proof must conclude

```text

forall chi in D_checked,j:
  P_source(chi) subset P_checked,j(chi)
```

on the same whole tuple and actual callable. Its admission domain and hard
envelope are independently supplied; the maps do not define them. In
particular, the two interpretations are not assumed to admit identical
challenges or to have inhabited fibers. Neither candidate has acquired an
annotation formation, checking or realization proof here.

## 4. Conditional entry-E/body-pure discriminator

Use an actual ordinary Value-entry callable whose source body returns its
rebound Int parameter. The unannotated parameter selects `Value(A)` before
body synthesis; a legitimate Int challenge fixes the returned value's local
typing, rather than changing entry by its effect row. This construction is
the charter §21 case and IF's Producer/entry case. Its schematic code is
`lambda x -> result(x)`, not a claim that a particular compiler input passed.

The proposed challenge supplies the same whole carrier `t` that, when its
designated one-layer Force executes, requests the resolved effect `E_m` and,
on an independently legal response/resumption, returns the Int `n`. At the
exposure event there is no eligible handler for that request. The statement
needs these actual independent premises:

1. An authentic operation/primitive contract and source carrier derivation
   produce that typed request, origin, packet and raw continuation; inert
   carrier formation and scope/capture validity have genuine local evidence.
2. The actual surrounding immutable/world and punctured context are typed,
   including the callable/carrier holes, original receipt/path dependencies,
   current configuration and absence of eligible handling at exposure.
3. That same carrier has a whole-carrier admission witness in the complete
   descriptor under test; independent checked-challenge assembly produces
   `chi_b in D_F` at the same `v_b,U_b,r_b`. Membership or the actual local
   receiver law supplies actual-U acceptance at those same indices.
4. For a reached-body observation, the original Response/raw Resume rule
   admits the actual continuation's typed response and current state, and
   its suffix returns `n`. No new response/provider witness is substituted.
5. For a permission difference, the authentic entry contribution's typed
   injection into the invocation field, attachment and chosen annotation's
   local permission/realization evidence are also supplied. Exposure alone
   does not prove subtraction eligibility.

Premises 1–3 are not derived by displaying a carrier that mentions `E`.
The present packet supplies no actual primitive/carrier/challenge instance
establishing them. Thus **admissibility of this discriminator is conditional**.
In particular it is not shown admitted at the concrete negative annotated
formal, under either proposed interpretation, or by the current Y3 candidate.
Premise 4 is unnecessary for its pending entry observation but necessary to
exhibit its later reached pure body. No success premise is silently moved
into the independent challenge assembly.

Under premises 1–4, apply the original Value entry and Bind equations:

```text
receipt for the actual U_b; Force_argument(t) >>= S
S(n,C') = RebindResultPath(t,n,C'); Run(result(n)); ReturnFromInvocation

if Force_argument(t) = Request(q_E,C,k_t), then
  Request(q_E,C,k_t) >>= S
    = Request(q_E,C, lambda(response,C'). k_t(response,C') >>= S).
```

The pending invocation exposes `q_E` with its original instance, origin,
receipt, continuation and `xi`. It retains `S` as the pending suffix; there
is no body witness at that prefix. If the same raw continuation is legally
resumed and returns `n`, `S` reaches the body in the actual resumed world and
that body performs `Return(n)` with no additional E request. Receipt is not
replayed and no expired handler is reinstalled. The complete history keeps
the earlier entry request. These are constructor reductions, not a row union
or a marginal combination of an entry execution and an unrelated pure body.

Consequently, conditional on these premises, the entry E field lies in the
complete invocation's effect catalogue and is absent from its reached
body-effect subfamily. `M_body` and `M_call` associate `h` with different
fields for this same invocation. With premise 5, only the latter proposed
association can make that entry route subject to this `h`'s negative
permission. This establishes a conditional **permission-incidence difference**;
it proves neither an actual subtraction/output nor an accepted scheme.
An end-to-end outward residual difference also needs the separate actual
Call/output, concrete Row demand and correlated residual/check consumers.

The adopted complete-observation rule rules out checking only the pure body
and declaring the complete invocation E-free. It does not determine whether
this particular written annotation permission targets the body subfield or
the complete effect subfield: both candidates kept the whole observation
obligation. No claim that both are valid source interpretations follows.
