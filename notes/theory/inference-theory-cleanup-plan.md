## Superseded wording to annotate on future edits

- “Regular-witness/FMP/BR is still open for the normalized pure package” is stale after finite-fence completion. It remains true only for source/effect/joint-predicate/production correspondence or other explicitly broader carriers.
- “Every variable needs two-sided constructor bounds” is a condition for the preclosed construction, not the general normalized pure FMP theorem.
- “A uniform-admission condition is needed to eliminate total fresh definitions” is superseded for the total-definition operation. It can still be needed for separately hidden old witnesses under the certified-hiding theorem's stated scope; Coverage is the exact later criterion for the linked marginal pair.
- “Callback bounds merely include on the checked domain” understates the exact linked reference result: equality holds there. Do not generalize equality to independently supplied conservative bounds or to production.
- “An explicit `m_V` map must be constructed” is not required by the ordinary retained-query use route. The all-view extension entailment remains open.
- “Theorem C proves the production callback bridge” is false: reference/source theorem conditions and production conformance are separate.
- “Matching four Function ports proves endpoint adequacy” is false; complete membership, effects, role/entry, all root alternatives and independent admission remain relevant.

The originals remain untouched; these phrases should be corrected only in a later authorized consolidation, with local source scope recorded.

## Machinery that may be unnecessary in an implementation

These are design-space observations, not deletion recommendations. Do not remove or skip any existing code based on this list alone.

- **Reverse-updating intrusion/SCC state across edits** may be unnecessary: the Authoritative lifecycle addendum permits rebuilding a changed component. Reuse still requires a proven complete generalized canonical interface equality and atomic publication.
- **A second parallel effect/Function carrier** may be unnecessary: F5c summaries omit effect ports, but the retained `ConstraintStore` keeps original Function terms, coupled effect constraints, occurrence provenance, and body-row linkage for current lambda forms. The missing item is denotational/source correspondence, not currently an established absence of data.
- **A separate explicit universal-preimage/map-construction calculus for each public view** may be unnecessary when a fresh-copy/graft use retains the client's constraints and one direct complete query. The all-view extension entailment and query evidence still have to be proved.
- **Additional type-only stage summaries or reconstructed marginal joins** are not a safe substitute for original source witnesses. The `higher` and selector obstructions show why provider/request incidence and whole-tuple composition must be retained in the relevant proof route.
- **A chosen finite-rank quotient/folding algorithm as a completeness argument** is unnecessary for pure structural existence now that finite-fence completion proves existence abstractly. It may still be useful as a separately justified implementation or characterization technique; no production algorithm is selected by the theorem.
- **A provenance/proof carrier added solely to encode finite transformation certificates** is not established as necessary. The certificate theorem is metatheoretic over existing presentation indices and does not select an emission format.

## Areas not ready for cleanup

- Production HIR Apply/inline-lambda lowering and B endpoint generation; parser/recovery surface and result-consumer incidence are actively characterized.
- Production complete Function/effect denotation and the actual-side membership factorization, including arbitrary conservative root alternatives and independent challenge admission.
- Extraction of certified transformation evidence/source decorations from current production `ConstraintStore`/routing/generalization.
- Coverage or retention policy for admission-live old existential witnesses at their legal source scopes.
- Common descriptor construction and totality for each source solution; valid typed effect joins and source/provider preservation.
- All-view direct-query extension entailment, designated root/evidence and resolver completeness.
- Generalized constrained-interface fields, canonical comparison, SCC split/merge and enclosing-environment behavior, atomic rebuild publication, and non-inference artifact invalidation.
- Broader source envelope: mutable state, arbitrary imports/handler images, annotations, recursive schemes, and all ordinary application/branch/block forms where the existing theorems explicitly exclude them.

Until these gates close, keep new proof drafts and checkers in their existing narrow research/progress locations and avoid implying that a finite model or a reviewed reference construction is production semantics.
