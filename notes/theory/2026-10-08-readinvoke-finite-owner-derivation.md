It does not run or alter an emitter. Its output is an evidence record referring


no lookup equality in E. The original owner must return, for each entry, an

```text

                 original rule address, s, m, ordered operands, origins)
```

This is the precise H_owner interface. There are two legitimate sources of
such records: retention of the actual original owner's insertion events during

**Conditional inventory proof.** Induct on this finite collection pass. The
empty record list proves no membership. A step obtains an actual owner record

schema list. Thus required local source-rule presence is an output of the
construction; it was not assumed as a completed finite conformance certificate.
