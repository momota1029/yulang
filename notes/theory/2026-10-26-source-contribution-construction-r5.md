## 2. Original local inputs, without a global license oracle

`Packet catalogue whole` retains an actual complete port and its entire
dependent packet at one original joint row. The row may contain all original
provider, world, history, source scope, `nu,K,D`, dictionary, admission and
future dependencies; no component is selected independently during packaging.

`Relations` contains the actual original **local** branch/result, conjunction
and forwarding relation-witness types. An alternative witness names its
selected original side and both complete original child/result packets.
A conjunction witness relates both children and the result at the identical
whole row. Forwarding witnesses retain the actual original reference,
substitution, scope or annotation boundary relation. These are not generic
whole-source inclusion or successful-Q predicates.

`OperatorContract` retains the genuine original local operator/C0 evidence,
its entire original governed-field index family and its declaration-local

`SourceEdge`. Each field carries its complete primitive/local packet and
original license witness. The contract has no receiving root, desired
enclosing attachment, `DeclContribution` or native L/A argument/conclusion.

These interfaces do not assert that any evidence exists. Authenticity and
completeness of the actual original local law and field inventory remain at
the original primitive/operator owner. Arbitrary Lean inhabitants of the
record are not automatically original Yulang semantics. This is the genuine
L1/L2/C0 boundary explicitly retained by the adopted source, rather than a
new assumption of the original whole soundness theorem.

## 4. Source-fidelity repair: operand ports versus image results

The initial construction had a generic `Forward.operand` case. Primary review
identified a concrete risk, which was repaired before final freeze.

The adopted source §3.4 does permit standalone inherited contribution at an
actual **contextual operand port**. It separately requires an actual local
operator/C0 law for an image's **result or phase port**. The frozen signature
catalogue does not yet provide a typed discriminator/injection between those
two port roles. A generic forwarding rule into its result catalogue could
therefore let one child bypass the full image law.

`Forward.operand` has been removed. Every image/Call result or phase
contribution now requires its actual `OperatorContract.Evidence`. The complete
operand schemas stay in the original graph/partition, and only the local law
can select their applicable governed fields. Unreached operand interfaces are
not required to have executed or produced values.

Standalone contextual operand-port contribution packaging remains an explicit
typed-slot-injection residual. This fragment is not advertised as exhaustive
for that original rule, and its absence is not a new source rejection or a
restriction of the supported language. The failed and superseded source
snapshots are retained as construction evidence.

## 5. Independent compiler/readback maps

`CompiledContribution` is a separate inductive datatype. It checks exact
registrations in the actual `build graph` output and retains the same original
local relation and operator evidence. It is not defined as an existential
projection of `DeclContribution`.

`compiled_lookup_iff` reads the original node from the compiler's literally
retained source field. `DeclContribution.compile` and
`CompiledContribution.read` recurse over every rule. Their two inverse
theorems establish:

```lean
read_compile (derivation : DeclContribution graph relations operators
  index whole node packet) : derivation.compile.read = derivation

compile_read (entry : CompiledContribution graph relations operators
  index whole node packet) : entry.read.compile = entry
```

These preserve the actual selected alternative, paired proof tree, complete
output packet, original relation witness and local operator evidence as data.
`compilation_injective` consequently rules out collapsing two different
contribution proof records merely because their endpoints or owner labels
coincide.

## 6. Declared-port licensing and actual attachment diagrams

`DeclaredKey` retains the actual insertion, registered root, clause, original
source node, whole row, node schema and complete output packet.
`DeclaredLicense` and `DeclaredAttachment` are independent constructor
grammars. Each requires an actual root-insertion node and the **entire**
`DeclContribution`, including all original local semantic evidence.
The A constructor consumes no L premise.

`DeclaredLicense.attach` and `DeclaredAttachment.license` construct the
counterpart rule without an assumed global license/attachment relation;
their roundtrip theorems preserve the whole original witness.
`DeclaredAttachment.place` constructs the complete governed-field insertion
diagram. Its checked equations retain original owner, exact receiving root,
the entire dependent leaf field and antecedent import classification.

`CompiledDeclared` is a third independent compiler-entry datatype.
Its compile/read inverses retain the actual registration and complete
composite contribution. `CompiledDeclared.attach` reconstructs an original
declared contribution from the compiled entry and then builds the independent
A rule. The resulting attachment/license/compile roundtrip recovers the
identical compiled entry.

This is the declared-port fragment, including nested Ref/alternative/pair/
operator contributions. The distinct **outer** L/A Reference, Annotation,
UpperCall and certified Whole/joint-hiding rules remain unformalized here.
No scalar upper evidence, complete Call certificate or missing original
whole-map action is invented to fill them.
