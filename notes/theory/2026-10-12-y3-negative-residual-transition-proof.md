## 3. What the negative filter establishes, and what it does not

Consequently the smallest filter continuation has this required sequence:

```text

  -> check C and actual active payload obligations; register on a row receiver
  -> retain check consequences and origin d/df
  -> consume only that checked filter

```

This is the Y3 §4 Negative-wrapper contract's required sequence, not an
actual emitted Y3 trace. `x` cannot be deleted because it was skipped during
concrete-atom collection. A filter-only wrapper does not justify allocating

```text

Lr = L0 after subtracting the selected retained heads from those payloads
```

This branch requires an actual Row-upper demand and nonempty retained heads;
the filter constructor alone supplies neither. Empty-head, unweighted and
