## 4. The missing embedding is a joint, local law

The predecessor §3.1 proposed assembling independently supplied fields into
the existing descriptor universe. Let `Fields_xi(F)` denote those fields of
an existing well-formed descriptor: its original parameter/entry relation,
whole inlet, admission/observation relations, profiles and typed incidences.
This notation is a metatheoretic projection, not a new compiler object.

**Candidate non-implication.** Merely proving a candidate tuple t well-scoped
does not prove that it lies in the image of `Fields_xi`. Even proving that
each separate field has some representation does not prove joint
representability at the original F2.

For a logical discriminator, a descriptor structure with two objects can
have the field pairs `(D0,O0)` and `(D1,O1)`, with all four families well-typed.
The well-typed pair `(D0,O1)` has no representative when the domains and
observation families are distinct. Each individual coordinate is represented.
This refutes the abstract implication from field typing and marginal
representability to joint representability. It is not a model of all Yulang
source laws, a source-qualified counterexample, or evidence that the actual
descriptor universe cannot express the required F2.

Applied to original c2, the discriminator exposes a missing proof in the
proposed assembly: its equality claims `D_F2=D2`, `P_F2=O2` and `L_F2=L2`
must hold **jointly**, with the same original `A1`, witnesses and scopes.
Choosing a descriptor for each projection, or choosing a new F after each
challenge, cannot discharge this obligation. A generic variable ranging over
already well-formed complete Function descriptors is sufficient only if its
actual source introduction and complete existing interpretation are supplied.
Declaring the variable's sort or emitting `WF_Dec(F2)` is not evidence for
those missing laws.

There is a precise candidate representation mechanism in
[source contracts](../design/2026-10-05-source-contracts-and-common-allowance.md)
§§2.1–2.2: a constrained presentation with original scoped relation clauses,
semantically active at their recorded descriptor incidences. Its interpretation
has both retained membership and independently interpreted `DescMem`.
Consequently even a correctly emitted source relation may have witnesses
discarded by the descriptor conjunct without its local typing/embedding law.
The section explicitly labels active interpretation an additional hypothesis.
Its source-base realization theorem presupposes local descriptor typing.

Section 3.7's W/Z construction is one **conditional** exhaustive production
grammar, not selected authority for c2. Selecting it here would decide which
non-source alternatives exist. Leaving it unspecified and then invoking its
conformance theorem would assume the missing premise. Neither route establishes
the original embedding. There is no need to posit a richer carrier to state
the outstanding law; whether the actual existing constructors suffice is the
question returned to their source/semantic owner.

formation/embedding laws. This explicitly keeps the order “description
witness, challenge, event proof”; it never becomes “challenge, new descriptor”.

This application advances the *location and extent of the preservation
obligation*: the missing work is the c2 rule's primitive stability and correct
