
`X` is a candidate whole original row, retaining complete demands, providers, worlds/current states, raw continuations, dependencies, role/entry, hard constraints and all independent clauses. Opaque/parameterized primitive alternatives, including W/Z, retain their whole independent interfaces. A primitive can have infinitely many witnesses/parameters while the registration/reference graph is finite.

The independent inputs are precisely:

1. Actual resolved source/definition/capture/field/reference derivations, original registrations and scopes. No successful comparison manufactures one.
2. Each independently typed complete primitive declaration, its complete parameter/tuple/evidence telescope, its **typed source-port catalogue**, contribution interfaces and original licenses/admissions. No primitive input mentions a desired receiving signature or an attachment at that signature. The source-port catalogue states the declaration's own typed ports and contribution evidence interface, before any use. Root insertion, operand packaging and reference use construct the receiving placement in §3.

## 3. Concrete declaration and reference packaging

### 3.1 Typed support is distinct from licensed contribution fibers

A registered source-port catalogue is a dependent typed family

```text
Ports(reg) = (H_reg, Port_reg(h), share_reg),
Port_reg(h) = (slot schema, observation-position schema,
               complete contribution schema, original scope/dependencies).
```

Here `h` is a declared original port occurrence. `share_reg` is only the actual typed registration/alias sharing, obtained from the resolved source. In particular, the adopted `SharedInvoke(beta)` is the anchor of the registered immediate invocation position. Actual upper elimination supplies its existing OSlot-SharedInvoke member introduction; merely allocating that anchor does not infer a typed Function demand or its membership.

The catalogue is not a licensed-incidence set. An authentic declaration may expose a typed port with an empty applicable contribution fiber at `X`. It still has a support slot. Conversely two licensed witnesses at one port remain two witnesses. An empty primitive witness domain does not erase the declared port from support. For ordinary source/contract constructors the catalogue below is constructed from their actual typed ports; for a genuinely opaque primitive its complete declared catalogue is the independent input. No source-node count or solved type traversal chooses it.

### 3.2 Actual typed slot injections

Construct IF at each clause/source node as selected in the IF definition. At a newly formed contextual slot store its complete telescope and its typed port catalogue. The following are explicit packaging operations; the arrows denote incidence in a contract **frame**, not functions sending arbitrary assignments to satisfying tuples.

```text
Primitive clause alpha[m]:
  v |-> (prim-slot(alpha,m), v[m])

Operand j of node n:
  v |-> (operand-slot(n,j), v)

Actual clause q inserted in registered root t:
  v |-> (root-clause-slot(t,q), v)

Actual reference occurrence v=Ref(t,m):
  z |-> (use-slot(v,t,m), z[m]).
```

Each operation is a dependent record injection. Projection returns the original field and its same dependent indices. Apply it to **every** field: original parameters, whole carrier, provider/role/entry, world, requests/responses/raw resumptions, return, output-dependent latent/future interfaces, guards, licenses and original dependencies. A contribution-port field gets the same contextual occurrence as its containing telescope. An introduced provider `p'` keeps `J(p')` and its actual future/admission certificate; it is never replaced by a structural provider of equal descriptor type.

For an alternative root the root interface retains the tagged sum of all actual clause slots together with the original result relation. An injection keeps its branch tag and whole witness. For conjunction it retains both operand slots with the **one shared tuple**. For Bind it retains first operand, dependent result/current-state rebind, second operand under that binding and the ordered pending suffix. Delay/Lambda retain their inert carrier/capture/body/future slots. Call retains distinct callee, whole carrier hole, dependent actual-provider/current-world receiver, phase, result/future and every declaration-arm slot. Return/Result preserves the actual returned provider and current state. A designated consumer uses only its original selected one-layer port. These are the actual IF slot constructors, with no new semantic operator.

At declaration packaging the source registration at the containing original position retains these typed port occurrences. Their canonical slot identity is the actual `share_reg` image, when sharing was independently registered; otherwise their actual contextual occurrence is retained. No quotient of evidence follows. At reference packaging the receiving signature keeps the actual operand/use occurrence of the antecedent port. Its index is obtained by the same typed injections and the receiving registration's actual sharing, **not** by choosing a slot whose effect value matches. This supplies the static destination slot/position without a premise named signature placement.

More explicitly, if `pi_h` is the original projection for port `h` in `IF(alpha)`, its declaration placement is

```text
j_decl(h) = ins_(t,q) o operand_(q,alpha) at pi_h.
```

At the actual source use `v` and its actual enclosing operand chain `J_v`, its use placement is

```text
j_use(v,h) = J_v o use_(v,t,m) o j_decl(h)[m].
```

`ins`, `operand`, `use` and `J_v` are created by the four record injections above and the actual source/operator tree. They are not missing local actions supplied to this proof. For a registered cyclic reference use the registered interface directly; the contextual path stops at that slot and does not unfold a recursive body. A primitive licensed at root `t` with no actual reference from the receiving source has no `use_(v,t,m)` and gains no receiving incidence.

### 3.4 Composite declaration ports are built, not primitive assumptions

Declaration packaging applies to actual complete **clauses**, including constructor images, not just naked primitive leaves. The complete clause catalogue is constructed together with IF, using these explicit port operations:

```text
Cat(Prim(alpha,m)) = the full original typed catalogue of alpha at m
Cat(Union(q1,q2)) = in_left Cat(q1) + in_right Cat(q2) + actual result ports
Cat(Conj(q1,q2)) = in_first Cat(q1) + in_second Cat(q2) + shared-result ports
Cat(Image_op(operands)) = sum_j in_operand_j Cat(q_j)
                         + the actual independently typed op result/phase ports
Cat(Ref(t,m)) = use_(v,t,m) Cat(t)[m]
Cat(Scope(b,q)) = Cat(q) beneath that same b
Cat(Rename(m,q)) = Cat(q)[m].
```

The sums are contextual occurrences, before the original registered sharing is applied. Result/phase ports are **actual original typed ports of the operator declaration**, not every field of its descriptor. Their inclusion retains the original op relation between operand and result telescopes. For Call these are the actual complete operation ports including the adopted upper case; for Delay/closure their latent interfaces are inert. Opaque primitive catalogues are the only primitive catalogue leaves. A primitive license family is not assumed to have the shape of this constructed composite catalogue.

**Construction of port provenance, before applicability.** Simultaneously with each catalogue above construct `PortProv(q,h;Omega,rho)`. This is an independent syntax-directed derivation, not a supplied owner/incidence predicate. `rho` retains the actual typed field injections and the source formation tree. `Omega` is a dependent family of original constituent *occurrences* in that port's complete contractual telescope. Each occurrence stores its actual owning introduction, original registration/binder, its origin kind, and its actual operand/reference route. Its owner is read from that introduction, not from the receiving root or from a shared coordinate. The original dependent field map identifies the occurrences governed by each constituent. The family is an occurrence partition: disjointly tagged constituents may govern the same shared coordinate, whose original equality is retained; they are not merged. Parameters/future schemas remain dependent families, not enumerated runtime providers. Write `K_Omega` for its occurrence index and `fields_Omega(k)` for the original dependent field correspondence. The original owner projection is `owner_Omega(k)`; the stored actual route determines its local/inherited placement. These projections are eliminations of the following constructors, not independently supplied relations. A full interface partition retains all contractual branches as indexed schemas; a particular contribution witness also retains which branch/constituent licenses its observed projection, without discarding the other original interface schemas.

For a whole port, `Omega` covers every original contribution/license constituent and its incident dependent fields, including the operand interfaces, local relation/evidence, provider/future, phase, guards and admission fields retained in §3.2. A data coordinate is not assigned a new signature origin merely because it contains a provider. Its origin incidence is the retained original constituent/field correspondence. Thus shared values can have multiple original incidences without an arbitrary choice of one owner. These are the following construction rules:

| Actual port constructor | Construction of `Omega` and `rho` |
| --- | --- |
| Primitive's own port | `PrimOrigin(intro_alpha,h)` at the primitive's actual owning introduction, with its original local contribution/field correspondence. The actual owning primitive/local source introduction in these rules is the resolved formation record that originally introduces that primitive/operation, including its original scope and registration. Reusing the same primitive semantics does not identify distinct actual introductions; importing an existing introduction retains it through the actual reference/operand route. Root insertion cannot allocate a new introduction or turn an import into a local primitive. The schema of an independently interpreted primitive/local operator is still genuine input. There is no new hypothesis giving ownership of an arbitrary composite or a desired receiving placement. Construction of all nonprimitive origin partitions is precisely the displayed recursion. A source Call's distinguished adopted upper origin uses its actual seed/exposure introduction, separately from these inherited partitions.

In a registered cycle, preallocate the typed original port/field schema, keep the actual primitive/local introduction nodes and reference edges, and derive port provenance as a finite registered graph. `Ref(t,m)` stores a pointer to that schema with its actual use/substitution; it does not guess an owner at the use or require infinite unfolding. Original occurrences in its interpreted partition are finite provenance developments ending at an actual introducing node. A cycle with no introducing node has no such origin development, and hence no licensed originating constituent, but still retains all registered support schemas. ## 4. Independently declarative native formation

Define native support, static license and attachment by the following **finite indexed derivation grammars**. They are independent judgments over the actual source/contract grammar; none is defined by existential projection of another. They share the concrete original facts in §§2–3. The displayed port families range over the actual declared catalogue, including indexed/opaque families, not a chosen subset of favorable observations.

### 4.1 Support grammar

`S(I;beta_r,s,p,T)` means the registered static slot supports complete typed contribution schema `T`, including the constructor-built port provenance/field schema of §3.4. Its constructors are:

- `S-Declared`: actual clause/root insertion, original catalogue port h and its independently constructed PortProv schema; package all of it by §3.2 at receiver `beta_root(r)`. No primitive license witness is required and the original owner partition is not changed.
- `S-UpperCall`: the actual adopted OSlot-SharedInvoke at a typed upper Call. It retains the full Call contribution schema, original `p0` and registration/route.
- `S-Reference`: antecedent support, actual resolved reference/operator formation and its typed substitution; package the same whole schema at its actual §3 receiving slot.
- `S-Annotation`: the same support under the actual annotation's scoped constraints. It creates no slot or effect field.
- `S-Whole`: a certified whole transformation of the entire support family at its original binders.

Registering root/operand slots alone creates typed support, not semantic membership, a live receipt or a licensed witness. The native complete `Slots_N(beta)` inventory is support under these actual registrations/introduction rules, preserving the original registered slot sharing. The definition is exhaustive **for this selected native grammar**. It is not a retrospective theorem about an independently fixed foreign Slots model.

### 4.2 License grammar

`L(I;Omega,beta_r,s,p,c;ell)` is original static contribution applicability at its full origin/receiver index. It grants no event authority, protection, removal or liveness. Its independently declarative constructors are:

```text

  PortProv(q,h;Omega,rho) constructed by §3.4
  d : DeclContribution(q,h,c;Omega,rho), with all independent semantics there
  j = ins_(r,q) o rho, using the actual §3.2 field injections/sharing
  -----------------------------------------------------------
  L(I;InsertRoute(r,q,Omega),beta_root(r),j(s_h),j(p_h),j(c);
    DeclLicense(r,q,h,Omega,rho,d)).

L-UpperCall
  exact q/reg/route/seed/O0/O1/Q/SpecCall/C0 inputs of adopted LC-OwnCall
  kappa_full = IF(q,Q), constructed; c = C-Call(Q)
  kappa_orig = kappa_full, or an actual same-operation IF construction
    with its original complete field correspondence to kappa_full
    (partition agreement is constructed from the same actual source by §3.4)
  a_owned = J-Owned(o,c,kappa_orig), retaining its actual constructor evidence
  actual UpperOrigin(e,beta), and constructor-built full frame partition Omega_Call
  -----------------------------------------------------------
  L(I;Omega_Call,beta,SharedInvoke(beta),p0,c;
    UpperLicense(inputs,o,c,a_owned,Omega_Call)).

L-Reference
  ell : L(I0;Omega_a,beta_a,s_a,p_a,c_a)
  actual typed source/root reference v, original scoped substitution m
  actual containing constructor with its registered typed destination interface
  -----------------------------------------------------------
  L(I;RefRoute(v,m,Omega_a),beta_r,j_v(s_a),j_v(p_a),j_v(c_a);
    RefLicense(v,m,ell)).

L-Annotation
  ell : L(I0;Omega,beta_r,s,p,c)
  actual original annotation rule at those same original governed fields
  its independent constraint/permission/realization evidence at X, when required
  -----------------------------------------------------------
  L(I;AnnRoute(ann,Omega),beta_r,s,p,c;AnnLicense(ann,ell)).
```

`InsertRoute`, `RefRoute` and `AnnRoute` store the displayed concrete formation steps in every constituent route; they keep original introductions/owners unchanged. Their source maps are the actual §3 field injections, never arbitrary actions. Reference adds inherited placement even if a constituent's owner equals the receiver. Annotation preserves the antecedent origin/classification; its permissions require its independent governed-port rule. Whole rules act on the full partition and one original package as in §6.

In L-UpperCall, `o,c,a_owned` are **exactly** the selected original scalar own-upper objects at their original `beta_o=beta_r=beta`, `s=SharedInvoke(beta)`, `p=p0` indices. `Omega_Call` additionally records every inherited callee/carrier/arm/result/future constituent and the distinguished `UpperOrigin(e,beta)` field correspondence. This does not claim that all fields of c are beta-owned. Define `OwnUpperEntry` to retain these identical original own-upper indices/objects, including `kappa_orig`, together with the canonical full-frame certificate. The native canonical case takes `kappa_orig=kappa_full`; the other case requires an actual IF construction/correspondence certificate at the identical operation and full dependent indices, never just equal endpoints. It is a projection of this tagged constructor, not a redefinition of `LC-OwnCall`, J-Owned or any scalar foreign L/A predicate. General multi-origin entries have no scalar owner index unless a particular original constituent or a homogeneous subfamily has been selected explicitly.

For Union/Conjunction preserve actual branch/paired evidence, shared tuple and the complete corresponding partition. W preserves predecessor/output witnesses and original changed-coordinate relation; Z retains its independent license without an execution predecessor. No rule consumes arbitrary native L as a leaf or embeds unknown `Lic_C` under `LegacyLicense`. Only independently specified original primitive/local operator semantic license laws are genuine leaves.

### 4.3 Attachment grammar and formation anchors

Define `A(I;a;Omega,beta_r,s,p,c;alpha)` independently:

- `A-DeclaredPort` has the exact clause/catalogue, `PortProv` and `DeclContribution` premises of L-DeclaredPort, and stores `ins_(r,q) o rho`. Its receiver is `beta_root(r)` and its entire origin partition is `InsertRoute(r,q,Omega)`; no rule sets owner equal to receiver.
- `A-UpperCall` retains the exact adopted upper inputs and identical original `o,c,a_owned`, independently formed kappa and both indexed facets, with the constructor-built full Call partition and distinguished upper origin.
- `A-Reference` retains antecedent attachment, whole partition and actual substitution/containing constructor, and prepends the real use/operand route. It preserves every original owner and marks receiving placement inherited.
- `A-Annotation` retains antecedent diagram, partition and actual annotation evidence at its original governed fields.
