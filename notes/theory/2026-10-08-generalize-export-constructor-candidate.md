## 1. Objective and exact contribution

Construct the bridge from an **actual source-derived Generalize judgment** to
one reusable exported component and independently fresh incoming uses. The
construction retains its input instead of recovering semantic ownership from
F5 Q/R ordinals, printed schemes, successful queries or graph locality.

The new candidate object is a lossless finite export record with explicit
view-selection references and a publication protocol. The derivation gives
decoding, reference preservation, a use-indexed inverse and simultaneous
visibility. It does not derive the Generalize judgment supplied to it. RS
already constructs whole-copy provenance; this note reuses that result and
connects it to lossless export/accessor obligations. Repeating RS's origin
proof or PG-1's eligibility attacks would leave the same semantic premise
untouched.

The smallest remaining supplier is a source judgment selecting each actual
generalized view, its eligible identities, fixed closure, original binder
placement and designated endpoints, with the source/view preservation and
coverage laws in §3. No checker that merely assumes those selections would
prove that supplier. All canonical gate statuses remain unchanged.

## 3. Exact dependent inputs

Let `Delta` be a finite retained constructor/rule graph for one component,
with complete original simultaneous member validation **for the selected
source interpretation**. This is stronger than resolved HIR, solved bounds
or a registered member-root table. No particular recursive equality versus
inclusion rule is chosen here.

The construction accepts the following dependent package, in this order:

1. `T_Delta`: the original typed binder tree, scope references, resolved roots,
   ordered operands, recursive-reference interpretation, source identities,
   origin/receipt/owner/continuation/path incidence and retained evidence.
   Each primitive has its independent meaning and applicable transport law.
2. `J_Delta`: the complete joint relation/evidence/admission presentation over
   that graph. It contains the original `xi=(nu,K,D)`, all imports/captures,
   non-generic and world dependencies, symbolic family obligations and every
   live bound. Universal world/history clauses remain at their original
   scopes; witnesses retain the dependencies prescribed by `T_Delta`.
3. For each **actual source export event** `b`, a Generalize derivation
   `g_b` referring to those same original objects. It determines its whole
   selected view `P_b`, designated endpoints, eligible template identities
   `Omega_b`, fixed identities/references `F_b`, and original scope placement.
   A view may export one member or an actual jointly exported tuple. The
   enclosing component stores all member roots, but does not thereby
   instantiate all members under one new binder block.
4. `g_b`'s finite legal transform derivation, when the view performs grafts,
   rewrites or hiding. Admission-live dependencies stay exposed or have the
   specific admissible-hiding proof; naming one joint hidden witness alone
   does not prove marginal Function containment. Recursive rewrites preserve
   defining operators pointwise with their fixed-point interpretation.
5. An incoming-use identity `u`, selected export reference `b`, fixed enclosing
   environment, and legal graft/client/query data at its actual scopes.
   The independent source/use judgment, rather than export-query success,
   says which use is legal. Its proof must supply any actual endpoint Direct
   evidence; packaging never changes a failed query into a successful one.

Two semantic laws of `g_b` must be stated separately from its syntax:

**Preservation/reflection.** The selected generalized view, with its certified
original-scope transforms, preserves and reflects the whole relevant source
relation and ordinary-use/evidence observations. A view cannot erase another
member dependency by projecting its printed ports.

**Authorized-instance coverage.** Every independently legal future view/use
in the selected source envelope has a finite legal whole-use construction at
the **designated exported endpoints**, with one coherent original-scope
witness strategy and required query evidence. Its graph is chosen before
assignment, challenge and history selection. This is not a premise that
every well-formed type substitution succeeds. No independently legal use is
defined by being an instance of `P_b`.

Neither law is derived here for unrestricted source. These are the exact
GENERALIZE/PROJECTION/ALL_VIEW seams, not opaque extra runtime predicates.
The structural theorem below does not need authorized-instance coverage to
preserve a supplied use; coverage is needed to conclude that no independently
legal future instance has been lost.

## 4. Candidate constructor and outputs

Define `Pack(Delta,{g_b}) = C`. This is proof notation for retaining existing
data, not a selected public API or source type constructor.

`C` contains one indexed copy of the **entire** supplied finite graph: sort
and scope tables, original logical binder tree, member/export/import tables,
ordered relation/primitive operands, recursive back references and their
interpretation, complete constraints/evidence/admission rules, dependencies,
and each `g_b`'s view/transform/root references. Source and Option 2 production
grammars are recorded separately with their actual `W,Z,G` parameters and
certificates. Keeping production extras does not require giving them a source
execution witness. Primitive identity includes its contract/dependencies;
copying a printable name is insufficient.

The constructor indexes declarations and roots first, then writes each
record's original fields and references once. Back edges target indexed
records; it never unfolds recursion. It discards no field, merges no source
occurrence and performs no optimization or existential projection. A record
whose referenced input is absent is incomplete, not a successful export.

Each export entry points to its supplied `g_b` and the corresponding selected
view. At use time, evaluate only that view and its actual retained transform
derivation; storing the whole component does **not** impose every unrelated
member's local predicates on a use or permit skipping its shared dependencies.

For a use `u` of `b`, construct the nominal action at once:

```text
iota_(u,b)(a) = (u,b,a)  for a in Omega_b
iota_(u,b)(f) = f        for f in F_b.
```

This formula addresses semantic template/free identities, not every record
ID. Copy each selected local graph record with its own tag `(u,b,node)` and
the original-node pointer. Alpha-rename locally bound presentation symbols
under the same scoped action; this renames their supplied logical binders and
does not declare those binders eligible template parameters. Ambient binder
references and fixed free/world inputs stay fixed. Which declarations belong
to the selected view versus its enclosing scope is read from `g_b` and
`T_Delta`, not guessed from graph reachability.

Namespace tagged template, record and bound-symbol keys separately from fixed
keys. Apply this one scoped action through the whole selected view, scopes,
operands, residual, evidence and admission dependencies, before a legal
uniform graft. Original source anchors remain references to their same
declarations; copied occurrence instances keep their original anchor pointers.
Static slot identity and dynamic activation identity are not collapsed.
Original logical binders retain kind, order and dependency position; eligible
template declarations get their use-specific names at the positions specified
by `g_b`. Concrete runtime events are not allocated by this static key
construction.

The dependent output is `(C, E_(u,b), iota_(u,b), inverse_(u,b))`, where
`E_(u,b)` retains the client and actual endpoint-query conjunction. A separately
specialized use has the source relation pulled back along its graft; no
bijection with all unspecialized satisfying assignments is claimed.

## 5. Conditional construction derivation

**Lossless packing.** Erase indices by looking up their original references.
Every declaration/field/ordered operand was written once, and no record was
merged, so `Decode(C)= (Delta,{g_b})` field for field, including cycles,
binders, incidence and certificates. This gives both directions of record
coverage without a global semantic equality oracle. For the supplied typed
relational interpretation, identical decoded operators and primitive
contracts give identical denotations at every original scoped assignment.
Positive recursive rules have the same finite derivations and fixed-point
meaning because their defining operators are identical.

**Accessor equation.** For each field/reference accessor actually present in
the input representation, define the encoded accessor by looking up the
corresponding record and following the same original references. Then

```text
Read_r(Delta,{g_b}; xi) = Read_r(Decode(C); xi).
```

This equation applies to a supplied read, including an evidence or admission
read, rather than inventing an `AllFuture` accessor. It does not enumerate the
actual future compiler consumers. A consumer that reads an unstated field or
consults external mutable state is outside this accessor proof until its
dependency is explicitly retained and matched.

**Whole fresh transport.** The key map is injective on the eligible identities,
fixes `F_b`, and has inverse `(u,b,a) -> a` on its owned image. Distinct use
events have disjoint eligible images; repeated occurrences within a use have
one image. Preserve binder ancestry by mapping its eligible declaration
identities, without moving binder nodes. Invoke the supplied primitive and
certified-transform transport laws through every incident field. Induction
on finite constructor/admission derivations gives preservation and reflection
of each complete membership/admission/evidence witness. A recursive reference
uses the indexed mapped root; finite witnesses need no bounded unfolding.
Option 2 abstract steps use their own complete grammar/certificates, not a
source inversion argument.

**Joint uses.** Construct all maps before conjoining the retained client:

```text
Client(F, public_1,...,public_n) and E_(u1,b1) and ... and E_(un,bn).
```

Conjunction uses one original fixed environment and the binder placement
prescribed for each view/use. Owned copies are structurally independent;
client equations or a shared enclosing receiver can constrain them jointly.
Consequently identity independence is not statistical independence or a claim
that every combination of separately satisfiable views is jointly satisfiable.
Scope-respecting inverses recover the same joint strategy use by use while
leaving fixed coordinates shared. They do not produce witnesses separately
for each challenge. Grafts can deliberately relate type ports without merging
source occurrence/owner/receipt identities.

**Every authorized future instance.** Conditional on §3's independent
coverage law, take its finite whole-use graph for any legal future instance.
Lossless decoding preserves its selected view and whole fresh transport
preserves its witness strategy and actual query evidence. Thus packaging
loses none of those instances. This is a consequence of the supplied coverage
law; it is not a new proof of that law or unrestricted principality.

### 5.1 Source-derived PG-1 anchor and concrete reference equations

Instantiate the record constructor on PG-1's ordinary projection source
derivation, before any F5 finalizer. Parameter registration gives record `p_x`
with endpoint `a`; the resolved formal Name `n_x` references `p_x`; its Result
record `r_x` references `n_x`; the Lambda record keeps the original entry,
body, provider and environment records. These references yield the exact
source-derived equations

```text
FormalEndpoint(p_x) = a
ResolvedValueEndpoint(n_x) = FormalEndpoint(p_x)
ResultEndpoint(r_x) = ResolvedValueEndpoint(n_x) = a.
```

The encoded accessors follow those same references, so they return `a,a,a`
before use and `(u,b,a),(u,b,a),(u,b,a)` after whole eligible freshening. This
is a field-level instance of the IFACE_FORM constructor equation, rather than
an alpha claim about an already printed arrow. In the captured projection
`pick y=z`, `n_z` references its original enclosing environment entry; its
Result endpoint is `b`, not `a_y`. The export retains the entire transitive
provider/environment/residual/admission capture closure reached from that
entry. Encoded accessors therefore return `(u,b_export,a_y)` for the formal
and the same original fixed `b` for the result, with all dependent capture
references fixed as the actual source derivation prescribes.

These equations describe source endpoint observations, not literal current
solver row identities or a proof of a current `root_value_for` result. The
full original Lambda/entry/history kernel is still retained; projecting these
three observations would not be a complete export. PG-1 derives this reference
family but leaves semantic eligible-view admission and actual designated-root
Direct evidence open. Conditional on those remaining judgments, two incoming
uses select distinct tags for `a` while retaining one captured environment.
This is the concrete anchor for the general construction, not a restriction
of its required source envelope to projection bodies. The existing shadow/F5
finalizer is not the source judgment supplying any missing premise.

## 6. SCC publication and failure

During component construction, internal uses refer to the same original live
member roots. `Pack` does not instantiate those internal references. Construct
the complete candidate `C` privately; validate every member/export reference,
scope, primitive dependency and required certificate before enabling any
incoming use. All incoming consumers require one successful component-ready
token covering **every** member. Member slots may be populated separately
while private; ready must be unobservable until all are valid. This preserves
the all-member visibility barrier without requiring one indivisible multi-slot
write.

Failure to obtain a Generalize derivation, missing semantic dependency, invalid
scope, failed required query, invalid transform or resource exhaustion leaves
the component unavailable. Nothing in this research selects source rejection
or rollback diagnostics. A later deterministic resource policy must reject
before partial publication; this note chooses no numerical threshold. Ready
tokens tied to a complete immutable input snapshot must not be reused after
that dependency snapshot changes. SCC split/merge, rebuild/cache validity and
an actual transactional implementation remain FRESH_LIFE obligations.

The protocol's state invariant is conditional on consumers enforcing the
ready token. It is not a proof that current production code implements it.

## 7. Scope discipline and failure detectors

The construction uses no solver/Oracle oracle. Its shared assumptions are the
input source judgments, primitive meanings/transport, complete active relation
grammar, original binder tree and certified transforms. Oracle observations
would be a separate historical capability reference, not evidence that these
rules hold. No executable experiment, mutation run, seed or range was used.

The following named mutations are **inspection obligations, not executed
tests**: dropping an original binder/dependency invalidates Decode; changing
an ordered operand invalidates constructor correspondence; renaming only a
Function port invalidates whole incidence transport; sharing one eligible
image across use events invalidates the inverse; freshening a fixed capture
invalidates the fixed-environment law; dropping `Z` or its hard guard changes
the actual Option 2 relation; enabling ready before the last member invalidates
the visibility barrier. A checker for these invariants would validate packing
relative to supplied rules, not prove source emission or semantic eligibility.

For PG-1's `id x=x`, the already derived endpoint family supplies a candidate
single template identity whose input/result references repeat. For
`pick y=z`, the captured result remains fixed with its whole closure. This
note contributes no additional source-eligibility theorem for those witnesses;
their actual designated-root query and full admission premises stay as in
PG-1. General calls, effects, annotations, recursive validation and every
ordinary source view are not silently reduced to that projection envelope.

The untouched critical premise is actual eligible-view selection with complete
admission/reflection and all authorized future instances. Existing lexical
ownership and whole-copy methods cannot supply it. The next different method
is a constructor-level source Generalize derivation retained at its creation
point, with exact evidence accessors identified by its real consumers; another
free-variable or alpha-copy toy probe would not resolve this premise.
