## 1. Result, obstruction and exact claim

There is a total decision and witness-construction algorithm for the finite
source fragment defined in section 3. Its inputs are source constructors,
declarations, original allocation/binder records and an authentic caller
JointWF parameter. They are **not** an argument strategy, an export adequacy
certificate, EPR/ER, or an oracle for possible J witnesses. The algorithm
constructs each actual argument's complete contract and Car evidence at its
source owner, solves all aliased description uses together, and emits one
finite typed symbolic strategy at the original binders. It also constructs
the generic id/pick introduction over the **full** independent admission and
history domains. No finite-world or finite-history approximation is used.

This is a complete theorem for the generated local boundary judgment, not
for every clause of an enclosing source program. The complete whole-Call
contribution owner is explicitly outside this judgment (section 3.3).

The proof does not settle arbitrary JOINT_DEC. The inspected source rules
still supply no complete decision law for a residual that can independently
constrain unknown J/Car/context/proof strategies, use an open method choice,
or observe arbitrary proof fields through client W. Finite syntax and
recognition of a submitted L term do not provide that law. Nothing here
proves undecidability of those source cases. The positive result deliberately
uses actual generated contracts and exact native constructor elimination;
it does not rename the general missing law as a premise.

The checked variable upper bounds below are **whole Function views at their
original prechallenge source checking positions**. They are not inferred
from scalar checking of the actual result of `id 0`. A post-Return check can
have retained literal, J or Echo evidence at that event. Requiring its proof
to use only the generic endpoint would be an additional rule. This note
makes no such rule and excludes those unknown post-Return checks from its
completeness claim.

There is a second distinction for pick: Fixed(J_z) returns the actual fixed
import. A widened printed capture type need not be its strongest available
evidence. Section 3 permits whole-result views of pick only for tight native
ground/record captures, and section 5 proves necessity using that actual
value. It never infers necessity from a printed capture endpoint alone.

## 3. An actual finite source fragment, not certificate input

Call the fragment NPB (native projection boundary judgments). Its source is
the native source-directed declarative constructor/check grammar and its
owning records, with the exact generated local package of section 3.3.
Membership is a finite inspection of those forms and records. A parsed-source
or HIR translation for arbitrary same-inlet Function view syntax is not
proved. This is an envelope for this theorem, not a proposed language
restriction or permission for production to reject other natural programs.

### 3.1 Structural endpoints and data source graph

Written endpoints have the finite nonrecursive grammar

```text
S,T ::= Unit | Bool | Int | Any
      | {l1:S1,...,lk:Sk}       distinct labels, k may be zero
      | S | T                  finite union
```

There is no singleton/refinement, negative type, intersection, recursive
description, computation or Function head in this **written target grammar**.
Inferred a below is not assumed to range only over this grammar. Any keeps its
ordinary whole-value meaning, including callables.

First form a finite acyclic **tight** graph of authentic ground literals,
immutable Name aliases, unannotated finite records/tuples and direct field
projections. Literal synthesis has its original Unit/Bool/Int endpoint;
records have their exact required field shape and tight child endpoints.
Those endpoints are atoms B with only ground leaves, no Any or union. A
direct projection selects a declared field of this tight record. There is
no dynamic selection from an Any or union boundary.

A final optional structural annotation/check may widen a tight binding B
to a written T. This is its **single initial checking boundary**. The proof
is unresolved; the solver constructs it. The resulting declared endpoint
is T, and the full actual output certificate plus all original input and
intermediate evidence are installed. Names/aliases of that checked binding
can be arguments; they cannot be annotated again, projected, or used as
fields of later data records in NPB. This syntactic condition excludes
`(0:Any):Int` rather than incorrectly calling it unsatisfiable. Likewise
the theorem does not decide a later anchored refinement by comparing only
the exported target. No method, conversion or singleton target is present.

For a tight source value z with endpoint B and any written T, section 4.2
proves `z has T membership iff Inc(B,T)=YES`. Thus all original native
checking alternatives, even a proof using the retained ground fact, have
the same existence result at this initial boundary. A generic structural
proof supplied by Inc is sufficient whenever one can exist. Distinct
Elementary/Check/Compose and independently sound local proof alternatives
remain in the source relation; none is normalized away.

The solver computes each final declared argument endpoint from this graph.
It rejects a missing tight-field projection or a failed initial check.
Forms requiring later refinement, union elimination or another source rule
are outside NPB, not declared semantically impossible. In particular a
checked `0:Any` argument **retains Any as its complete J payload**, despite
the retained original Int evidence.

projections. Its declared endpoint B is an atomic record tree with ground

The fixed J_z is constructed from this data source once, including its actual
provider and complete established/free dependency closure. Captures are not
freshened at new. General wider finite captures exist under PE-PICK; their
view decision is outside this narrower theorem.

The theorem starts at an **unsolved** source-generated description package,
not an arbitrary residual after an earlier witness was frozen. Every a_i
below is an eligible, unassigned Desc at its original scope. A monomorphic

An imported already fixed a, a supplied proof-tag choice, or an earlier

## 4. Exact structural proof search with a computable bound

No enumeration of semantic worlds, complete J values or arbitrary finite
proof terms occurs. The sole finite search is the written structural check.

### 4.1 Normal form and atomic comparison

An atom is Unit, Bool, Int, Any, or a finite record of atoms. Define N(T)
recursively: primitive gives a singleton; union concatenates its two lists;
record forms the Cartesian product of its fields' normal lists. Its list
denotes the same membership union as T. Each distributivity step has an
explicit proof using original union case/injection and record depth/field
constructors on the **same** decorated tuple. Missing or additional fields
are not guessed. A certificate is transformed, not normalized by proof
irrelevance; its original evidence remains in the generated proof tuple.

Define `P <=at Q` by these total recursive clauses:

* every P compares to Any;
* a ground atom compares to its same ground atom;
* a record compares to a record iff every Q field exists in P and its P
  child compares to the Q child;
* all other pairs fail, including Any to any non-Any atom.

Each positive comparison emits hereditary Top, ground Identity, or record
width/depth with its full child terms, original provider/field sharing and
scope witnesses. It follows by those independent local laws that it proves
same-decorated-value inclusion at every independently compatible event.

The inclusion algorithm is

```text
Inc(S,T):
  normalize S and T;
  for every P in N(S), find Q in N(T) with P <=at Q;
  if any P has none, return NO and that P;
  otherwise return YES and source-case/atomic/target-injection proof.
```

Union cases cover **all** source alternatives. Searching for the chosen
target summand does not erase any source or production alternative.

### 4.2 Completeness is proved, not an inclusion oracle

For an atom P construct a characteristic decorated value c(P): a ground
value of the indicated ground tag; for a record, exactly its required fields
holding their characteristic values; for Any, an authentic empty-capture
native id closure as an opaque non-ground/non-record value, with its ordinary
Top certificate. The closure's generic certificate is constructed in section
6 independently of this structural lemma. It has no ground or record tag.
No new opaque primitive is assumed. The characteristic values have genuine
native formation/hereditary evidence in a compatible empty test world; record
children share that world. This construction does not require a world chosen
to satisfy a pending source comparison.

For the necessity application in section 5.1 the same construction is
available at the **original** compatible event. Ground and finite record
constructor facts are introduced in that very world. At each Any leaf use
the already present authentic id receiver as the non-ground/non-record
value, restricting its existing native membership and applying hereditary
Top there. No fresh closure registration, different world or new earlier
witness is needed. Record fields share that event and keep the genuine
constructor/provider incidences. Thus the standalone empty test world above
is not substituted for the fixed prefix of an actual boundary judgment.

Induction on P,Q proves

```text
c(P) has Q membership  iff  P <=at Q.
```

For Any's witness only target Any accepts its non-ground/non-record tag.
For a ground witness disjointness leaves the same tag or Any. A minimal
record witness has no extra fields, so target width demands are necessary;
apply induction to each field. Conversely the atomic proof supplies Q.
The grammar observes no singleton values, provider identity or proof tags,
so a ground representative suffices. In particular an **actual** tight pick
capture z of shape B satisfies the same equivalence for B,Q: replace each
ground representative by z's corresponding ground-tag value. It follows
that `z has T membership iff Inc(B,T)=YES` for those tight captures.

If S membership implies T membership, c(P) for every P in N(S) has T
membership. Union inversion selects some Q in N(T). The equivalence gives
P<=at Q, so Inc returns YES. Conversely its emitted finite term proves
S-to-T inclusion. Hence Inc decides true hereditary structural inclusion
and finds an actual L proof, not merely a proposed-certificate check.

Additional sound generic inclusion laws, Compose trees and their arbitrary
finite intermediates cannot create a failed inclusion: their interpretation
implies the same semantic containment contradicted by c(P). No bound on
their syntax is needed because a successful inclusion already has the
constructed normal proof. We do not identify their distinct proofs with it.

### 4.3 Explicit finite bound

Let n(T) be the written syntax-tree node count, including repeated copies
when a DAG is unfolded, and r(T)=|N(T)|. Then

```text
r(ground or Any)=1
r(S|T)=r(S)+r(T)
r({lj:Tj})=product_j r(Tj)
r(T) <= 2^n(T).
```

The inequality follows by induction: at a union `2^a+2^b <= 2^(1+a+b)`;
at a record multiply the child bounds. Each atom has at most n(T) nodes.
Testing all pairs for Inc(S,T) therefore takes at most
`2^(n(S)+n(T)) * (n(S)+n(T))` elementary atomic-node visits, up to a fixed
implementation constant for registered field lookup. A proof DAG with shared
normalization and chosen comparisons has size bounded by
`O((n(S)+n(T))*2^(n(S)+n(T)))`. These are deliberately coarse computable
bounds. No pure FMP, regular-witness theorem or unbounded proof enumeration
is re-proved or assumed here.

## 5. Exact joint elimination of description choices

For frame i let C_i be its finite collection of actual Calls, including Calls
through all aliases. Define the single early endpoint

```text
L_i = union_{j in C_i} S_j.
```

For each id whole-result view k, the finite condition is `Inc(L_i,T_k)=YES`.
For each tight pick view k it is `Inc(B_i,T_k)=YES`. The finite data graph's
initial tight-value checks are decided by Inc as well. No lower proof is
chosen separately at aliases. A new frame gets its own L, and no Shared or
fixed capture field is copied along with it.

**Elimination lemma.** For an NPB boundary graph with authentic initial JointWF, its
original joint BoundaryBuild_NPB clauses have a scoped satisfying strategy iff every
displayed finite Inc condition holds and every required declared data
projection exists.

### 5.1 Necessity for id uses the full contract

In an arbitrary successful original strategy, even with a_i outside the
written grammar, Call checking gamma includes genuine
`mu_j : S_j -> a_i` on the actual declared `J_j=DataArg(S_j)` result interface.
There is one a_i above its challenges and all aliases. This lower proof
cannot be supplied using only the actual source result: its independent
checking signature concerns **every** member of the complete J image.

Suppose Inc(L_i,T_k) fails. Union inversion and section 4's original-world
characteristic construction give some call j and a decorated value v in S_j
but outside T_k. The original
mu_j puts that **same** v/provider in a_i. The complete `DataArg(S_j)` Return
rule licenses v even when argument j's actual execution returns another
value. Use the **same original actual carrier** t_j and its complete J_j;
the licensed v Return is not asserted to be its execution. Assemble an
independent minimal initial target challenge at that carrier's original
world/registration with the same inlet and an authentic return/discard
context. Its gamma uses the original mu_j at those same operand incidences
and the bare Delta projection schema. No proof is transported to a different
carrier or later world. Target admission needs no result safety or successful
execution; no future-hole demand is necessary for this challenge.

VP-Force may use that licensed Return. VP-Entry, the same formal rebind,
BodyReturn and InvocationReturn with Echo give an original source-root
production Done carrying v. Every original guard/incidence and current-world
action is supplied at its real event. The whole Function view's domain
and production proofs would put this same member into V_i(T_k), whose
completed Return requires hereditary T_k. That contradicts the characteristic
value. A prior actual literal fact or an alternate proof tag cannot delete
this independent production member. Thus Inc(L_i,T_k) must succeed.

Equivalently, generic source lower bounds and the full result-view proof
force every member of L_i into T_k. The argument never treats successful
concrete Function queries as transitive, or concludes that all arbitrary
unknown a_i have finite structural codes.

### 5.2 Necessity for pick uses its actual fixed capture

Pick's source root has Fixed(J_z), so its result is that actual z/provider,
not an arbitrary member of the printed B_i. At least one real Call supplies
a legal completed-entry challenge in its initial compatible source context.
The fixed import's authentic hereditary restriction supplies z in that same
world, and VP+Fixed produces its completed Return. A successful whole-result
view entails z membership at T_k. Tight-capture equivalence in section 4.2
then entails Inc(B_i,T_k)=YES. This proof would fail for a widened capture;
that is why the tight condition is explicit and no B_i-only theorem is
claimed for arbitrary pick.

Failed initial data checks have the section 4.2 tight-value membership
contradiction for their actual source value. A missing tight-field projection
has no instance of that source rule. Those conclusions are about NPB's
selected structural grammar, not later retained-evidence refinements or
adapters at the same source spelling.

### 5.3 Sufficiency gives actual finite proof trees

Choose a_i:=L_i at its original sigma, once for its frame. This choice uses
the finite prior source constraint graph, not any actual invocation challenge
or history. For call j, union injection gives S_j->L_i; compose it with any
required normalized structural term while retaining all intermediates.
Construct mu_j, never change J_j's declared contract. Every successful id
Inc(L_i,T_k) supplies a genuine hereditary result proof. Pair the complete
VP+Echo source and VP target phases, using that proof only at the actual
completed result/future port. All unfinished/Force/Request cases retain J's
entire complete relation and original current-world fields. Precompose the
target's declared result-hole context evidence with this local proof as in
PE-ID section 6.3; binder congruence gives the real domain proof. This is a
finite **whole Function** L term with all original operands, not scalar
input covariance or a composed concrete query receipt.
