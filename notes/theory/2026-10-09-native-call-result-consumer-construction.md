into the original staged relational image. Its inverse on exactly that

**Soundness.** Induct on the native derivation. Start inserts the already
constructed pure prelude and the exact independent challenge, then the

**Completeness and exact inverse.** Invert a witness of this particular
constructor image. The prefix tag either gives its pre-receiver position or

## 9. Why this does not close the selected typed source gate

`R_c=ReadInvoke(F_id,D_c,IF_e)` and apply `ReadInvoke-Desc` using only the

Consequently CallMem/C0 and general JOINT_DEC remain open. Even at this
