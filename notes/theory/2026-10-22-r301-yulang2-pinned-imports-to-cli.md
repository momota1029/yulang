## Deferred role-member selection boundary

The pinned Y2 source shows that Types `pair.say` belongs to the default member `Display.a.say` in `std::core::fmt`, not to the top-level `std::io::console.say` binding. The default member writes `a.show + "\\n"`; the exact tuple `Display` implementation supplies `show`. The focused source-index test now checks that `say`'s member identity belongs to the exact imported `Display` role and that its original member body contains both the host write and the `show` selection.

The pinned Y2 lowering in `crates/infer/src/lowering/expr/tail.rs` represents a selection as a deferred method value applied to the receiver and carries the receiver's evaluation effect into the call. `analysis/session/selection.rs` probes available method values and selects a role method only when its candidate is unique; `analysis/session/lifecycle.rs` waits for selection readiness before resolving it and routes the selected method as a real definition use. This means the next candidate step must carry the exact `Display.a.say` member identity as a deferred selected method and retain the required `show` role obligation on the receiver through Capture/FreshUse. The tuple implementation can be selected only after receiver constraints identify the ordered pair. Y3 still rejects `FieldTail` and nonempty `where` clauses in candidate preflight; no method or implementation has been selected by the new source test. The unsupported `format_spec` annotation and recovered unrelated roots remain outside any admission shortcut.
