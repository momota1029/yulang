## R301 resumed seam: deferred field obligations

A code-path reread against design §5.1 and §6 and proof companion §3.6 narrows the next change. `SourceRoleObligation` currently represents only a declaration `where` prerequisite: provider, exact syntax keys, optional imported role identity, and one subject `RowKey`. Capture explicitly interns only that subject row, and `prepare_candidate_fresh_use` maps only that row into a fresh use. `LocalSourceForm::FieldTail` is still rejected before a candidate relation is retained. Therefore the existing role-obligation bridge cannot yet carry method selection evidence or its receiver across a generic provider's Capture/FreshUse boundary.

The next record must preserve the exact FieldTail occurrence/member source identity, authenticated role/member identity, receiver and result rows, and the receiver-bound `show` prerequisite as one source-owned obligation. Capture must include both rows and any semantic payload needed to constrain them; FreshUse must reconstruct them with the same use's row map. The later resolver must wait for receiver bounds in the common inference session before selecting the unique tuple implementation and discharging `show`. The proof companion's existing Capture/FreshUse results apply only after that record is admitted into the captured graph; they do not prove the new source-to-record registration or solver discharge. No implementation selection or sample acceptance is claimed here.
