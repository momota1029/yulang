## 1. Frozen objective and original statement

For **every finite successful native construction history** starting from
independently justified initial coverage, every newly formed lower/upper pair
must have an owned pending processing obligation or an independently justified
completed/cache/transport disposition, including its dependencies. A pair is
indexed by its actual row owner and **both bound incidences**, including the
physical emissions and their admitted relation fibers. Equal endpoints do not
identify two incidences or their evidence. Finite successful construction means
the relevant constructors return successfully; it does not mean an unsolved
inequality is a completed semantic judgment.

The statement includes both polarities, Value and Effect rows, selected bound
copies, incoming Allowances, retained origins, and subsequent native consumers.
For histories that reach canonicalization, reentrant replay or retained
restoration, coverage must survive those actual changes. Source binders,
original lexical/event scopes, attachment/provider/witness identities and
residual lineage remain fixed. No independently selected marginal witnesses,
source restrictions, mandatory registry or generation-time solve requirement
are introduced. Initial coverage is an input owned by initialization, not an
assumption that arbitrary populated stores are covered.

This is the operational premise of
[rebuilt-core Lemma 1](2026-10-11-simple-sub-rebuilt-core-proof.md#lemma-1-opposite-bound-replay-preserves-the-constraint-solution-set)
and its Bounded repair. The full source/Car/Call/L4, soundness, required
principality, general termination and F5 parents remain OPEN and are excluded
from claimed closure. These exclusions do not narrow their original domains.
The candidate is inspected as an actual native owner, not adopted as the
semantics of the rebuilt successor.

**Outcome:** selected copying supplies a concrete candidate disposition for
parent-side scalar inequalities, so lack of direct enqueue is not a
counterexample. The full operational statement is neither proved nor refuted:
the first missing law is joint contextual disposition of the parent link and
selected copied bound, with both original incidences. No actual-constructor
successful uncovered countertrace or source-admitted counterexample is claimed.

## 3. What the actual extrusion constructor represents

At `candidate_extrusion.rs:21–175`, each invocation has fresh local `rows` and
`structure` maps. At a wrong-level row `a`, it snapshots the selected direct
and atom lengths, creates fresh target-level `c`, records the actual parent,
memoizes before following bounds, and inserts the opposite-side parent link.
The selected list becomes `Work::Bound` actions with the original `BoundKey`.
At `:308–324`, each action stores the mapped endpoint on `c` and transfers
origins with the actual extrusion operation/polarity/target. The maps are
dropped at return; this is not cross-call polar memo reuse.

Let `E_p(t)` name the **actual result of this one operation**, not a fresh
semantic construction. Its row links and selected-copy dispositions are:

| Polarity | New parent incidence | Selected copied incidence | Candidate route for the new parent opposite pair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positive | upper `a <: c` at `a` | lower `E_+(l) <: c` at `c`, for each selected lower `l <: a` | `l <: E_+(l) <: c` |
| Negative | lower `c <: a` at `a` | upper `c <: E_-(u)` at `c`, for each selected upper `a <: u` | `c <: E_-(u) <: u` |

This route is not simply old-pair replay: a newly created parent link has no
old opposite-pair disposition to rename. The new copied bound itself is a
retained unsolved inequality. It may be the correct accumulation disposition
without generating the original pair again, provided its joint operational
meaning is established.

The directional clauses in the last column have direct constructor evidence
for unchanged atoms/acceptable rows and newly copied rows. A copied row uses
exactly its retained parent link; cyclic selected lists do not require choosing
another row valuation. For a rebuilt Function, `:224–250,328–355` visits four
ports with relative polarities `(-,-,+,+)`. Thus the syntactic variance route
is fixed jointly by that one operation. Concluding a semantic directional law
for the complete four-port Function still needs the Effect children's actual
meaning, especially remapped views; this note does not import it from the
reference's two-port Function theorem.

A Value copy initially receives just one physical bound side, so at that cut
it has no local opposite pair. If both polarities of `a` are visited during
one invocation, the later visit sees the earlier parent link on its selected
side. For example, positive first gives `a <: c+`; later negative copies that
upper unchanged because `c+` is already at target level, retaining `c- <: c+`
at `c-`. The dual order retains the same comparison at `c+`. This grounds the
scalar route for the new cross-polar pair; it is not yet joint contextual
coverage for its two parent emissions.

Effect rows use the same ordinary selected-list mechanism, but there is an
additional real pair producer. Positive tail copying visits incoming capture
incidences at `:164–175`. `Work::IncomingAllowance` at `:293–306` maps the view
to the copied tail and inserts an **upper** Allowance on the mapped source,
which may already have selected lower bounds. Such a copied Effect row is
not guaranteed to stay one-sided. Its initial selected lowers, upper emission,
view mapping and source-bound transport all need one joint law. Registration
is owned by `candidate_effect.rs:553–561`; it records incoming incidence for
an actual upper Allowance with a tail. No inequality replay is performed by
the incoming-copy action itself.

## 4. Exact joint operational premise left open

Physical insertion at `candidate_extrusion.rs:406–567` canonicalizes endpoints,
constructs an admission and relation, pushes a physical slot, registers capture
incidence and emits `BoundEmissionId`. It has no universal completion or replay
side effect. `candidate_context_bound` (`candidate_context.rs:2814–2856`)
attaches the new bound using the actual retained processing parent's
post-check context, except the explicit checked-Allowance case. Hence the
new parent link has its own admitted fiber, not just endpoint text.

`candidate_transfer_bound_origins` (`candidate_effect.rs:563–601`) then does
two separate things. The fiber loop calls
`candidate_context_transport_witness_with_evidence` (`candidate_context.rs:3182–3196`):
it snapshots the specific source fiber, constructs a child with the source's
post-check context, records `Dependency::Transport` and `FiberTransform`, and
attaches it to the copied bound. The origin loop uses `relation=None`, imports
a snapshot of the source bound and creates a separate destination admission.
It explicitly marks `MissingOriginAssociation`. The transform itself also
marks that gap (`candidate_context.rs:589–607`). This is an evidence-owner
omission, not a demonstrated runtime typing defect.

For a positive parent pair with lower fiber `f` and parent-link upper fiber
`g`, actual ordinary replay would construct the ordered
`Replay(context(f),context(g))`, or `IDENTITY` for two identity contexts,
and ordered `Dependency::Replay` containing both
RelationIds and both input BoundKeys (`candidate_context.rs:2911–2988`).
The selected-copy route instead retains a transported source fiber and the
fresh emission's own fiber. The existing transport operation is unary: its
dependency identifies its source fiber and remap, not this pair's two input
incidences. Negative polarity reverses which bound is new while retaining
lower/upper order. The incoming-Allowance route additionally changes the
destination view/tail and may create two-sided copies.

**Missing law:** for each actual pair `(owner, lower emission/fiber, upper
emission/fiber)` produced by these writes, establish that its selected-copy
and descendant-link disposition preserves the **joint ordered context**, both
source admissions and dependency scopes through future consumers; alternatively
identify owned pending work that does so. This must include the real incoming
Allowance mapping when reached. It cannot be supplied by independently
matching origins and fibers, endpoint equality, or scalar transitivity alone.

The note does not assume unary transport is semantically wrong: a compositional
law may justify represented accumulation without eagerly creating a Replay
child. Such a law is precisely what remains unproved at the actual owners.
The distinction matters even when all scalar clauses are correctly retained.

## 5. Restoration and downstream consumer bridge

replay it enumerates new-lower × all-upper and old-lower × new-upper;
incoming-use replay owns the full snapshotted fiber product and its diagnostics.
This establishes the activation construction only when the owner is called.

The consumer chain is concrete: `candidate_context_consume_replay_child`
(`candidate_context.rs:3060–3104`) enqueues ordinary children with an ack,
or uses the retained `start_typed_drain` path. `lib.rs:12207–12290` processes
Transport, Context and Dispatch in order. Context checks run before endpoint
memo dispatch; `pair_is_current` (`:11463–11474`) requires the actual processing
RelationId completed at the current intrusion generation as well as the raw
typed-pair memo. A same-shaped endpoint is insufficient. `lib.rs:12412–12454`
enqueues the rewritten extrusion demand; this does not itself enqueue every
parent-side opposite pair. Function dispatch retains four separate ports at
`:12493–12536`.

Replay retirement is bound to the actual child drain. The terminal owner at
`lib.rs:12617–12635` completes child acknowledgements after diagnostics and
retains the destination return receipt before removing the drain frame.
`candidate_context.rs:3011–3059,3106–3125` requires the matching return and
Idle/retired operation for completion; a queued child remains the outer
worklist's responsibility. Stored/emitted bounds are not those receipts.

Reentrancy/canonicalization are reached in this consumer cone. Saved count
with live flattened index (`candidate_extrusion.rs:570–631,736–739`) does not
by itself prove retention of each original physical opposite incidence when
direct-row lengths or representatives change. The exact all-history coverage
proof must justify that mapping or show replacement coverage; it is not
asserted here. The Authoritative [suspension contract](../design/2026-10-11-private-candidate-suspension-contract.md)
§4 expressly selects saved count/live index, including same-component callback
appends and current canonical owners. The earlier
[restoration correspondence](../progress/2026-10-11-resumed-restoration-source-correspondence.md),
Actual source bridge and Minimized operational noncommutation witness, already
distinguishes that selected order from original-vector enumeration. A shifted
index is therefore not code/design nonconformance. Whether another disposition
covers the original pair or the universal claim exceeds the actual native
consumer requirement remains separate. Freezing endpoints to repair an index
would change the selected contract and is not proposed.

The merge owner `candidate_intrusion.rs:548–604` transfers both
sides, canonicalizes third-owner fibers, increments generation, reopens its
root and replays canonical lowers. Those actual actions do not supply the
missing joint transport law or independently authenticate origins/fibers.
Successful suspension retains its handles and accepted state; global progress
or convergence is not inferred from these finite owner actions.
