action. This differs from the earlier
[shared-witness falsifier](2026-10-08-open-import-root-extension-falsification.md):
no shared coordinate disagrees, no action moves one side independently, and
no closed provider identity is substituted for an incidence.

The full candidate survives. Its `cross` premise expressly includes every
old clause whose meaning changes on extension, so the conditional theorem
cannot be falsified by satisfying all its premises and denying its conclusion.
The new discriminator instead shows why exact data restriction and lawful
action alone do not retain a **proof** of an old callable obligation if its
independent demand domain grows. A positive universal acceptance clause can
be the frontier; negative absence tests are unnecessary.

The selected hereditary meaning can already exclude this mutation. If the
old certificate covers every demand of the independently compatible new
world, applying its restriction suffices. What remains unprovided for a
general semantic importer is the independent rule identifying that new world
as compatible and supplying the demand restriction/embedding at the actual
import incidence. Calling the installed world compatible because its old
certificates remain valid assumes the conclusion of this particular task.
This is a premise diagnosis, not a demonstrated circularity in the candidate.

## Conditional restriction lemma

Fix original provider evidence `U` and complete independent challenge sets
`D_A`, `D_P` before and after installing region N. Let `V(U,d)` abbreviate
both actual acceptance and the complete observation/future obligation at d,
retaining its original event, witnesses and every production alternative.
Suppose an old certificate supplies

```text
c_A : forall d in D_A. V(U,d).
rho : D_P -> D_A, with same-original-challenge interpretation.
transport : V(U,rho(d)) -> V(U,d), preserving the original provider/evidence.
```

Then `c_P(d)=transport(c_A(rho(d)))` proves every obligation over D_P without
choosing a new U or old witness. This is an established elementary conditional
lemma. If the sets use identical challenge coordinates and interpretation,
`D_P subseteq D_A` with identity transport is sufficient. For general scopes
and current events, mere set inclusion of printed challenge labels is not
the required dependent map.

Where old hereditary evidence quantifies over a larger family `F_A` of all
independently compatible future demands, replace D_A above with F_A. Selected
§4.1 supplies restriction once that independent compatibility and original
challenge embedding are given. It does not manufacture the new compatible
world from an arbitrary root-local certificate. This lemma neither adds a
new mandatory language clause nor proves an exhaustive host-domain rule.

If some `d_new` has no justified image in that family, its V proof is a new
frontier obligation. The candidate correctly places a previously checked
clause whose meaning changes in `J_cross`. Reclassifying it as `J_old`
requires the missing restriction equation, even if the provider data and
all shared fields are literally unchanged.

## Smallest finite structural mutation

The partial structural theory below is deliberately weaker than the selected
hereditary Function/world meaning. It tests omission of the new frontier,
not the full candidate or complete independent Yulang import rules.

Take one retained provider U, one old demand 0, and one new demand 1 exposed
by the imported region. A demand is abstract index data, not a new carrier,
operation, runtime value or selected source rule. Fix

```text
D_A={0}; D_P={0,1}; Acc_U={0}.
old certificate: forall d in D_A. d in Acc_U.
new acceptance frontier: forall d in D_P-D_A. d in Acc_U.
```

Grant an independently described local import record `n` in this partial
theory, whose structural effect is to expose demand 1. Its local predicate
does not establish V for U. A and N share U's unchanged reference, its entire
acceptance data, `C_e`, scopes and xi. Their overlap restrictions are equal;
`glue(a,n)` copies a and n with exact restrictions. The lawful action family
contains identity only on all regions, coordinates, proofs and predicates.
It satisfies both restriction squares, glue naturality, identity and legal
composition. Every old coordinate and certificate datum is retained exactly.

Nevertheless the old acceptance proof does not prove acceptance of demand
1. The installed acceptance predicate fails at zero local execution steps.
No receipt, invocation or unsafe observation is needed to expose the missing
universal premise. This establishes the non-entailment

```text
old-local validity + local import record + exact overlap/glue
  + lawful joint action + exact old data restriction
  !=> new-world validity with the retained old callable proof.
```

The frontier is exactly `1 in Acc_U`, which is false. Thus this witness does
not satisfy `cross`, and cannot instantiate full OpenImportExt-0. Setting
`cross` to true without supplying this proof is the rejected mutation.

Minimality is relative to nonempty equality-preserving demand growth: one
old and one distinguishable new demand, one retained provider and one added
region suffice. With a singleton demand universe there is no fresh demand;
without an added region there is no growth. Allowing an empty old domain
gives a still smaller but vacuous old-certificate variant; it was excluded
to test an actual previously discharged obligation. No smallest source
program or globally minimal semantic axiom basis is claimed.

## Premise classification and precise blocker

1. **Exact dependent overlap/glue:** a candidate structural input, not
   independently obtained from equal types or endpoint IDs. Granting it
   leaves the positive semantic frontier above untouched.
2. **Action commuting with restrictions and glue:** required for the claimed
   action theorem. Global laws on every legal action are stronger than a
   single zero-step identity extension needs. Identity satisfies them in the
   finite mutation, so a stronger structural action law cannot repair it.
3. **Fixed-semantic forward IDs plus every cross proof:** sufficient for
   regional assembly. They are not inherently circular when proved from
   independent owner clauses. Defining cross as completed EnvStore/JointWF,
   or supplying an installed-root membership proof as the independent import
   primitive, assumes this introduction's conclusion. The current candidate
   explicitly forbids those substitutions and has not performed them.
4. **Reverse ID-world equivalence:** stronger characterization than the
   forward constructor needs. No use of it is required by this audit.
5. **Selected hereditary restriction:** an established selected constructor
   case under independent compatible-world/history inputs. It is not the
   general semantic import introduction or a theorem that every separately
   valid root has those compatible inputs.

The precise blocker is an owner-sourced, filling-independent frontier and
challenge restriction at the actual original importer, including any old
hereditary clauses affected by installation. The broad domain decision and
Lemma W do not supply that new-root last rule. The prior overlap attack and
this materially different demand-frontier attack both leave that premise
untouched. Another finite world toy would add no source evidence. The next
method must consume the frozen importer owner instance and check its actual
independent clauses, demand maps and fixed-semantic IDs.

## Commands, finite coverage and independence

One inline Python probe enumerated all four acceptance masks over demands
`range(2)` with fixed D_A={0}, D_P={0,1}. No seeds, random sampling, shards or
generated output files. The exact central checks executed were:

```python
from itertools import product
rows=[]
old_demand={0}; extended_demand={0,1}
for bits in product((False,True), repeat=2):
    acceptance={d for d,b in enumerate(bits) if b}
    old_certificate=all(d in acceptance for d in old_demand)
    local_structure=True  # exact glue and identity action, granted structural inputs
    extended_certificate=all(d in acceptance for d in extended_demand)
    frontier=all(d in acceptance for d in extended_demand-old_demand)
    rows.append((bits,old_certificate,local_structure,extended_certificate,frontier))
assert len(rows)==4
assert sum(old for _,old,_,_,_ in rows)==2
failures=[r for r in rows if r[1] and r[2] and not r[3]]
assert len(failures)==1 and failures[0][0]==(True,False)
assert all((old and frontier)==(old and extended) for _,old,_,extended,frontier in rows)
assert all(all(d in {0,1} for d in extended_demand) for _ in [0])
assert all(all(d in {0} for d in old_demand) for _ in [0])
assert not all(d in {0} for d in extended_demand)
```

Command wrapper: `python3 - <<'PY' ... PY`; exit 0. Result: four masks,
two old-valid masks, one failure under structural premises alone, zero
failures when the frontier is actually required. Sanity mutations check
widening acceptance to {0,1}, no demand growth, and old hereditary coverage
already including the new demand. Widening acceptance changes U's retained
data and is not an authorized repair of the witness.
