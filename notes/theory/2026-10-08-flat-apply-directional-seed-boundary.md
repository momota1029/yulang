## 1. Result

The flat argument does not enter the binder-to-exposure part of the selected
directional rule. Given authentic registration of the selected unannotated
inferred formal and a source-owned upper demand at the actual `f 1` occurrence,
the registration/Name/exposure prefix derives protection at that upper
occurrence. It uses neither literal `Int` nor successful checking. This is a
conditional specialization of the existing source constructors, not an
extension of S1's established nested theorem by citation alone.

That source certificate is not yet the exact `SeedExposure(k,e_c)` of paired
O1. The latter is indexed by an **actual original emitted record**, and retains
that record's original registration, lexical route, checking occurrence,
Function demand and scope. An original source-owner attachment must identify
those projections with the independently constructed source exposure and
preserve their formation derivations. Equal endpoints or displayed tuples do
not supply that attachment.

Thus the source seed calculation can remain independent of authentic literal
installation. Its final record-indexed packaging is conditional on the
installation lane supplying an actual `e_c` and the exact source attachment.
There is no claim that `P_c^cand = P_c^orig`, that candidate source proofs inhabit
original judgments automatically, or that O0/O1 generate their own seed.

## 3. Source derivation before emitted-record membership

Instantiate the binder/Name part of S1 at these actual source indices:

```text
ordinary formal registration in S-Reg
  --> Gamma(d_f) = Value(A_f), original shared root R_f
actual binder annotation absence + selected inferred-formal treatment
  --> SeedOrigin_src(k,d_f,A_f,R_f,sigma_0)
direct S-Name route, preserving this registration and inherited packet
  --> I(Name(u_f)) = Value(A_f)
  --> Result(Name(u_f)) : Comp(empty,A_f)
S-Upper on this still-inferred endpoint in the body's source derivation
  --> ProtectedVarAt_src(k,A_f,sigma_c,u_c)
```

The last arrow is the S1 registration-to-exposure composition: the formal's
selected seed is registered before this body use, the actual Name preserves
its source identity and packet, and the authentic upper constructor exposes
that same still-inferred endpoint. It does not infer a stage witness from
the Boolean fact that annotations are absent. Its dependencies are expressly
part of S-Reg/S-Name/S-Upper. Physical worklist order is not a premise.

The positive directional instance then gives

```text
ProtectedVarAt_src(k,A_f,sigma_c,u_c)
SourceUpperUse_src(u_c,A_f,U_c,sigma_c)
------------------------------------------------ Dir-Protect at this source occurrence
NewProtection_src(k,beta,u_c,sigma_c,p_c)
```

Bundle the independent source certificate as proof notation:

```text
Exposure_src(c,k) =
  (S-Reg,S-Name,S-Upper;
   UnannotatedFormal(d_f),SeedOrigin_src(k,d_f,A_f,R_f,sigma_0),
   ProtectedVarAt_src(k,A_f,sigma_c,u_c),
   SourceUpperUse_src(u_c,A_f,U_c,sigma_c),
   beta,u_f,u_c,Delta_c,U_c,p_c,xi)
```

**Conditional source theorem.** S-Reg/S-Name/S-Upper construct this exposure
and its one directional conclusion without any actual Gen-Call-0 record,

fact, runtime receipt or admitted provider.

**Proof.** The parameter and selected treatment supply the first two arrows.
Apply the ordinary Name and Result constructors to the given direct route.
The source-stage dependency retained by S-Upper makes these the same seeded
variable at the indexed exposure, which is the registration/Name prefix of

This is argument-independent **once S-Upper is supplied**. It does not prove

## 4. Exact original-record packaging and the required owner attachment

The selected owner proof defines the consumer's input as follows:

```text
SeedExposure(k,e_c) retains
  UnannotatedFormal(d_f) at its original binder
  SeedOrigin(k,d_f,A_f,R_f,sigma_0)
  ProtectedVarAt(k,A_f,scope(e_c),e_c.u)
  SourceUpperUse(e_c.u,A_f,U_e,scope(e_c))
  the same registration and Route(e_c), with e_c.u_f resolving to d_f
```

Consequently `Exposure_src(c,k)` alone cannot construct that dependent
object: it has no actual original `e_c`. This is an index/interface boundary,
not a new countermodel to E-flat. Its source upper certificate is useful
before original emission exists.

Given an independently produced actual original `e_c`, the needed bridge is
an owner-retained source attachment, whose eliminators recover:

```text
original source parent(e_c) = c
e_c.d_f = d_f; e_c.A_f = A_f; e_c.R_f = R_f; e_c.beta = beta
e_c.u_f = u_f                              [lexical occurrence]
e_c.u = u_c; e_c.U_e = U_c                 [checking/demand occurrences]
e_c.Delta = Delta_c; scope(e_c) = sigma_c
e_c.xi = xi
Reg(e_c.beta) corresponds to the same S-Reg derivation
Route(e_c) corresponds to the same direct S-Name derivation
original upper origin at e_c.u is the image of S-Upper's actual source origin
```

These equations must come with typed formation correspondence, including the
original binder's annotation/seed treatment and seed-stage dependency at the
upper exposure. Bare ID equalities cannot convert a candidate judgment into
an original judgment. For exact original-index preservation, the occurrence
map is identity on the retained source identities, with distinct lexical and
checking components. For a genuinely freshened presentation, one lawful
whole sorted map must transport all bindings, scopes, xi, source derivations
and protection applicability together; its provenance preservation must be
supplied. No per-port choices are permitted.

This is a restricted projection/formation obligation of the original source
owner. It does not demand the full Code-Call package, complete-result truth,
receipt, contribution, admission or a satisfying descriptor. It also does
not assume `SeedExposure` as a premise: it supplies the correspondence of
the primitive registration, route and upper source constructor from which
the source derivation in §3 can be repeated at original indices.

**Conditional packaging theorem.** If authentic original emission supplies
`e_c` and this formation-preserving source attachment for the independently
constructed `Exposure_src(c,k)`, the exact selected `SeedExposure(k,e_c)`
can be constructed without O0/O1.

**Proof.** Recover original S-Reg/S-Name/S-Upper from the attachment. Reapply
the ordinary registration, selected formal treatment, Name and exposure
composition of §3 to those original derivations. The attachment's checking
projection indexes the resulting `ProtectedVarAt` and `SourceUpperUse` at
`e_c.u`; its lexical projection supplies the separate resolution at
`e_c.u_f`. Its registration/root/scope/xi projections identify all remaining
fields with that same original tuple. Bundle precisely the fields in the
consumer definition. No owner or Call-effect derivation is an input. QED.

This proves neither existence of `e_c` nor the attachment. H-E's authentic
source-introduction embedding is the natural owner of that attachment.
If its implementation preserves only displayed atoms/IDs and loses the
registration/route/upper constructor origins or logical exposure dependency,
its stated correspondence is too weak for this use. If it retains those
origins with their lawful interpretation, the above is a restricted
specialization, not a second independent seed-generation policy.

Only **after** that construction may the selected O0/slot and paired O1
consumers run with their other independent premises. Both Own facets have
the same seed and owner evidence; their indexed occurrences stay `u_f` and
`u_c`. This note provides no O0-to-seed or ownership-to-seed arrow.

- Remove selected-formal eligibility from S-Reg: generic annotation absence

  S-Upper/source-stage applicability is missing.
- Keep a genuine candidate exposure but omit actual `e_c`: the original

- Keep only equal IDs in the bridge: candidate/original formation provenance
  and protection at this exposure remain unproved.
- Substitute `u_f` for `u_c`: the ProtectedVarAt/SourceUpperUse consumer
  indices are wrong; the original lexical resolution cannot repair them.
- Transport a lower provider effect or forget a source scope/xi dependency:
  the directional rule or whole-map premise fails.
