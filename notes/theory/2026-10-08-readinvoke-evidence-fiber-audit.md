## 1. Objective, method and result

Audit whether the selected original formation rules admit two genuine derivations of the approved inner `f x` with the same complete factual projection but a downstream-observable evidence difference, or instead prove source uniqueness sufficient to collapse every relevant fiber. This is a source inversion/falsification audit, separate from constructing forward/back evidence maps.

**Result:** no such genuine source pair is established, and no complete uniqueness theorem follows from the inspected rules. The displayed rules determine the constructor skeleton and locate any possible difference in the original resolution, formation-origin or independent evidence operands. They do not specify whether distinct proofs of those operands exist or which proof distinctions an Early consumer observes. The nominal-token countermodel in the existing projection audit is therefore not a selected-source counterexample.

There is also an exact projection seam: the selected interface grammar indexes contextual slots by original formation q and inserts original child derivations. A projection retaining those q-indexed operands has not removed q. A projection replacing them by just their fields needs an original evidence reindexing/elimination law. The selected clauses expose field projections, but do not supply that law or a proof-identity observation.

Claim classes:

- **Established inputs reused:** the selected pure-read, source-interface and captured-source constructor meanings, within their declared scopes. This producer does not independently certify their prior reviews.
- **Bounded derivation:** constructor inversion and the leaf localization in §3, plus the exact q-indexed interface dependency in §4.
- **Conditional theorem:** a constructor-uniqueness induction under explicit functionality of all original evidence leaves, §5. Its leaf premise is not established here.
- **Open source premise:** the actual evidence signature/equality/actions for retained formation and its independent leaves, §6. No counterexample, proof irrelevance, full factorization or production theorem is claimed.

## 3. Actual constructor inversion and minimized difference location

```text

qf = Code-Result(Data-Name(b_f,edge_f),o_f)
qx = Code-Result(Data-Name(b_x,edge_x),o_x).
```

This is inversion of original input construction §§3.1,3.4. Call has one displayed source head; each Result has a Value data premise; each Name returns the actual binding and use-to-binding evidence. The Value and Computation cases are separated by the original source tag. Code-Consumer cannot be another derivation of these same Value-tag Result children. The argument carrier is formed from that same qx at the Call's own registered reify origin. The Call bundle retains its actual Application/check/result/suffix origins.

Bind inversion similarly retains q1, the original actual-result rebind, and q2 under the resulting dependent telescope (input construction §3.3). The selected ME rules distinguish initial, Return and Request phases; at the fixed early event the actual phase and unreached suffix are fixed (captured source §§4.2–4.3). They do not introduce a choice of world or result to differentiate q.

Consequently, two derivations at the same complete projected facts cannot first differ by changing:

- the source constructor head, Value/Computation tag or actual entry role;
- the captured root, x's result/rebind root, source/check/result/reify occurrence or phase;
- the retained typed substitution, dependent interface, guard/action field or original arm placement, when these are genuinely included in the projection.

Each change alters a retained field, so it is not a same-projection witness. Substituting an equal endpoint for a different root violates Data-Name rather than creating a second admitted derivation. Choosing another independent challenge or receiver observation is unavailable at this prefix. W/Z choices have their own tags/regions and do not create an alternative structural formation of q.

For a possible nontrivial fiber, follow the first differing premise below the identical heads. The discrepancy must reach an original resolution/capture evidence operand, a formation-origin proof, or an independently declared evidence field that the projection retained only through its readouts. The same binding, composed typed map or judgment proposition does not by itself assert equality of these proof operands. No inspected clause exhibits two such proofs. Nor does it assert functionality of all those evidence sorts. This is a minimized **potential difference location**, not a minimized actual counterexample.

The selected semantic readouts that are explicitly expanded are factual: W restricts the same binding certificate, Return keeps the same tuple/world, ME-Result keeps actual incidences, and early ME-Call keeps stored formation plus suspended suffix. ## 5. Conditional source uniqueness: exact hypotheses

The following constructor statement explains what a uniqueness proof would actually need; it is not adopted evidence equality.

**Hypotheses:** the fixed finite resolved source judgment has exactly the displayed syntax-directed constructor algebra; every original resolution/capture/origin/registration/primitive evidence operand has a uniquely determined evidence value at its complete retained indices, or an already specified lawful equivalence preserved by every actual consumer; constructor records have the corresponding original congruence and inversion laws; registered cycles are finite references with functional registration, rather than cyclic proof trees.

**Conditional theorem:** structural induction on the finite formation derivation makes any two derivations at these indices equal (respectively lawfully equivalent). Name uses leaf functionality. Result uses its child's induction result and result-origin functionality. Carrier-Delay uses the same argument proof and reify-origin functionality. Bind uses first-leg uniqueness, the same dependent middle telescope, second-leg uniqueness and origin functionality. Call then uses both child results and all original check/result/suffix operands. A Ref compares its registered reference and scoped substitution without unfolding a cycle. Congruence supplies the whole-record result.

The nontrivial hypotheses are leaf functionality/equivalence and the original evidence congruence, not finite syntax. None is derived by saying “the rule is syntax directed.” In particular, the selected IF theorem is a constructor supplier and naturality result, not evidence uniqueness for every independent primitive contract.

Even proved uniqueness of q at fixed j would not construct q from the reduced certificate without source access, nor establish lawful evidence maps through the reduced certificate. It would remove a two-q separating-pair route only. It cannot by itself close the transformed-export supplier.

## 6. Exact missing discriminating source premise and stop condition

The discriminating premise is the **actual evidence interface of the original retained formation field**: its constructors, equality or lawful equivalence, every projection consumed after formation, and its whole-substitution/event-restriction actions, including the q-indexed contextual-slot interface and independent resolution/origin leaves.

For a source counterexample, that interface must supply both genuine inhabitants q0/q1 of the same original formation judgment, establish equality of their complete projected records, and name an actual original consumer whose readouts differ or whose required transport is impossible. No such three-part witness was found. Positing two tokens plus a newly supplied identity observer supplies the two missing assumptions rather than falsifying the selected source.

For source uniqueness, the interface must instead supply functionality/inversion or a specified consumer-preserving equivalence of the independent evidence leaves. For factorization without strict uniqueness, it must expose which existing consumers depend only on the retained fields and provide their lawful evidence actions. The exact point requiring this signature is ReadInvoke §3's retained original formation operand, used in captured source §§4.2–4.3 and indexed by IF construction §§3.2,4–5.

This is a localized lack of a discriminating original evidence law, not a theorem that Yulang proof identity is observable, that formation is proof irrelevant, or that the whole source must be retained. The selected raw transition equations already determine the early operational facts. A third transition toy probe would leave this premise untouched. The next method should inspect/expand this original evidence signature or prove its actual elimination laws, not add equivalent transition counts or new semantic restrictions.
