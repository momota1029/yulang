## 3. Minimal required invariant and why the baseline does not establish it

Claim class: proposed invariant for later prototype review, not a theorem
conditional on a fabricated live constructor. No new ContextExpr is defined.

A representation of the selected law needs both an occurrence-qualified pending
upper-field obligation and a semantically justified effect-position operation.
These are logical responsibilities, not a requirement for two new data types.
The construction must retain the original `(O,L,f,v,s,u,U_u,ResultEffect)` and
seed witness; it must introduce the new protection at the authenticated upper
output-effect child only. Inherited context/profile packets remain separate
from this new contribution. In particular:

- Seeding must join the actual checking occurrence and original upper demand
  before endpoint memoization; a source-set member or equal row is insufficient.
- A Function child may introduce this contribution only when its exact field
  is ResultEffect and its upper occurrence is the one licensed by that record,
  or a justified transported copy of that occurrence. Result and Argument
  children retain their inherited evidence without receiving this new mark.
- Copy/replay must retain the source witness and its upper-side/field path,
  with one shared renaming per actual use. An introduction belonging only to
  another replay input cannot be granted by membership in their unioned source
  set. Equality reconciles coordinates; it does not manufacture a boundary,
  receipt or port license.
- Independent lower/provider protection survives. The upper witness cannot
  be written onto the provider's original lower output, including after row
  equality. Distinct original Apply witnesses remain distinguishable even if
  their rows, demand shapes or source sets coincide.
- Successful retention of this obligation must eventually mean protection at
  the selected effect view under its original scope/receiver semantics, with
  no new removal grant or nested-result mark. A pending source marker alone
  cannot satisfy that last clause.

`candidate_function_port_admit` propagates the same inherited context through
