## 1. Result and boundary

For the authentic import-free `my id x=x`, the pinned source grammar identifies
the witness **families and their original scopes**, including arbitrary local
checking terms. The proposed `I_q/Delta/IF/VP/Echo` fields describe an inlet,
incidences and production relation. They do not yet specify placement and
allocation of the entire native certificate tuple. Equality of raw returned
values/providers does not reconstruct non-definitional proof choices.

This is a more precise gap than an unspecified witness-normalization theorem:
the baseline already has reviewed native certificate constructors and CE.
CE retains the whole proof tuple and substitutes only forced raw aliases.
The remaining task is to attach that source-free certificate interface, with
its original binder incidences, to the eventual public representation and its
ordinary use allocation. No concrete public representation is selected here.

The census is exhaustive over the displayed native source/checking rule
families in the selected one-component envelope. It is not an enumeration of
all witnesses of every independently supplied ground/primitive/argument
contract. Their exact field telescopes are independent inputs, not recoverable
from a displayed arrow or provider identifier. No finite observer language
is silently invented: the source contract permits arbitrary **typed** W at its
original scope. Lossless tuple transport handles that class conditionally;
an observer-by-observer reduction requires further supplied local contracts.

## 3. What the observer contract actually permits

Source proof §3.4 gives finite clients `new/alias/view/run/join/bind`, with
their real source/checking occurrences. W may read public, fixed, instance or
event fields and correlate frames and aliases on one scoped strategy. GC §7.0
defines the public fiber by designated public endpoints and approved whole
observations at original indices, admitting a solution only when one strategy
satisfies the complete proof grammar and W. Mathematical forgetting of private
proof fields is not permission to drop the fields before evaluating W.

Thus a proof field affects the fiber if a well-typed W can constrain it, even
when the proof field itself is absent from the designated output coordinates.
There is no claim that source programs dynamically inspect arbitrary proof
objects. This is the selected inference/evidence observation contract.

The exact W predicate vocabulary is deliberately open in source §3.4; primitive
field interfaces are supplied under L1 (§2), and additional checking laws are
adopted under §3.2. The pinned sources recover the native constructor grammar,
but not a closed universal catalogue of typed predicates on opaque external
evidence. This is the exact locator boundary for any stronger concrete census.

## 5. Conditional discriminating derivation

Established pinned inputs are the reviewed native CE and uniform inlet results.
The following is a **conditional attachment lemma**, not public adoption.

Hypotheses:

1. Source introduction/checking witnesses use the reviewed native grammar and
   its genuine independently supplied local laws. No foreign-kernel identity
   is assumed.
2. A proposed public certificate schema declares the full native X tuple and
   actual AliasGraph, with every row of §4 at its original telescope. Its
   allocator preserves the source §5.2 Shared/frame/event/EventProof keys,
   check-grammar nodes and complete dependent operands. This is the missing
   construction premise, not something proved by Joint-ID.
3. W is well-typed on those same transported incidences, and all independently
   supplied input/world/history evidence remains unchanged.

For a fixed allocated joint grammar, apply CE at each local actual id phase.
On the retained full tuple its F/G maps are identity on all logical fields;
on eliminated private raw aliases G uses the total AliasGraph. Hypothesis 2
routes these maps to the same shared/frame/event keys. Hence shared fields
cannot be copied, and local identity maps agree on overlaps. Conjunction and
sequential joins retain exactly the same current-world operands. Finite
proof/history development induction therefore preserves one entire scoped
strategy and W. Its restriction to the designated public coordinates gives
equal fibers for that attached certificate presentation and its native source
proof clauses.

This separates the remaining premise from proof normalization: it asks for a
source-free **schema and allocation map**, preserving already defined proof
slots. It does not ask for a new relation accessor or for every proof to be
canonical. It also proves no equation between source executions and VP's
complete production relation, and supplies no chosen public root or Direct law.

The already committed concrete discriminator is `Identity(A)` versus
`Compose(Identity(A),Identity(A))` in native §§1,3,6. Both local terms are
permitted; their tags, intermediate evidence and proof scopes remain distinct.
A typed W constraining the selected term retains that distinction under CE.
Replacing all such terms by Identity loses the Compose-selected fiber even
when raw values, endpoints and Echo agree. This is a citation of the existing
native discriminator, not another constructed toy countermodel or a claim
that every external witness has that precise grammar.

## 6. Failure conditions and stopping point

The conditional derivation fails if an observed proof field is absent, a local
choice is canonicalized, a Shared binder is copied, ViewLogic moves below a
challenge, EventProof loses its frame/event incidence, a partial phase gains
future completed data, port/root fields are identified from raw provider
equality, or Delta fails to cover a complete J arm. It also fails for foreign
witness rules without their independent bridge. These are named mutations;
none was executed.

The transformed candidate §6 asks for exact witness transport; native CE now
supplies its local constructor seam. A further alpha-copy or assumed-transition
checker would leave the public attachment/allocation premise untouched.
Recommend one next action: construct the finite public certificate schema and
allocation map using the §4 census and native CE, then submit that frozen
artifact for independent review. Actual public-root selection and ordinary
query recognition remain separately adjudicated gates.
