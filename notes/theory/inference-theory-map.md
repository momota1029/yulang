# Yulang inference theory map

Date: 2026-10-05 snapshot (assembled 2026-10-05)
Status: Research synthesis; navigation and classification only; not an Authoritative design
Scope: Current Yulang3 SCC-intrusion inference theory and its proof/implementation boundaries
Authority: Underlying documents retain their own status and scope. This map adds no theorem, semantics, carrier, rejection rule, or implementation authority.

This is a snapshot of a fast-moving research branch. “Current” below means supported by the cited reviewed/Authoritative source as of the date above, not that every upstream compiler path conforms to it. Bounded checkers and finite probes remain characterization evidence. Drafts, failed routes, and older progress descriptions are retained in place as research history.

## Reading the result labels

- **Theorem**: a general mathematical result as stated in its reviewed source.
- **Conditional theorem**: a result whose stated hypotheses or generated-source predicate remain part of the claim.
- **Characterization**: bounded executable or exhaustive evidence; never promoted here to a theorem.
- **Counterexample / obstruction**: refutes the specified shortcut or proof route, not a broader goal unless the source says so.
- **Open**: a remaining gate with its source quantifiers intact.
- **Historical / superseded**: still useful evidence, but no longer the active central condition for the stated gate.

## Current spine

### 1. Semantic core: endpoint inequality and complete callable interface

**Theorem/contract — one endpoint-dependent `A <: B`.** Concrete comparisons are resolved as a single directed query. Variable-bound propagation may use transitivity; two successful concrete comparisons are not composed as a substitute for a middle witness. Optional-record compatibility supplies a concrete nontransitivity witness. See [concrete compatibility boundary](../design/2026-10-03-concrete-compatibility-boundary.md) and the [endpoint-dispatch characterization](../progress/2026-10-05-inequality-endpoint-dispatch-playground.md).

**Contract — complete Function and coupled effects.** A Function is not just a value arrow. Its complete view contains the polarized value ports, entry/role information, and correlated effect ports. Effects are not independently subtyped Types. Source introduction/context chooses Pure or Handler before port interpretation; a prebuilt Pure callback value retains its actual role and entry, while the callback slot supplies an invocation view. Covariant rows use canonical flat form; concrete-bearing contravariant descriptors retain only structure supported by witnessed partial reverse addition. The user-approved mixed-row fragment distinguishes polarity: covariant `['e, write int]` allows the shared `'e` plus the concrete contribution when `'e` also occurs contravariantly; in the matching contravariant case, the supplied compatibility example is `int <: 'a` for `write 'a` in `'e`; the illustrated deep-handler covariant result carries `'e` with the attached `write int` contribution removed. This is a scoped semantic contract, not a general row-combination or family-variance theorem. Removal still needs source-owned attachment and complete deep-handler-image evidence; same-family support alone is insufficient. `never`, `Any`, empty rows, and polarized solver bounds stay distinct. See the [approved polarity-sensitive fragment](../design/2026-10-03-concrete-compatibility-boundary.md#9-user-directed-mixed-effect-row-fragment-2026-10-05), [approved handoff receipt](../../questions/2026-10-05-function-effect-row-denotation/receipt.md), [descriptor derivation audit](../progress/2026-10-05-function-effect-descriptor-derivation.md), [coupled effect interface core](../design/2026-10-01-coupled-effect-interface-core-draft.md), [callback context delivery](../design/2026-10-03-callback-context-delivery.md), and the accepted decisions in [tasks/current.md](../../tasks/current.md).

**Draft / open projection question — handler hygiene provenance.** The new `'e?` notation note explores an optional input-to-result provenance edge while capture authority ends at the receiver boundary. A later event in the same operation family, or mere causal succession, does not by itself witness the edge: evidence must identify the same request event or an existing typed-flow lineage. Projection alignment at receiver exit remains open. No syntax or `'e?` edge semantics, carrier, or implementation was selected; this notation question is distinct from the separately approved mixed-row fragment above. See [handler hygiene provenance notation](../design/2026-10-05-handler-hygiene-public-provenance-notation.md) and its [design-index summary](../design/INDEX.md).

**Required semantic indices.** The source/complete-presentation arguments depend on one joint assignment and relation, with occurrence, source owner, scope, provenance, typed path, receipts, `K,D` incidence, and existing adaptation/subtraction evidence preserved where they are live. A printed four-port descriptor is not by itself a complete membership or admission explanation. These are proof coordinates in existing source/presentation descriptions, not an approved new runtime carrier. See [source-indexed realization](../design/2026-10-04-source-indexed-callback-realization.md) §§2–5 and [certified transport](../design/2026-10-04-certified-callback-and-constrained-use.md) §§2–3.

**Counterexample / obstruction.** Direct composition of concrete successes is unsound as a complete solver rule; value-only Function shadows lose entry behavior (Value may Force where a retained Computation entry does not). These witnesses reject those reductions, not the full endpoint solver or a production source case. See [inequality endpoint characterization](../progress/2026-10-05-inequality-endpoint-dispatch-playground.md) and [Function-shadow obstruction](../progress/2026-10-05-function-shadow-obstruction-playground.md).

**Conditional result — callback execution composition.** For the reviewed Value-entry source rules, one invocation derives the operational skeleton `receipt; Force(D) >>= rebind >>= body >>= designated result consumer` within the complete `J_call` image. The body must include its designated consumer or that consumer must be bounded separately; the full image retains the receiver, native return delimiters, surrounding view and result adaptation. This is execution composition, not mixed-row descriptor membership or callback denotation adequacy. See [Function effect descriptor derivation](../progress/2026-10-05-function-effect-descriptor-derivation.md).

**Characterization / obstruction — shallow same-family resumption.** A bounded probe over 96 fixed two- or three-event histories from one source-flow origin finds a distinct later same-family request after one shallow handler resumes a raw suffix once; family-wide cancellation would erase it. The first event is assumed eligible and selected, and the outer context is only a terminal family filter. It rejects support-only global cancellation for that model, not attachment-aware partial reverse addition. The checker does not prove the source machine or any annotation rule. The selected mixed-row removal concerns the illustrated deep-handler case and still requires complete-image evidence.

**Open — effect descriptor bridge.** Source rules do not yet derive comparison-independent membership/admission for a mixed abstract/concrete descriptor at a role-derived polarized Function port: abstract components need correlated typed-port views, concrete components need owned typed family incidences, and their combination must share one nonempty `Rel_C` `(nu,K,D)` fiber. The source annotation must tie those views to request, continuation, path, attachment and handler-image evidence. The existing record does not establish that current evidence is insufficient, so no new carrier follows; shallow-handler principality remains conjectural.

### 2. Source to finite constrained presentation

**Conditional theorem — source adequacy target.** The source-interface adequacy draft defines fiberwise forward coverage from source executions into a complete interface, under the same `nu` and retained joint `K,D`, including future uses, latent closures/thunks, requests and resumptions. Its exact complete-interface embedding is a semantic reference construction, not a finite solver or proof that current HIR generation emits such a presentation. See [source-to-complete-interface adequacy](../design/2026-10-02-source-interface-adequacy-theorem.md) and [typed source owner realization](../design/2026-10-02-typed-source-owner-realization.md).

**Contract — callback literal B.** Expected context chooses Handler/boundary before visiting the inline literal body. Parameter, body, and result endpoints are synthesized independently; the completed Function is checked once by ordinary `F_lit <: F_cb`. Endpoint copying/equality is not permitted. A is allowed only as B-equivalent constraint scheduling/partial evaluation preserving acceptance, principal solutions, method/adapter choice, and residual/evidence behavior. Prebuilt Pure values take their distinct actual-role path. This is a source contract, not a claim that current HIR implements Apply or inline lambdas. See [callback context delivery](../design/2026-10-03-callback-context-delivery.md) §2 and [production callback endpoint-generation draft](../design/2026-10-04-production-callback-endpoint-generation-draft.md).

**Conditional theorem — entailed early propagation preserves B solutions.** If `F` is independently proved entailed by the complete B solution relation `S_B`, then `S_B ∧ F = S_B`, including all method/adapter/residual/evidence alternatives and the final B query. This holds without a bounded-domain assumption. It gives a mathematical criterion for an A scheduling step, not a production algorithm for discovering entailed predicates; endpoint-only projection cannot delete B witnesses. See [source factor coverage and complete-use preservation](../design/2026-10-05-source-factor-cover-and-query-preservation.md) §4.1 and its [progress record](../progress/2026-10-05-factor-cover-main-gate-attack.md).

**Conditional theorem — Theorem C.** For the explicitly decorated, finite source envelope and positive source relation generator stated in [source-generated callback structural theorems](../design/2026-10-04-source-generated-callback-structural-theorems.md), the generated actual/checked bounds transport under its conditions, with the original whole tuple and scopes retained. It covers a restricted immutable source envelope; it is not arbitrary HIR, all effect components, or production conformance.

**Theorem — source-indexed reference realization.** A finite constructor interpretation in the existing relation carrier gives membership and independent query-free admission rules for the specified reference source objects. Whole-observation typed projection preserves the stated Theorem C crosswalk, including finite latent/future/resumption histories. This proves the reference's crosswalk only, not that the compiler's production endpoints denote it. See [source-indexed callback realization](../design/2026-10-04-source-indexed-callback-realization.md) §§1–5.

**Conditional theorems — source-contract package.** C-realization proves membership/admission correspondence for its finite source base under active-root, local descriptor-typing, finite emission/admission, and transport-certificate hypotheses. C-abstraction extends this to the declared positive `H_G(R)` grammar only with paired `W/Z` parameters, hard-envelope, independent-admission, and transport hypotheses. These are reviewed conditional routes; `W/Z` is not the selected production rule and neither result defines Option A's still-underdetermined endpoint/admission clauses. See [source contracts and common allowance](../design/2026-10-05-source-contracts-and-common-allowance.md) §§3.5, 3.7.

**User-selected production policy — Option 2 with the approved Option A basis; complete rules open.** Production Function membership is based on complete typed observations in the actual descriptor's original `Rel_C` and fixed `(nu,K,D)` fiber, restricted by independently interpreted endpoint, role/entry, typed-path, origin, continuation, original-scope, authority, and retained-dependency conditions. Challenge admission is derived separately from the punctured typed context, independent of the pending comparison. This selects the semantic basis, not exhaustive clauses for endpoint satisfaction or admission; neither `P_ref` equality nor arbitrary port-shaped extras follow. The production target remains `D_C(xi) ⊆ D_A(xi)` and, for every `c ∈ D_C(xi)`, `P_A(c;xi) ⊆ P_C(c;xi)`. The source-constructor crosswalk is one sufficient route; extra members need an exhaustive rule and direct checked-containment proof preserving the complete observation and evidence. Theorem C and `P_ref` remain source-generated results, not production semantics. See the [approved Option 2 policy](../../questions/2026-10-05-production-function-bound-membership/approved-answer.md), [approved Option A basis](../../questions/2026-10-05-production-function-denotation/approved-answer.md), [derivation follow-up](../progress/2026-10-05-production-function-denotation-followup.md), and revised [production callback draft](../design/2026-10-04-production-callback-endpoint-generation-draft.md) §4.

### 3. Structural solving

**Theorem — normalized pure structural FMP / finite-fence completion (classification A).** For every satisfiable fixed normalized pure structural package in the stated carrier, an arbitrary permitted tree model yields a permitted simultaneous regular graph model of at most `8^N` states. Exact shared recursive descriptors, mandatory Record width, Function variance, invariant coordinates, and arbitrary rigid permission sets are included. The proof also closes complete finite-quotient conflict reflection and the former bounded-rank residual. It assumes no source-generated regularity predicate. See [finite-fence completion](../design/2026-10-04-structural-fmp-fence-completion.md) §§1–2, 9–12 and [proof/review record](../progress/2026-10-04-structural-fmp-proof.md).

**Historical / superseded — structural regularity sufficient conditions.** The earlier two-sided constructor-bound [preclosed witness theorem](../design/2026-10-04-preclosed-structural-regular-witness.md) remains a valid constructive conditional result, including its explicit bound and multiple-anchor cases. It is no longer the condition needed to establish pure structural FMP; its one-sided/unbounded-root gap is covered by finite-fence completion. Likewise the source-generated/root-only/one-open-anchor/selector theorems remain useful conditional source results, but are not premises of the unrestricted normalized pure FMP theorem. See [source-generated theorems](../design/2026-10-04-source-generated-callback-structural-theorems.md) and their [progress record](../progress/2026-10-04-source-generated-theorems.md).

**Characterization.** The finite-fence Python checker and generated package searches check the construction on bounded families and retain shrunk defects as regressions. They do not establish the theorem, choose production algorithms, or set resource limits. See [finite-fence playground](../progress/2026-10-05-structural-fence-playground.md).

**Open — finite constrained residual and effective projection.** The reviewed scoped-solving work gives equality quotienting, closed structural comparison, and a candidate factorization of solutions through finite open-bound residual edges under stable finite guard/evidence contexts. It does not yet close source-wide context finiteness, the full effective joint solver for residuals plus `Phi/K,D` and structural projection, or an effective principal projection. Pure FMP supplies regular-model existence for the normalized pure structural part; it does not itself solve the residual constraints, jointly project effects/guards, or give a production decision procedure. See [scoped constraint solving](../design/2026-10-03-scoped-constraint-solving.md), [open residual factorization](../design/2026-10-03-open-residual-factorization.md) §§1, 4–7, and the active-gate summary in [tasks/current.md](../../tasks/current.md).

### 4. Callback transport and safe hiding

**Theorem — total-definition elimination.** Fresh total coordinates defined at their original scopes can be forgotten while preserving old witnesses, complete observations, and their independently generated admission certificates. This needs no admission-uniformity condition for those new derived coordinates. **Theorem — linked equality.** The specified actual and checked reference bounds are equal on each checked-admitted complete fiber. Both results are in [callback coverage and source-preserving composition](../design/2026-10-05-callback-coverage-and-source-joins.md) §§2–3 and strengthen the earlier inclusion-only formulation for that exact linked pair.

**Theorem — old-witness Coverage characterization.** After a legal existential marginal over old witnesses, the exact linked comparison holds iff every actual complete observation (at a checked challenge) has a checked-admitted old-witness representative with the *same* complete observation and retained challenge. Uniform admission is sufficient but stronger. This does not move quantifiers across rigid binders and does not permit changing retained owner, request/continuation, path, or shared fiber. The finite safe-hiding dependency closure is a sufficient source-derived class, including proof that exposed requests/providers were produced and retained. See [coverage/source joins](../design/2026-10-05-callback-coverage-and-source-joins.md) §§4–5.

**Theorem — finite certified transport.** The certificate class transports every finite membership and punctured-context admission derivation for the complete presentation, including latent/future/resumption histories, under its approved finite transformations and their original scopes. For additional hiding, admission-uniformity across the forgotten fiber is a sufficient condition in that theorem; dropping it has a countermodel. The later Coverage result refines the condition for the specified linked pair. Neither theorem derives certificates from production generation. See [certified callback transport](../design/2026-10-04-certified-callback-and-constrained-use.md) §§2–4 and [admission-hiding characterization](../progress/2026-10-05-callback-admission-hiding-playground.md).

**Counterexample / obstruction — wrong joins.** Independently joining stage/type marginals can lose the original source witness or provider incidence (`higher` diagonal); substituting a local representative can also fail under a scalar selector. The source-preserving composition route retains whole original witnesses and their joined request/provider correlation. These refute shortcuts, not every common allowance. See [coverage/source joins](../design/2026-10-05-callback-coverage-and-source-joins.md) §6 and [experiment-guided record](../progress/2026-10-05-experiment-guided-main-gate-attack.md).

**Theorem — factor coverage and complete-use preservation.** For a finite source relation `T` presented as factors `A_i` with complete operand scopes `E_i`, and proposed coordinate bags `B_j`, if every factor scope is contained in some retained bag, then joining the projections of the same `T` reconstructs exactly `T`. Over independent factors on nontrivial product domains this coverage condition is also necessary for a *uniform* guarantee; that converse is not claimed for a particular constrained fiber or after an observation projection. Exact reconstruction is stable under adjoining any unchanged joint client and direct-query/evidence relation, with its original scopes. A fixed XOR/returned-provider construction shows that every proper coordinate projection can lose an observation-visible source dependency. This supplies a finite certificate for an existing logical decomposition; it does not authorize a descriptor formation rule or produce a common allowance. See [source factor coverage and complete-use preservation](../design/2026-10-05-source-factor-cover-and-query-preservation.md) §§2–5.

**Characterizations.** Scoped total-lift, admission-hiding, consumer-factorization, A/B equivalence, HIR-backed `id`/`zero`, and shallow-resumption effect probes cover only their finite models; counts and deliberately broken mutants are in the respective [2026-10-05 records](../progress/2026-10-05-callback-scoped-lift-playground.md), [consumer factorization](../progress/2026-10-05-callback-consumer-factorization-playground.md), [A/B equivalence](../progress/2026-10-05-callback-ab-solution-equivalence-playground.md), [source-indexed Function realization](../progress/2026-10-05-source-indexed-function-realization-playground.md), and [Function effect descriptor derivation](../progress/2026-10-05-function-effect-descriptor-derivation.md).

**Characterization — dependent use after scalar saturation.** In a two-value/two-provider package model, widening a returned scalar while preserving the callable owner keeps source observations included, but a later invocation can turn the extra scalar into a distinct request family. A separate same-challenge owner swap changes request provenance. This shows that local scalar erasure alone cannot prove projection equality after arbitrary dependent result consumption; it does not refute one-sided inclusion or interpret production membership. See the [mixed-value future-call probe](../progress/2026-10-05-callback-mixed-value-future-call-playground.md).

### 5. Principality

**Theorem — constrained-use construction, conditional exactness.** Fresh-copying a finite presentation, applying a supplied uniform descriptor graft, retaining the client's constraints, and adding one direct complete query at the designated exported root constructs an ordinary finite use. Its solution relation is exact iff the scoped extension entailment `C_V(v) => Ext_P(v)` holds for each public assignment. Thus no separate explicit `m_V` map-construction calculus is required for this route. The theorem does not prove the entailment for every valid view. See [certified callback and constrained-use results](../design/2026-10-04-certified-callback-and-constrained-use.md) §§5–8.

**Historical / superseded as a machinery requirement — explicit `m_V`.** Earlier proof attacks formulated a public-view map `m_V` as a construction target. Ordinary fresh-copy/graft/direct-query use removes the need for a separate map calculus when the query is retained; it does not remove the all-view completeness obligation. The current exact remaining criterion is `C_V(v) => Ext_P(v)` for every independently valid public view, plus legal common-descriptor realization. Do not report the universal all-view gate as solved.

**Conditional theorems and remaining open gate — common allowance.** A-extension constructs one legal common descriptor and proves `forall xi. forall s in S_xi. exists a. Q_xi(s,a)` for scoped guarantee-only active views satisfying the reviewed formation, source-allocation, complete-query, and envelope hypotheses. A-allocation proves ordinary fresh-copy/graft/direct-query extension through the actual common export for `V_alloc(S)`, with explicit scope, non-coverage/value, and allocation-certificate premises. Its A-allocation-abstraction corollary covers `V_alloc,H(S)` under paired positive `H_G(R)`/`W/Z`, hard-envelope, and transport certificates, including unanchored extras in that grammar. These conditional theorems narrow the earlier blanket formation/totality open statement; they do not solve unrestricted common formation, all independently valid public views, or production conformance. The exact clauses and grammar remain unselected for production. The `choose` witness rejects equality consolidation only; typed-pullback joins remain finite characterization, not descriptor realization. See [source contracts and common allowance](../design/2026-10-05-source-contracts-and-common-allowance.md) §§5–7, the [direct formation audit](../progress/2026-10-04-principal-contributor-bound-theorem.md#direct-common-descriptor-formation-audit-2026-10-05), [principal-scheme acceptance criteria](../progress/2026-10-04-principal-scheme-acceptance-criteria.md), [coverage/source joins](../design/2026-10-05-callback-coverage-and-source-joins.md) §8, and [contract-join characterization](../progress/2026-10-04-callback-principal-playgrounds.md).

**Conditional theorem and open extension — direct-query completeness.** A-allocation proves this extension for every `V ∈ V_alloc(S)` through the actual designated common export, with its allocation, scope, non-coverage/value-kernel and local-query premises. A-allocation-abstraction extends it to the matched `V_alloc,H(S)` class under paired positive grammar and certificates. For views outside those classes, prove `C_V(v) => Ext_P(v)` through the direct complete Function query at the actual designated exported root, with required occurrence/evidence and adapter resolution. Replacing that root with a common export additionally requires its own direct comparison; totality alone does not suffice. Neither finite public-use construction nor semantic containment implies the current resolver emits all evidence. The preserved quantifiers are `forall V. exists m_V` as the earlier statement and, for the retained-query route, its exact `C_V => Ext_P` criterion.

Factor coverage can prove that a *specified exact relation rewrite* preserves an already present direct query and all of its evidence alternatives. It does not prove that a valid `V` has a satisfying direct query, that the production resolver finds all such evidence, or that a required common descriptor is legal.

### 6. Production conformance and implementation boundary

**Open — production callback conformance under Option 2 and the approved Option A basis.** Derive exhaustive endpoint-satisfaction and independent admission rules from the approved complete-`Rel_C` basis and retained evidence, independently of whether the pending comparison succeeds, then prove `D_C(xi) ⊆ D_A(xi)` and `∀c ∈ D_C(xi): P_A(c;xi) ⊆ P_C(c;xi)` with complete observation, tuple/scope, authority/dependency, occurrence, and `nu,K,D` incidence preserved. Factoring every actual member through source entry/rebind/body/result-consumer/call composition is a sufficient reference route, not a mandatory production-membership rule. Any production-only observations still need exact membership and checked-containment evidence; port shape alone is not enough. The bounded HIR tests reach selected lambda/name forms, but current HIR lacks ordinary Apply and inline-lambda lowering and does not emit this complete relation. The endpoint-generation design remains Draft with no implementation authority. See the [approved Option A basis](../../questions/2026-10-05-production-function-denotation/approved-answer.md) and [derivation follow-up](../progress/2026-10-05-production-function-denotation-followup.md), [production callback draft](../design/2026-10-04-production-callback-endpoint-generation-draft.md) §4, [source-indexed realization](../design/2026-10-04-source-indexed-callback-realization.md) §7, the [approved policy and receipt](../../questions/2026-10-05-production-function-bound-membership/approved-answer.md), and [tasks/current.md](../../tasks/current.md).

**Theorem/contract — rebuild boundary.** Intrusion, variable/bound, and SCC internal state need not reverse-update across source edits; a changed inference component may be rebuilt. Downstream *inference* reuse may stop only when the complete generalized canonical interface is unchanged. Interface fields/equality, SCC split/merge handling, non-inference artifact invalidation, and atomic publication remain open design obligations. This is lifecycle direction, not implementation authority. See [Authoritative cross-edit rebuild addendum](../design/2026-10-04-scc-intrusion-cross-edit-rebuild-addendum.md).

**Implementation observations.** Current HIR/F5 behavior is not generally the new target: listed higher-order/application schemes are acceptance criteria, not current outputs. The relevant current body-effect row link is retained in the `ConstraintStore`; F5c summary nodes omit effect ports, but they are not the only available artifacts. The retained original Function terms, effect constraints, provenance, recipes and source-owner facts must be inspected before proposing a parallel carrier. See [F5 value-skeleton/HIR incidence](../progress/2026-10-04-production-f5-value-skeleton-hir-incidence.md), [principal acceptance criteria](../progress/2026-10-04-principal-scheme-acceptance-criteria.md), [F5 general Function foundation](../design/2026-09-21-f5-general-function-scheme-foundation-draft.md), and [scoped constraint solving](../design/2026-10-03-scoped-constraint-solving.md).

## Open gates at this snapshot

1. Derive exhaustive actual/checked production endpoint-satisfaction and independent admission clauses from the approved complete-`Rel_C`/fixed-`(nu,K,D)` basis under Option 2, then prove the full `D_C ⊆ D_A` and `P_A ⊆ P_C` crosswalk for the claimed envelope. The source-indexed rules and Theorem C give a sufficient route for source-generated members, not a mandatory witness form for production-only members. Close B-literal generation separately under its unchanged source contract.
2. For any proof route that hides admission-live old coordinates, establish Coverage or retain them at their original scopes; an independent direct containment proof may avoid source-witness factorization but must preserve the complete joint evidence.
3. Extend A-extension beyond its scoped guarantee-only active-view envelope: derive the legal common-descriptor interpretation at every original typed Function path, preserving shared descriptor identity alongside path-specific challenge/value carriers, then prove totality under the existing quantifiers for the broader claimed class.
4. Extend A-allocation and its matched abstraction corollary beyond `V_alloc(S)` / `V_alloc,H(S)`: prove the all-view extension entailment/direct-query completeness through the designated exported root, including actual resolver evidence; if a common descriptor replaces that root, prove the separate direct query from that common root.
5. Connect retained constraints and generalized constrained interfaces to soundness/principality, then specify canonical interface equality and production lifecycle behavior before any cutover.
6. Close finite residual factorization's source-context premises and the effective joint solving/projection of structural residuals with guards and `Phi/K,D`; regular existence alone is not effective inference.
7. Close the separate source State/reference and global environment/import bridge, including dynamic ownership, update/restart, captured access, repeated resumption, and `EnvStore`/`JointWF`.
8. Derive the mixed descriptor's annotation-to-occurrence/membership rule and justify targeted deep-handler removal from the complete image; keep the shallow-handler principal-type candidate conjectural. Then complete ordinary effect/handler semantics and the charter's later mandatory method-selection, role, and implementation-resolution gate for the claimed source envelope.

No new mathematical gap discovered while assembling this map is silently repaired here. Any proof hole noticed during subsequent work belongs in the relevant open gate with its exact quantifiers.
