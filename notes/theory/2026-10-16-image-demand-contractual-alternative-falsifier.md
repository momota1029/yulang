## 2. The original clause requiring a consumer

[Source contracts](../design/2026-10-05-source-contracts-and-common-allowance.md)
§2.1, lines85–105 supplies Primitive, constructor image, conjunction, union,
renaming, scoped binding and reference clauses. The primitive means its
independently specified **whole-tuple local relation**, not just a support set.
Its §3.7, lines295–331 permits the actual declared positive closure

```text
S = H_G(R)
S(y) iff G(y) and (R(y) or Z(y) or exists x,z. S(x) and W(x,y,z)).
```

Here G is the final tuple's complete descriptor/guarantee/admission/provider/
K/D/authority/scope envelope. Z may have no source anchor; W retains its
declared fixed and changed coordinates and provider/future rules. These are
real original clause forms. No particular new W or Z interpretation is
invented in this audit, and no unprovided relation is asserted inhabited.

Let `Image_H(y,u)` abbreviate the original complete shallow-image relation
with all original dependent indices, not an effect-set operation. For an
exact original outgoing target T, demand validity includes

```text
forall y,u. S(y) and Image_H(y,u) implies T(u).
```

Its **Z branch**, before any structural source decoding, therefore requires

```text
forall y,u. G(y) and Z(y) and Image_H(y,u) implies T(u).
```

For a W branch the corresponding operands are one shared `x,y,z`, the
original relation `W(x,y,z)`, final `G(y)` and `Image_H(y,u)`. A proof about
the image of x alone does not, without that W's local law, supply the demand
on the image of y. Original certification against G is not by itself a
certificate against an arbitrary later target T. This extracts an actual
consumer obligation; it does not assert that its premises hold or that no
effective local rule can establish it.

## 4. Why typed-core section 8 does not discharge this handoff

[Typed core](../design/2026-10-02-typed-computation-core-elaboration.md)
§8 lines747–768 fixes a finite regular complete-interface graph and compares
two descriptions of the **same component and actual entry/consumer code**,
with matching constructors, operations and labeled correspondences.
Lines789–820 generate constraints for genuine support upper-bound fields;
all non-support fields remain unchanged. An admission predicate outside
the membership grammar is retained identically, explicitly without an
effective equality solver (lines803–812).

Accordingly that checker can weaken an already supplied certificate while
preserving its fixed challenge domain and routing. It does not calculate
the demand preimage through Z/W and a shallow handler, derive a missing
primitive certificate, or normalize a previously unknown provider's full
phase family. Simply retaining G, Z/W or an admission predicate identically
does not produce the displayed target implication. This is the exact rule
domain exclusion, not an inference from missing enum variants.

In particular, one may first establish a clause's image using a genuine
local rule and then use section 8 on a suitable same-component certificate.
The ordering matters: section 8 supplies no reason that the first local rule
exists for every original primitive/alternative.
