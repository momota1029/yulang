The reference-only lift therefore stops exactly at `demand_introduction.C` (and independently D), before a well-formed allocating record can be constructed. It would be false to report a failed original Call witness: no complete witness refuting conservativity has been exhibited. What fails is the proposed **definitional construction from the available fields**. Erasure of any genuinely well-formed allocating decoration would retain the original Call clauses, but lifting every original witness requires this additional authentic typed introduction and its laws.

Two proposed completions expose different obligations; neither is established conservative:

* **Shared completion S.** A genuine Shared/retained active-environment introduction at sigma_C/sigma_D would allocate C,D once. These are concrete competing required fields and lifting tests, not two lawful conservative completions.
