## 3. The single construction attempt

Grant the favorable case in which both endpoints have complete callable
clauses, retaining the same actual decompositions, captures, independent
domain, event restrictions, scopes and whole witnesses. General union-valued
`A_f` still needs its original exhaustive alternative action.

Unfold the supplied source front at one actual tuple:

```text
p : Phi(Z).V(A_f,f,r_f,e).
```

Contextual membership exposes every actual provider decomposition, its
hereditary captures, complete actual admission at each independent challenge,
and all actual whole observations/developments/futures at `P_A_f[Z]`.
Because the provider and whole challenge are retained, the target admission
component can reuse these witnesses when the complete target domain and
immediate preconditions match. The remaining constructor goal is the target
whole observation/future envelope `P_F_c[Z]`, including its hereditary
result, world, carrier and continuation fields and all target immediate
guards. Source comparison inversion supplies only its final-M counterpart.

Typed core §8 offers a more concrete route: paired finite complete graphs,
fixed assumptions/imports/routing fields, and weakening of genuine guarantee
support leaves. Attempt to use that construction on these one-step clauses.
Its exact additional condition must be stronger than paired shape equality:

> For every hereditary occurrence in the complete matched clauses, the
> source and target read the **same component of Z at the same complete
> dependent index**, under the actual legal whole map. This includes endpoint
> or descriptor identity, actual value/provider/root, current event, original
> assignment, scope/binder position, incidence, shared witnesses and every
> restriction/future-demand coordinate. No endpoint-changing recursive
> membership lookup is discharged merely by ordinary final-model inclusion.

In notation, each paired hereditary leaf must be literally

```text
Z.Q(i_source) = Z.Q(i_target), with i_source = i_target under the actual map,
Q in {V,W,T,Car}.
```

The equality is between actual indices, not printed payload shapes, equal
final-M denotations or matching raw IDs. A source-owned definitional alias
with a proved complete indexing equality would suffice; no such equality is
constructed for `A_f,F_c` in the inspected selected source rule.

### Conditional complete-clause action

Suppose a supplied finite clause correspondence satisfies all of these:

1. Identical independent complete challenge/history/future domains and actual
   provider operations/decompositions, with one original legal whole map.
2. The displayed exact index equality at **every** hereditary lookup,
   including recursive descriptor references interpreted in Z. No scoped
   binder is moved or independently hidden.
3. Matched constructor tags/ordering/whole operands and complete alternative
   inventories. Opaque/import/W/Z alternatives retain their own independent
   contracts, including introduced providers and future interfaces.
4. Each target immediate predicate is identical to its source predicate or
   has an independently supplied, background-independent implication proof.
   Only genuine support guarantees may use the §8 weakening proof. Every
   remaining guard, operation instance, authority, bound and incidence
   predicate retains its full independent meaning.

Then there is a conditional `N_Z` for every fixed-sort relation tuple Z,
without requiring Z to be a fixed point or to satisfy VIncl.

**Derivation.** Rebuild the target one-step membership clause from the
source clause's evidence. At a hereditary leaf reuse the identical `Z.Q(i)`
evidence, using condition 2. At an immediate leaf apply condition 4's exact
identity or implication. Conjunction reuses the shared tuple; an alternative
uses its matched arm; an image/scoped witness reuses the entire original
witness at its original position. At each fixed universal challenge or
future binder, instantiate the source proof on the identical target
challenge and apply this same clause construction pointwise. Constructor
ordering, raw handles and current resumed states remain unchanged.
Recursive hereditary references are Z lookups at this **one-step** front;
condition 2 reuses them rather than claiming to unfold or convert Z itself.
The exhaustive arm correspondence handles every independently licensed
alternative, including unanchored extras. The rebuilt field is precisely
`Phi(Z).V(F_c,f,r_f,e)`. This is the whole non-hole front. QED, conditionally.

This is a bounded sufficient construction criterion, not a new source
comparison rule or an acceptance restriction. It specializes the proposed
action to a checkable unchanged-index case. It does not prove that every
accepted comparison, or the particular generated `A_f,F_c`, meets it.

### Where this source attempt stops

The selected Call generation constructs no complete paired graph/certificate
whose hereditary indices satisfy condition 2. Its independent existential
`F_c` may have a different result/latent descriptor while final-model VIncl
still holds. Typed core §8 requires matching ordinary endpoints and complete
evidence for its conditional certificate-weakening theorem; it does not
identify arbitrary-background membership coordinates merely because graph
nodes are paired. The shared package also keeps the incoming and upper-use
coordinates distinct. Literal complete-clause identity remains a sufficient
special case already recorded by Shared-NonHole; it is not established by
this source's registration or capture route.

The first unresolved last-rule premise is therefore an actual source-owned
complete clause correspondence proving the unchanged hereditary indices
(and every target immediate predicate), or a different justified observation
action at precisely `Z=B_as` and the enlarged backgrounds. It is not absence
of provider identity or root/incidence data. Those data are already retained.
Adding a field named `Cmp` or retaining `e_value` again would not prove this
missing action. Defining its new source semantics is beyond this assignment,
so the attempt stops here.

## 4. Consequence for RT.2 and the next falsifier

Conditionally, a returned complete indexed tuple outside the declared
`H_a union H_s` holes unfolds its relative membership into
`Phi(B_as).V(A_f,f,r_f,e)`. A supplied clause correspondence from §3 then
gives the requested non-hole target front. The same construction is uniform
over later lawful restrictions because its full indices and predicates,
including current configurations, are retained. This does not replace an
exact apply/step hole assumption with a constructed front; that remains the
separate simultaneous source-construction obligation.

**Next named falsifier:** audit one actual source comparison certificate's
completed returned-provider occurrence. If its source clause reads
`Z.V(C,v,r,e')` and its target reads `Z.V(D,v,r,e')` with different complete
indices, yet its claimed uniform action has only final-model VIncl(C,D) or
matching printed structure at that edge, condition 2 fails. That single
occurrence invalidates this certificate route. It does not by itself refute
an action proved specifically for B_as, a lawful complete indexing equality,
or full Yulang semantics. This is a source-certificate audit condition, not
another executed arbitrary-Z/Boolean model or an asserted legal source pair.

Proof-economy classification: the non-hole readout required for sound closure
introduction remains A, with approved ordinary inference constraints retained.
The source occurrence/identity/scope facts are construction data and can be
retained. This audit does **not** show that the owner already knew and
discarded the relative action, so classifying that action as reconstruction
debt D would be unsupported. Two earlier methods left the same semantic
premise open; this attempt changes method to constructor-output inspection
and identifies the exact missing retained-clause invariant. No further toy
probe or semantic rule is proposed as a completed repair.

Recommended next action: have the comparison owner expose one complete
actual paired-clause derivation for this source, then audit every hereditary
lookup for the exact index equality above. If an endpoint-changing edge is
required, return that edge and its consumer to the primary for a genuinely
background-specific proof or comparison-construction design; do not infer
its action from ordinary final VIncl.
