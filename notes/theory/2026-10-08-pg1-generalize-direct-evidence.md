## 6. Finite first-query certificate

Construct one whole fresh use at event `u` as certified-use §5.1 prescribes.
Freshen `a_L` to `a_(u,L)` and all its incident fields while fixing `rho_L`;
then graft **uniformly** `a_(u,L):=T`. The graft/link graph is selected from
`V` before `v`, challenges and histories. Retain `C_V` unchanged. No other
port gets an independent graft.

By the source-rule occurrences and §5's independent complete-clause checks,
the grafted `D_L^u,M_L^u` and `D_V,M_V` have matching original operators,
ordered operands, source/provider references and scopes. A finite certificate
can therefore be constructed, not supplied as an extensional equality oracle:

```text
for each matched primitive/descriptor/typed predicate occurrence p:
  same relation contract, entire operand tuple and original scope
  --------------------------------------------------- Eq identity leaf
                  Eq_Gamma(p_L^u,p_V)

same original operator C, non-child tuple, paths and binder position;
Eq_Gamma(child_L^u,child_V) for every actual child
--------------------------------------------------------------- Eq-C
                 Eq_Gamma(C_L^u,C_V)

these rules through the complete admission and membership graphs
  yield Eq_Gamma(D_L^u,D_V), Eq_Gamma(M_L^u,M_V)
Eq_Gamma(M_L^u,M_V) ------------------------------- Eq as Le
                            Le_Gamma(M_L^u,M_V)

same Function head, actual role/entry/consumer, non-coverage interface;
Eq_Gamma(D_L^u,D_V); Le_Gamma(M_L^u,M_V);
one original scope-preserving assignment/incidence map throughout
--------------------------------------------------------------- Function
          Direct(R_L^u,R_V; the displayed finite proof DAG)
```

Every source-aligned Call/future-invocation occurrence keeps its actual
entry/receiver and designated consumer operands; Bind keeps the ordered
suffix/current state. Matching a static activation reference does not assert
that a concrete receiver has executed. Non-positive children use `Eq`, not
an unjustified monotonicity leaf. A supplied registered positive recursive
provider is paired by its original equations with complete alternative
accounting; the projection body itself is nonrecursive. Infinite history or
provider unfolding is unnecessary. Identity leaves refer to complete
primitive meanings; they do not derive those meanings or new `DescMem` laws.

The same final whole-observation projection is used on both sides. Semantically
the certificate gives identical complete challenge domains and observations
at the same original assignment, including arbitrary finite future histories.
The `Function` step is valid **under H-query**. This is stronger than
asserting semantic reflexivity and weaker than proving an actual resolver
contains such a step. In particular it cannot prove `Admit` from successful
`Direct`, supply H-active, or compose child concrete successes.
