## 3. Bounded inversion and the reduced witness

The reduced source is `my apply f = f 1`. Removing the other declarations
does not remove the missing inner attachment. Its resolved shape is


This is a reduced dependency witness, not a semantic counterexample or a
claim of globally shortest text. The following four cuts distinguish the
available evidence without executing a model.

| Supplier | Actual conclusion/output | Missing conversion to original emission |

**Inversion proof.** A Code-Call conclusion for c in the selected grammar
inverts to its actual Application/Gen-Call-0 origin, original ReifyOrigin,
operand checking origins and original complete result port (§3.4). These
premises are not semantic truth, but are required formation derivations.
The general §4.4 producer introduces a local relation and ReceiveSchema,
not a rule with original emitted-membership conclusion. The selected
Gen-Call-0 introduction in source-generation §4.2 requires its actual
Name/Name singleton: substituting literal 1 leaves its `u_x -> d_x` premise
unsupplied. O0's OC-CallEff inverts to an already emitted e; owner introductions
also require e. Thus none of these rules introduces the missing membership

inventory stops at that unsupplied premise. Adding an actual original supplier
would invalidate the negative inventory claim and is precisely the next cut.

This does not infer that a literal cannot have an argument occurrence, or that
all original rules require a Name argument. It establishes that the supplied
