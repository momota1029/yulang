actual captured closure's membership, and clause/reference incidence at
original registered roots. They do not instantiate an exhaustive active
declaration catalogue for the distinct `g_step`, or construct its public
registration/exposure certificate. Thus H-declarations and H-register cannot

impossibility theorem for a finite public representation.

this new public registration from an inclusion of frames. Even if original
kernel inputs are granted as H-local, that root join remains unsupplied.

at Strict's independent initial context/whole-carrier domain contract. The
selected sources specify its required complete operands and quantifier order
but take its complete laws as inputs. They do not enumerate or select a
concrete exhaustive context/history/arm kernel. The distinction matters:
these laws may be supplied in an actual H-local instance; this note does not
claim they are false or that any particular unnamed Z arm exists.
