admission and physical emission. `a` includes bound, admission, emission,
physical owner/lane/index/current operand and applicable processing relation.
Raw occurrence and current canonical representative are checked separately.
The predicate permits several witnesses per native member and several proofs
of one witness; it requires neither native-ID inversion nor a new runtime ID.

For each FunctionPort node, check the actual Function endpoints, field,
Swap/Preserve orientation, child endpoints, scoped flow and source comparison.

common bound owner, lower/upper polarity, actual child endpoints, and the
composition of their dependent contexts. The upper premise need not itself be
a contribution witness; it supplies the actual constraint used to transport
the lower witness. Derived/Transport nodes require their specific owning rule;
their enum names alone supply no source judgment. Cycles supply no leaf.

## Minimal shortcut falsifiers and scope derivation

These are specification countermodels to weakened predicates, not executed
source counterexamples or proof that current code performs subtraction.

1. **Wrong negative position:** two distinct negative incidences D1 and D2
   permit the same family k; one authentic w reaches only D2. A predicate
   checking `Permission(D1,k)` plus an equal-family seed returns At(D1,w)
   without a delta into D1. Two incidences and one witness suffice. Even if
   ordinary canonicalization shares their underlying row, raw D/path and source
   transport remain necessary; that row equality supplies no missing delta.
2. **Replay with one premise erased:** keep a lower carrying w but replace its
   upper constraint by one reaching a different incidence. A lower-only search
   cannot distinguish the derivations. The ordered upper BoundKey and source
   comparison are the discriminator; swapping inputs is also invalid.
3. **First Origin:** one interned relation can retain Origins o1 and o2, while
   only o2 belongs to the selected source occurrence/instance. Selecting o1
   before checking authentic source realization can report the wrong source;
   rejecting o1 without considering o2 can miss a valid alternative. Correctness
   cannot depend on Origin vector order. This is not a claim that a concrete
   run with those exact Origins was executed.
4. **Direct versus annotated route:** the same w can reach a demand directly
   and through its annotated incidence. An annotation-only traversal can miss
   the direct arrival; a family-wide transfer can remove another contribution
   w2 of k. Sharing the association requires an actual same-w derivation, with
   the corresponding demand/use/lineage, and retained pending checks when the
   direct route arrives first. One route's failure does not refute all routes.

There is also a concrete static discriminator for treating all scope changes
as identity. From `candidate_scope.rs:111–143`, take empty inputs/protections,
scope h and absent input t. `protect_body(h,t)` adds h; `release(h)` removes h.
Thus protect-then-release yields `{}`, while release-then-protect yields `{h}`,
by `then`/`evaluate` (`:148–212`). No execution is needed for this substitution.
This is a characterization of the native transformer clauses, not proof of
source scope semantics. The module explicitly excludes members/capture grants;
injective remap checking (`:214–263`) is not a proof that theta or dependent w
is transported lawfully, nor that extrusion identification is injective renaming.
`candidate_context.rs:2569–2580` explicitly refuses nonidentity scoped effect
self-relations before publication, since their transport semantics are not
established in that bounded experiment. This is an inspected failure boundary,
not permission to exclude such original source cases from the parent theorem.
