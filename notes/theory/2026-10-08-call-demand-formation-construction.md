## 3. Conditional construction theorem

**Assembly cut.** With the registered hole values, context and guards in item
3 fixed, the selected checked-challenge *assembly constructor case* has exactly
the remaining checked whole-carrier admission obligation in item 5.
Supplying that obligation constructs a complete `d_c in D_F_c` with these
same fields; inversion of an application of this assembly constructor retains
that same admission field. This is a characterization of this constructor,
not an exhaustive iff theorem for every foreign or abstract admission rule.

**Proof.** Semantic input realization §2 gives four independent inputs:
punctured context, other-value environment, actual registered hole values and
incidences, and checked whole-carrier admission. Item 3 fixes the first three
and their consistent shared coordinates. Item 5 supplies the fourth. Ordinary
dependent record introduction therefore produces `d_c`; its projection
recovers the admission field. Assembly contains no actual-U acceptance,
invocation-output safety or Q predicate. No argument execution is needed.

**Conditional receiver consequence.** Under items 1–5, the exact returned U
accepts that same whole carrier at `C_e,w_e`, and every actual complete,
pending, administrative or zero-step receiver observation and admitted
continuation/future development belongs to `P_F_c(d_c)` with its full envelope.

**Proof.** Apply item 1's same-value action to obtain
`ValueMem_F_c(v,r,e)` on the identical decorated tuple. Assemble `d_c` as
above. Eliminate contextual Function membership at the retained actual
decomposition `Act(v,U,r)`: clause 2 gives `ActualAdm(v,U,d_c)`, whose
projection includes actual `CarrierContract(U)` acceptance and its original

## 6. Failed routes and failure conditions

Two routes were inspected and stopped at the same premise:

1. **Argument-result matching.** Complete argument evidence or matching
   printed result endpoints does not by itself supply the declared inlet's
   admission, contextual guards or entry/role incidence. The needed whole
   interface transport is not a selected source rule in the inspected scopes.
2. **Strict/operational inversion.** Expanding Strict exposes the designated
   Force/rebind/body/return operation and complete observation contract. Its
   demand domain already requires independent inlet admission. Using that
   domain to derive its missing admission reverses constructor dependence.

This is an identified missing premise, not a proof that lawful source
formation is impossible, and not an admitted-source counterexample.
No third equivalent proof variant or toy checker was run.

Dropping item 5 prevents the selected challenge constructor application.
Dropping complete context/guard fields likewise prevents assembly even if a
raw inlet admits t. Dropping same-value membership/inclusion leaves no
actual-U elimination. Replacing U or independently choosing shared witnesses
invalidates the conclusion at the retained CalRet. Discarding entry effects,
pending/future fields or changed-domain actions narrows the claimed contract.
These are logical missing-premise tests, not executed mutations or minimized
Yulang witnesses.
