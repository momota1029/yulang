## Smallest proposed admission challenge, and why it stops

The smallest interface obligation found here needs no request, recursion,
capture or second export:

```text
one id invocation receives a returning argument with latent provider p;
id returns p through the typed result path;
the context makes one future invocation of returned p with carrier t.
```

For this to be a source-admitted discriminator, independently supply:
`p`'s complete contract and actual provider identity, admission of the original
argument/receipt, one original joint `xi`, and admission of future carrier `t`
under `p`'s declared challenge domain. Then identify a concrete transformed
admission clause that loses a required challenge or admits a new challenge with
an unsatisfied complete guarantee. Those certificates and that clause are not
provided by the selected formation rules or the candidate P2/P4 table.

Under the existing conditional typed transport, this challenge instead returns
the same `p`: the source and correctly interpreted wire can use the same future
contract. Thus it is **an unsupplied obligation template, not a counterexample**.
Assigning an arbitrary admission bit, choosing a provider with a mismatched
challenge contract, or treating a matching endpoint type as admission would
manufacture the missing supplier. This lane stops before doing any of those.

Removing the latent provider/future invocation reduces the obligation to current
entry/result transport and no longer tests future-provider admission. There is
no established smallest bad source program: source admission and a failing
transformed rule would both have to be established before minimization can
start. The predecessor's request/divergence entry controls are not repeated.

Failure conditions for this bounded finding: an in-scope adopted constructor
actually supplies all these clauses; independently typed source evidence and
a concrete candidate clause establish a discriminating challenge; or the
primary narrows the intended theorem to a contract already supplying P2/P4.
Any such new supplier warrants reviewing that dependency rather than treating
this absence finding as permanent. Option 2 extras also require their separate
complete admission/domain accounting; positive membership alone proves none.
