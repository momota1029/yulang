## 3. Construct the original frontier: merged and nonmerged cases

```text

```

The rewritten first demand uses y as its result. Its actual Function Result

`candidate_apply_value:847–872` chooses the negative-upper side at a. Thus
there is a physical outgoing upper `a -> y`. This is variable elimination,
not transitivity of successful concrete comparisons.

This proves the bounded frontier/contribution route. It does not assert a
