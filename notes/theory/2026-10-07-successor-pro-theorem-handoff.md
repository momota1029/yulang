## How to use this handoff

Do not consume the ninety-node DAG as ninety separate tasks. Use the original
node statements as coverage and historical proof evidence, then discharge the
constructor/transition cases of these four families. Preserve the current
canonical status until an actual proof is independently reviewed. “Four” counts
independent proof families, not atomic obligations or a claim that the hard
work is finished.

There are **specification inputs before proof**. In particular, independent
original Function position formation (`H_eff`), original occurrence/owner/
contribution/license introduction and the complete selected production
descriptor/member/admission rules are not all supplied. Identify and formally
specify these under existing Authority; do not fill them with the conclusion
of a desired theorem. The local OC-CallEff proposal remains unadopted. A new
semantic choice requires its own reviewed, approved scope. Missing
formalization alone does not demonstrate a choice for the user.

### Shared notation and immutable constraints

- `L` is the independently specified approved source/abstraction contract.
  `Legal_L` does not mean “accepted by the current inference implementation”.
- `S` is any supported source component, `B` its original binder tree, and
  `rho` its rigid imports and non-generic captures. The supported envelope may
  not be narrowed by a proof implementation or certificate checker.
- `xi=(nu,K,D)` retains its original scoped quantifier tree. “Strategy” means
  a coherent assignment/witness choice at that tree, never a prenex collection
  of independent per-port witnesses.
- `J_S` is the complete generated relation with original alternatives and
  evidence; `P_S` is the actual designated finite public interface and residual.
  A source may generate inconsistent constraints. Demand formation does not
  require a successful comparison and does not promise acceptance.
- `U` is any finite ordinary client graph: independent incoming instances,
  aliases, internal SCC uses, and joint client constraints/evidence. There is
  no fixed bound on the number of permitted finite future interactions.
- `D_A,Sat_A,P_A` and the checked interpretation are independent original
  production judgments. Include all approved compatible contexts and every
  licensed Option 2 production alternative, even without a source witness.

No theorem may use numeric IDs, source spelling/position, endpoint equality,
type shape or `Q` success to establish semantic formation. IDs may index already
justified evidence. No theorem may add annotations, user proof modes, source
restrictions, inference regressions or a closed-world replacement. Preserve
actual roles/entry, directional upper protection and independently existing
lower/provider protection. Concrete comparison successes are non-transitive.

## THEOREM 1: original source construction and constructor agreement (F1)

### Exact statement:

For every independently well-formed source component `S` in `L` at `(B,rho)`,
the source elaboration/constraint-generation construction produces a finite
`J_S` and staged derivation `E_S` at the same original scopes, with:

1. Every source form in the approved envelope has its proper construction
   case. Every emitted source rule and every alternative is justified by that
   case or an independently justified transformation. The construction is
   independent of successful `Q` and does not exclude a form because this proof
   lacks a case.
2. Resolved lexical/capture identities, shared roots, original typed paths,
   source upper occurrences, owner/slot/contribution incidences and licensing
   evidence are introduced only by their legitimate original judgments. Their
   symbolic dependencies and original scopes agree with `J_S`.
3. Where original semantic typing/realization needs a declarative input
   strategy or local-law instance, the constructor gives a uniform derivation
   from that **independent input** at its original scope. It does not require
   solver success to create a slot, path, receiver, seed or authority. A staged
   structural result is not mislabeled a completed semantic certificate.
4. The full source constructor/evidence tree agrees with the original
   interpretation on generated instances. For a complete owned invocation,
   it constructs the original joint witness before the family member:

   ```text
   exists a in I_orig(X).
     Inc_orig(a,beta,s,p0,u_f,c;X) and
     forall z in F_C(X;xi). Cover_orig(a,z;X).
   ```

   All surrounding original binders stay outside/inside this local expression
   exactly as required by their dependencies. `I_orig` is not redefined as a
   builder image, and equal observations do not identify distinct witnesses.
5. Emitted attachment/license access and profile-entry access return the exact
   witnesses carried by this construction. Complete profile coverage and
   inversion over all original licenses use an exhaustive, independently
   justified rule inventory; unknown or conservative arms are not omitted.
6. For every original-scoped strategy satisfying the **complete** generated
   obligations of `J_S` and carrying every required evidence result, the same
   constructor induction discharges the staged semantic premises of `E_S`
   and constructs an independently defined source/contract typing and
   realization derivation at the same assignments, original scopes and joint
   witnesses. Satisfaction of a subset of emitted atoms, separate marginal
   witnesses or missing query/method evidence is insufficient. This direction
   does not use successful `Q` to introduce source formation, and it does not
   define `Legal_L` or the independent relation as a builder image.

F1 therefore owns both generation and the generated-satisfying-strategy to
independent-derivation direction. Clause 6 completes staged semantic inputs
only after their full obligations and evidence are supplied; source formation
itself stays independent of successful queries. F4 owns the other direction:
every independent legal source derivation must lift through the generated
relation and the actual export. Both use the same independent original rules;
neither direction defines that rule inventory by compiler success.

The construction statement does not by itself prove source execution safety,
solver satisfiability, all-program inference completeness, or exhaustive
production membership. Those are F2–F4. In particular, joint ROWS realization
under the independent local semantic inputs is not inferred from individually
inhabited fields or an empty observed effect support.

### Why genuinely semantic:

Original typed occurrence, owner/contribution incidence, actual complete Call
typing, licensing and uniform coverage are judgments about the intended
language. A structurally matching tuple can violate any one of them. The
currently missing O0/O1/C1/J0 cases cannot be synthesized by a retained ID.

### Why design cannot remove it without changing behavior:

An ordinary higher-order Call still needs a typed invocation position and
correctly protected source contribution, and a natural unannotated formal
must obtain them from source inference. Removing the original rule or accepting
arbitrary owner/license fields would change meaning or permit invalid evidence.
Design can remove **later recovery**, not the initial justification.

### Dependencies:

- Function-view Authority §§2,5; nested-block and directional decisions;
  independently specified source/original rule family.
- Existing `Gen-Call-0` schema, `DIR_LOCAL`, `RS_LX`, conditional `CALL_REL`,
  `CALL_TYPE`, and source-contracts §§2–3 with their actual local inputs.
- Actual C0 inputs: CI-Operands and retained CalRet; argument typing at actual
  `C1` jointly compatible with the same provider's whole carrier; ordered
  receipt/entry/body/consumer/return/resumption laws.
- Original owner/contribution/joint-incidence introduction and complete
  licensing elimination. These are declared missing specification/proof cases,
  not assumed “WellFormedSourceCertificate” input.

### Shared proof pattern and stop condition:

Induct on source derivations and the original evidence constructors. Name,
Result, Lambda/Delay, record, Call, Bind/request, original license and
scoped/recursive-reference cases are the architecture's §6.1 table. Each
case proves all its field invariants together. If a last rule is absent,
return the exact missing formalization and governing scope; do not introduce
another transport relation or a theorem with that missing conclusion as premise.

## THEOREM 2: independent open-context and production safety (F2)

### Exact statement:

For every F1-generated component, original admitted environment/strategy and
independently compatible punctured context in the approved domain, construct
the original configuration/provider relation and prove it invariant under
each allowed execution/interaction step:

```text
Related(C,I;B,rho,xi) and C -> C'
  implies exists I'. I -> I' and Related(C',I';B,rho,xi).
```

The relation retains actual provider/root, current world/store, roles/entry,
original operation/event/authority, scopes, live captures, receiver lifetime,
raw continuation and ordered pending suffix, plus all symbolic family `K,D`
incidences. Returns, requests, divergence's finite observable prefixes,
admissible responses, raw resumes and all finite future calls/forces are
included. On a raw resume use the current resumed state and original suffix;
do not replay receipt. Initial/import root extension is proved, not assumed
from the existence of an abstract world relation.

The same independent interpretation must also satisfy the required production
square, at **every original scoped `xi`**:

```text
actual source observations inhabit their actual descriptor and Sat_A;
D_C(xi) subseteq D_A(xi);
forall h in D_C(xi). P_A(h;xi) subseteq P_C(h;xi).
```

Membership is exhaustive for the selected independently specified Option A/2
rules. Include every unanchored conservative member and every abstract provider's
independent future-use/admission law. Neither membership nor admission is
defined by comparison success or source reachability. No source execution is
required for a production-only observation.

The compiled executable's operational behavior must agree with the source
under the implementation relation I5; a conservative type denotation is not
permission for additional executable effects. This theorem permits a sound
conservative denotation and does not demand equality with exact trace support.

### Why genuinely semantic:

This is the protection against wrong provider/world, illegal capture/release,
invalid operation handling, lost continuations and unsound production extras.
Open imports and future users need not have participated in the current source
derivation, so source-record retention alone cannot certify their compatibility.

### Why design cannot remove it without changing behavior:

The approved inlet answer explicitly includes all independently compatible
contexts, and Option 2 explicitly permits observations without a source
witness. Narrowing either domain, or making admission depend on `Q`, would
change approved semantics. The economy is proving the required universal laws
directly, rather than first reconstructing a duplicate generated-world domain.

### Dependencies:

- F1 original source constructions and independently defined descriptor,
  membership, admission, initial/import, State and operation/method rules.
- Function-view Authority §5.5, approved Option A/2 and inlet-domain answers.
- Closed guarded knot and exact `REC_INIT_SELF` scopes; actual joint recursive
  descriptor/member/environment validity and all other included initializers
  remain required. Do not replace their semantics with inference rejection.
- Conditional SV, Call, history/transport results only after actual source
  premises hold. Finite-history reflection may be replaced by a justified
  guarded introduction proof, not by unfolding equations alone.

### Shared proof pattern and stop condition:

One configuration/provider invariant; induction on finite transition/history
derivations (with the legitimate guarded recursive argument); and one
induction on the actual independent admission/member grammar. Primitive,
State, dispatch, release and method cases are explicit modules of this proof.
There is no unproved generic “all primitives safe” assumption to hide them.
If using source-contracts §3.7's positive W/Z grammar, first establish that it
is the selected complete production grammar and its parameters/domains meet
the hypotheses; its conditional theorem does not select that grammar.

## THEOREM 3: exact constraint solving and whole-phase preservation (F3)

### Exact statement:

On each finite F1-generated input inside the exact approved practical
resource envelope, every actual solver, role/method fixed-point, generalizer,
residualizer and instantiator transition preserves and reflects the complete
original scoped solution-and-evidence relation.

For a transition `J -> J'` with its declared legal coordinate action, this
means both:

```text
every scoped solution/strategy of J' reflects to one of J;
every scoped solution/strategy of J extends/transports to one of J'.
```

Fixed public/import coordinates and every actual direct-query/method evidence
alternative are preserved. Fresh coordinates are chosen at their original
dependency positions; hiding is a certified scoped operation, not arbitrary
existential projection. For an equality quotient use its legal quotient map;
for freshening/graft use the same map on all incident facts. These laws are
about full strategies and query evidence, not equality of separate root marginals.

The algorithm terminates with an exact decision or an exact finite retained
residual within its specified contract. An unsatisfiable result reflects
actual inconsistency; an accepted residual still imposes its pending
obligations. An executable success includes evidence for each required
query. Exhaustion rejects deterministically at the documented structural
boundary without truncating, misreporting a typing failure or publishing
partial output.

Projection produces the actual legal finite interface `P_S` with exact
original-fiber **and ordinary-use/evidence** factorization. Distinct incoming
uses receive independent eligible identities, aliases of one use share its
copy, internal SCC uses share live roots, and rigid imports/captures remain
fixed. A legal method/associated-type witness follows the same relation and
is reconsidered when its actual dependencies change.

### Why genuinely semantic:

Elimination, SCC solving, origin guards, hiding and projection can discard a
real source solution or admit an invalid joint witness even when each local
shape looks correct. Optional-record concrete comparisons are non-transitive;
effect/provider correlations and scope dependencies prevent naïve closure
or independent marginal solving.

### Why design cannot remove it without changing behavior:

The compiler must decide or faithfully retain its actual obligations while
preserving the natural source solution family. Carrying certificates avoids
reconstructing maps and ownership but cannot prove that a chosen rewrite or
decision procedure preserves meaning or terminates. Returning an arbitrary
safe restriction would violate B; returning arbitrary solutions violates A.

### Dependencies:

- Independent complete predicate and query interpretation, F1's actual emitted
  clauses, and the relevant F2 domain/primitive safety laws.
- Exact pure FMP/PURE_DEC scopes; do not extrapolate their result to arbitrary
  active Phi, State or effect/world predicates.
- Existing EQ_RES, WHOLE_REL, TRANSPORT, USE_GRAPH, certified graft/hiding and
  alpha laws with their actual source eligibility and independent admission inputs.
- Source-derived finite guard-context coverage, origin-relative insertion and
  terminal rules; effective full joint predicate/resolver treatment; exact
  public projection and resource design. These remain genuine cases.

### Shared proof pattern and stop condition:

Prove one semantic law for each actual transition kind, then compose the
transition derivation. Freshening/graft/hiding move the whole evidence family;
owner/path/license preservation is a corollary of that same step. Reuse the
proved pure carrier result. Do not count per-field projections as new theorems.
Do not replace a primitive's missing joint decision/reflection argument by
pointwise decidability or a bounded finite-world experiment.

## THEOREM 4: natural inference, principal source solutions and actual export (F4)

### Exact statement:

Let `Deriv_L(S,V)` be the independently defined legal source typing,
annotation/refinement and component-use derivations in the approved
conservative abstraction. Its definition precedes and is independent of
`J_S`, `P_S`, compiler success and the certificate image. It includes every
approved source form, unannotated higher-order use, legal source result
widening, recursive use, independent instance and source-authorized method/
adapter/effect alternative. Printed syntax is not the limit of legal inferred
public contracts; use the approved public/internal abstraction.

For every supported `S`, generate and generalize **one** actual `P_S` before
selecting its legal public use/refinement `V` or client graph `U`. Then:

1. Every independently legal source derivation and compatible original scoped
   strategy is represented by the generated relation and actual `P_S` without
   adding annotations or losing the approved solution/evidence family.
2. For every `Deriv_L(S,V)`, every finite legal ordinary client-use graph `U`,
   and every satisfying public/client strategy of that independent derivation,
   there is one compatible original-scoped joined strategy of the actual
   exported-use relation with the same visible assignments and required
   direct-query/method/evidence results.
3. Every actual accepted exported-use strategy reflects through F3 to an
   original-scoped strategy satisfying all generated obligations and required
   evidence; F1 clause 6 then constructs the independent source/contract
   typing and realization at the same assignments, scopes and joint witnesses.
   Therefore the published scheme is principal for these source derivations
   in the declared abstraction, with any required
   completed-contract/profile uniqueness or equivalence proved at that level.

Quantifier order is part of the statement:

```text
for S, construct P_S;
for each independent legal source derivation/refinement V and finite U;
for each compatible public/client strategy at the original binders;
construct one coherent original-scoped lift through the actual P_S.
```

The lift must use the actual designated export, including a replacement
`B_common` when that is what gets published. Retaining an unexported source
root is insufficient. When common allowance is used, prove legal descriptor
formation, joint ROWS/nonemptiness and totality at all original source
solutions; keep actual nonidentity result-check evidence. Successful queries
from the original root to two targets do not supply a query between those
targets.

### Why genuinely semantic:

Sound inference can still unnecessarily reject ordinary programs or lose their
most general uses. Whole-relation retention does not automatically prove that
the public common descriptor is legal or that a later ordinary result refinement
succeeds through that descriptor. These are the real natural-inference claims.

### Why design cannot remove it without changing behavior:

Required completed-contract/profile principality and the accepted ordinary
inference behavior cannot be replaced by fixture success, mandatory annotations
or sound-but-restrictive output. A natural construction can make source-derivation
induction possible; it cannot abolish the need to cover all legal derivations.

### Dependencies:

- F1 source construction; F2 original source/production/domain meaning; F3 exact
  solving, generalization and full ordinary-use/evidence projection.
- An independent, exhaustive legal source/refinement rule inventory in the
  approved abstraction. Its construction is not delegated to solver success.
- Actual common-descriptor formation/totality where required; generalization
  eligibility and complete ordinary-use maps; visible method/adapter alternatives.
- Function views §5.4, normative B-equivalence, charter principality directions
  and the accepted principal scheme criteria. Final Oracle capability remains
  within the approved compatibility boundary, not inference-stage formatting.

### Shared proof pattern and decisive separation test:

Induct on the independent source typing/refinement derivation, construct its
constraint/evidence lift, then apply F3 to the actual export and whole client
graph. No extra induction over every arbitrary independently semantic-valid
public view is required **unless** the approved legal source/public-contract
class itself requires that completeness.

If a permitted public contract can be certified only by the universal semantic
validity tested in ALL_VIEW, identify that case. Prove its necessary lifting or
retain the ALL_VIEW dependency; do not silently redefine `Deriv_L` to exclude it.
Likewise, ordinary Record/union result widening remains here even if arbitrary
semantic-view characterization is separated. Successful completion of this
test is required before adopting the reduced cutover route.

## Implementation invariant I5: hand to compiler engineering

Implement the approved F1–F4 operations at their real owning entrypoints and
prove/check representation simulation, actual result identity, scope/resource
limits, SCC freeze/visibility, independent incoming uses and failure atomicity.
Current F5 Q/R ordinals, a shadow marker or a final printed scheme are not the
complete successor interface. Verify actual collector, solver, generalizer,
instantiator, publisher and consumers against the approved abstract operations.

I5 remains safety work before cutover; it is not another universal semantic
characterization to hand to Pro. Its proof pattern is a phase/state invariant
and focused code/transition review. Its actual implementation is not authorized
by this research note.

## Do not send these as new Pro theorems

- Existence of a retained owner recovered from its own certified constructor.
- Separate preservation of every field already carried by one lawful whole map.
- Reconstructing source incidence from numeric identity or a solved endpoint.
- Another theorem whose premise is the missing OC-CallEff/source certificate.
- A source witness for every Option 2 observation.
- A global generated-world equality before deciding whether any retained
  consumer needs equality rather than direct independent-domain laws.
- Arbitrary-view characterization with no identified ordinary-source or
  Authoritative consumer. Keep ALL_VIEW as a strong research target, with its
  current canonical status and premises, until the F4 separation test is met.

When a constructor case remains unproved, report its precise semantic rule and
input, not a fresh proof-obligation DAG. When a preservation case fails because
an owner dropped evidence, repair its output design before inventing an inverse.
