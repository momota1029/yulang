application at Lambda-before-body, using the selected source rules. Method:
instantiate the original rule premises at their actual source indices, trace
the actual HIR/action/paired constructors, and stop at the first premise that
those owners do not supply. No residual arithmetic or assumed transition

and binders, and every actual use/copy and Call/output route. This packet takes
the formal-annotation constructor case within that domain; its unhandled cases
remain obligations of H. Candidate preflight acceptance does not define the
domain. Successful lowering does not promise successful typing or publication.

For each actual Lambda f and formal binding cb, retain:

```text

C_a = ((source member ordinal m, resolved operand E_m))_m,
```

worlds, current states, continuations, hard constraints and independent clauses.

The set C_a has one attachment identity at this exact annotation occurrence;
member ordinals and resolved operands remain separately indexed. Distinct
annotations/fresh local instances remain distinct. Membership in the same
nominal set does not identify contribution witnesses.

The required introduction is a genuine finite original local annotation
derivation at these indices. It must provide the governed-port correspondence,
the selected local permission, and its actual contribution/route incidence and
realization obligations. Those are required outputs, not assumed inputs named
"license" that close the claim by construction. Where checking is required,
its same-value/computation and complete same-callable premises must also be
