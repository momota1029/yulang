## 1. Objective and result

Try to obtain two independently admitted providers with the same printed
endpoint and a typed client separating admission information omitted by a
public import. Method: invert selected same-provider membership and immutable
Import/Name constructors **before** applying information-collision reasoning.
This changes the method of the earlier endpoint-erasure attack; it does not
run its singleton-versus-empty-domain model again.

**Result:** the inspected suppliers do not establish the requested source
pair or distinguishing client. Import and terminal Name restrict an already
valid hereditary binding certificate; they do not construct providers with
different admission domains. If “same endpoint” means one identical complete
descriptor admitting the separating challenge, the suggested rejecting
provider cannot be admitted at that descriptor. If it means only identical
printing, a distinction in retained complete clauses is possible as a
conditional premise, but its legal presentation and source realization remain
unsupplied. The local countermodel below makes those premises explicit and
keeps a common nonempty domain. No universal metadata necessity follows.

## 3. Source-admissibility inversion and the excluded shortcut

Fix one independent complete descriptor F, original fiber, scope and event.
Let `d_h` be a genuine checked challenge of the tested value at F, assembled
from the independently valid punctured context, other values, actual hole
incidences and checked whole carrier. Selected membership gives

```text
ValueMem_F(q,r,e) + Act(q,U_q,r) + d_h in D_F
    => ActualAdm(q,U_q,d_h).
```

Thus `not ActualAdm(q,U_q,d_h)` contradicts membership of q at that same F.
This is a direct elimination of the selected rule, without changing its
meaning. The challenge must remain compatible at q's actual incidence;
merely reusing a challenge assembled for p does not establish this premise.
Every retained actual decomposition is covered. Lack of output cannot make
rejected dispatch vacuously valid, because actual acceptance is a separate
membership obligation.

Consequences for a concrete pair:

1. Giving p and q one **identical complete F** admitting h, then making q
   reject h, cannot establish two admitted F members.
2. Giving them one identical printed B, with different complete F clauses,
   avoids that contradiction only after legal formation of those clauses is
   supplied. Equal printing alone provides no such formation.
3. Making h incompatible at q's world can preserve q's F membership, but then
   the independently typed separating context/world changed. One must prove
   that the proposed import summary erases that compatibility information too.

The available source shape is only the relative construction

```text
independent world + Gamma(z)=Value(B) with hereditary binding certificate
    -> capture restriction -> terminal Name -> Value Result of same z.
```

It preserves an imported provider and its contract. It cannot run backwards
to introduce the world, that provider certificate, its complete domain, or a
different provider with the same printed B. Ordinary `id` instances and aliases
also preserve their actual provider; a second spelling supplies no second
contract. The selected positive recursive pair introduces its exact stored
closures under independent guards, not arbitrary same-B admission variants.
No inspected selected constructor supplies the required differing domains.
This is a bounded supplier finding, not a repository-wide absence theorem.

## 4. Conditional local countermodel with a common admitted demand

The following is a **candidate-assumption model**, not a source program or
an exhaustive model of all Yulang descriptors. It discriminates the missing
supplier rather than inventing that supplier. Supply independently typed
complete challenge families L and H. L is nonempty; H contains a separating
challenge h. Both include their lawful finite prefixes and later compatible
developments. They share one original joint scope/dependency interpretation,
but the independent clauses keep L and H disjoint. No carrier must Return.

Assume legal complete contracts F0 and F+, and two genuine providers p,q:

```text
print(F0) = print(F+) = B
D_F0 = L                  D_F+ = L union H
ActualAdm_p contains L union H
ActualAdm_q contains L and rejects h in H
```

Assume all actual observations at accepted challenges satisfy the same
independently supplied full observation/hard envelope P, at their original
worlds and incidences; P covers prefixes, pending developments and future
uses. Assume the independent laws justify extension to all other required
cases, rather than defining their membership as the two rows above. Then
selected membership derives

```text
p realizes F0 and F+;       q realizes F0;       q does not realize F+.
```

The last conclusion follows from h and acceptance elimination; it requires
no observable effect difference or `Pi` injectivity. The first conclusions
still consume every challenge/observation premise, not merely one successful
execution. This is a conditional characterization of the supplied domains.

Supply an independently typed punctured client `C[-]` whose callable hole is
checked at F+, whose other values and whole carrier independently assemble
h after filling that hole with either actual provider. Client typing must
not require the tested provider's membership in advance. Selected challenge
assembly then gives h for both fillings before actual acceptance. The p
filling meets the complete contract, and the q filling fails its admission
obligation. No ordinary resolver success is inferred from these semantic facts.

For captured `pick`, assume the source realization separately supplies the
two binding certificates and closure introductions, an admitted outer call
whose Value entry completes, and the typed returned-provider path to this
client. Terminal Name then transports the same p or q into that later hole.
This is a conditional consequence of the existing forwarding rule, not an
established typed whole-Call or Generalize derivation. It consumes no pending
ReadInvoke/source-presentation result.

Now **hypothesize**, rather than select, a public import summary S with
`S(F0)=S(F+)` that omits the h-admission distinction. Assume uniform type-use
rules cannot inspect source or obtain that distinction from another supplied
input. If raw provider identities remain, additionally assume the judgment
is invariant under renaming opaque identities preserving the supplied
incidence graph. There is no extensional lookup from identity to the missing
contract. Under these premises the two type-use inputs are indistinguishable
modulo that renaming, so the uniform judgment cannot accept precisely the p
case and reject the q case.

Acceptance of both loses safety for h. Rejection of both is consistent with
safety; it loses required natural inference only **if** the F+ p view is
independently required to remain accepted. The exact semantic membership
discriminator does not prove that ordinary Direct must already accept it.
Neither the lossy S nor that natural-view obligation is selected here.

Minimum discriminator size under a common nonempty domain: two provider
alternatives and two challenge classes, one shared and one separating.
Dropping the common class gives the earlier empty-domain toy. This is a
symbolic local size, not a claim that a complete history universe has two
elements. The independent completion laws are explicit unproved hypotheses.
