selected immutable Name/Name Call, exposing its inputs, observation/evidence

complete, not another native tagged observation model. The source stage and

initial event/world/environment/admission evidence, and **zero executed
source-child premises**. It retains the whole suspended suffix and the
uninstantiated result/future interfaces. It has no actual callee Return,
runtime argument carrier, checked challenge, receipt or receiver execution.

is introduced, and no assertion of a fully typed complete rule is made.

own initial-domain transfer and decorated image/evidence laws. Erased Bind
equations and static interface inclusions do not supply those laws. Either
