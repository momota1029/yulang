## Two rejected recursive-candidate shortcuts

The earlier research witness in
`2026-10-13-source-check-positive-transport-proof.md` §§3–4 shows that ordinary
checking at completed membership does not act automatically on a family
with provisional value holes. Its actual Function/Return endpoints remain
distinct even at the same raw observation.

Let `U_R(S)` be finite same-tuple check closure. The tempting compatibility
`U_R(Phi S) ≤ Phi(U_R S)` already fails as a general argument for Union
injection: a source one-step readout gives `Phi S p`, whereas the target
Union field needs membership of `p` in `U_R S`. Those are different premises.

Keeping source membership as well as its readout repairs that injection
step, but still does not validate all original rules. Take an actual value
excluded by a descriptor A's independent ground guard, let U be Union(A,A),
and let C contain the same raw tuple at both A and U, including its relevant
future copies. C is closed under the genuine finite check U≤A whose two
children are identities. Both C(U) and Phi(C)(U) hold, but Phi(C)(A) fails.
Union cases needs to unfold the selected positive child. This is a
counterexample to the proposed generic closure step, **not** an admitted
source-program counterexample or an impossibility theorem for original L4.
The present proof unfolds only completed Meaning and therefore does not use
either rejected shortcut.
