### 3.3 Why this does not impose raw ViewInlet as necessary

Function realization quantifies over complete independently admitted
challenges. A raw `Acc(CarrierContract(F);t,e)` lacking a compatible context,
scope, typed continuation or authority witness need not form such a
challenge. The predecessor's uniform ViewInlet quantifies over that raw
acceptance relation at all events; that is a useful sufficient action when
it exists, but no such extra standalone action is required here.

For an exact separation, fix an independent raw checked inlet that admits
carriers t0 and t1, and an independent complete contextual domain that has
valid challenges using t0. Let t1 require an authority that this particular
joint world lacks. Therefore no compatible complete challenge at this
world uses t1. Let actual U accept t0, retain every admitted divergence and
observation on t0, and reject t1 at its raw inlet. Let the independently
fixed complete output envelope cover U on all its t0 histories. This is a
Function realizer by §3.1 and a counterexample to raw ViewInlet. It does not
delete an independently compatible t1 challenge: the independent authority
rules already prevent one at this world. In another independently valid
world with that authority, the domain would include it and the Function
claim would have to be proved again there.

Conversely, bare comparison relations cannot prove provider admission.
Take `D_F={t1}`, `D_A={t0,t1}`, `Acc_U={t0}` and equal empty output bounds.
The two displayed inclusions hold, but t1 is not accepted by U. This is
excluded by the same-provider realization clause for A, not by removing t1
from D_F or requiring t1 to return. Empty output bounds provide no admission
fact. Theorem F requires genuine A membership and therefore does not admit
this model. These are semantic separation witnesses, not Yulang source
counterexamples or a reason to reject ordinary inferred functions.
