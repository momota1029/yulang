## 1. Objective and result

Trace every outer Return carrier in the unchanged complete challenge family
of [outer apply introduction](2026-10-08-outer-apply-closure-introduction.md)
§3 RT.1–RT.2, for `my apply f = { my step x = f x; step }`.
The question is whether actual selected input formation supplies an ordinary
final-model `M.Car(I_f,t,r,e)` certificate for every admitted carrier.

The bounded correspondence pass finds **no such universal supplier**. Checked
admission, full carrier validity, and the interpretation of that validity are
distinct. Ordinary full carrier evidence supplies ordinary Return membership;
the outer relative packet instead supplies `B_a.Car`, whose Return supplies
`B_a.V`. The selected local Step/Block construction consumes an independently
ordinary captured-f certificate; it does not manufacture that certificate for
every arbitrary outer filling.

This is a precise missing-premise result, not a countermodel showing an actual
admitted carrier outside M. The inspected definitions do not construct such a
carrier either. No claim is made that every admitted carrier has only relative
evidence, or that ordinary certificates cannot be supplied in a particular use.
No previously authored RT.2 countermodel or transfer proof was read or used.

## 3. Exact conditional certificate elimination

Let `D_apply` be the unchanged complete challenge/demand family; let `t_d`
be a challenge's same actual outer whole carrier. All event/world/result
incidences below are the original ones. Set

```text
M   = nu Phi
B_a = nu (Phi with only V augmented by the exact H_a value tuples).
```

The selected simultaneous clauses state

```text
PhiCar[Y](I,t,r,e):
  inert formation/license/incidence guards;
  lexical projections in Y.V/Y.Car and actual world in Y.W;
  every compatible designated Force observation/development in Y.T;
  hereditary restrictions at the original roots/events.

PhiT[Y](PureReturn(A),Return(v,r,C,w),e'):
  original incidences and Y.W(C,w,e') and Y.V(A,v,r,e').
```

The typed I_f result clause uses its actual result endpoint A_f. Consequently,
for either selected family `Y=M` or `Y=B_a`, full carrier elimination gives

```text
Y.Car(I_f,t_d,r_d,e_d)
and an actual admitted Force Return (v_f,r_f,C_f,w_f,e_f)
  => Y.V(A_f,v_f,r_f,e_f) and Y.W(C_f,w_f,e_f),
     with original hereditary/result/incidence fields retained.
```

This conclusion follows from the carrier's whole execution certificate and
the local Return clause. Raw Acc, inert formation alone, or registration
alone is insufficient. It does not assert that an admitted carrier returns:
pending, zero-step, divergent, Response/Resume and FutureUse cases keep their
full original domains and obligations.

The full-domain ordinary supplier that would justify the proposed ordinary
route is exactly

```text
OC:
forall d in D_apply.
  the original independently supplied input evidence at d constructs
  M.Car(I_f,t_d,r_d,e_d), with its actual full hereditary fields,
  without final apply membership or installed-world validity as a premise.
```

Given OC and the already independently supplied ordinary same-value
`VIncl_M(A_f,F_c)`, each actual Return yields the same ordinary f certificate
at F_c. Unfolding it produces `Phi(M).V(F_c,f)`. Monotonicity embeds M into
`B_as`, and maps that full readout into `Phi(B_as).V(F_c,f)`, preserving every
original provider, observation and future operand. This would supply RT.2's
particular non-hole readout. It does not independently discharge every RT.1
or RT.3 field.

OC is not obtained by renaming the relative input packet. Outer introduction
§3 RT.1 supplies B_a interpretation for the outer fields, and §5 explicitly
unfolds B_a carriers. That yields only B_a.V/B_a.W at Return. M embeds into
B_a, but the reverse inclusion is not supplied before H_a is discharged.
No admission constructor changes this direction.

## 4. Ordinary source diagonal and the local theorem boundary

Input realization Lemmas W/D and Theorem R give a real ordinary source-input
supplier for their stated Name/Return/Delay case: an independently valid
ordinary environment supplies the resolved binding certificate; pure Return
preserves it; Delay proves inert formation and all designated execution
obligations. Its checked admission is then a separate whole-argument action.
This is a full carrier proof for that constructed argument, not a proof about
every external outer carrier of apply.

Captured introduction §2 lists ordinary actual-f membership as a local input.
Its §5.1 embeds that supplied certificate into B_H. Step §6 first unfolds
the independently ordinary f certificate and then maps its readout into the
relative family. Block §7 assumes a completed outer Return satisfying those
inputs; OuterTrace uses the full carrier and result evidence needed for that
same local argument. The authoritative captured definition §3 retains this
ordinary-input requirement. These statements establish what follows from
the ordinary input; they are not a source-independent introduction of OC
over apply's new B_a family.

The decisive provenance question remains at each outer filling's actual
input-construction rule: where was its full carrier certificate constructed,
and was its result/world/continuation interpretation M or B_a? The inspected
interface and challenge records give no universal final-model answer.

## 5. Falsifier and failure conditions

A falsifier for OC must give an actual independently admitted `d`, its full
original formation/admission evidence, and an outer carrier whose supplied
full certificate is relative while no ordinary M.Car certificate can be
constructed without the target conclusion. A raw carrier with Acc alone is
not enough; a counterexample to a separate ordinary VIncl law is not enough.

A discriminating incidence to inspect is a carrier that returns the exact
declared apply tuple at a coincident A_f/F_c. Relative Return may use H_a at
that V operand. This is a **candidate falsifier schema**, not an established
admitted witness or a countermodel. Domain membership, full inert/history
evidence and the ordinary-certificate obstruction would all need proof.
Likewise M.Car at a world containing apply includes M.W and hence every
installed binding's ordinary evidence: supplying it by assuming the completed
apply world would make OC circular.

The correspondence result fails if an uninspected actual source/context
constructor already carries a uniform M.Car supplier with these exact domains
and independent evidence. Such a supplier would resolve the identified gap;
the adjective “independently typed” without its law would not. Changing domains,
discarding relative carriers, or treating every filling as the source diagonal
does not resolve this obligation under the selected scope.

Recommended next action: inspect or construct one canonical input certificate
at the outer carrier formation/admission owner that records the full relation
family and original hereditary operands; prove its uniform applicability on
the unchanged D_apply. For genuinely relative fillings, use an independently
justified simultaneous transfer rather than asserting OC or narrowing D.
