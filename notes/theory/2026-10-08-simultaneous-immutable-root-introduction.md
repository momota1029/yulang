## 5. Finite-inspection soundness

**Theorem Fin.** Every certificate in the proposed greatest fixed point
passes every finite chain of the displayed descriptor/world/continuation
eliminations. In particular Knu's actual providers satisfy the complete
descriptor envelope at every finite prefix and independently admitted finite
Initial/Response/Resume/FutureUse history.

**Proof.** Induct on the number of certificate eliminations. At zero there
is no inspection assertion. One unfolding of the greatest fixed point
exposes all immediate guard evidence, actual admission, and every demanded
raw prefix, including zero-step observations. It does not filter observations
by whether they are safe. Every recursively required hereditary certificate
remains in the same greatest fixed point; apply the induction hypothesis
to each further finite inspection. A returned provider's future application
is an explicit independent challenge and receives the same universal check.
The source/history cases in Knu identify the actual operations inspected;
finite history induction then gives the stated operational consequence. QED.

This is not arbitrary cyclic acceptance: admission, actual decomposition,
formation, effects, zero-prefix worlds and live guards are demanded immediately
at each inspection. A false one cannot be discharged by a back reference.
The cycle concerns immutable captured/returned certificates; the corresponding
values are not executed merely to validate their references.

No converse finite-failure reflection, all-index characterization of a foreign
kernel, or existential witness compactness follows. In particular the proof
does not exchange `forall history exists evidence` with a stronger order.
One failing evidence extension still does not refute that quantified property.
