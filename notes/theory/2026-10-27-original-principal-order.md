Date: 2026-10-27. Producer: `/root/principality`, bounded `lean_prover` leaf.
Status: **source-backed formulation/application boundary; original principality OPEN**.
This packet adds no Lean declaration and makes no language or compiler change.
Independent review: not yet performed.

## 1. Finding and correction to the earlier boundary

There is an original, explicit finite construction for a constrained scheme
use. It is incorrect to report that an additional instance-map calculus is
missing. The [certified-callback owner §§5.1–5.2](../design/2026-10-04-certified-callback-and-constrained-use.md#5-ordinary-constrained-uses-already-supply-the-map-construction)
constructs `Use(P,V)` by whole-copy reindexing, optional admissible descriptor
grafting, retention of the client constraint, and one complete direct query
at the **designated exported root**. The
[principal acceptance criteria](../progress/2026-10-04-principal-scheme-acceptance-criteria.md)
expressly withdraw the separate map-construction objection for this route.

The authoritative [Source-Generalize definition](../design/2026-10-08-source-generalize-definition.md)
also selects a concrete `ScopedClosure` and a finite joint-client allocation
judgment. Its maps include symbolic endpoint terms, binder incidences and
actual event routing. They are selected before assignments and histories;
they are not arbitrary maps chosen after observing a solution.

What remains unconstructed at the Lean principal root is the **source-produced
complete constrained presentation and its original scoped interpretation**,
together with the all-valid-view extension theorem through that
presentation's actual exported root. The root still has abstract `Solution`,
`solves`, `sameScoped`, `principal` and `instanceOf` parameters. The existing
algebraic quotient theorem does not instantiate them. An equality or inclusion
of algebraic solution sets cannot be installed as the original instance
relation without the source interpretation and ordinary-use correspondence.

This refines the [earlier interface-countermodel packet](2026-10-27-original-principality-resolution.md).
Its two Boolean orders still demonstrate that the abstract coverage interface
alone does not determine principality. They do **not** show ambiguity in the
original ordinary-use operation. No new countermodel is needed here, and no
original theorem is refuted. The exact remaining theorem is below.

## 3. Exact ordinary-use theorem still needed

The original owner uses a finite presentation and an independently supplied
public view:

```text
P = (rho, Omega, I, K_P, R_P)
V = (rho, Y, I_V, C_V, R_V).
```

`rho` is fixed; `Omega` contains eligible owned semantic binders; `I` retains
the complete scope/incidence presentation; `K_P` is the whole residual;
`R_P` is the actual exported complete root. Validity of `V` comes from the
independent source/semantic criterion. It is not defined by instantiability
from `P`.

The syntax of `Use(P,V)` copies the whole eligible presentation, keeps
`C_V`, and appends `Direct(R_P^fresh,R_V)` with its complete dependencies.
The source's exact remaining condition is:

```text
for every public assignment v,
  C_V(v) implies Ext_P(v).                                  (E_V)
```

In the existential-only display,

```text
Ext_P(v) = exists z.
  K_P^fresh(v,z) and Direct(R_P^fresh(v,z), R_V(v)).
```

This display is not permission to flatten the original binder tree. In a
scope of shape `exists shared; forall challenge; exists body`, `Ext_P` must
retain that order and one shared strategy. `Direct` is the existing complete
whole-root inequality and its resolution evidence, not independently subtyped
effect ports, a safety alias or a newly invented semantic predicate.

Certified callback Theorem 3 proves that the projection of `Use(P,V)` is
exactly `C_V` **iff** `(E_V)` holds. It does not prove `(E_V)`. The required
all-view quantifiers remain:

```text
for every independently valid finite V,
  there exists a finite solution-preserving ordinary-use map m_V,
  selected before public assignments and challenges.
```

The map may depend on `V`. Its local witnesses may depend on public inputs at
their allowed scopes. Neither fact permits a different Shared witness for
each use or a fresh pre-challenge witness after each challenge. For joint
clients, the whole allocation `J_m(U,W)` and its global strategy are required;
proving separate `(E_V)` facts for marginal views does not establish a joint
instance with arbitrary cross-use `W`.

The authoritative [SG theorem GC](2026-10-08-source-generalize-definition-and-proof.md#7-joint-generalize-soundness-and-lawful-use-completeness)
constructs an allocation for each **independent finite source/checking proof
grammar**, uniformly over its finite developments. It does not assert that
every semantically valid public contract has such a grammar. Its §7.2 also
retains the separate obligation that the actual native resolver accepts the
exact source checking certificate at the submitted root. Consequently GC
cannot be substituted for all-valid-view direct-query completeness.

The exported-root distinction in certified callback §6 is material. If the
presentation actually exports the unchanged source root `R_G`, the original
totality `(T)` for an auxiliary common allowance and the original `(G_V)`
direct-query witness imply `(E_V)`. If it exports `B_common(s,a)`, the required
witness is instead:

```text
C_V(v) implies exists s,a.
  K_G(s,v) and Q(s,a) and Direct(B_common(s,a), R_V(v)).       (B_V)
```

Totality of `Q` alone does not supply the last conjunct for the same witness.
There is no authorization to use a hidden `R_G` when the source exported
`B_common`, to treat a common public descriptor as mere formatting, or to
compose concrete comparison successes to obtain that conjunct. The current
seven public criteria retain their stated presentations, including the later
compose clarification; this packet certifies none of them principal.

## 4. Why the existing Lean theorem is not the missing application

[Contracts.SourceProblem.PrincipalCoverage](lean/YulangClosure/Contracts.lean)
has this exact body:

```lean
def PrincipalCoverage (principal : S → X → D → A → Prop)
    (instanceOf : X → A → A → Prop) : Prop :=
  ∀ s x d, p.supported s → p.generates s x d →
    (∃ e, p.sourceJudgment s x e) →
    ∃ a, principal s x d a ∧ p.solves d a ∧
      ∀ e, p.sourceJudgment s x e →
        ∃ b, p.solves d b ∧ p.sameScoped s x e b ∧ instanceOf x a b
```

The one `a` is chosen before the universal source witness `e`. The original
instance operation must relate the actual representations chosen for `A`,
fix the original scope `x`, and retain the shared source evidence through
`sameScoped`. `A` cannot silently alternate between a constrained scheme and
one valuation without an explicit interpretation of both in that carrier.
The definition does not itself impose a preorder law. Taking transitive
closure of a concrete direct query to manufacture one is not justified by
this interface or by the original source rules.

[OriginalPrincipality.joint_solution_coverage_iff](lean/YulangClosure/OriginalPrincipality.lean)
instead fixes one finite store/use allocation, one canonical quotient and
one whole-valuation predicate `observe`. With its stated boundedness and
anchor hypotheses, it proves:

```text
exists sigma. PostStore(sigma) and observe(sigma o transport)
  iff
exists rho. PreStore(rho) and FiberConstant(transport,rho) and observe(rho).
```

Here `Valuation = (k : Kind) → Nat → Term k`; it is the existing algebraic
term substitution. The theorem preserves every constructor, both kinds and
all four Function ports. Nevertheless, the type has no original binder tree,
runtime event routing or complete scoped strategy interpretation built into
it. An arbitrary `observe` can express a predicate of a valuation; it does
not prove that the original source package is represented by that predicate.

A genuine application first needs the original source derivation to supply
a satisfying **fiber-compatible representative**, with the same complete
source observation and scoped strategy, for the actual qualifying occurrence
and joint uses. That is exactly the localized source obligation in
[source preservation §§1, 3, 5](2026-10-13-intrusion-source-preservation-proof.md),
with [immutable birth-level qualification](../design/2026-10-24-birth-level-scc-intrusion.md).
It is not a theorem about every arbitrary old solver assignment. Even after
such representatives are constructed, principal coverage also needs the
single generated presentation `a` and the ordinary-use witnesses relating it
to every relevant `b`.

[SourcePrincipalTransport](lean/YulangClosure/SourcePrincipalTransport.lean)
supplies the actual nested recursive source, both complete App premise lists,
the monomorphic recursive Name and actual Return receiver update. It does not
provide a native-extrusion-to-source map, a qualifying occurrence with the
required source representative, or a `ScopedClosure`/`Use` interpretation in
`SourceProblem`. Its source ownership facts must be reused when building
those suppliers; they cannot stand in for them.

The coupled core's extensional interface preorder is not a replacement. Its
“Principality and finite presentation” section is explicitly non-authoritative
and conditional on a chosen representable language; its point-row theorem
requires exact source generation for that restricted fragment. Parametric
linking §3 likewise states principal-denotation preservation only for an
already chosen representable constraint language ordered by solution
inclusion. Neither identifies that order with the original full instance
operation required here. Later SG selects the internal source closure but
expressly excludes public/source principality from its theorem scope.

## 5. Exact next construction and acceptance test

The missing datum is not a freely selectable preorder. It is the **actual
source-produced complete presentation with an interpretation of its scoped
ordinary uses in the original problem**. The following is a construction
sequence under the existing authority, not additional assumed laws:

1. Represent SG's complete source rule program and `ScopedClosure` with the
   original owner classifications above. Construct it from the full actual
   source component and selected final formation, including complete Call and
   effect/guard/evidence clauses. Do not fill undecided clauses with success
   predicates. The inferred-call-view authority §§2 and 5 explicitly requires
   this source construction independently of pending-query success.
2. Implement the existing §5.2 allocation and certified-callback §5.1 ordinary
   use on those represented objects. Prove its rigid-import, Shared,
   ViewLogic, actual-event, alias and recursive-incidence properties from the
   constructors. The finite term maps and proof-grammar nodes are outputs of
   this operation. This step does not require a new map calculus.
3. Give `SourceProblem` its actual source/constraint/solution interpretations,
   including how a generated constrained presentation and its ordinary
   instances inhabit `Solution`. Establish `solves` and `sameScoped` using
   the full original rules. The instance relation must be the interpretation
   of step 2, with an explicit correspondence theorem, rather than inclusion
   of an arbitrarily selected solution set.
4. Prove `(E_V)` for independently valid views through the actual export and
   its genuine direct evidence consumer. For arbitrary joint clients, derive
   one full scoped strategy over `J_m(U,W)` and all legal future developments.
   Prove the source-generated fiber representatives needed by the existing
   quotient theorem for each actual qualifying retained occurrence.
5. Only then apply `joint_solution_coverage_iff` to those represented stores
   and observations and assemble the single `a` required by PrincipalCoverage.
   No component of this sequence may assume the target coverage or
   principality proposition under a field name such as `LocalLaws`.

The first implementable next task is steps 1–2's faithful source presentation
and allocation, using the existing full source constructors. Its meaningful
exit is a computed whole-client clause graph plus proved original binder and
root incidences. A record accepting an entire source judgment, complete Call
certificate or `(E_V)` as its constructor payload does not discharge that task.
The remaining extension theorem must stay explicitly open until its source
and direct-evidence premises have actually been derived.

No newly required user choice is established by this bounded inspection.
The originals already determine the allocation discipline and actual export
policy. A proposal to change the public representation, comparison semantics
or supported source domain would be a separate decision, not a way to close
this proof obligation. This packet makes none of those changes.
