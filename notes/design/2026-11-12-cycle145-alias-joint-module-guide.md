# Cycle145: one common Joint witness per registered alias class

Goal: reduce the dictionary `evidenceNode` residual without changing the target.
A `Joint` proof contains one model satisfying every site in an alias class. It
can therefore be rerooted at any registered member of that class, retaining the
same model, rather than reproving independent existential witnesses per site.
No claim is made that local survivor witnesses at distinct aliases can be joined.
The same registered-site premise is used in the reverse direction for an iff.

The actual output-equation merge transport is investigated separately; it must
retain one simultaneous model and may not silently combine two existential
models. All results are helper/soundness-side results, not full V5 completion.

## Exact merge residual and sequence semantics

`GeneralDictionaryJointMerge` transports BranchAt and complete recursive Evidence
through the actual associated-output `merge`. Linked output targets preserve the
source domain and freshness; the target-membership conditions can be recovered
from Prepared inside a root branch. Its exact iff retains packet-key coherence
and ONE common pre-merge model for the union of the two old alias classes.
`GeneralDictionaryJointMergeResidual` reduces two independent old Joint proofs at
the same key to the remaining simultaneous-model condition, without identifying
their independent witnesses. Key conflict is a separate obstruction.

`GeneralDictionaryMergeSequence` and `GeneralDictionaryJointMergeSequence` extend
these equivalences to finite folds of the existing merge transformer. Actual
final aliases are exactly the old edges plus reversed event edges. Models,
full source-indexed Evidence, and Joint are invariant under reordering/repetition
of the same merge events (Joint retains linked endpoints). This is NOT a new
executor or general schedule confluence: operational enablement and interleaved
install/administrative steps are outside the theorem.

## Scalar outputs without an assumed global associated interpretation

`GeneralDictionaryScalarMerge` derives every output equation directly from actual
materialized scalar requests at one model. `GeneralDictionaryScalarJournalMerge`
uses current Models to read historical packets at their original contexts and
registries. Therefore already-recorded scalar requests imply merge output
constraints at every current model, without claiming historical BranchAt equals
current BranchAt. Full recursive Evidence is transported at those models.

`GeneralDictionaryScalarMergeExample` instantiates the first bridge;
`GeneralDictionaryOutputMergeScalarFacts` instantiates the journal bridge with
one actual parent packet supplying both emitted child output targets.

## Scope-separated regressions

* `JointMergeExample`: nonempty scalar-output merge with common BranchAt, Joint,
  and two complete terminal dictionaries. `JointMergeEnabled` proves an actual
  enabled support merge from this manually registered state. SourceFormed is not
  supplied. `JointMergeJournalScope` proves why inserting a scalar packet into an
  empty-facts journal is not a substitute for a SourceFormed construction.
* `OutputMergeReachable`: genuine Initial→supported install→guarded merge, modeled
  and source-formed, with two distinct output targets and two directed equations.
  Child implementations are deliberately absent. `OutputMergeScope` formally
  excludes their BranchAt/Joint/Instance/Evidence. This is not a V5 obstruction.
* `OutputMergeSuccess`: a separate registry adds the terminal implementation.
  The SAME reached finish and SAME model now support child Joint and both full
  terminal dictionaries; parent-origin allocations and current freshness are
  proved. This is an inhabited positive regression, not a general V5 bridge.
* `FailedDiagnosticScope`: prior cycle144's selected parent with failed children
  has neither completed selected Classify nor all child Evidence. It therefore
  does not refute DictionarySound/evidenceNode; the local node implication is
  proved at every generated point of that fixed snapshot.

## Remaining mathematical obligation

`Checked` certifies individual declarations and unique IDs, not overlapping-head
coherence. `SameInputs` gives semantic equivalence of observed inputs, whereas
`capture_determines_associated` requires syntactically agreeing captures and a
common child-output interpretation for one declaration. The missing general
bridge must compare semantic associated expressions across actual captures and
recursive child interpretations. Scalar requests are one proved case; arbitrary
variable/output-only/recursive associated expressions remain open. No concrete
counterexample to enabled source-formed same-input merging was established.

`OutputMergeRootEvidence` then builds the parent's current Instance from the
historical prepared branch and the verified ordinary extension. The SAME
reached positive fixture now has full root Evidence
`node 1 [node 7 [], node 7 []]`, retaining two distinct child source identities
and their ordered payloads. This conclusion is stronger than separate child
outputs but still does not assert general Stable/completeness.

`OutputMergeAllModels` strengthens the positive regression to EVERY final
journal model. The historical parent packet forces both output targets to int;
terminal branches are rebuilt at that same arbitrary assignment, then extended
into the current context. `OutputMergeJointModels` supplies current root/child
BranchAt with explicit Linked/Fresh and packages inhabited Joint with universal
full-root Evidence. No semantic witness is swapped to the example's fixed model.

`OutputMergeClassification` proves the actual support Local selections and
Classify tree, not just semantic evidence. Source Heads supply DecisionGuards;
model-height arguments exclude K2. `OutputMergeExactPayload` proves that every
full Evidence at the root has exactly the ordered payload
`node 1 [node 7 [], node 7 []]`, and every final model validates it. This is a
specific fixture readback/uniqueness result, not general principality.
