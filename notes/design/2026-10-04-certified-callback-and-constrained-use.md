## 1. Result and governing objects

Two auxiliary gates can be closed using the existing presentation operations.

1. A finite certificate of the transformations in §2 yields inversion of
   **every** finite endpoint membership and transports challenge admission.
   This proves the Pure-value callback bridge for independently supplied
   presentations certified in this class. It does not assert that the
   production generator already emits such presentations.
2. A finite retained constrained presentation has an ordinary fresh use
   against every well-formed finite public target. Its graph is constructed
   before any completeness claim. Exact public projection is equivalent to
   the scoped extension entailment in §5; no additional map-construction
   rule or ground witness-selection operation is required.

Neither result proves common-allowance descriptor realization or the
completeness of the general direct Function query. The full callback and
principal-scheme requests remain open at the explicit obligations in §8.

The inputs and semantics come from:

- [the callback contract](2026-10-03-callback-context-delivery.md), especially
  independent B endpoint synthesis and the separate prebuilt Pure-value path;
- [the source-generated theorem](2026-10-04-source-generated-callback-structural-theorems.md)
  §§2–4 and [its source-indexed realization](2026-10-04-source-indexed-callback-realization.md)
  §§2–5, including complete finite-history observations and independent
  punctured-context admission;
- [parametric component linking](2026-10-02-parametric-component-linking.md)
  §§2–3, for finite graph grafting and joint scoped projection;
- [the coupled core](2026-10-01-coupled-effect-interface-core-draft.md),
  “Generalization and fresh use as abstraction and reindexing,” “Joint uses
  under a shared receiver context,” and its complete quotient condition;
- [concrete compatibility](2026-10-03-concrete-compatibility-boundary.md) §1,
  for the single endpoint-dependent inequality and complete Function checks;
- [the accepted principal schemes](../progress/2026-10-04-principal-scheme-acceptance-criteria.md)
  and their original totality and all-view quantifiers.

The successor target is a retained constrained presentation. Charter §§1–2
do not require its equality with F5 closed schemes. Conversely, retaining
constraints does not by itself prove their source adequacy or their
principal public projection.

Throughout, a fixed old fiber includes the entire live assignment and its
`K,D`, all shared dependencies, and the original binder structure. Source
occurrences, receipts, owners and typed paths remain existing presentation
indices. A type substitution does not merge these indices.

## 2. A finite certificate class for existing presentations

### 2.1 What is supplied and what is certified

Let `E_G` be the finite source-associated presentation constructed by the
reviewed realization theorem from a valid decorated source derivation.
Its local primitive/provider relations may be conservative; their validity
is supplied independently. Its root bounds are generated by its complete
constructor rules, not merely by observed executions.

Let `E_N` be a separately supplied finite presentation with its declared
membership and admission rules. We do not define its denotation to equal
`E_G`. A certificate must account for the **entire** rule presentation of
`E_N`, including all root alternatives. A record of matching four printed
Function children, some executions, or a subset of the rules is insufficient.

The theorem concerns presentations using the existing source constructors,
whole-tuple relations, binder tables and declared finite-observation
interpretation. A finite transformation certificate is a proof DAG about
these objects. It is neither an added semantic coordinate of `Rel_C` nor a
new source/solver predicate. No compiler emission format is selected here.

### 2.2 Allowed transformations

**Alpha transport.** Supply a sort-preserving, capture-avoiding bijection on
renamed local identities, fixing imports. Transport the entire operand tuple,
formula, incidence, captured root, source-provider reference, receipt and
path together. Repeated occurrences of one identity use one map.

**Finite descriptor graft.** Supply the substitution of linking §2. Freshen
locals first, then graft finite target roots without unfolding their cycles.
Every occurrence of one external type port has the same target. Preserve
owners, boundary identities and source occurrences under their separate
injective transports. This is a substitution-relative result: it preserves
the source relation pulled back along the graft, not every unspecialized
source assignment.

**Joint local hiding.** Forget only eligible existential coordinates at their
original binder position, retaining the full relation and the witness behind
the projection. A coordinate used by another fragment, public view, latent
provider or challenge rule is still shared. It must stay in the interface or
be bound once around all those uses. In particular, independently projecting
two segments does not qualify as this transformation.

**Checked logical rewrite.** Supply a finite derivation from the existing
logical/equality laws and source equations. The rewritten fragment has the
same free tuple, scope, source-provider references and latent dependencies.
Examples are conjunction reassociation, elimination of an identical
conjunct, capture-avoiding replacement of an explicitly proved equal term,
and existential/conjunction distribution with its free-variable condition.
No rule may use the pending comparison or its success as an assumption.

For recursive definitions, a rewrite must preserve the defining operators
pointwise in their predicate arguments, together with their designated
least/finite-derivation interpretation. Equivalence of the bare equations
without their fixed-point interpretation is insufficient. An asserted
equivalence of two arbitrary recursive predicates is not a finite checked
rewrite under this rule.

**Allocation sharing.** Share identical formula/constructor fragments only
with a finite correspondence of their ordered operands, scopes, captures
and provider references. Every original occurrence-indexed incoming use
remains identifiable. The correspondence covers every rule and premise in
both directions and retains the fixed-point interpretation. Thus allocation
sharing changes a graph representation, not the source occurrence relation.
Equality of printed Function descriptors alone does not qualify.

**Entailed-equality quotient.** A sufficient noninjective case merges only
type ports in the same eligible scope, with a finite derivation that the
retained source constraints entail equality of each merged pair. All
incident primitive relations must respect this equality under their declared
whole-tuple interpretation. Source ownership, receipts, occurrences, provider
identities and rigid binders are not merged. This gives quotient-constant
representatives because every old satisfying assignment is already constant
on the merged classes. Mere overlapping bounds, a common inhabitant,
`FamAgree`, or two successful concrete comparisons do not establish this
equality. A more general quotient still needs the separate complete
observable-representative theorem from the coupled core.

These are sufficient proof constructors, not new compiler rejection rules.
An unrecognized transformation receives no theorem from this certificate
class; it may instead have a separately proved preservation argument.

### 2.3 Finiteness and proof checking

A certificate contains operation tags, references to the input/output graph
fragments, explicit maps, scope references, and finite logical derivations.
Their checking reduces to finite graph traversal, sort and freshness checks,
incidence/operand comparison, rule coverage and checking the stated proof
steps. A finite recursive graph is inspected through its back references;
the checker does not enumerate its histories.

The proof checker is relative to the supplied primitive relations and their
already established transport laws. It does not decide arbitrary predicate
equivalence or derive a missing primitive certificate. All proof dependencies
must be accounted for; a query-free final formula with a proof depending on
the pending query does not meet the independence condition.

## 3. Complete transport theorem

**Theorem 1.** Suppose a finite certificate as in §2 relates `E_G` and `E_N`.
Then it gives a bidirectional correspondence of their complete membership
relations and their punctured challenge-admission relations, under the
declared assignment/observation transports and scoped hiding. The result
holds for every finite latent, future-use and resumption history.

For a graft `sigma`, the source side is evaluated at the pulled-back
assignment `sigma*nu`. For a hidden local, the correspondence retains its
original existential witness. It does not claim a bijection of all old
assignments with all specialized or projected assignments.

### 3.1 Whole-tuple transport

Induct over the certificate DAG.

Alpha transport uses the bijection and its inverse. Grafting uses linking
§2's substitution lemma: every constructor and primitive sees the same whole
operand tuple under the pulled-back assignment. Hiding introduces or
eliminates precisely its retained existential witness. A logical rewrite
uses its checked pointwise equivalence without moving the original binder
tree. Allocation sharing follows the bidirectional rule/operand
correspondence. For an entailed-equality quotient, restrict an old assignment
to the equal-port classes; the equality premise makes this well-defined.
Pullback gives the inverse satisfying assignment on old ports. Congruence
transports all incident formulas, and the other indices stay separate.

These operations preserve the joined relation; they never reconstruct a
joint tuple from unrelated marginals. Their composition proves whole-tuple
transport through the finite certificate.

### 3.2 Behavioral membership and recursive labels

For a checked logical rewrite, use its whole-tuple equivalence directly;
conjunction reassociation need not preserve individual rule-premise syntax.
In a recursive block, equality of the defining operators at every predicate
assignment gives equality of their designated least interpretations. These
are the finite-derivation interpretations supplied by the source theorem, so
a membership on either side has a finite witness on the other. No
one-constructor correspondence is asserted for arbitrary logical rewrites.

For the other transformations, take an arbitrary finite membership
derivation of `E_N`. Their bidirectional rule coverage provides a
corresponding original constructor, and §3.1 supplies its whole tuple.
Induct over this finite derivation:

- A primitive leaf belongs to the same supplied local relation.
- A name uses the same lexical root. A lambda or reify result retains the
  corresponding source provider and its captured roots.
- At bind, a return passes the same descriptor and current configuration to
  the suffix. A request retains its operation witness, response port and
  continuation, with that same suffix appended.
- At call, the callee, whole inert argument, distinct receipts, actual entry,
  typed rebind, body and designated result consumer correspond jointly.
- A future force/invocation unfolds the retained provider label. A response
  or raw resumption uses the corresponding request and current configuration.

Every used premise has a smaller finite derivation. A recursive back edge
may be visited arbitrarily many times, but any given witness unfolds it only
finitely often. The induction therefore covers all finite histories without
a uniform history bound. Reverse rule coverage gives the opposite
membership direction by the same proof.

In particular, this inverts arbitrary conservative alternatives already
admitted by the supplied primitive relations. It is stronger than proving
that all source executions are included in an endpoint bound. There is no
certificate constructor introducing an unexplained root alternative.

### 3.3 Admission and the approved observation projection

Admission is not inferred from a nonempty output bound. Mutually with the
finite behavioral derivation, transport its finite punctured-context
certificate:

1. Initial admission retains the declared whole carrier, provider, typed
   interface/path and compatible owner/view context.
2. A response uses the corresponding request already exposed in that same
   history, at its original response port and rigid scope.
3. A raw resumption retains its handle and association with that request.
4. A future use retains the actually returned provider, its source path and
   current context.

Thus divergence can have an admitted carrier without a completed return.
No unrelated same-typed response or returned closure can replace the
history's own witness. Pending-query independence follows from §2's proof
dependency condition.

Apply the approved projection `Pi` once, to each entire joined observation
after the correspondence. This preserves its type, request, origin,
continuation and authority coordinates while erasing the already approved
concrete data identities. The proof uses image preservation of a complete
witness; it does not commute erasure with independently projected segments.

All equalities here are equalities of the appropriately transported complete
relations. Hiding a witness is not permission to choose a new hidden value
independently at each use, stage, or challenge.

## 4. Callback bridge for certified presentations

**Corollary 2.** Take Theorem C's actual/checked pair `(G_A,G_C)` and a
compatible pair of certificates to independently supplied presentations
`(E_A,E_C)`. Compatibility means that all old shared coordinates and their
scopes are transported together: the source argument, entry, body/consumer,
`nu,K,D`, occurrence paths and providers remain the same old tuple on both
sides. Specialization uses the same external assignment. **For this
corollary, every coordinate on which checked admission depends remains in
the fixed common fiber/challenge interface of the comparison.** In
particular, a shared local affecting checked admission is not existentially
forgotten from that interface. Other eligible local witnesses keep their
original joint scopes. The further hiding theorem below states a sufficient
condition for projecting an additional shared coordinate.

Then, pointwise at this fixed complete old fiber, in common coordinates,

```text
D_C ⊆ D_A
for every h in D_C: P_A(h) ⊆ P_C(h).
```

**Proof.** Theorem 1 transports checked admission to `G_C`. Theorem C gives
its admission in `G_A`, and Theorem 1 transports it to `E_A`.

For a fixed `h in D_C`, take any `O in P_A(h)`. The actual certificate
inverts this membership to a generated `G_A` witness at the same complete
old tuple. The premise `h in D_C` concerns **this fixed tuple**, including
every checked-admission dependency; no different existential witness is
chosen for it. Theorem C extends the witness
using its total fresh-coordinate definition, preserving every old coordinate
and the complete observation. The checked certificate sends it to `P_C(h)`.
The approved whole-observation projection preserves this equality. This
proves the bound inclusion. No concrete comparison successes are composed.

The displayed claim is not an inclusion of independently existentially
projected domain and bound marginals. Binding a shared name once in a joint
formula alone does not establish that stronger inclusion.

### 4.1 Exactly when additional hiding is safe

Fix the remaining visible coordinates `x`, with a common set `W(x)` of old
witnesses `z`. Suppose the fixed-fiber corollary holds for every `z in W(x)`:

```text
D_C(z) subset D_A(z)
for h in D_C(z): P_A(z,h) subset P_C(z,h).
```

All challenge/observation coordinates below already have the common
source-typed transport; no differently typed stages are identified. Consider
the existential marginals

```text
D_A^exists = union_z D_A(z)       D_C^exists = union_z D_C(z)
P_A^exists(h) = union_{z: h in D_A(z)} P_A(z,h)
P_C^exists(h) = union_{z: h in D_C(z)} P_C(z,h).
```

These marginals need not preserve the comparison. The finite countermodel is:

| Old witness | `D_A(z)` | `D_C(z)` | `P_A(z,h)` | `P_C(z,h)` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `z=0` | `{h}` | `{h}` | `{quiet}` | `{quiet}` |
| `z=1` | `{h}` | empty | `{request q}` | undefined outside its domain |

Each fixed-fiber comparison holds. But the projected checked domain admits
`h` using `z=0`, while the projected actual bound contains `request q` using
`z=1`, and the projected checked bound does not. This is a countermodel to
the hiding step, not a Yulang program counterexample or a carrier failure.

**Admission-uniform hiding lemma.** The projected comparison does hold if
checked admission is constant across the forgotten fiber:

```text
for all z,z' in W(x) and h:
    h in D_C(z) iff h in D_C(z').                           (H)
```

**Proof.** An admitted projected checked challenge has some witness `z_0`.
It is therefore admitted by `D_C(z)` for every `z in W(x)`, by `(H)`, and
by each `D_A(z)` by the fixed-fiber inclusion. Take any projected actual
observation and its witness `z`. The fixed-fiber theorem at that same `z`
puts it in `P_C(z,h)`, hence in the projected checked bound. Domain inclusion
also follows from the original witness `z_0`. If `W(x)` is empty the projected
checked domain is empty, so both claims hold. No actual witness is selected
anew after the checked-admission proof.

`(H)` is a sufficient condition, not a claimed necessary condition for every
particular pair of bounds. It has a finite syntactic sufficient check: the
forgotten coordinates are absent from the **transitive** free dependencies
of the checked-admission definition, including its residual, provider,
history and typed-path references. Under the already supplied primitive
transport/interpretation laws, the checked predicate then depends only on
`x,h`, so `(H)` follows. Merely absent printed endpoint fields or a
query-free-looking final formula do not establish that dependency condition.

Thus an additional shared hiding step has two proved routes: keep its
admission-dependent coordinate in the comparison interface, or establish
`(H)` before taking independent domain/bound marginals. A separately proved
observation-preserving checked-admitted representative theorem can cover
more cases, but it is not inferred from shared binding alone. The
certificate theorem's preservation of a complete jointly scoped relation
remains valid without asserting this extra marginalization law.

### 4.2 Production applicability

This corollary derives the previously global crosswalk from finite local
transformation proofs for this class. It does not choose the reference
interpretation as production semantics. To apply it to a selected production
generator one must establish its decorated source input, declare its complete
rule presentation, and account for every emitted transformation/rule by the
certificate. None of those source facts follows from storing four Function
children or from retaining a source label alone.

The inline B path is separate. A certificate can preserve its independently
synthesized completed interface and final query. It cannot establish the
success of an arbitrary `F_lit <: F_cb` query. Role/boundary selection still
precedes body generation; the actual Pure value retains its original entry.

## 5. Ordinary constrained uses already supply the map construction

### 5.1 Finite construction

Let a supplied finite generalizable presentation be

```text
P = (rho, Omega, I, K_P, R_P),
```

with rigid imports `rho`, eligible owned semantic binders `Omega`, its full
incidence/scope presentation `I`, residual `K_P`, and its **designated
exported root** `R_P`. Let an independently supplied finite public view be

```text
V = (rho, Y, I_V, C_V, R_V).
```

`V` is not defined to be an instance of `P`. Its validity, if claimed, comes
from the independent source/semantic criterion being studied. Both operands
must have the source-authorized sorts and boundary data required for the
ordinary query.

Construct the following ordinary use:

1. Fresh-copy every eligible owned identity and the whole `I,K_P`, fixing
   `rho`, by the coupled core's one injective identity action.
2. Apply any supplied admissible finite descriptor graft uniformly to all
   its occurrences. If no graft is needed, omit it; fresh roots can remain
   separate from the client's roots.
3. Retain `C_V` as a joint client constraint, and append one existing direct
   whole Function query `R_P^fresh <: R_V` with its complete dependencies.
4. Keep the copied local residual and original binder tree in the instance.

This is a well-formed finite constrained-use graph before its query is
solved. Its construction requires no new map rule: whole-copy reindexing,
grafting, arbitrary joint client conjunction and the ordinary single
inequality are already available. The size is

```text
|P| + |V| + number of graft/link entries + O(1),
```

counting graph, scope and incidence entries. Recursive back edges are copied,
not unfolded. The bound concerns representation, not solving complexity or
termination of the general complete Function resolver.

Call this graph and its freshening/graft data `Use(P,V)`. A successful,
solution-preserving `m_V` is obtained from it only after proving the property
below. Syntactic availability is not an assertion that its query succeeds.

### 5.2 Exact projection theorem

Write `Ext_P(v)` for satisfaction of the copied `K_P` and its direct exported
root query by a well-scoped extension of the fixed public assignment `v`.
Only eligible fresh coordinates are hidden, at their original binder
positions. In the existential-only display this is

```text
Ext_P(v) = exists z.
    K_P^fresh(v,z) and Direct(R_P^fresh(v,z), R_V(v)).
```

`Direct` abbreviates the existing complete inequality, including its existing
resolution evidence. It is not a new semantic predicate or an independently
subtyped effect port. With rigid/alternating binders the displayed notation
means the original scoped presentation, not a flattened existential block.
For example, `exists shared. forall kappa. exists body. K` retains that order.

**Theorem 3.** Projection of `Use(P,V)` is exactly the original public
solution relation `C_V` if and only if

```text
for every public assignment v: C_V(v) implies Ext_P(v).       (E_V)
```

**Proof.** Its projected relation is `C_V(v) and Ext_P(v)`. Projection cannot
introduce a public assignment outside `C_V`, which is retained conjunctively.
It loses none precisely when every `C_V` assignment has the indicated scoped
extension. This is `(E_V)`, in both directions. The freshening and graft
lemmas ensure that the copied residual is the actual original residual under
its allowed transport, not a newly reconstructed marginal formula.

Thus for every finite valid `V` the map's finite syntax is available. The
all-view proof must establish `(E_V)` for every such `V`. The quantifiers stay
`forall valid V. exists solution-preserving m_V`; the construction does not
replace this with a ground substitution chosen once for all views. Local
witnesses may vary with the public assignment while the finite retained
constraint graph stays fixed. They need no source-level existential type or
syntactic Skolem function.

This removes a separate **map-construction** obstacle for this ordinary-use
route. It does not prove all-view completeness. The earlier preimage note's
warning that arbitrary relational maps are not scheme instances remains
correct; this construction uses the already specified scheme operations.

### 5.3 Why no explicit universal-preimage descriptor is necessary here

The [safe-preimage theorem](2026-10-04-common-allowance-context-preimage.md)
characterizes containment-valid public observations by an admission clause
and a universal observation clause. Its positive-formula separation shows
that an arbitrary universal preimage cannot be generated solely by positive
forward relational operations.

`Use(P,V)` does not try to eliminate that universal condition into an effect
descriptor. It retains the ordinary whole Function query. The finite graph
therefore exists without a general universal-preimage constructor. To prove
`(E_V)` for independently containment-valid `V`, one still needs the existing
query to recognize the required complete source relation. Merely naming the
query supplies no soundness/completeness theorem for it. Validity obtained
through registered adapters additionally needs the corresponding direct
resolver evidence; containment alone is not claimed necessary for all such
views.

## 6. Exactly what common allowance must still prove

Let the original residual be `K_G(s)` and let the proposed legal common
allowance extend it as

```text
K_P(s,a) = K_G(s) and Q(s,a).
```

All old coordinates and evidence remain. `Q` must be independently generated
from source, using one legal descriptor target and its existing occurrence
paths, with all required direct completed invocation queries. It cannot be
defined by the desired success or by an arbitrary tuple of stage relations.

The original totality gate remains exactly

```text
for every xi and every s in S_xi: exists a. Q_xi(s,a).       (T)
```

The reverse projection is supplied by retained `K_G`. `(T)` still needs a
source-defined descriptor realization that preserves the required challenge
domains. Neither a derived semantic relation nor a wide support row proves
it.

There is an important distinction concerning the exported root.

**Retained-root corollary.** If `P` actually retains `R_G(s)` as its designated
exported complete root and the auxiliary `a` does not change that root or
its direct query, then `(T)` and

```text
C_V(v) implies exists s.
    K_G(s,v) and Direct(R_G(s,v),R_V(v))                    (G_V)
```

imply `(E_V)`. Take the same scoped source witness supplied by `(G_V)`, extend
it using `(T)`, and retain the unchanged direct root query. This needs no
separate leastness theorem for the auxiliary allowance. All scope conditions
of `(T)` apply under the original shared environment of that use.

**Replacement-root case.** If the designated public root is instead
`B_common(s,a)`, then `(E_V)` requires

```text
C_V(v) implies exists s,a.
    K_G(s,v) and Q(s,a)
    and Direct(B_common(s,a),R_V(v)).                     (B_V)
```

`(T)` does not supply compatibility of its allowance witness with this
additional direct query. One cannot infer `B_common <: R_V` from successful
`R_G <: B_common` and `R_G <: R_V`, or compose other concrete successes.

The retained-root corollary is conditional. This note does not silently
change the designated root of any accepted public scheme, route a use through
an unrelated hidden root, or decide that the printed common descriptor is
only a display. Its applicability must be established from the presentation
actually exported. In particular, the seven accepted common-allowance
schemes are not declared principal by this corollary.

## 7. A source witness against importing the pure structural comparison

The pure structural greatest fixed point cannot discharge the missing
complete query theorem. Its Function clause compares the value argument
contravariantly and value result covariantly; its scope explicitly excludes
effectful contracts. See concrete compatibility §3 and the
[scoped structural projection](2026-10-03-scoped-structural-projection.md)
§2. Adding recursive structural back edges does not supply source entry or
request clauses.

A nonrecursive proof-core witness already separates the two. Take a declared
well-typed request `q` and an argument carrier `t` that exposes `q` and then
returns Unit. Use a compatible ambient context with no eligible handler for
`q`. Compare receivers with the same constant Unit body and different
already-selected entry roles:

```text
f: Value(Unit) entry;              body returns Unit
g: retained Computation(E,Unit);   body returns Unit, argument unused
```

By charter §21 and [typed core](2026-10-02-typed-computation-core-elaboration.md)
§§6 and 9, invocation of `f` forces the whole inert carrier, then performs
typed rebind and the constant body. The bind request rule therefore exposes
`q` with its original continuation and the pending suffix. Invocation of `g`
retains but does not execute the unused carrier, so the exact source
presentation returns Unit without `q`.

Their value-only structural shadows both have argument/result
`Function(Unit,Unit)`. A procedure seeing only those shadows cannot
distinguish the two source behaviors, although the approved observation
projection retains the request. In particular, it cannot infer the complete
containment of the first invocation in the quiet bound of the second.

This is a counterexample to **shadow-based import of the pure structural
comparison**, not to complete descriptors, the FMP theorem, or principal
inference. It uses a proof-core reduction, not a claim that the exact surface
forms have run in the production compiler. A correct complete descriptor can
keep the different entry/provider paths. No new carrier is shown necessary.

| Pure structural solver reuse | A finite source witness refutes importing its value-only Function comparison as complete effect comparison. | A complete source-to-direct-query theorem in the existing effect descriptor semantics. |

The transformation rules are mathematical consequences of existing source
and presentation operations. They are not a claim that a production emitter
or every source program supplies their premises. The source-indexed
generator still requires its finite decorated immutable envelope, certified
primitive/provider relations, source paths/receipts and existing attachment
evidence. Current source derivability of these inputs remains separate.

Likewise, the ordinary-use graph is an existing finite representation, but
its constraints are not proved solvable for all semantically valid clients.
Neither a finite graph nor the pure structural FMP supplies that result.

Classification for the remaining callback/principality request: **C**.
There is no full main-gate proof or genuine counterexample. The advances are
the finite certificate theorem, the ordinary-use construction, and removal
of the extra map-calculus and general explicit-universal-preimage demands.
The residual is actual production certificate derivation, preservation or
safe hiding of checked-admission dependencies, and legal common
descriptor/direct-query completeness under the original quantifiers.

No solver carrier, descriptor denotation, Record width, Function variance,
rigid-scope rule, existential inference feature, rejection rule or compiler
implementation is changed or authorized.
