# C317: versioned where compiler prototype

Keep declaration choice, source pattern, role, requested outputs, clause
identity and actual center. Supply new endpoint addresses through the
existing allocation parameter of the checked canonical emission itself,
so that the emitted ground rows and child demand carry the same addresses.
Bare inputs, associated outputs and actual schema keep their old paths.

The C316 counterexample stays valid on the old boundary. Separately test
the new compiler and a corrected, explicitly bounded successor/installation:
derive the new lower/upper slot facts from the candidate frame and one
preserved live assignment, instantiate the real terminal child at the new
descriptor, then prove the same dictionary tree under the new certificate.
Do not infer an updated source kernel from rewriting one model. A stored
child's numeric owner may stay the same although its demand record changes;
the bridge must retain its actual center and recorded evidence honestly.

## Result and next interface

The fixture's repair reaches a concrete isolated successor and recursive
same-model evidence for the unchanged dictionary. The old boundary remains
a counterexample. Re-observation preserves its erased report/footprint,
current compiler rows, exact emitted occurrence, and checked declaration;
both the historical and current edges remain recorded. Their owners are
equal but the full occurrences differ. A current frontier must not be
recovered by owner identity or by treating cumulative generated edges as
the current ordered child list.

The two endpoint addresses are truly absent on the first installation,
but already locate the original two new slots on an unchanged re-read.
The isolated lookup distinguishes a fresh branch, a present-slot branch,
and a distinct occupied-without-slot case. The fixture's read-only reuse
proves its equations from the corrected journal. The generic
`StoredRow317.same_target_iff` transports a certified old row under the
same checked registry, interpretation and seed, without requiring whole
erased-report equality. A production successor still needs to show that
a present slot actually comes from a particular recorded row. Do not keep applying a fresh-only witness to
an already versioned journal; do not append a duplicate merely to assert
that a re-read is correct. Production integration and arbitrary-history
soundness are unsettled.

### Next invariant, with a non-circular proof split

For each address written by the versioned journal, track seed, checked
registry, interpretation, source declaration and clause position,
mode, translated RHS and target. Prove the lookup invariant by
induction through the actual updated state constructors:

1. If a current endpoint target is already backed by a versioned
   record, extract the exact packet and a stored `Endpoint` proof.
   Initial legacy snapshots cannot create these purpose tags by
   their allocator (the C315 high-purpose origin separation); do not
   assume a proof from mere slot presence. In the append case derive
   visibility/checked-configuration preservation separately from all
   equation satisfaction. In the reuse case use
   `StoredRow317.same_target_iff` and the already modeled record.
   `RecordedReuse317.from_recorded_rows` tests both obligations through
   precisely this route on the present fixture.
2. For truly fresh targets, the C315 batch method plus the C317
   old-support projection is the right starting point. However,
   freshness must be proved against the enlarged **semantic journal
   support**, not against the set of raw slots alone. A raw mention
   is neither a stored row nor a license to overwrite it.
3. Partition one generated demand into reuse and fresh writes. Preserve
   the old model on supported coordinates while assigning only genuinely
   fresh targets. Do not use the whole repaired successor model as an
   assumption to prove that the successor is feasible. Child argument
   intervals depend on both kinds of writes. Preserve the old
   descriptors and qualify the new frontier by the whole occurrence.
4. Prove application-dictionary compatibility for the actual ordered
   current roots. This needs both common-model class/output evidence
   and a same-model recursive certificate for each selected child;
   staged evidence should not get silently replaced with historical
   packets. The unrelated (4a) lookup/alias choice issue remains,
   even if (4b) closes for every versioned row.

These steps are about dictionary soundness, not how often a compiler
updates the frame or whether the run terminates. They do not require
changing which source programs are accepted.
