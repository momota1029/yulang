# C306 raw presentation bridge

The generic minimizer consumes a finite graph, not a finite list of raw states.
For an arbitrary raw step relation, the proposed finite presentation specifies
an admitted/reachable subset, an executable projection, equality of observation
color, raw successor closure, and the exact edge-lift law from *every* admitted
representative. Then a projected union of any raw color-preserving strong
bisimulation is a bisimulation of the finite graph. Conversely, compiled
quotient edges exactly reflect all possible raw successor classes. Missing
lift evidence cannot be replaced by checking only the finite graph.
