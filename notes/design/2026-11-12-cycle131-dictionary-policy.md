Status: Draft proof contract. I-22/I-23, 2026-11-12.

K2 policy. Raw reconstruction has no policy guard. Retain those interfaces and
prove the policy only for completed diagnostic trees justified by V3 Local.

classification implies SafeKnown at the root. A finite selected-child path
preserves classification and therefore excludes root/candidate K2 at every
visited occurrence. A rejected child prevents completed dictionary extraction.
The statements quantify over the actual SourceBoundary K2 predicates; they do
not implement the new cycle predicate or claim that Astra's current predicate
already accepts all I-22 distinctions.

of the stronger final-state semantic evidence premise in B4SkeletonV4. Evidence
alone and raw readback are deliberately not treated as policy certificates.
