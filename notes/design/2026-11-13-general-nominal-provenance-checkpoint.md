endpoints is a proved fact, not an inference from the presence of one impl.

reserved aliases. It copies a recorded nominal descriptor to one previously

readiness alone.

GeneralNominalProvenanceInputSupport proves these fresh input heads from the
empty support slice, using P input facts alone. P's provisional output is not
a premise. This supplies actual ReadyAt certificates at both recorded

GeneralNominalProvenanceExtractModel constructs a model family for arbitrary

## Concrete representative exclusion

GeneralNominalProvenancePair uses the same Checked six-role registry and source
model. It stores the actual W and G declarations, their real emitted C/D
children, every ordinary/input/output packet fact, and an explicit model.
Its requests have point intervals and direct named targets A=source 2 and
B=source 5. They represent the relevant equations after the two Extracts,
without claiming to be the P-emitted requests.

Two concrete cut models establish unavoidable support:

- Removing W-dependent output facts permits A=int, B=list int, U=str, retaining
  all input facts and G's output. Every nominal-head support for A must use W.
- Removing G-dependent output facts permits A=list int, B=int, U=str, retaining
  all input facts and W's output. Every nominal-head support for B must use G.

Both full output equations are satisfiable together. C's actual first input
is source 4=list B, and D's is source 1=list A. The normalized journal paths
4→5 and 1→2 are proved with explicit nominal argument observations. Every
packet in this representative journal has an unresolved child. Consequently
mutual_value_trap applies: W and G are not Grounded, and no Supported
alternative can supply A's or B's nominal head.

GeneralNominalProvenancePairViews strengthens this result to every certified
View of that journal. It transports the source basis, explicit nominal edges,
waiting paths, and discharge facts; support slices themselves are unchanged.
Neither A nor B has any finite HeadDepth after such a View. Thus the exclusion
does not depend on leaving either head absent from the cached observation.
This still does not establish a complete candidate DecisionGuard theorem for
the real P child: its actual frames and shared Extract targets remain to be
connected to these cuts.

The representative journal cannot simply be identified with the actual
checkpoint: it omits P/Extract packets, uses different occurrence IDs and
point bounds, and its current source basis does not reserve the W/G output
identities. Its semantic HeadSupported witnesses are not certified refresh
steps. No literal reachability or ready observation is inferred from them.

endpoints, their outer list inspection likewise duplicates binder 0 and creates
fresh merged endpoints. The point representative's input-model lemmas therefore
cannot be reused by merely changing occurrence IDs. A reusable nonpoint

independent. The current Extract-only witness defaults unrelated fresh IDs to A;
using it unchanged for W would impose A≈list A. That is a witness-construction
mistake, not evidence of a recursive type in the program.

The actual W/G/ExtractB packets, their full guards, model witnesses and Steps,
and the cut-model transport retaining P and both discharged Extracts remain
open. No genuine obstruction to the requested execution was established, so
no owner question is posed. The first requested obligation is still incomplete;
