finite JournalSubst249 operations. Prove model congruence for this inductively

fresh block. Under old/fresh SafeOrder, fresh-target separation from rewritten
metadata/consumers and public separation from both domains, prove exact public
model existence iff using one witness in each direction. Establish that the
