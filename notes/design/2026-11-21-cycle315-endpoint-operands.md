clause/index cell, then soundly reobserves nominal arguments with a
strictly stronger lower endpoint. The source input's actual value and
associated output are unchanged. The old lower row cannot be overwritten
in its live models. The C314 contract asked for operand agreement even in

a failure of the recursive theorem when its child premise is impossible.
