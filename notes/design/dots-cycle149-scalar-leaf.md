input binders, an actual located point slot with no nominal descriptor, scalar
view matching the scalar query, and role agreement. Compute its report directly,
derive private source models/projection/empty materialization for every current
model, construct Instance, and compare with current selection by uniqueness.
Instantiate the structural predicate at the existing installed recursive finish,
not just its earlier context. No historical leaf packet is required.
