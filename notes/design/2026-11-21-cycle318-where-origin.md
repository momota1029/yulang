# C318: record provenance for versioned where slots

The proof must not infer an equation from an address lookup. First prove
that an identified, materialized compound input cell produces the exact
row via the checked canonical compiler. Record the appended endpoint
bindings as an explicit, checkable delta against the real before/after
raw source maps. Then prove that successful lookup of a versioned slot
in a history built from those deltas yields the actual generating
packet plus its recorded compiler row. Crucially, refresh must preserve
source IDs and configuration; a fresh delta cannot write any already
present raw, reserved, or journal-supported target.

Only after the provenance result, combine the old-row proof with a
fresh assignment on disjoint coordinates. Aim for a genuine mixed
lookup in one checked child interval (one same source footprint, one
new footprint). Expose exactly where raw feasibility, final frame
closure, and recursive selected children remain unproved. Keep original
history, order, and choices. The current kernel and public language
are not changed.
