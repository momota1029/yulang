# Keep witness answers aligned to the ordered plan entries

A finite symbolic question has an ordinal tied to its actual source
representative, not a portable declaration ID. A declaration can have
several concrete plan representatives. Attach each plan's answers to
that very plan while mapping the original finite sequence. Classify
known and unknown head entries in the original order. The existing
eligibility test then reads that annotated row; no equality test on
entire plans, no guessing a lookup by semantic ID, and no deletion of
duplicate or equivalent roots.

The annotated scan must equal the existing scan for every original list
and supplied probe functions, including partial head replies. For
symbolic replies, project positive evidence only on a committed
`some true`; absence is not a negative certificate. Retain the existing
missing-edge novelty guard, and do not assert V8 diagnostic completeness.
