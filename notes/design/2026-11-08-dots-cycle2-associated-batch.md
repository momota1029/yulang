# Fixed determined associated requests: semantic stabilization (Draft)

This is a finite, explicit fragment, not the general saturation driver.
A fixed list of requests has first-order receivers; the actual assocOf result
is independent of the ordinary state. Known scalar results append equations;
unknown/unsupported results remain pending data and do not cause rejection.
The ordinary solver is applied only to the accumulated constraints.

For every shared assignment the fold's models are exactly old-state models
and every computable associated equation. From this derive semantic
idempotence, preservation of all old constraints, and permutation invariance
of solutions for this FIXED request list. General sibling order independence
is not assumed or asserted. State-dependent receiver materialization, new
child generation and final pending classification are excluded explicitly.
