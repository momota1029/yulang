## What the two existing descent interfaces actually quantify over

`CycleReadback.Readback roles registry context` chooses ONE pair of functions
`lower upper : Occurrence roles -> List Pattern` for the entire context. For
EVERY CURRENT `CycleK2.Local ... parent (.selected key children)` and EVERY
child in that list, it supplies a visible checked declaration, actual current
argument/report/emission witnesses, and a requirement clause with the allocated
child identity. One pair of finite substitutions `lo,hi` must simultaneously
satisfy the four parent-head/child-clause label equations. Checked Paterson
capture-stability then decreases the summed lower/upper checker mass. This
supports same-role recursion, but the Readback producer remains a premise;
the interface alone does not establish semantic adequacy of those labels.

Cycle174 `RoleStratified` instead ranks declaration roles and proves descent
for EVERY current agreed-Local selected edge by extracting its actual emitted
clause from the Survivor. It needs no finite readback, journal membership,
model, or installation. It excludes all same-role clauses and role cycles,
including language-valid structurally decreasing recursion. Role DAG exactness
therefore does not finish the general `whereAccessible` field.

## Minimal bridge, and what is NOT that bridge

**First small adapter:** prove current agreed-Local selection implies current
`CycleK2.Local` selection. `selected_old` already returns FeedbackK2.Local;
its selected safety excludes the old raw CycleK2 RootK2/CandidateK2 by their
left injections, with the same Unique/Survivor. Then an existing
`CycleReadback.Readback roles registry state.current.context` gives accessibility
of agreed Local by relation inclusion. This changes only the consumer interface,
not the missing source producer. It needs neither stability nor provenance-gap
policy premises, unlike `selected_available`.

**Next substantive minimum:** construct one coherent finite labelling for all
current selected edges (or a root-restricted downward-closed family with a new
restricted accessibility theorem). Quantifiers must be
`exists labels, forall current selected parent/child ...`, not separately chosen
labels per edge, per packet, per model, or per path. A direct agreed-Local
readback consumer is also possible and avoids labelling irrelevant old-Local
selections, but still needs this simultaneous source producer. Preserve actual
source constructor keys and opaque finite leaves for uninspected cyclic values.

The newer canonical-hole note provides uniform model-indexed labelled meanings,
shared substitution transport, and compatible **historical nonterminal packet**
presentations with `Below` comparisons. It explicitly leaves new-packet
presentation, newly relevant centers/captures/binders, and universal
`Simulation.next` production open. A history ancestry bound is not a bound on
all current Local Child edges. Moreover its `Below`/mass presentation is not
literally the older Readback's four exact syntax equalities; a weighted
inequality consumer is a sensible alternative to forcing those equalities.

There are TWO distinct gaps even at a halted state:

1. `selected_committed` proves a matching packet is **Committed**, meaning all
   current models satisfy its Holds and its child edges are in the journal.
   It does NOT prove that this packet belongs to `state.current.packets`.
   Available may use the installable branch; stability rules out novel effects,
   not nonmembership. Thus historical `History` cannot simply be specialized
   to this packet. Current witnesses also need not be historical witnesses
   after refresh, even if their key/children agree.
2. Birth lineage concerns installed ancestry from the root. General
   `whereAccessible` concerns current selected descent. To use a historical
   presentation, prove literal matching coverage for EACH relevant current
   selected edge, or construct its fresh compatible presentation directly.
   A Generated-edge conclusion alone supplies neither packet presentation nor
   the global label consistency needed for a well-founded rank.

For centered transitions, request only the explicit current selection/emission,
model restriction, journal/lineage and actual K2 interfaces required by this
bridge. The integration note correctly does not convert absent Labelled,
Presentation or Backed proofs into semantic pending. This investigation supplies
no such rule and does not equate interval endpoints with actual centers.
