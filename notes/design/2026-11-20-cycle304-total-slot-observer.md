# C304: total finite observation of source slots

A successful single-direction lookup was already transported by
`GeneralDictionaryRefinedLocations`. For two source-preserving refinements in
opposite directions, prove the stronger total equality, including `none`.
The two slot maps are mutual inverses by source-ID injectivity. A missing
source is not an allocated but unresolved slot. Compute a three-way
`missing/unresolved/known` observer and prove its exact interpretation and
reindexing stability. Also compute B4 input pending independently by scanning
the finite argument vector.

This supports source-index changes and unused shape-catalogue changes
without claiming that arbitrary changes to recursive reports preserve
selection. Report erasure, child emission and finite satisfiability tests
remain separate obligations for a general compiler.
