## Remaining representation cut for declared covariance

The approved unary covariant example is implementable in the declaration-directed inspector: actual same-tag arms produce a merged constructor-sized payload, both comparisons to the whole union are ordinary atoms, and each binding in the normalized payload gets that same whole. The basic positive fixture reads two distinct names and does not split the arms. Its companion tests distinguish an unmentioned arm, other tag, variance and nominal identity.

The existing general tagged route goes through the primitive/invariant expression interpreter. When neither interpretation applies, the real application and enclosing tagged translate both return none. Enabling the invariant flag is semantically unsound for this use: on the existing invariant carrier, neither Box<Int> nor Box<Bool> is below Box<Int|Bool>. This is an artifact coverage limit, not language rejection and not an ambiguity for the owner to decide.

A narrow next step is to add a source-backed declared variance adapter with an independent type/kind/variance registry. Establish covariant projection-to-capture and order of side conditions in the correct carrier before incorporating it into an ordered dictionary. Keep the released origin record, prior/current labels and runtime witness production independent; do not fill the gap by changing source policy, selecting endpoints as centers, or splitting the capture into separate arm values. C337 open/closed observations and C334 literal semantics must remain explicitly distinguished.
