For a used constructor parameter keep the source child ordinal, actual kind and the effective mode with inherited invariant context. Project an original bound to its exact directed faces in the already-defined contextual comparison jobs:

| effective mode | original lower argument | original upper argument |
| --- | --- | --- |
| covariant | point, job 0 left | point, job 0 right |

Each listed one-sided port is the actual endpoint at its same ordinal and face **if that local paired job is formed**. This list of comparison ports does not assert that a candidate actually visited any one of them, or that an absent opposite read necessarily gives pending or rejection.
