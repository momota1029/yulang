# C304: the first operational edge after early observation

I-54 keeps this wholly on the Lean B4 boundary. C302 gives the concrete
pre-promotion phases `Phase n k` and a classification of every legal promotion
out of those phases. C303 separately supplies observation-certificate transport
over matched installs. Neither says that the real kernel takes the next install.

The first target is the actual `GeneralNominalVersionedMixedKernel234.Step`,
not an auxiliary execution relation: for every phase `p : Phase n k`, `k ≤ n`,
and context `ctx` with `Refresh p.state ctx` and
`Known ctx.state (n+1)`, the successors of `refresh p.state ctx` should be
exactly the installation of a transported list-display effect. The latter
knownness is precisely the strict source observation under discussion, not a
claim that arbitrary context updates become enabled raw edges.

* Existence: transport the frame using the source embedding; derive reservation
  freshness before applying the compiler. Recover the inherited trusted cache
  from actual reachability, and establish all current and emitted output-class
  obligations independently. The new generated edge witnesses noncommitment.
* Uniqueness: eliminate enqueue, further strict refresh, semantic equality,
  point revision and merge through the existing source observations and
  separating model. For a real install, keep the generated occurrence from
  the action: previously visited occurrences are already semantically
  committed, while the frontier's frame and effect are forced.
* Regression: legal promotion can widen the shape catalogue by an unused
  empty-record entry; then it cannot be identical to a phase of the previous
  zero-shape context family. Do not confuse a different raw state with a
  different dictionary.
* Continuation: apply C303's certificate lift to the selected install and
  verify model and context alignment. This does not alone transport a second
  kernel step. The next invariant must carry equality of emitted sites,
  generated edges, aliases and semantic commitment, but need not identify
  the historical plan/effect lists of the two raw states. Exact suffix length
  and terminal dictionary equivalence for arbitrary refreshes require that
  invariant.
