# C304: what a general current-head observer must keep

For I-54 the exact ladder theorem does not prove a general compiler or general
termination. To move toward a general observer, use the extensional contract
actually consumed by the mixed journal's B4 classifiers. Work with an arbitrary
Lean registry, current journal and role occurrence, not Rust CST.

The dynamic head view supplies equality of current context, raw/centered rows,
edges and aliases, and equivalence of full models. This is enough for candidate
selection, raw and semantic K2, pending guards, shared-argument feasibility and
the ordinary post-choice output contradiction. These use the exact current
frame, output requests, and models. The recursive dictionary evidence relation
is separately transported one local certificate at a time.

For the merged-class contradiction (V7), full models are not the right oracle:
the rule checks a feasible *history slice* before activating a further class.
Preserve the history model predicate and each class's equations on those history
models. Raw rows, aliases and the ordinary atom pool determine which contexts
may be sliced. The transfer proof must preserve both the positive feasible slice
and the absence of a model after activating the focused class.

Target: same exact V8 frontier for all registries/demands/frontiers under the
above independent inputs. Do not infer history-slice agreement from full-model
agreement. In particular, this is an observation/proof contract, not a claim
to compute its semantic predicates by finite search. The next general compiler
task is to discharge the contract for a new compact representation rather
than simply assuming equality of all histories or of the classifier itself.
