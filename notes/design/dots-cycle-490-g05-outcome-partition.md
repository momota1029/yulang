Candidate rejection by an actual visited constructor return is separate from an unsuccessful document or token replay. Keep the existing `routeG05WithDeferredLocal489` and C488 tagged checker unchanged.

1. For every exact original demand, independently permitted Trail prefixes and each supplied callback, establish K2 iff the original preflight lists precisely those authored positions. Show the full report cannot introduce another K2 branch. A positive carries the same finite quotient return for every model of the original State.
2. On all-safe input, define an ordered family of local choices on the finite original visible roster. Its judgment reads original slot, ordinal and declaration and uses the independent local meaning at that slot. Do not build a new matcher. The exact report can replay any such family, not just a canonical local choice. Characterize the observed continuation and non-replay when only some evidence has been supplied.
3. For two finite local protocols for the same independent relation, re-encode a successful full report with fresh finite witness under the other protocol. Do not transport raw tokens as authority. Verify K2 is invariant without local assumptions, and continued outcomes agree when the same chosen mark list is replayed. If required, single-valuedness is a separately named hypothesis rather than a new language rule.

These are components of the C487 goal, not a declaration of `G05Goal` or a proof of `G04Entry`. Compiler provision of independent visit and matching authority is outside this work.
