## 3. Meaning of the claimed theorems

[SRC, SRC-J, GS and GC](../theory/2026-10-08-source-generalize-definition-and-proof.md#6-direct-rule-adequacy-lemma)
are independently reviewed direct source-rule construction and inversion
proofs. They are not the previous Pack/Decode or freshening results.

The soundness assertion is that one global satisfying assignment
and strategy of an allocated finite joint client program, including arbitrary
well-scoped client constraints W, constructs one independently legal joined
source derivation. It preserves the actual binding object, source partition
between independent instances and monomorphic aliases, original binder
dependencies, current configurations, histories and sequential source joins.

The completeness assertion ranges over finite independent joint
source derivations and finite recursive proof grammars, including nonidentity
result checking, lawful narrower domains, structural checks and actual admitted
conversions. Inversion constructs a finite allocation schema before assignments,
challenges and histories. Both directions preserve the original scoped
strategies and exactly the client's public solution-and-observation fiber.
Legality is not defined by exporter or resolver success and is not restricted
to identical complete kernels.

The genuine primitive, source constructor, original-world, checking,
annotation, recursive-introduction and lifetime laws remain the explicit
local premises L1–L5. Selecting Generalize does not prove an unprovided law
or redefine a foreign primitive. For an extension whose own local source law
has not been established, this definition gives no instance of that law;
it does not reject the source form or add an annotation requirement.
