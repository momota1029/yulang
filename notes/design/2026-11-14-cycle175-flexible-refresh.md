# Nominal-only query refresh and whole dictionary evidence

## Quantified contract

For any fixed-carrier observation satisfying Refines and any translated query
forest consisting only of variables and nominal constructors, a finished
inspection retains exactly the same report even when the normalized view
changes. For a declaration query family satisfying the fragment condition,
bindings and ordered child occurrences are then preserved through that report. Scalar, node, and deferred
queries are not members of this syntactic fragment. Refines still preserves all
already-present nominal descriptors. This is not descriptor replacement.

Every ordinary actual history step and any finite number of actual refreshing
steps may interleave. Each refresh has the original Unknown-to-Known certificate
and additionally either equal views or the nominal-only fragment for all current
registry queries. SourceFormed and the current-model packet invariant are
constructed from InitialSource and empty journal. Instance/Evidence are not
transition guards. Each retained packet carries its registry membership.

At a full actual halted endpoint, the boundary closure theorem supplies stable
root and generated children. Independent successful Classify then yields whole
recursive dictionary Evidence in every final model. No recursive child Evidence
is supplied. The old174 fixed-view relation embeds in the new relation.

## Limits and next exact bridge

This does not prove that the existing general ground executor chooses only
these steps. It neither constructs successful classification nor handles a
carrier change or arbitrary replacement of nominal descriptors. It consequently
does not close the general evidenceNode field or claim general B4 completion.

Existing InspectionLocality and KeyedRevision already provide finite-footprint
transport and untracked revision traces. A future tracked-Actual bridge must distinguish disjoint
inspection footprints from touched descriptors. Models equivalence and source
Improves alone do not preserve captured bounds or child order. For disjoint
footprints, reuse existing inspect_local/report_local instead of reproving them.
The missing step is a guard producer and transfer to the tracked actual relation. For touched descriptors,
derive compatible packet/ordered-child mapping rather than assume the desired
Instance/Evidence conclusion. General canonicalLocalOrder remains separate.
