## 1. Contradiction

The Gate3b matrix §1 defines every row as ordered equality between the
embedded AST fact stream and direct committed records.  T2 assigns
`A ->@ B` to Type(ArrowRhs); inside `R({type T = A ->@ B})` its local range
shifts by `+15` to `19..21`.

The retry-leading amendment consequently named that literal as required T2a
evidence.  Its successor direct construction is capable of the local
`Type(ArrowRhs)`, Error, `4..6`, TypeExpression record and whole-run
unexpected evidence, but it has no Yumark entrypoint at O3a.

The current legacy AST helper
`recover_required_type_item_for_ast` is intentionally source-free: it scans
the malformed prefix and continuation only.  It does not publish an embedded
recovery fact.  `parse_gate3_ast` therefore has no ArrowRhs fact for the
literal, while the legacy direct adapter records `19..21`.  Changing
`rewrite/{emit,item,type_expr}.rs` cannot affect that AST stream.  Adding the
missing legacy `EmbeddedObservedAst` transport would restart an uncompleted
legacy owner-adoption slice, which the rewrite plan explicitly pauses.

At the same time, the typed-output amendment defines O3a as a construction
checkpoint, O4 as the Type/PV and rollback certification gate, and O6 as the
successor Yumark-convergence gate.  Requiring an actual successor embedded
AST/direct fact equality before O6 is therefore a false timing requirement,
not a reason to weaken the matrix or to restore legacy transport.

## 2. Decision

T2a keeps every approved local successor contract.  In particular,
`A ->@ B` continues to produce exactly one successor direct
`Type(ArrowRhs)`, Error record at `4..6` with primary TypeExpression,
committed-rule source, primary expectation zero, and one `OtherCharacter`
unexpected fact at `4..6`; its Error CST is `@` at `4..5`, and the valid retry
Item retains its leading space and produces RHS `6..7`.  The two typed Missing
sites, PendingBoundary coordinate rule, frozen reconciliation, and
successor-native RB-T vector also remain mandatory.

The legacy literal `R({type T = A ->@ B})` remains the exact embedded oracle:
the mapped direct fact is Type(ArrowRhs), Error, `19..21`, TypeExpression.
During O3a it establishes the fixed outer range mapping and legacy structural
control only; it is not treated as evidence that the private successor parser
has entered Yumark or that legacy AST fact publication occurred.

The matrix's ordered equality contract is preserved intact but is timed as
follows:

- O3a/T2a construction proves the local successor direct record, CST/Item
  ownership, local fresh/frozen/reconciliation/rollback controls, and the
  `+15` mapping to the named embedded oracle.  It does **not** assert an
  absent legacy AST recovery fact or fabricate/shadow/replay one.
- O4 may close only T2a's local successor subproof: direct CST/Item/recovery
  evidence, frozen reconciliation, and RB-T.  The T2 Gate0/matrix row remains
  Open and earns no complete-T/PV or common-template certification credit
  before successor Yumark adoption; it still requires the omitted embedded
  equality at O6.
- O6, when successor output is adopted by Yumark, re-executes the embedded
  matrix equality.  For this literal it must compare the actual embedded AST
  fact stream and successor committed records in order at Type(ArrowRhs),
  Error, `19..21`, TypeExpression, then prove lossless prefix, exact
  remainder, balanced frame pop, and a clean following literal through the
  real successor route.  It retains the matrix §7 fixed frame-pop literal
  unchanged and adds this T2 literal separately.  Direct-only auxiliary
  expectation unions remain excluded as matrix §1 already says.

No range, record, CST, Item, AST shape, diagnostic identity, source-order, or
rollback fact is relaxed.  This is an evidence-placement correction only.

The phrase in the retry-leading amendment's original pre-approval scope that
its proposal did not authorize implementation was true while it was Draft.
After the recorded user approval, its §3 is the operative scope: the sealed
operation and only T2a implementation are authorized.  This correction makes
that status transition explicit without authorizing any further owner.

## 3. Required evidence and exclusions

Before the O3a T2a construction checkpoint can close:

- retain every retry-leading amendment §4 local ArrowRhs/output control,
  including direct negative capability cases, fresh/frozen equality and
  mismatch, exact fenced PendingBoundary `9..9`, and successor-native RB-T;
- assert the `R({type T = A ->@ B})` byte mapping as `+15 -> 19..21` only as
  a shifted-origin successor local run plus the existing legacy direct outer
  structural/lossless/remainder baseline; it earns no successor-Yumark frame
  pop or matrix §7 credit and does not assert a non-existent legacy AST
  ArrowRhs fact; and
- do not modify `grammar/type_expr.rs`, Yumark driver/session recovery
  transport, a legacy AST test contract, or public dispatch for this purpose.

At O6, add the actual successor-Yumark embedded test that proves the matrix
fact equality and the T2 committed-recovery -> frame-pop -> clean-following
literal sequence, while retaining the matrix §7 fixed literal as an
independent control.  It must use the real integrated successor route, not a
copied direct record, a second parse, source replay, or a test-only fact.

PathSegment/T2b and every other matrix owner remain outside this correction.
They receive no construction or certification credit from T2a.
