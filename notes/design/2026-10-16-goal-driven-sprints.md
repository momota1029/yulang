## Implementation boundary

Use one fixed sprint contract and one small observed-state snapshot. Internal
steps are not independent research goals. Proof, source/native correspondence,
implementation and production closure remain separate; only a fresh independent
review and primary adjudication can promote a claim in its original scope.
An Astra assignment can span construction, lemmas, counterexample search,
source constructors and native correspondence under one bounded output lease.
Sol coauthors/counterexample contributors are not eligible to certify the same
result; appoint a fresh isolated reviewer for its frozen evidence.

No worker was started by this maintenance change. Existing sessions must reread
these entry points and reconcile all known/unknown workers and leases before
adoption. The offline checker validates a protocol model and file consistency;
it cannot authenticate runtime receipts, enforce an OS sandbox, assess a proof,
or certify that a particular model actually ran.
