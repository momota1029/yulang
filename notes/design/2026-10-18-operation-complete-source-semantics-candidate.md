## 6. Typed action words: normalization, evaluation, composition

Let `a:t->u` be a primitive transition whose WHOLE dependent data are fixed:
source substitution/insertion/use, an actual Bind/Call/consumer phase,
R/Z introduction, a P W edit, actual capture/use/extrusion/intrusion map,
or an opaque original action with missing owner body. Indices include the
original witness, provider/use/receiver and residual lineage. They do not
consist just of endpoint row IDs.

Raw typed words and their normal forms are [P definitions]:

```text
word(t,u) ::= id_t | atom(a:t->u) | comp(v:u->r,w:t->u)
nf(id_t)       = []_t
nf(atom(a))    = [a]
nf(comp(v,w))  = nf(w) ++ nf(v)       # execute w BEFORE v
quote([]_t)    = id_t
quote(a::xs)   = comp(quote(xs),atom(a))
```

Only dependent lists whose adjacent full indices agree are well formed.
Normalization removes syntactic identities and parentheses. It NEVER sorts,
deduplicates, cancels inverse-looking steps, equates two providers, identifies
two uses, or deletes administrative frames. Even extensionally equal endpoint
maps are not an extra rewrite law.

Use a transition result consisting of the current whole presentation plus
an ordered diagnostic log. A presentation retains every original membership,
occurrence, future/symbolic/dependency and pending-consumer record. `Need` is
a diagnostic attached to that presentation, not loss of its original tuple.
A primitive step `step_a` performs the bodies in §§3–5,7–8 when supplied;
for an unavailable original body it retains the target whole witness and
records that exact missing owner. Thus traversal is total as a mathematical
operation with diagnostics. Its SUCCESSFUL incidence action is partial.

Evaluation is [D over P]:

```text
ev([],S)       = S
ev(a::xs,S)    = ev(xs,step_a(S))
eval(w,S)      = ev(nf(w),S).
```

Pending and missing bodies remain in the log, with source action position.
A later source proof can resolve a dependency through its real rule; removing
an earlier log entry is not a normalization equation or an invented proof.
No state is treated as a completed original incidence merely because the
traversal reaches its last letter.

Candidate laws, with explicit proofs:

* `nf` produces a composable list with the same full source/target indices:
  structural induction on the raw word; comp concatenates at its shared index.
* `ev(xs++ys,S)=ev(ys,ev(xs,S))`: induction on xs, with the cons case reducing
  both sides to the induction hypothesis at `step_a(S)`.
* `eval(id,S)=S` and
  `eval(comp(v,w),S)=eval(v,eval(w,S))`: substitute the definition of nf and
  use the preceding list law. This includes the diagnostic/pending states.
* `nf` is invariant under the syntactic unit and associativity equations:
  empty-list units and associativity of concatenation. Hence evaluation is
  invariant under those equations. No semantic coherence equation is assumed.
* `nf(quote(xs))=xs`, by list induction; consequently
  `nf(quote(nf(w)))=nf(w)`. Induction on w using the unit/associativity
  equations also gives `w = quote(nf(w))` in that SYNTACTIC quotient. Thus
  these lists are canonical for precisely that quotient. No identity between
  distinct atomic histories follows from this normal form.

These laws specify action-word normalization/evaluation/composition completely
at the stated syntax. They do not prove that an opaque atom has a successful
body or that a successful P body reflects into O. An original action is not
proved by storing it as a letter. Forgetting a successful P edit commutes with
the STORED actual whole edit by direct projection; this does not supply its
missing old incidence or domain laws.
