## Whole-domain reduction before another restricted nominal fragment

EvidenceNode assumes ordered child evidence in ONE specified model. Child evidence
implies registration. In corrected traces, registration comes from the initial
root or an actually installed parent; enqueue cannot add an unregistered generated
node. Child identity encodes its parent invocation and declaration. Thus evidence
for even one child of a current selected plan forces a historical installation
of the same parent identity and declaration, provided its identity is not the
initial root. Numeric allocation gives that exclusion.

Prove this provenance inversion without syntax/point/terminal restrictions.
Careful: identity equality alone does NOT imply equal parent occurrence/endpoints,
nor current report equality, nor a current Frame for the historical plan. The
result therefore separates genuinely fresh invocations from historical replay;
it does not pretend to close replay. It should reduce the fresh branch of I40(B)
to terminal parents when the full EvidenceNode child premise is used.

Remaining largest tasks: terminal fresh model gluing; historical current-frame
and packet replay across nominal report/endpoint changes; choice compatibility
for merged historical aliases. These remain full-domain obligations, not new
selection guards. All new theorems are auxiliary to dictionary soundness.
