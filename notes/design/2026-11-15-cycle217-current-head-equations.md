## Counterexample search: a live, nonempty sharing obstruction

A stronger candidate than216's helper regression is a ground root
`P[Foo[variant0 int, variant0 bool]]`, represented by a nominal descriptor whose
parameters both have loose interval `[variant0 bottom, variant0 top]`.
A distinct-variable parent impl `P[Foo[x,y]] where R[x,y]` preserves Paterson
size descent. Its child has differing actual centers but overlapping raw bounds.
The terminal candidate `R[T,T]` may therefore pass raw matching while actual
sharing fails. This is NOT a proved reachable/stable/selected counterexample.

The naive `[bottom,top]` envelope fails immediately: its lower endpoint is
unknown and RootPending prevents selection. The variant envelope has a known
outer structural tag, while a variable head does not inspect its payload.

Independent read-only review confirms that GroundRoots fixes semantic root
values, not singleton nominal descriptor parameters; Paterson does not exclude
the proposed parent/child. Refresh cannot introduce a new source identity:
its support stays inside the prior raw source list. Reobserve retains the
slot set and can improve existing descriptors to point parameters. Thus avoid
exact-parameter raw slots; actual child centers are in the high-purpose
center namespace rather than raw allocation support. This needs a formal proof
covering reserved/allocated and row source identities, not an intuition.

Remaining counterexample obligations: construct a checked compiled registry and
source, prove actual parent Installable/common model; refresh child low endpoints
with valid trusted cache certificates; saturate semantic equate transitions;
exclude every further refresh/reobserve/merge/install at the chosen child;
show current selection and nonempty Models; rule out packet meaning for EVERY
eligible frame, not just one chosen representation. Terminal child dictionaries
are empty. No language change or I40 refutation is claimed from this probe.
