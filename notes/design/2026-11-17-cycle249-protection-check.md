# Finite support protection certificate

Validate a materialized finite typed-read list against fresh entry targets with
a terminating Bool traversal and prove exactness. Given old-target closure, only
fresh targets absent from the old domain need additional protection. A failed
certificate is not a language rejection and cannot replace a source privacy
invariant. Any wrapper over actual Journal.compile remains noncomputable because
its existing alias request enumeration is noncomputable; the checker itself on
finite materialized lists is executable. Public raw coordinates require their
own old/fresh separation, even when metadata is compiled to expressions.
