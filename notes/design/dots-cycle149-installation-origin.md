The existing successful recursive fixture provides a concrete limit:
`GeneralDictionaryRecordedCoverageLimit` proves that its entire actual trace
belongs to the new ordinary-only subclass, its selected terminal child is
root-generated and stable, and no packet at that child's occurrence is stored.

## Exact edge origins

`GeneralDictionaryEdgeOrigin` proves direct edge membership iff a recorded
packet at that exact source occurrence emits the child. This reverse direction
survives arbitrary actual support steps, including descriptor changes. A
committed nonterminal packet therefore forces some actual recorded packet at
the same occurrence, without claiming it is the same packet or has the same
key. Combined with stable selected Joint it discharges occurrence coverage.
Conversely, a truly unrecorded stable selected nonterminal focus cannot satisfy
Joint. This does not assert existence of such a focus or refute V5; the model
intersection/Joint obligation is still explicit, and terminals are excluded.
