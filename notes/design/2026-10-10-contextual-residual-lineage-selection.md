## Selected policy

Keep each residual recipe's constructor lineage as its identity. Do not merge
separately created recipes merely because their source rows become equal or
their current residual keys compare equal. For every applicable lower bound
admitted to an identified source row, propagate it to every retained recipe,
including future lowers. Preserve the attachment, filter, origin, and exact
context of each recipe's emitted relation.

The selected parent-copy intrusion rule still applies to each actual recorded
pair that qualifies in one SCC. Equate those row variables and transfer both
bound sides, origins, and every contextual replay obligation. Parent-copy
equality does not erase separate residual-constructor lineage. This includes a
gamma pair when that pair's own provenance and SCC membership qualify.

Finding an exact-image congruence theorem later does not automatically switch
the implementation to residual-key coalescing. Such a policy change requires a
new reviewed design and renewed user approval.

## Preserved contract and open obligations

The selected annotation meanings remain unchanged: contravariant concrete
`[E]` permits local subtraction at that annotation position, and covariant
concrete `[E]` permits that effect. The callback target remains

No resource cap, source restriction, rejection boundary, diagnostic change,
annotation semantic change, or F5 cutover is selected. The original reviewed
design remains applicable for every section other than the identity choice
selected here; its open obligations are not weakened.
