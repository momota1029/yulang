# T18 — unmatched opening delimiter recovery

Completed 2026-11-09 on `parser/t18`; changes remain in the working tree. No commit or push.

## Change

Applied the approved option 1 policy. The existing single linear source/token matching pass now also records paired closers and closure evidence for opaque apostrophe brackets/effect rows and rule bodies. Matched delimiters retain ordinary multiline parsing. Only unmatched owners yield at declaration/control heads or colon-application heads at or outside the enclosing statement's indentation. Colon applications are recognized by structure, independently of the callee name (`say:`, `emit:`, and Unicode names).

Use groups, braced declaration companions, and rule bodies now enter the same delimiter scope as expressions, types, patterns, and declaration fields. A wrong closer produces one local close failure instead of a foreign-close Error followed by another Missing. Paired outer closers stay unread for their owners. Close diagnostics expose `delimiter not closed`; ordinary absent closers retain zero-width Missing nodes.

Unclosed declaration fields yield `with` to the declaration shell, including from field/type recovery. The shell preserves the companion after the missing closer. Field recovery cannot reinterpret its body as more fields. This intentionally updates the historical actual-close-only recovery restriction under T18's explicit authorization; accepted syntax is unchanged.

The pass remains O(source bytes), with iterative matching/opaque stacks. Paired-closer storage adds one boolean per source byte. No new recursive grammar cycle or unbudgeted descent was added; `nesting.rs` is unchanged.

- `crates/yu-syntax/src/declaration/{fields,struct_decl,use_decl,declaration_companion}.rs` and `src/rule/mod.rs`: owner integration and companion sealing.
- `crates/yu-syntax/src/structural_diagnostic.rs`: close messages and brace/field-close schema slots.
- `crates/yu-syntax/tests/unmatched_delimiters.rs`: 13 integration tests covering all six mixed pairs, nested unmatched groups, `say`, `for` before/inside headers, nested indentation, caller closes, companions, additional delimiter owners, valid multiline shapes, UTF-8/CRLF typing prefixes and delimiter mutations. Every parse checks exact reconstruction and token byte ranges.
- `crates/yu-syntax/src/tests/recovery_mutation.rs`: two malformed-input expectations updated to the approved policy: `for` is a recovery boundary; a foreign-close failure no longer also requires Missing. Ordinary-name/deeper-declaration controls remain.

No changes to `lean/`, `spec/`, or `tools/`. The protected fingerprint file and fingerprint test are unchanged; no fingerprint rows were replaced or regenerated. Three additional valid CST hashes were captured before implementation and pass unchanged.

| Nested `([{1` followed by statements | 3 | 3 | One diagnostic per opener; later statements are independent. |

E2's diagnostic count was already minimal: its improvement is statement preservation and locality, not eliminating the necessary close diagnostic. An incomplete `for x in (xs` has two diagnostics: one close failure plus its independently missing body/introducer.

The full audit's maximum diagnostics improved from 21 to 19 for token deletion and from 27 to 21 for opener insertion. It is not entirely repaired: the E3 string-interpolation deletion still yields 19 diagnostics. Ordinary multiline-string ambiguity and the actual LS completion/hover/semantic-token layer were not changed or verified.
