# Orchestration budget and operating modes

For the unchanged general contract, read [orchestration-budget.pre-sprint.md](orchestration-budget.pre-sprint.md).
That byte-preserved baseline remains binding outside the precise supersession
scope in [the sprint decision](../notes/design/2026-10-16-goal-driven-sprints.md).

For authorized ongoing `research/simple-sub-intrusion` work,
[goal-driven-sprints.md](goal-driven-sprints.md) is the active scheduling authority.
Its explicit scoped overrides take precedence regardless of read order.

M0-M3 reviewer limits, independent-review requirements, delta review, finding
batching, expected-output protection and measurement/verification safety remain
unchanged. They apply to a coherent artifact, not to every internal sprint step.
The sprint budget separately caps agents, Astra assignments and local processes.
Record synchronization goes to compact current state plus a durable milestone
receipt; do not append a duplicate narrative to every historical task ledger.
